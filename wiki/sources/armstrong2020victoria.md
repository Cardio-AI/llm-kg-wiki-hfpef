# VICTORIA

> Vericiguat (sGC stimulator) reduced CV death/HF hospitalisation (HR 0.90) in high-risk HFrEF — a positive result in HFrEF that directly contrasts with neutral VITALITY-HFpEF, establishing EF-specific divergence of sGC stimulation.

**File:** `raw/[pending ingest]` · **Authors:** Armstrong PW, Roessig L, Patel MJ, et al. · **Year:** 2020 · **Journal:** N Engl J Med  
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** 5,050 · **Population:** HFrEF, LVEF <45%, worsening HF (recent hospitalisation/IV diuretics), elevated NT-proBNP · **Median follow-up:** ~10.8 months  
**Intervention:** Vericiguat titrated to 10 mg vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Composite of CV death + HF hospitalisation

**Note:** This is a different paper from [[armstrong2020vitality]] (VITALITY-HFpEF, JAMA 2020). Same first author (Armstrong PW), same year, same drug — different EF population and different journal. Citekeys: Armstrong2020VICTORIA (HFrEF) vs. Armstrong2020VITALITY (HFpEF).

---

## Key Findings

HR 0.90 (95% CI 0.82–0.98; P=0.02) — statistically significant. NNT approximately 24 over ~10 months. Benefit concentrated in higher NT-proBNP strata (worse prognosis). Contrast with VITALITY-HFpEF: same drug, KCCQ-PLS neutral (P=0.47/0.80) in HFpEF.

## Methods (brief)

High-risk worsening HF enrichment: recent (<3 months) HF hospitalisation or IV diuresis. Dose titration to 10 mg (starting 2.5 mg). NT-proBNP ≥1,000 pg/mL (no AF) or ≥1,600 pg/mL (AF) required for eligibility.

## Results

Primary composite HR 0.90 (95% CI 0.82–0.98; P=0.02). CV death alone: HR 0.93 (NS). HF hospitalisation: HR 0.90 (P=0.05). [Full secondary endpoints pending ingest.]

## Limitations

Modest treatment effect (HR 0.90) with wide CI. Short median follow-up (~10.8 months). High NT-proBNP enrichment limits generalizability to lower-risk HFrEF. No HFpEF arm — any extrapolation to HFpEF requires separate trial data (VITALITY negative).
