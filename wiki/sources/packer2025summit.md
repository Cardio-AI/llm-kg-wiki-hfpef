# Tirzepatide for Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction and Obesity — SUMMIT

> Tirzepatide (dual GLP-1/GIP agonist) reduced CV death and worsening heart failure by 38% and improved KCCQ-CSS by 6.9 points in HFpEF with obesity — the first RCT to demonstrate hard event reduction with a GLP-1RA class agent in HFpEF.

**File:** `~ # pending ingest` · **Authors:** Packer M, Zile MR, Kramer CM, et al. · **Year:** 2025 · **Journal:** N Engl J Med  
**Study type:** Phase 3 RCT · **N:** 731 (357 tirzepatide, 374 placebo) · **Population:** HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%), obesity (BMI ≥30), NYHA II–IV · **Follow-up:** 52 weeks  
**Intervention/Exposure:** Tirzepatide (GLP-1/GIP dual agonist, LY3298176), 2.5 mg SC weekly up-titrated to max 15 mg, vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Co-primary: (1) KCCQ-CSS change at 52 weeks; (2) time-to-first CV death or worsening HF event

---

## Key Findings

Tirzepatide reduced the composite of CV death and worsening heart failure events by 38% (HR 0.62; 95% CI 0.41–0.95; P=0.026) and improved KCCQ-CSS by 6.9 points (95% CI 3.3–10.6; P<0.001) over 52 weeks vs. placebo. Body weight fell approximately 13–15% with tirzepatide vs. ~2% with placebo. This is the first HFpEF trial to show a significant reduction in CV death/worsening HF with a GLP-1RA class agent.

## Methods (brief)

Multicentre international Phase 3 RCT. Inclusion: LVEF ≥50%, BMI ≥30 kg/m², NYHA II–IV, 6MWD 100–425m, KCCQ-CSS ≤80. T2DM permitted (HbA1c ≤9.5%) — distinguishes SUMMIT from [[step-hfpef]] (T2DM excluded). Randomised 1:1 to tirzepatide or placebo. NCT04847557. Sponsor: Eli Lilly and Company.

Baseline characteristics: age ~69y, BMI ~38 kg/m², KCCQ-CSS ~52–57, 6MWD ~278–340m, 33–35% AF, 18–23% SGLT2i use, ~61% with CKD (eGFR <60 by cystatin C). (source: raw/2025-JACC-Packer-SUMMIT_study.pdf; raw/2025-JACC-Kramer-SUMMIT-CMR_substudy.pdf)

**CMR substudy (N=106 completed; Kramer 2025 JACC):** LV mass −11 g placebo-corrected (P=0.004); paracardiac adipose tissue −45 mL (P<0.001). (source: raw/2025-JACC-Kramer-SUMMIT-CMR_substudy.pdf)

**CKD subanalysis (N=731; Packer 2025 JACC):** Benefit consistent across CKD/no-CKD strata; interaction P=0.86. (source: raw/2025-JACC-Packer-SUMMIT_study.pdf)

## Limitations

- Primary PDF not yet in raw/; results reconstructed from secondary analyses — may lack granularity on pre-specified secondary endpoints and sensitivity analyses
- CMR substudy geographic distribution (80% Latin America/Asia-Pacific) may limit generalisability
- T2DM inclusion means some benefit may reflect glycaemic/metabolic pathways specific to diabetic cardiomyopathy; interpretation differs from [[step-hfpef]] (non-diabetic)
- Open question: whether LV mass reduction (tirzepatide by CMR) vs. no reduction (semaglutide by echo) reflects GIP co-agonism or measurement modality difference
