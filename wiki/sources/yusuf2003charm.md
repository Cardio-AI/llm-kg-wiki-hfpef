# CHARM-Preserved

> Candesartan missed its primary endpoint of CV death + HF hospitalization in HF with LVEF >40% but showed a hospitalization trend; enrolled pre-phenotyping-era patients who include what would now be classified as HFmrEF.

**File:** `raw/[not yet ingested]` · **Authors:** Yusuf S et al. · **Year:** 2003 · **Journal:** Lancet  
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** ~3,025 · **Population:** HF with LVEF >40%  
**Follow-up:** ~36.6 months · **Intervention:** Candesartan (target 32 mg/day) vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** CV death + HF hospitalization

---

## Key Findings

- Primary endpoint **missed**
- Trend toward reduction in HF hospitalizations
- Benefit concentrated in patients with LVEF in the HFmrEF range (would now be LVEF 41–49%)
- Recurrent-event analysis suggested reduction in HF hospitalizations across the overall cohort

## Methods (brief)

[Expand on paper ingest]

Enrolled LVEF >40% — pre-dates the 2016 three-way EF classification. A substantial fraction of patients would be classified as [[hfmref]] (LVEF 41–49%) under current criteria. Part of the broader CHARM programme (Candesartan in Heart failure Assessment of Reduction in Mortality and morbidity).

## Limitations

[Expand on paper ingest]

- LVEF >40% inclusion means a substantial proportion would now be classified as [[hfmref]], not [[hfpef]] — limits interpretation as a pure HFpEF trial
- Enrolled in 2000–2003; HF diagnosis less rigorous than modern criteria; limited echocardiographic phenotyping
- Recurrent hospitalization analysis was post-hoc
