# REDUCE LAP-HF II: Atrial Shunt and Cardiac Structural Remodelling

> Post-hoc echocardiographic analysis of REDUCE LAP-HF II (n=621, 24 months): atrial shunting reduced LV end-diastolic volume (−5.65 mL, P<0.001) and LA minimal volume (−2.8 mL, P=0.01), improved LV systolic function (s' +0.69 cm/s, P<0.001) and LA emptying fraction (+1.88 pp, P=0.02), with no RV systolic dysfunction overall; responders (PVR <1.74 WU, n=313) showed more favourable structural remodelling and better RV accommodation of shunt flow.

## Core Arguments

**Trial:** Post-hoc echocardiographic analysis of REDUCE LAP-HF II (NCT03088033). N=**621** (shunt arm 309, sham control 312). Sham-controlled, double-blind, international RCT across 89 centres. Population: HFpEF/HFmrEF (LVEF ≥40%, confirmed exercise PCWP >25 mmHg at catheterisation). Serial echocardiography at baseline, 1, 6, 12, 24 months. Analysis period April 2023–January 2024.

**Intervention:** Corvia Atrial Shunt Device (IASD) — permanently implanted interatrial shunt creating controlled LA-to-RA blood flow, reducing LA pressure and exercise PCWP. Compared to sham (venous puncture and echocardiography without shunt placement).

**Responder definition:** Peak exercise PVR <1.74 Woods Units (WU) AND no cardiac rhythm management device (pacemaker/ICD/CRT) at baseline. N=313 responders (50%), N=265 non-responders (43%), N=43 uncategorised (missing peak PVR). Basis: REDUCE LAP-HF II overall neutral result driven by non-responders.

**LV/left-sided structural effects (overall shunt vs sham, 24 months):**
- LV end-diastolic volume (LVEDV): **−5.65 mL** (P<0.001) — preload reduction from LA decompression
- LA minimal volume: **−2.8 mL** (P=0.01) — direct LA offloading
- LV s' tissue Doppler velocity: **+0.69 cm/s** (P<0.001) — improved LV longitudinal systolic function
- LA emptying fraction: **+1.88 percentage points** (P=0.02) — improved LA mechanical function
- Mitral E wave velocity: **−4.75 cm/s** (P<0.001) — reduced filling pressure/preload
- Average E/e': **−1.17** (P<0.001) — improved diastolic filling

**RV/right-sided effects (overall):**
- RV end-diastolic volume: **+9.58 mL** (P<0.001) — expected; shunted blood increases RV preload
- RA volume: **+9.71 mL** (P<0.001)
- **RVEF, PASP, RA pressure: NO significant change** vs sham — RV systolic function preserved
- Tricuspid regurgitation: increased modestly (P<0.001) — consequence of RV volume loading

**Responder vs non-responder differential (key interactions):**
- RV EDV increase: responders **+5.71 mL** vs non-responders **+15.18 mL** (P_interaction=0.01)
- RV/LV ratio change: responders **+0.07** vs non-responders **+0.20** (P_interaction<0.001)
- Transmitral A wave velocity: responders **+5.08 cm/s** vs non-responders **−1.97 cm/s** (P_interaction=0.02)
- Responders showed RVEF increase (+2.01 pp) while non-responders did not
- Responders: smaller/better-accommodated RV volume loading — the pulmonary vasculature can accept shunted blood

**Mechanism:** Responders (low PVR) have compliant pulmonary vasculature that accepts shunted blood without RV volume overload; the LA is offloaded without RV compromise. Non-responders (high PVR or CRM device) cannot accommodate shunt flow; RV volume increases disproportionately without LA benefit.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

REDUCE LAP-HF II overall trial was neutral for clinical events (HF hospitalisations + CV death), but the echocardiographic analysis confirms that the shunt produces the intended haemodynamic and structural effects in responders — supporting the responder hypothesis developed post-hoc from REDUCE LAP-HF II. The absence of RV systolic dysfunction in responders is a key safety confirmation. Ongoing RESPONDER-HF trial (NCT05233358) tests atrial shunt exclusively in confirmed responders (PVR <1.74 WU, no CRM).

The structural remodelling observed (reduced LVEDV, improved LV s') suggests normalisation of filling conditions without worsening systolic function — mechanistically plausible for a pressure-offloading strategy.
