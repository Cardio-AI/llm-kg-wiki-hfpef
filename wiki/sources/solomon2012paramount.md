# PARAMOUNT — LCZ696 (ARNi) vs. Valsartan in HFpEF (Solomon 2012)

> Phase 2 RCT demonstrating that LCZ696 (sacubitril/valsartan precursor, ARNi) significantly reduced NT-proBNP by 23% vs. valsartan alone at 12 weeks (p=0.005) and improved LA reverse remodelling at 36 weeks in HFpEF — establishing the mechanistic rationale for PARAGON-HF.

**File:** `raw/2012-LANCET-Solomon-PARAMOUNT_study_LCZ696_inhibitor.pdf`  
**Authors:** Solomon SD, Zile M, Pieske B, Voors A, Shah A, Kraigher-Krainer E, et al. (PARAMOUNT Investigators)  
**Year:** 2012 · **Journal:** Lancet 2012;380:1387–1395 · **DOI:** 10.1016/S0140-6736(12)61227-6  
**ClinicalTrials.gov:** NCT00887588  
**Study type:** Multicentre double-blind RCT · **N:** 301 (LCZ696 n=149; valsartan n=152)  
**Sites:** 65 centres in 13 countries  
**Population:** HFpEF; LVEF ≥45%; NT-proBNP >400 pg/mL; NYHA II–III; sinus rhythm  
**Follow-up:** 36 weeks (primary endpoint at 12 weeks)  
**Intervention:** LCZ696 (sacubitril/valsartan; initially 50 mg BID, uptitrated to 200 mg BID) vs. valsartan (initially 40 mg BID, uptitrated to 160 mg BID)  
**Primary outcome:** Ratio of NT-proBNP at 12 weeks (LCZ696/valsartan, adjusted for baseline)

---

## Key Findings

- **Primary endpoint MET**: NT-proBNP ratio LCZ696/valsartan = 0.77 (95% CI 0.64–0.92; p=0.005) at 12 weeks — 23% greater reduction with LCZ696
- 36-week NT-proBNP: ratio 0.85 (0.65–1.09; p=0.20) — difference no longer significant; suggests early effect that attenuates
- **LA reverse remodelling (36 weeks)**: LA volume −4.6 mL (LCZ696) vs. +0.37 mL (valsartan); p=0.003 — structural benefit persists beyond NT-proBNP attenuation
- LA volume index: similar improvement (p=0.007)
- NYHA class: significantly improved at 36 weeks (p=0.05)
- SBP: −9.3 mmHg (LCZ696) vs. −2.9 mmHg (valsartan; p=0.001); NT-proBNP benefit at 12 weeks independent of BP difference in sensitivity analysis
- eGFR: decline was smaller with LCZ696 (−1.6 vs −5.2 mL/min/1.73m²; p=0.007) — cardiorenal signal
- **No significant improvement in diastolic function parameters** (E/e', E/A ratio, e') at either timepoint

## Methods (brief)

**Baseline characteristics (mean ± SD or median):** Age 71 years; 57% female; LVEF 58% (79% ≥50%); AF 28%; HTN 95%; DM 38–41%; NYHA II 82%, NYHA III 18%

**Uptitration protocol:** Both arms started at lowest doses, uptitrated at 2 and 6 weeks to target (LCZ696 200 mg BID; valsartan 160 mg BID), provided tolerability.

**Subgroup analysis (pre-specified):** Diabetes subgroup showed significantly greater NT-proBNP reduction with LCZ696 (interaction p=0.02).

## Limitations

- Phase 2 trial; underpowered for clinical outcomes — designed as a biomarker/mechanistic proof-of-concept
- NT-proBNP difference attenuates by 36 weeks — unclear whether early biomarker benefit translates to sustained clinical benefit (answered in PARAGON-HF)
- No LVEF stratum below 45%; selection of sinus rhythm excludes AF patients common in HFpEF
- Lack of improvement in echocardiographic diastolic function parameters despite NT-proBNP and LA volume improvement suggests different domains of effect
- Small N limits definitive subgroup conclusions (DM interaction, BP-independence of NT-proBNP effect)
- Valsartan comparator (not placebo) means the benefit is *incremental over ARB* — absolute benefit vs. placebo not directly measured

## Contradictions

NT-proBNP benefit significant at 12 weeks (p=0.005) but not at 36 weeks (p=0.20) — temporal attenuation of biomarker effect. However, LA volume structural benefit *persists* at 36 weeks despite NT-proBNP attenuation. These diverge: a structural effect without sustained natriuretic peptide suppression may indicate distinct mechanisms. This raises the question of which endpoint best captures ARNi benefit in HFpEF — addressed in PARAGON-HF. Noted in `wiki/contradictions.md`.
