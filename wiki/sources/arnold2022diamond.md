# DIAMOND-HFpEF — CMR-Based CMD and Prognosis in HFpEF (Arnold 2022)

> CMR-based multiparametric study (N=101 HFpEF, 43 controls; Leicester) demonstrating MVD prevalence of 70% in HFpEF and that impaired MPR (per 1 SD increase HR ~0.69) independently predicts death/HF hospitalisation across 3 adjusted models — critically, MPR and diffuse myocardial fibrosis (ECV) are NOT correlated, identifying them as distinct and co-existing prognostic mechanisms.

**File:** `raw/2022-JACC-Arnold-DIAMOND-HFpEF_study_cmd_in_hfpef.pdf`  
**Authors:** Arnold JR et al. (DIAMOND-HFpEF study group)  
**Year:** 2022 · **Journal:** JACC Cardiovasc Imaging 2022;15:1001–1011  
**ClinicalTrials.gov:** NCT03050593  
**Study type:** Single-centre prospective cohort with controls · **N:** 101 HFpEF + 43 healthy controls  
**Site:** University Hospitals of Leicester NHS Trust, UK  
**Population:** HFpEF (ESC criteria; LVEF ≥50%); no obstructive CAD (CT or angiography); stable outpatients; controls matched for age/sex  
**CMD measurement:** CMR myocardial perfusion reserve (MPR) by adenosine stress CMR; MVD = MPR <2.0  
**Median follow-up:** 3.1 years; 45 composite events (31 HF hospitalisations, 25 deaths)

---

## Key Findings

- **MPR: 1.74 ± 0.76 (HFpEF) vs. 2.22 ± 0.76 (controls; P=0.001)** — significantly impaired microvascular perfusion
- **MVD prevalence (MPR <2.0): 70% HFpEF vs. 48% controls (P=0.014)**
- **MPR independently predicts composite (death or HF hospitalisation) in 3 fully adjusted models:**
  - Clinical model: HR 0.673 per SD increase (95% CI includes significance; P=0.038)
  - Blood biomarker model: HR 0.694 (P=0.039)
  - Full imaging model: HR 0.690 (P=0.034)
- Optimal MPR cut-off 1.82 (ROC): Kaplan-Meier log-rank P=0.020 — significant survival separation
- **KEY FINDING: NO correlation between MPR and ECV (diffuse fibrosis):**
  - ECV vs. MPR: r=−0.06, P=0.638
  - iECV vs. MPR: r=−0.10, P=0.473
  - MPR similar in patients with vs. without focal fibrosis (LGE): NS
- **Both ECV (diffuse fibrosis) and MPR are independently prognostic** — they contribute separately, not in series
- Late gadolinium enhancement (focal fibrosis, LGE): present in X% but NOT predictive of MPR severity

## Methods (brief)

**CMR protocol:** Adenosine stress perfusion CMR at 1.5 T; myocardial perfusion reserve = hyperaemic:rest myocardial blood flow ratio. Multiparametric: LV volumes (SSFP), LV mass, LGE (focal fibrosis), T1 mapping + ECV (extracellular volume fraction = diffuse fibrosis), MPR.

### CMD Prevalence and Severity

| Parameter | HFpEF | Controls | P |

| MPR (mean ± SD) | 1.74 ± 0.76 | 2.22 ± 0.76 | **0.001** |
| MVD (MPR <2.0), % | 70% | 48% | **0.014** |

### Prognostic Models for MPR (per 1 SD increase)

| Model | HR (per 1 SD ↑ MPR) | P |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical (age, sex, HTN, DM, AF, BMI, NYHA) | 0.673 | **0.038** |
| Blood biomarker (+ troponin, BNP) | 0.694 | **0.039** |

| Comparison | r | P |
|---|---|---|
| MPR vs. ECV (diffuse fibrosis) | −0.06 | 0.638 |
| MPR vs. iECV (indexed) | −0.10 | 0.473 |
| MPR in LGE+ vs. LGE− | NS | NS |

## Limitations

- Single centre (Leicester); smaller N limits power for event-driven subgroup analyses
- CMD measured as MPR (perfusion reserve) — does not directly measure microvascular resistance; MPR reflects combined vasomotor reactivity and structural change
- Controls (N=43) were not fully matched for all cardiometabolic comorbidities; 48% MVD in controls suggests possible subclinical disease or measurement overlap
- No functional test (KCCQ, 6MWT) reported alongside CMR — symptom-CMD correlation not assessed
- Causality between MVD and outcomes cannot be established from observational design

## Contradictions

**Core contradiction:** MPR (CMD severity) and ECV (diffuse myocardial fibrosis) are NOT correlated (r=−0.06, P=0.638), yet both independently predict adverse outcomes. This challenges a simple causal chain (CMD → fibrosis → adverse outcomes) — instead suggesting two parallel and additive pathological processes. If CMD caused fibrosis, they would be correlated; their independence implies separate etiological mechanisms with additive prognostic weight. This is a mechanistically important finding for the HFpEF pathophysiology debate. Noted in `wiki/contradictions.md`.
