# FINEARTS-HF (Solomon 2024)

> In patients with HFmrEF/HFpEF (LVEF ≥40%), finerenone significantly reduced total worsening heart failure events and cardiovascular death (RR 0.84, 95% CI 0.74–0.95; P=0.007) — the first non-steroidal MRA trial to show outcome benefit in this population.

**File:** `raw/2024-NEJM-Solomon-FINEARTS-HF_study.pdf` · **Authors:** Solomon SD, McMurray JJV, Vaduganathan M, Claggett BL, Jhund PS, Desai AS, Henderson AD, Lam CSP, Pitt B, Senni M, Shah SJ, Voors AA, Zannad F, et al. · **Year:** 2024 · **Journal:** N Engl J Med 2024;391:1475–85 · **DOI:** 10.1056/NEJMoa2407107  
**Study type:** International, multicenter, parallel-group, event-driven, double-blind RCT · **N:** 6001 (3003 finerenone; 2998 placebo) · **Population:** HFmrEF/HFpEF, LVEF ≥40%, age ≥40y, symptomatic HF, structural heart disease, elevated NPs · **Follow-up:** Median 32 months  
**Intervention:** Finerenone (max 20 mg/day if eGFR <60 or 40 mg/day if eGFR ≥60) vs. placebo + usual therapy · **Primary outcome:** Total worsening HF events + CV death (composite, total-events analysis) · **NCT:** NCT04435626 · **Funded by:** Bayer

---

## Key Findings

- **Primary outcome met:** RR 0.84 (95% CI 0.74–0.95); P=0.007 — significant reduction in total worsening HF events + CV death
- **Consistent across prespecified subgroups:** including LVEF <60% vs ≥60%, SGLT2i use at baseline, T2DM status, geographic region
- **Symptomatic benefit:** KCCQ total symptom score +1.6 pts (95% CI 0.8–2.3) vs. placebo; P<0.001
- **CV mortality:** No significant reduction (HR 0.93, NS) — driven primarily by reduced worsening HF events
- **Hyperkalemia:** Increased risk; no deaths from hyperkalemia; 0.5% hospitalised (vs 0.2% placebo)

## Methods (brief)

**Enrollment:** September 14, 2020 – January 10, 2023; 7463 screened; 654 sites; 37 countries; 6016 randomised; 6001 in efficacy analysis  
**Population:** LVEF ≥40%; age ≥40y; symptomatic HF; structural heart disease on echo; elevated natriuretic peptides  
**Dose titration:** Finerenone 20 mg/day if baseline eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m²; 40 mg/day if eGFR ≥60 mL/min/1.73m²  
**Primary outcome analysis:** Semiparametric proportional rates method; intent-to-treat; stratified by geographic region and baseline LVEF (<60% vs ≥60%)  
**Endpoints tested hierarchically:** (1) total WHF events + CV death; (2) total WHF events; (3) KCCQ TSS change; (4) NYHA improvement at 12 months; (5) kidney composite  
**Two interim analyses performed** (futility and efficacy); DSMC recommended continuing unchanged each time

**Baseline characteristics:**
- Mean age: 71.9±9.6y (finerenone); 72.0±9.7y (placebo)
- Female: 1355 (45.1%) / 1377 (45.9%)
- Race: White 78.8%/79.0%; Asian 16.6%/16.6%; Black 1.6%/1.3%
- Geographic region: Eastern Europe 44.3%/44.1%; Western Europe/Oceania/other 20.8%/21.1%; Asia 16.4%/16.3%; Latin America 10.7%/10.6%; North America 7.8%/7.9%
- Mean LVEF: 52.6±7.8% (finerenone); 52.5±7.8% (placebo)
  - LVEF <50%: 36.5% (1093/3003); LVEF 50–<60%: 44.3% (1329/3003); LVEF ≥60%: 19.2% (575/3003)

| CV death | 242 (8.1%) | 260 (8.7%) | HR 0.93 (0.78–1.11) | NS |
| All-cause death | 491 (16.4%) | 522 (17.4%) | HR 0.93 (0.83–1.06) | NS |
| First WHF event or CV death (sensitivity) | 624 (20.8%) | 719 (24.0%) | HR 0.84 (0.76–0.94) | — |

### Secondary Outcomes

| Outcome | Finerenone | Placebo | Effect | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KCCQ TSS change (6, 9, 12 mo) | +8.0±0.3 pts | +6.4±0.3 pts | +1.6 pts (0.8–2.3) | <0.001 |
| NYHA improvement at 12 mo | 557/3002 (18.6%) | 553/2998 (18.4%) | OR 1.01 (0.88–1.15) | NS |
| Kidney composite | 75 (2.5%) | 55 (1.8%) | HR 1.33 (0.94–1.89) | NS |

Note on kidney composite: numerically higher events with finerenone (75 vs 55, HR 1.33), likely reflecting the eGFR decline from aldosterone receptor blockade — a known class effect. No significant increase. Trial population had low baseline albuminuria (median ACR ~18 mg/g) and low absolute kidney event rates.

## Limitations

- Predominately White (78%) and Eastern European (44%) — limited generalisability, especially for Black patients
- Low SGLT2i use at baseline (13.6%) — reflects enrolment period 2020–2023 but lower than contemporary practice; subgroup analysis suggested similar effect with/without SGLT2i
- No mortality benefit demonstrated; total-events analysis method weights towards recurrent HF hospitalisations
- NYHA class insensitive to finerenone effect (expected given low sensitivity of this instrument)
- Kidney composite showed numerically higher events with finerenone — renal safety warrants monitoring
- TOPCAT comparison limited by different drug, trial design, and geographic contamination issue

## Contradictions

FINEARTS-HF (finerenone, positive, RR 0.84 P=0.007) vs. TOPCAT (spironolactone, neutral overall, HR 0.89 P=0.14): different drug class, geographic contamination resolved in FINEARTS-HF, eGFR eligibility threshold. The question of whether MRA class benefit in HFpEF is drug-specific (finerenone ≠ spironolactone) or trial-execution-specific (TOPCAT contamination) remains open. See [[contradictions]] #21.

NYHA functional class unchanged despite positive primary outcome and KCCQ improvement — instrument insensitivity, not treatment inefficacy. (source: [[armstrong2020vitality]] also failed to show NYHA improvement).
