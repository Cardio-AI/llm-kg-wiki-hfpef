# I-PRESERVE

> Irbesartan fully neutral on all outcomes in HFpEF (primary HR 0.95, P=0.35); all-cause death HR 1.00 (P=0.98); zero heterogeneity across all 8 prespecified subgroups; NT-proBNP unchanged at 6 months; cleanest null result in RAAS-HFpEF trials; together with CHARM-Preserved closes the ARB hypothesis for the overall HFpEF population.

**Full citation:**
Massie BM, Carson PE, McMurray JJ, et al.; I-PRESERVE Investigators. Irbesartan in Patients with Heart Failure and Preserved Ejection Fraction. *N Engl J Med.* 2008;359(23):2456–2467. doi:[10.1056/NEJMoa0805450](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa0805450)
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** 4,128 randomised · **Population:** HF, LVEF ≥45%, age ≥60, NYHA II–IV, hospitalised or NYHA III/IV with structural evidence  
**Follow-up:** Mean 49.5 months (16,798 patient-years) · **Intervention:** Irbesartan 300 mg/day vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Death from any cause or CV hospitalisation (composite)  
**NCT:** NCT00095238 · **Enrollment:** Jun 2002–Apr 2005 · **Sites:** 293 centres, 25 countries  
**Sponsor:** Bristol-Myers Squibb and Sanofi-Aventis

---

## Key Findings

- Primary composite: HR **0.95** (0.86–1.05), **P=0.35** — fully neutral
- All-cause death: HR **1.00** (0.88–1.14), **P=0.98** — dead-flat null on mortality
- CV death: HR 1.01 (0.86–1.18), P=0.92 — neutral
- HF hospitalisation: HR 0.95 (0.81–1.10), **P=0.50** — neutral
- NT-proBNP change at 6 months: −13 (irbesartan) vs. −2 (placebo) pg/mL, **P=0.14** — no biomarker signal
- MLHF quality-of-life score change: −8 vs. −7, **P=0.85** — neutral
- **Subgroups:** Zero heterogeneity across all **8 prespecified subgroups** (age, sex, EF, ACEi use, beta-blocker, diabetes, HF hospitalisation, region) — all interaction P >0.14
- Safety: creatinine doubling 6% vs. 4% placebo, **P<0.001**; K >6.0 mmol/L 3% vs. 2%, P=0.01

## Methods

**Design:** Double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled trial. Run-in: 1–2 weeks single-blind placebo before randomisation. ITT. Independent endpoint adjudication committee blinded to treatment assignment.

**Dose:** Start 75 mg → 150 mg at 1–2 weeks → 300 mg after further 1–2 weeks (forced titration). Target: 300 mg/day. Patients seen at 8, 14 weeks, 6 months, then every 4 months.

**Eligibility:**
- Age ≥60, NYHA II–IV
- LVEF ≥45% within 6 months
- One of: (a) HF hospitalisation within prior 6 months, or (b) ongoing NYHA III–IV with corroborative structural evidence (pulmonary congestion on CXR, LV hypertrophy on echo, LV hypertrophy or LBBB on ECG)
- ACEi permitted; randomisation stratified by ACEi use; ACEi cap 33% per site

**Exclusion:** LVEF <45% previously; creatinine >2.5 mg/dL; prior ARB intolerance; SBP <100 or >160 mmHg or DBP >95 mmHg on therapy; ACS/revascularisation/stroke within 3 months; significant valvular, hypertrophic, restrictive, or pericardial disease; life expectancy <3 years; Hgb <11 g/dL

**Statistical power:** Originally 90% power, 18% annual event rate, N=3,600 target, 1,440 events, detect 14.5% RRR (hazard 15.75%). Blinded review 2004 → event rate lower than anticipated → sample increased to 4,100. Study termination April 17, 2008.

## Limitations

- Mean LVEF 59–60% — highest among early RAAS trials; may reduce generalisability to lower-EF HFpEF (35% had LVEF ≤59%)
- NYHA III 76% — more severe than CHARM-Preserved, PARAGON-HF, DELIVER; enriched hospitalisation-risk population
- Age ≥60 required — cannot inform younger HFpEF phenotypes (contrast with CHARM ≥18, PARAGON ≥50)
- ACEi cap 33% per site; actual ACEi use rose 25% → 39% during trial; spironolactone rose 15% → 28% — progressive concomitant RAAS background may have diluted incremental ARB effect
- No systematic exclusion of infiltrative cardiomyopathy, amyloid — non-HFpEF mimics included
- NT-proBNP entry not required for hospitalised pathway (44% enrolled via HF hospitalisation alone without NP threshold) — diagnostic uncertainty
- Pre-modern diagnostic era: no H₂FPEF/HFA-PEFF criteria; clinical + LVEF diagnosis only

## Contradictions
- Zero heterogeneity across 8 prespecified subgroups (I-PRESERVE) vs. significant sex and LVEF interactions in PARAGON-HF (2/12 subgroups). Same drug class (ARB vs. ARNI), different era. If PARAGON sex/LVEF signals are real, I-PRESERVE lacked power in subgroups or the mechanism requires neprilysin inhibition to manifest. See [[contradictions]].
- CHARM-Preserved borderline HF hospitalisation signal (adjusted P=0.047) not replicated: I-PRESERVE HF hospitalisation HR 0.95, P=0.50. Candesartan vs. irbesartan; different entry criteria (LVEF >40% vs. ≥45%); mean LVEF 54% vs. 59–60% — LVEF difference may explain, or CHARM signal was spurious.
- NT-proBNP trend (−13 vs. −2 pg/mL, P=0.14): numerically favours irbesartan but non-significant; 300 mg irbesartan did reduce BP by 3.8 mmHg but produced no NP response. Mechanism of RAAS failure in HFpEF may not involve neurohormonal activation as the primary driver.
