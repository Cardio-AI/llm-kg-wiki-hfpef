# I-PRESERVE

> Irbesartan was fully neutral on all-cause mortality + CV hospitalization in HFpEF (LVEF ≥45%), with no significant subgroup signals — one of the most definitively negative HFpEF trials.

**File:** `raw/[not yet ingested]` · **Authors:** Massie BM et al. · **Year:** 2008 · **Journal:** NEJM  
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** ~4,128 · **Population:** HF with LVEF ≥45%, age ≥60  
**Follow-up:** ~49.5 months · **Intervention:** Irbesartan (target 300 mg/day) vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** All-cause mortality + CV hospitalization (composite)

---

## Key Findings

- Primary endpoint **fully neutral** — no benefit on all-cause mortality or CV hospitalization
- No significant subgroup signals across pre-specified analyses
- Most definitively negative RAAS blockade trial in HFpEF

## Methods (brief)

[Expand on paper ingest]

LVEF ≥45% inclusion with clinical HF diagnosis. Included patients ≥60 years. Placebo-controlled, double-blind.

## Limitations

[Expand on paper ingest]

- Enrolled LVEF ≥45% rather than ≥50%; includes patients who might now be classified as [[hfmref]]
- Age restriction (≥60) limits generalisability to younger HFpEF patients
- Clinical HF diagnosis without mandatory echocardiographic confirmation of diastolic dysfunction
