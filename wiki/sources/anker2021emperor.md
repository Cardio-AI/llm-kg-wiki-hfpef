# EMPEROR-Preserved — Empagliflozin in HFpEF (Anker 2021)

> Empagliflozin 10 mg significantly reduced CV death + HF hospitalization vs placebo in HFpEF (HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.69–0.90, P<0.001) — the first major positive outcomes RCT in HFpEF — driven primarily by a 29% reduction in HF hospitalizations; CV death alone was not significantly reduced.

**Full citation:**
Anker SD, Butler J, Filippatos G, et al.; EMPEROR-Preserved Trial Investigators. Empagliflozin in Heart Failure with a Preserved Ejection Fraction. *N Engl J Med.* 2021;385(16):1451–1461. doi:[10.1056/NEJMoa2107522](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2107522)
**Year:** 2021 · **Journal:** N Engl J Med 2021;385:1451–1461 · **DOI:** 10.1056/NEJMoa2107038  
**ClinicalTrials.gov:** NCT03057951  
**Study type:** Multinational double-blind RCT · **N:** 5,988 (empagliflozin 2,997; placebo 2,991)  
**Sites:** 622 centers in 23 countries  
**Enrollment:** March 2017 – April 2020 · **Median follow-up:** 26.2 months  
**Population:** HFpEF/HFmrEF (LVEF >40%), NYHA II–IV  
**Intervention:** Empagliflozin 10 mg once daily vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** CV death + HF hospitalization (time to first event)

---

## Key Findings

- **Primary endpoint: POSITIVE** — HR 0.79 (95% CI 0.69–0.90; P<0.001); empagliflozin 415/2997 (13.8%) vs placebo 511/2991 (17.1%)
- Benefit driven primarily by reduction in HF hospitalization (HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.60–0.83)
- CV death: HR 0.91 (95% CI 0.76–1.09); numerically lower, not statistically significant
- Total (recurrent) HF hospitalizations: HR 0.73 (95% CI 0.61–0.88), P<0.001
- eGFR slope: −1.25 vs −2.62 mL/min/1.73m²/yr; difference +1.36 (95% CI 1.06–1.66), P<0.001
- All-cause mortality: 422 (14.1%) vs 427 (14.3%); HR 1.00 (95% CI 0.87–1.15)
- KCCQ-CSS change at 52 weeks: 4.51 (empagliflozin) vs 3.18 (placebo); difference +1.32 (95% CI 0.45–2.19)
- Consistent benefit across T2DM Yes (HR 0.79) and T2DM No (HR 0.78); interaction NS
- Benefit attenuated with higher LVEF strata (LVEF <50%: HR 0.71; ≥50 to <60%: HR 0.80; ≥60%: HR 0.87, 95% CI 0.69–1.10)
- First major positive RCT in the HFpEF population; published after ESC 2021 guidelines cutoff

## Methods (brief)

Double-blind placebo-controlled RCT. Randomization stratified by T2DM status, LVEF, and geographic region. Empagliflozin 10 mg once daily added on top of background standard of care.

**Disease definition for enrollment:**
- LVEF >40% (includes HFmrEF 41–49% and HFpEF ≥50%)
- NYHA Class II–IV
- NT-proBNP: **>300 pg/mL (patients without AF)** OR **>900 pg/mL (patients with AF)** — higher threshold with AF because elevated HR in AF inflates NT-proBNP, so the higher threshold was required to ensure equivalent degree of structural disease
- **Echocardiographic criteria** (≥1 required): LA volume index >34 mL/m² OR LA diameter >3.8 cm OR LV septal/posterior wall thickness ≥1.1 cm OR E/e' ≥10
- eGFR ≥20 mL/min/1.73m²

**Key exclusions:** ACS/stroke/cardiac surgery in prior 90 days; systolic BP >180 mmHg; T1DM; prior SGLT2i or GLP-1 RA use within 3 months; estimated ≥2-year survival <50% from non-cardiac cause.

**Parameters collected:**
- *Imaging (echo)*: LVEF, LA volume index, LV wall thickness, E/e' (obligatory for eligibility)
- *Biomarkers*: NT-proBNP (qualifying and serial), BNP, serum Mg, creatinine, eGFR, HbA1c, potassium
- *Functional*: NYHA class, KCCQ-CSS, 6MWD
- *Safety labs*: Serial renal function, electrolytes

## Limitations

- LVEF >40% inclusion: includes HFmrEF (41–49%) patients; benefit attenuates at higher LVEF strata (HR 0.87 at LVEF ≥60%, CI crosses 1.0) — uncertain degree of benefit specifically in HFpEF ≥60%
- Published August 2021: after ESC 2021 publication cutoff; incorporated into AHA 2022 at Class 2a (see [[heidenreich2022aha]])
- Primary composite driven by HF hospitalizations; CV mortality alone not significantly reduced — the mortality gap in HFpEF remains
- AF-adjusted NT-proBNP threshold enriches for structural disease but introduces differential selection by rhythm status
- KCCQ improvement (difference +1.32 pts) is statistically significant but of uncertain minimum clinically important difference relevance
- Functional data (6MWD, NYHA change) not the primary focus; KCCQ showed modest signal
- External validity: 622 international sites — translation across health systems variable

## Contradictions

- Published after ESC 2021 cutoff — incorporated into AHA 2022 at Class 2a for HFpEF (see [[heidenreich2022aha]]); ESC 2021 recommendations on this wiki remain pre-EMPEROR-Preserved.
- Serum Mg interaction: [[ferreira2026emperor]] shows opposite Mg-treatment direction compared to HFrEF studies (higher Mg → more benefit in HFpEF; lower Mg → more benefit in HFrEF). See [[contradictions]].
- LVEF attenuation: benefit is strongest at lower LVEF strata (HR 0.71 at LVEF <50%) and numerically weakest at LVEF ≥60% (HR 0.87, CI 0.69–1.10) — the question of whether HFpEF ≥60% derives meaningful benefit remains open; DELIVER data partially addresses this.
