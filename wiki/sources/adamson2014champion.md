# CHAMPION HFpEF Subgroup Analysis (Adamson 2014)

> In the 119 CHAMPION patients with HFpEF (LVEF ≥40%), PA pressure-guided haemodynamic management reduced HF hospitalisations comparably to the overall trial — confirming device benefit specifically in HFpEF and providing the first prospective evidence for any haemodynamic-guided intervention reducing hospitalisation in HFpEF.

**Full citation:**
Adamson PB, Abraham WT, Bourge RC, Costanzo MR, Hasan A, Yadav C, Henderson J, Cowart P, Stevenson LW, Bauman JG. Wireless pulmonary artery pressure monitoring guides management to reduce decompensation in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction. *Circ Heart Fail.* 2014;7(6):935–944. doi:[10.1161/CIRCHEARTFAILURE.113.001229](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCHEARTFAILURE.113.001229)

---

## Core Arguments

**Population:** N=119 HFpEF patients (LVEF ≥40%) from the 550-patient CHAMPION trial (NCT00531661). Represent 37% of the full CHAMPION cohort.

**Primary analysis:** HF hospitalisation rate in HFpEF patients (active vs. control group).

**Results:**
- HFpEF active group showed lower HF hospitalisation rate consistent with the overall CHAMPION effect (~46% reduction in this subgroup)
- PA diastolic pressure tracking demonstrated similar decompensation-warning trajectories in HFpEF as in HFrEF — rising PAD >18 mmHg preceded clinical congestion in HFpEF patients as expected
- Device safety profile equivalent in HFpEF

**HFpEF-specific mechanism:** HFpEF patients often have relatively "normal" resting PA pressures that rise dramatically with minimal provocation (volume challenge, exercise, dietary Na⁺). The PA sensor detects this subclinical congestion before symptoms prompt clinical attention. The failure of multiple pharmacological HFpEF strategies meant that mechanism-targeted haemodynamic surveillance — treating the specific abnormality (elevated filling pressures) in real time rather than attempting molecular reversal — was the first approach to succeed.

**Clinical significance:** Published simultaneously with FDA approval of CardioMEMS for NYHA III HF (April 2014). This subgroup analysis provided the HFpEF-specific evidence base justifying device use in this population. At the time, no pharmacological therapy had demonstrated meaningful HF hospitalisation reduction in HFpEF; CHAMPION HFpEF was the sole positive RCT-level evidence.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

This paper closes the evidentiary loop for CardioMEMS in HFpEF. It should be read alongside the primary CHAMPION results ([[abraham2011champion]]) and the complete follow-up ([[abraham2016champion]]). Together, the three papers establish haemodynamic monitoring as the one device-based intervention with robust hospitalisation evidence across the EF spectrum at that time point.

Context: GUIDE-HF ([[lindenfeld2021guidehf]]) later tested the same device in a broader EF population but was COVID-affected and missed its primary endpoint; the pre-COVID subgroup analysis was directionally positive in HFpEF.
