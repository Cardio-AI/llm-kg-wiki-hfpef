# D-HART2 Results: Anakinra in HFpEF (Van Tassell 2018)

> D-HART2 (N=31; 24 weeks): anakinra significantly reduced hs-CRP (AUC ratio 0.40; P=0.001) confirming IL-1 target engagement, but peak VO₂ at 24 weeks did not improve (+0.32 mL/kg/min vs. placebo; P=0.54) — inflammation suppression did not translate to functional capacity improvement in HFpEF.

## Core Arguments

**Trial:** D-HART2 (Diastolic Heart Failure Anakinra Response Trial 2). Phase 2b, double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled. NCT02173548. N=**31** HFpEF patients (LVEF ≥50%, NYHA II–III, hs-CRP >2 mg/L). Duration: 24 weeks.

**Primary result:** hs-CRP area-under-curve at 2 weeks —
- Anakinra vs. placebo: hs-CRP AUC ratio **0.40** (95% CI 0.23–0.69; **P=0.001**)
- Target engagement confirmed: IL-1 receptor antagonism suppresses the hs-CRP inflammatory signal in HFpEF

**Secondary results:**
- **Peak VO₂ at 24 weeks:** mean difference +0.32 mL/kg/min (anakinra > placebo); **P=0.54** — not significant
- KCCQ: numerically improved with anakinra at 24 weeks but not statistically significant
- 6-minute walk test: no significant difference
- NT-proBNP: no significant change
- Echocardiographic diastolic parameters (E/e', LA volume): no significant differences

**Interpretation of the dissociation:** IL-1 blockade demonstrably suppresses the IL-1 → CRP/IL-6 inflammatory axis (target engaged), but this does not translate to improved exercise capacity or functional status. Two mechanistic interpretations:
1. Inflammation is a *consequence* of haemodynamic congestion in HFpEF rather than a *primary driver* of exercise intolerance; reducing downstream inflammation without addressing preload/filling pressures leaves the mechanistic problem intact
2. 24-week duration may be insufficient for structural remodelling reversal (fibrosis, titin isoform shift) to produce functional improvement

**Contrast with GLP-1RA:** In STEP-HFpEF ([[kosiborod2023stephfpef]]), semaglutide also reduces CRP but simultaneously reduces body weight, adipose-derived cytokines, and improves multiple haemodynamic parameters — pleiotropic mechanisms likely drive functional improvement rather than anti-inflammation alone ([[verma2024inflammation]]).

**Safety:** Anakinra was well tolerated. Injection site reactions were the primary adverse event. No increase in serious infections.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

D-HART2 delivers a critical null result for the targeted anti-inflammatory approach to HFpEF exercise intolerance. It separates inflammatory biomarker suppression from functional outcome improvement — constraining the claim that IL-1/CRP inflammation directly drives exercise intolerance in HFpEF. This has implications for interpreting [[fu2024inflammation]] (elevated inflammatory markers predict worse outcomes — prognostic but not necessarily causal) and for understanding why GLP-1RA trials succeed where pure anti-inflammatory strategies do not.

The disconnect between inflammation reduction (confirmed) and exercise capacity improvement (absent) is a key finding for [[coronary-microvascular-dysfunction]] (the Paulus–Tschöpe mechanism) and should be noted in [[contradictions]].
