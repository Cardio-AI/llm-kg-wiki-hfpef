# Sung 2023 — QRS Fragmentation as a Prognostic Marker in HFpEF

> In 960 HFpEF patients followed for a median 657 days, anterior/lateral QRS fragmentation (fQRS) was present in 8.7% and independently predicted HF hospitalisation (HR 1.90; P<0.001), CV death, and all-cause death, and was associated with higher rates of myocardial perfusion defects and coronary slow flow.

## Key Findings

### fQRS Categories

| Category | N (%) | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Non-fQRS | 657 (68.4%) | Normal QRS morphology |
| Inferior fQRS | 220 (22.9%) | fQRS in inferior leads (II, III, aVF) |
| Anterior/lateral fQRS | 83 (8.7%) | fQRS in anterior/lateral leads (V1-V6, I, aVL) |

### Outcomes by fQRS Category

- **Anterior/lateral fQRS vs. non-fQRS:**
  - HF hospitalisation: HR 1.90 (P<0.001)
  - CV death: significantly higher (P<0.05)
  - All-cause death: significantly higher (P<0.05)
- **Inferior fQRS vs. non-fQRS:** No significant difference in outcomes
- The territorial localisation of fQRS (anterior/lateral) matters — inferior fQRS is not prognostically significant in HFpEF

### Microvascular and Perfusion Findings
- Anterior/lateral fQRS associated with higher rates of **myocardial perfusion defects** and **coronary slow flow** on nuclear imaging/angiography
- Suggests underlying microvascular disease or subclinical myocardial ischaemia as mechanism

## Methods

Retrospective chart review; HFpEF defined as LVEF ≥50% + clinical HF. fQRS defined as splintering or notching of QRS complex in ≥2 contiguous leads (RSR' pattern without RBBB, or fragmented QRS in various configurations). Multivariable Cox regression adjusting for age, sex, NYHA class, LVEF, echocardiographic parameters, BNP, and medications.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

QRS fragmentation (distinct from wide QRS/LBBB) identifies a subgroup of HFpEF patients with probable microvascular disease or subclinical coronary artery disease. The anatomically specific finding (anterior/lateral > inferior) correlates with perfusion defects. Complements [[joseph2016qrs]] (wide QRS/conduction delay) and [[lin2023cmd]] (microvascular dysfunction in HFpEF). Together they suggest ECG-based screening could help identify HFpEF patients with concurrent ischaemic phenotype.
