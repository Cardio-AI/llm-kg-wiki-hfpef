# 2023 Focused Update of the 2021 ESC Guidelines

> The ESC 2023 Focused Update upgrades SGLT2 inhibitors to Class I, Level A for both HFmrEF and HFpEF — the first time the ESC has given any pharmacological agent a Class I recommendation for HFpEF.

**File:** `raw/2023-ESC-McDonagh-Focused_update_guidelines.pdf` · **Authors:** McDonagh TA, Metra M, et al. (Task Force, ESC/HFA) · **Year:** 2023 · **Journal:** *Eur Heart J* 2023;44:3627–3639 · **DOI:** 10.1093/eurheartj/ehad195

---

## Core Arguments

This Focused Update revises the 2021 ESC Guidelines based on new RCT evidence published through 31 March 2023. Sections updated: (1) chronic HF — HFmrEF and HFpEF; (2) acute HF; (3) comorbidities (CKD+T2DM, iron deficiency).

### HFpEF — Class I SGLT2i (Recommendation Table 2)

| Recommendation | Class | Level |
|---|---|---|
| SGLT2 inhibitor (dapagliflozin or empagliflozin) to reduce risk of HF hospitalization or CV death in HFpEF | **I** | **A** |

Based on EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER trials plus a subsequent aggregate data meta-analysis (HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.73–0.87 for primary composite). **Note:** the footnote explicitly acknowledges that "there was a significant reduction only in HF hospitalizations and no reduction in CV death."

### HFmrEF — Class I SGLT2i (Recommendation Table 1)

Same recommendation: SGLT2i Class I, Level A for HFmrEF (LVEF 41–49%). Evidence base: EMPEROR-Preserved (LVEF >40%), DELIVER (enrolled prior LVEF ≤40% improved to >40%), plus individual patient meta-analysis (Vaduganathan et al., *Nat Med* 2022) showing dapagliflozin reduces CV death (HR 0.86, P=0.01) across EF spectrum. Additional dapagliflozin: benefit consistent regardless of T2DM status.

**HFmrEF management algorithm (Figure 1):** Diuretics (Class I) + SGLT2i (Class I) + ACEi/ARNI/ARB (Class IIb) + MRA (Class IIb) + Beta-blocker (Class IIb)

**HFpEF management algorithm (Figure 2):** Three equal green arms — Diuretics (Class I) + SGLT2i (Class I) + Treatment for aetiology/comorbidities (Class I)

### Acute Heart Failure Updates

**STRONG-HF** (Mebazaa et al., *Lancet* 2022): Rapid intensification of oral HF therapy starting within 2 days of hospital discharge + close follow-up (Week 1, 2, 3, 6) reduced HF readmission or all-cause death by 34% at 180 days (aRR 0.66, P=0.0021) vs. usual care. High-intensity group achieved median >50% of target doses by 90 days.
→ **Class I, Level B recommendation:** intensive strategy with rapid up-titration before/after HF hospitalization.

**EMPULSE** (Voors et al., *Nat Med* 2022): Empagliflozin in haemodynamically stable, hospitalised acute HF (LVEF-agnostic); primary endpoint: clinical benefit (hierarchical composite of death, HF events, KCCQ ≥5 pts) at 90 days — stratified win ratio 1.36 (95% CI 1.09–1.68; P=0.0054). Benefit independent of LVEF and diabetes status.
→ Supports initiating SGLT2i before hospital discharge across LVEF spectrum.

### CKD + T2DM (Recommendation Table 4)

| Recommendation | Class | Level |
|---|---|---|
| SGLT2 inhibitors in patients with T2DM + CKD | I | A |
| Finerenone in patients with T2DM + CKD | I | A |

Finerenone (non-steroidal MRA): based on FIDELIO-DKD (HR 0.82, P=0.001 for primary renal endpoint) and FIGARO-DKD (HR 0.87, P=0.03 for CV composite). Pooled FIDELITY analysis (n=13,026): HR 0.86 for CV death/MI/stroke/HF hosp (P=0.0018) and HR 0.77 for HF hospitalization (P=0.003).

### Iron Deficiency (Recommendation Table 5)

| Recommendation | Class | Level |
|---|---|---|
| IV iron supplementation in symptomatic HFrEF + HFmrEF with iron deficiency: improve symptoms/QoL | I | A |
| IV iron supplementation with ferric carboxymaltose or ferric derisomaltose in HFrEF + HFmrEF: reduce HF hospitalization | IIa | A |

Based on meta-analysis of 10 trials (n=3,373): IV iron reduced total HF hospitalizations + CV death (RR 0.75, P<0.01) and first HF hospitalization or CV death (OR 0.72, P=0.01).
**Note:** Evidence base is largely HFrEF/HFmrEF (LVEF ≤45% in most trials); HFpEF-specific evidence pending (FAIR-HFpEF trial, [[trials-pending]]).

### Nomenclature Deliberation
Task Force explicitly considered but did **not** change HFpEF/HFmrEF terminology or EF thresholds in this Focused Update; deferred to next full ESC guidelines.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

**Critical update:** Resolves the primary ESC vs. AHA divergence in [[guideline-comparison]]. As of 2023, all three major guideline bodies (ESC, AHA/ACC/HFSA, ACC ECDP) endorse SGLT2i for HFpEF, now at Class I (ESC 2023) or Class 2a (AHA 2022). The "ESC 2021 had no SGLT2i recommendation" statement in wiki pages is now superseded — should reference ESC 2023 as current ESC position.
