# DETERMINE

> Dapagliflozin improved KCCQ total symptom score in HFrEF (P=0.022) but did not improve KCCQ or 6-minute walk distance in HFpEF; the discordance between symptom benefit and functional capacity (6MWD) across both arms challenges the validity of 6MWD as a surrogate for HF treatment response.

**Full citation:**
McMurray JJV, Docherty KF, de Boer RA, et al. Effect of Dapagliflozin Versus Placebo on Symptoms and 6-Minute Walk Distance in Patients With Heart Failure: The DETERMINE Randomized Clinical Trials. *Circulation.* 2024;149(11):825–838. doi:[10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.123.065061](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.123.065061)
**DOI:** 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.123.065061  
**Study type:** Two parallel RCTs (phase III) · **Total N:** 817 (DETERMINE-Reduced: 313; DETERMINE-Preserved: 504)  
**Follow-up:** 16 weeks · **Intervention:** Dapagliflozin 10 mg/day vs. placebo  
**Primary outcomes:** KCCQ-TSS, KCCQ-PLS, 6MWD at 16 weeks (three co-primary endpoints)  
**NCT:** NCT03877237 (DETERMINE-Reduced); NCT03877224 (DETERMINE-Preserved)  
**Enrollment:** First patient Apr 9, 2019; DETERMINE-Reduced completed Mar 7, 2020; DETERMINE-Preserved completed Jul 9, 2020

## Key Findings

### DETERMINE-Reduced (HFrEF, LVEF ≤40%, N=313)
- KCCQ-TSS: +4.2 (95% CI 1.0–8.2), P=0.022 — **significant**
- KCCQ-PLS: +4.2 (0.0–8.3), P=0.058 — borderline non-significant
- 6MWD: +3.2 m (−6.5 to 13.0), P=0.686 — **neutral**

### DETERMINE-Preserved (HFpEF, LVEF >40%, N=504)
- KCCQ-TSS: +3.2 (0.4–6.0), P=0.079 — **non-significant**
- KCCQ-PLS: +3.1 (−0.1 to 5.4), P=0.232 — non-significant
- 6MWD: +1.6 m (−5.9 to 9.0), P=0.668 — neutral

### DETERMINE-Pooled (Post-hoc exploratory, N=817)
- KCCQ-TSS: +3.7 (1.5–5.9), P=0.005 — significant
- KCCQ-PLS: +4.0 (0.3–4.9), P=0.036 — significant
- 6MWD: +2.5 m (−3.5 to 8.4), P=0.499 — neutral

**Key take-home:** Dapagliflozin improves patient-reported symptoms (KCCQ) preferentially in HFrEF; HFpEF KCCQ signal did not reach significance; 6MWD neutral across all arms — raises questions about 6MWD as an outcome measure.

## Methods (brief)

**DETERMINE-Preserved:**
- LVEF >40%; NYHA II–IV; NT-proBNP ≥250 pg/mL (≥500 pg/mL if AF); evidence of structural disease (LVH or LAE)
- Age ≥40y; no severe obesity (BMI ≥50 kg/m²); eGFR ≥25 mL/min/1.73m²; 6MWD 100–425 m
- Median age 73y; 37.1% female; median LVEF 50%; median NT-proBNP 776 pg/mL; 52% AF; 44% T2DM
- MRA use 36%; beta-blocker 82%; ACEI/ARB/ARNI 79%; NYHA II 81–86%

**DETERMINE-Reduced:**
- LVEF ≤40%; NT-proBNP ≥400 pg/mL (≥300 if prior HF hospitalisation in 12 months; ≥800 if AF)
- Median age 69y; 28% female; median LVEF 30%

## Limitations

- Short duration (16 weeks) — may not capture plateau of functional benefit
- COVID-19 pandemic affected DETERMINE-Preserved follow-up visits; remote visits introduced
- 6MWD entry criterion (100–425 m) selects ambulatory patients — may exclude most impaired
- DETERMINE-Preserved not powered for mortality/morbidity — functional outcomes only
- Pooled analysis is exploratory/post-hoc; individual HFpEF arm did not meet primary endpoints
