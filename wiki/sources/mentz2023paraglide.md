# PARAGLIDE-HF: Rationale and Design

> Design paper for PARAGLIDE-HF (NCT03988634): n=467 with LVEF >40% and recent worsening HF event randomised to sacubitril/valsartan vs valsartan; 52% women, 22% Black patients, median LVEF 55%, median NT-proBNP 2009 pg/mL; primary endpoint: time-averaged NT-proBNP reduction at weeks 4 and 8.

**Full citation:**
Mentz RJ, Ward JH, Hernandez AF, Lepage S, Morrow DA, Sarwat S, Sharma K, Solomon SD, Starling RC, Velazquez EJ, Williamson K, Zieroth S, Braunwald E. Rationale, Design and Baseline Characteristics of the PARAGLIDE-HF Trial: Sacubitril/Valsartan vs Valsartan in HFmrEF and HFpEF With a Worsening Heart Failure Event. *J Card Fail.* 2023;29(6):922–930. doi:10.1016/j.cardfail.2023.02.001

## Core Arguments

**Design:** Multicentre, double-blind, randomised controlled trial. NCT03988634. N=**467** randomised. Population: HF with LVEF >40% and recent worsening HF (WHF) event — defined as HF hospitalisation, emergency department visit, or ambulatory urgent HF visit requiring IV diuretics. Published as design/rationale paper.

**Study population (baseline characteristics):**
- **52% women, 22% Black patients** — substantially more diverse than most prior HFpEF trials (PARAGON-HF: 52% women but predominantly white)
- Median age: **70 years**
- Median LVEF: **55%** — straddles HFmrEF and HFpEF; trial includes LVEF 40–49% (HFmrEF) as well as ≥50%
- Median NT-proBNP: **2009 pg/mL** — markedly elevated, high-risk post-WHF population

**Intervention:** Sacubitril/valsartan (Sac/Val) titrated to 97/103 mg BID vs Valsartan 160 mg BID, initiated within 30 days of a WHF event. Post-stabilisation criteria required: SBP ≥100 mmHg for ≥6 hours, no IV diuretics/vasodilators for 6 hours, no inotropes for 24 hours.

**Primary endpoint:** Time-averaged proportional change in NT-proBNP from baseline to weeks 4 and 8 (geometric mean of Week 4 and Week 8 ratio of change).

**Rationale:**
- PARAGON-HF showed numerically favourable Sac/Val trends in patients with LVEF ≤57% and women
- WHF events are high-risk transition points with elevated renin-angiotensin activity — potentially optimal window for ARNI initiation
- NT-proBNP trajectory captures early biological response before clinical event accrual (feasible in shorter-duration trial)

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

PARAGLIDE-HF fills a critical gap left by PARAGON-HF: it targets the high-risk post-WHF period (not stable chronic HFpEF), includes a more diverse population, and uses a biomarker primary endpoint. The 52% women and 22% Black enrollment directly addresses representativeness concerns from prior trials. Primary results (McMurray JACC 2023) showed significant NT-proBNP reduction with Sac/Val in LVEF ≤60% subgroup.
