# RELAX — PDE5 Inhibition in HFpEF (Redfield 2013)

> Sildenafil (PDE5 inhibitor, 20→60 mg TID × 24 weeks) failed to improve peak VO₂ or any secondary endpoint vs placebo in HFpEF — and produced a renal and neurohumoral safety signal — establishing PDE5 inhibition as an ineffective NO/cGMP approach in unselected HFpEF.

**Full citation:**
Redfield MM, Chen HH, Borlaug BA, et al.; NHLBI Heart Failure Clinical Research Network. Effect of Phosphodiesterase-5 Inhibition on Exercise Capacity and Clinical Status in Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction: A Randomized Clinical Trial. *JAMA.* 2013;309(12):1268–1277. doi:[10.1001/jama.2013.2024](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2013.2024)
**Year:** 2013 · **Journal:** JAMA 2013;309(12):1268–1277 · **DOI:** 10.1001/jama.2013.2024  
**ClinicalTrials.gov:** NCT00763867  
**Study type:** Multicenter double-blind RCT · **N:** 216 · **Sites:** 26 (US and Canada) · **Sponsor:** NHLBI Heart Failure Clinical Research Network (HFCRN)  
**Population:** LVEF ≥50%, NYHA II–III (NYHA IV excluded per eligibility criteria), exertional limitation (reduced peak VO₂ or 6MWD), stable HF therapy  
**Follow-up:** 24 weeks  
**Intervention:** Sildenafil 20 mg TID × 12 weeks, then uptitrated to 60 mg TID × 12 weeks vs. matched placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Change in peak VO₂ (mL/kg/min) at 24 weeks

---

## Key Findings

- **Primary endpoint (peak VO₂ at 24 weeks): NEGATIVE** — placebo −0.20 vs sildenafil −0.20 mL/kg/min; P=0.90
- All secondary endpoints neutral: clinical rank score, 6MWD, MLHFQ, E/e', LVEDV, LVEF, NT-proBNP
- **Safety signal:** sildenafil worsened creatinine, cystatin C, NT-proBNP, endothelin-1, uric acid vs placebo
- cGMP increased in sildenafil arm (P=0.006) and arterial elastance decreased (P=0.02) — target engagement confirmed but no functional benefit
- Subgroup analyses (AF, pulmonary hypertension, chronotropic incompetence) all neutral

## Methods (brief)

NHLBI HFCRN multicenter double-blind placebo-controlled RCT. Eligibility: LVEF ≥50%, NYHA II–III, exertional limitation (peak VO₂ ≤70% predicted or 6MWD ≤450m), stable HF therapy ≥30 days. Key exclusions: NYHA IV, systolic BP <90 or >180 mmHg, eGFR <30, nitrate use. Sildenafil 20 mg TID for 12 weeks then uptitrated to 60 mg TID for 12 more weeks. Primary endpoint: peak VO₂ by cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET) at 24 weeks.

## Results

### Primary Endpoint

| Endpoint | Placebo | Sildenafil | P |
|----------|---------|------------|---|
| Peak VO₂ change at 24 wk (mL/kg/min) | −0.20 | −0.20 | 0.90 |

### Secondary Endpoints

| Endpoint | Placebo | Sildenafil | P |
|----------|---------|------------|---|
| Clinical rank score | 95.8 | 94.2 | 0.85 |
| 6MWD change (m) | +15.0 | +5.0 | 0.92 |
| MLHFQ change | −8 | −8 | 0.44 |

All CMRI, echocardiographic, and biomarker secondary endpoints: NS.

### Safety / Biomarker Signal (Sildenafil vs Placebo)

| Variable | Change with sildenafil | P |
|----------|----------------------|---|
| Creatinine | +0.05 mg/dL | 0.047 |
| Cystatin C | increased | 0.01 |
| NT-proBNP | +15 pg/mL | 0.03 |
| Endothelin-1 | +0.38 | 0.046 |
| Uric acid | +0.3 mg/dL | 0.02 |
| cGMP (target engagement) | increased | 0.006 |
| Arterial elastance | decreased | 0.02 |

cGMP increase confirms drug reached its molecular target; absence of functional benefit implies the mechanism is not rate-limiting in HFpEF.

## Limitations

- Unselected HFpEF population — possible heterogeneity dilutes any subgroup benefit (e.g., PH phenotype)
- No biomarker enrichment for NO/cGMP pathway deficiency
- Safety signal (renal, neurohumoral) may reflect haemodynamic unloading in a volume-sensitive population
- 24-week follow-up may miss longer-term benefits
- Target engagement confirmed (cGMP rise) but functional endpoint unchanged — mechanism failure, not engagement failure

## Contradictions

PDE5i (sildenafil) reduces pulmonary vascular resistance and improves outcomes in pulmonary arterial hypertension but fails completely in HFpEF — despite confirmed cGMP target engagement. Supports the hypothesis that HFpEF exercise limitation is driven by myocardial stiffness and skeletal muscle deconditioning rather than vascular cGMP deficiency. Noted in `wiki/contradictions.md`.
