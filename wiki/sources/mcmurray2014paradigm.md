# PARADIGM-HF

> Sacubitril/valsartan (ARNI) was superior to enalapril in reducing HF hospitalizations, CV mortality, and all-cause mortality in HFrEF; established ARNI as a pillar of HFrEF therapy and motivated the HFpEF trial PARAGON-HF.

**File:** `raw/[not yet ingested]` · **Authors:** McMurray JJV et al. · **Year:** 2014 · **Journal:** NEJM  
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** ~8,442 · **Population:** HFrEF with LVEF ≤40% (amended to ≤35% during study), elevated NPs, NYHA II–IV  
**Follow-up:** ~27 months · **Intervention:** Sacubitril/valsartan 97/103 mg b.i.d. vs. enalapril 10 mg b.i.d.  
**Primary outcome:** CV death + first HF hospitalization

---

## Key Findings

- Sacubitril/valsartan **superior** to enalapril on primary composite endpoint
- Significant reductions in: CV mortality, all-cause mortality, HF hospitalization
- Additional benefits: improved symptoms and QOL, reduced diabetes incidence, slower eGFR decline, reduced hyperkalaemia
- Trial stopped early due to superiority

## Methods (brief)

[Expand on paper ingest]

Run-in period with both enalapril and sacubitril/valsartan to exclude patients with intolerance. Required eGFR ≥30 mL/min/1.73m². LVEF cut-off changed from ≤40% to ≤35% during trial following protocol amendment.

## Limitations

[Expand on paper ingest]

- Active comparator (enalapril), not placebo — effect size vs. no RAAS blockade unknown
- Run-in period excluded ~20% of screened patients (intolerant to either drug) — enriched tolerable population
- LVEF amendment from ≤40% to ≤35% during trial
