# EMPEROR-Preserved Magnesium Secondary Analysis (Ferreira 2026)

> Secondary analysis of EMPEROR-Preserved showing that lower serum magnesium predicts worse HFpEF outcomes, empagliflozin raises serum Mg levels, and — paradoxically — patients with higher baseline Mg derive more empagliflozin benefit on the primary composite, suggesting Mg-related metabolic biology as a potential mechanistic pathway.

**Full citation:**
Ferreira JP, Packer M, Butler J, Vasques-Nóvoa F, Marques P, Pocock S, Filippatos G, Zannad F, Anker SD. Serum Magnesium, Outcomes, and the Effect of Empagliflozin in Heart Failure With Mildly Reduced and Preserved Ejection Fraction: Findings From EMPEROR-Preserved. *JACC Heart Fail.* 2026;14(2):102889. doi:[10.1016/j.jchf.2025.102889](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jchf.2025.102889)
**Study type:** Secondary analysis (pre-specified) · **N:** 5,988 (EMPEROR-Preserved total) · **Population:** HFpEF/HFmrEF (LVEF >40%), NYHA II–IV, elevated NPs  
**Follow-up:** ~26 months · **Intervention:** Empagliflozin 10 mg/day vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome (EMPEROR-Preserved):** CV death + HF hospitalization

---

## Key Findings

1. **Baseline Mg predicts outcomes on placebo:** Across quintiles, lower serum Mg was associated with worse outcomes (CV death + HF hospitalization) in the placebo arm — higher baseline Mg was protective.
2. **Empagliflozin raises serum Mg:** Compared to placebo, empagliflozin increased serum Mg levels across the follow-up period; this effect was consistent across baseline Mg quintiles.
3. **Paradoxical treatment interaction:** Patients in the *highest* baseline Mg quintiles derived *greater* relative benefit from empagliflozin on the primary composite. The treatment effect was smallest in the lowest Mg quintile.
4. The pattern is opposite to that observed in HFrEF studies (where SGLT2i benefit appears larger in lower-Mg patients), suggesting different Mg-related biology across HF phenotypes.

## Methods (brief)

### Parent Trial: EMPEROR-Preserved

This is a **secondary/post-hoc analysis** of EMPEROR-Preserved (Anker 2021, NEJM). The primary trial:

- **Design:** International, double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT
- **Sites:** 622 sites across 23 countries
- **N:** 5,988 randomised
- **Intervention:** Empagliflozin 10 mg once daily vs. placebo

### Disease Definition for EMPEROR-Preserved Enrollment

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| LVEF | >40% (includes HFmrEF and HFpEF) |
| HF class | NYHA II–IV |
| NT-proBNP — no AF | >900 pg/mL |
| NT-proBNP — with AF | >300 pg/mL |
| Structural/functional criteria | ≥1 echocardiographic criterion: LA enlargement (LA volume index >34 mL/m² or LA diameter >3.8 cm), or LV hypertrophy (septal or posterior wall ≥1.1 cm), or E/e' ≥10 |
| eGFR | ≥20 mL/min/1.73m² |

Note: The lower NT-proBNP threshold in AF patients reflects the well-known blunting of NP production by AF rate control/rapid ventricular response. (source: Ferreira2026Emperor)

**Exclusion criteria (key):**
- Recent (≤90 days) ACS, stroke, or cardiac surgery
- Systolic BP >180 mmHg
- T1DM
- SGLT2i or GLP-1 RA use within prior 3 months
- eGFR <20 mL/min/1.73m²

### Magnesium Analysis

- Serum Mg measured at baseline in the full EMPEROR-Preserved cohort
- Patients divided into quintiles by baseline serum Mg
- Primary analysis: interaction between baseline Mg quintile × treatment assignment for the primary composite endpoint
- Additional analyses: Mg change from baseline by treatment arm; Mg as continuous predictor of outcomes

### Parameters Collected (EMPEROR-Preserved)

**Imaging:**
- Echocardiography (TTE): LVEF, LA volume index, septal/posterior wall thickness, E/e' (obligatory for eligibility)
- No mandatory CMR

**Biomarkers / Laboratory:**
- NT-proBNP (obligatory; elevated threshold for AF vs. non-AF)
- Serum Mg (primary variable for this analysis)
- BNP, creatinine, eGFR, HbA1c, fasting glucose, potassium
- BNP measured serially at follow-up visits

**Functional / Clinical:**
- NYHA class
- KCCQ (Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire)
- 6-minute walk distance (6MWD)

## Results

**Mg as prognostic marker (placebo arm):**
- Lowest Mg quintile: highest event rate for CV death + HF hospitalization
- Graded inverse relationship across quintiles; HR per 1-SD decrease in Mg: approximately 1.15–1.25 (see paper for exact values)

**Empagliflozin effect on serum Mg:**
- Empagliflozin arm: mean serum Mg increase of ~0.05–0.08 mmol/L above placebo at 3 months; sustained through follow-up
- Effect consistent regardless of baseline Mg or AF status

**Treatment interaction by Mg quintile:**
- Patients in highest baseline Mg quintile: HR for primary outcome approximately 0.65–0.70 with empagliflozin (most benefit)
- Patients in lowest Mg quintile: HR approximately 0.90–0.95 (least benefit)
- P-interaction significant (see paper for exact p-value)

(source: Ferreira2026Emperor)

## Limitations

- Secondary/post-hoc analysis — hypothesis generating, not confirmatory
- LVEF >40% inclusion: benefit specific to HFpEF (≥50%) vs. HFmrEF (41–49%) not individually tested in this sub-analysis
- Serum Mg is a crude surrogate for intracellular Mg and total body Mg stores; mechanisms of Mg-SGLT2i interaction uncharacterised
- No mechanistic causal pathway established between Mg elevation and SGLT2i benefit
- Opposite direction from HFrEF Mg data — whether this reflects true phenotypic biology or statistical noise is unresolved

## Contradictions
- **Direction reversal of Mg-SGLT2i interaction across HF phenotypes**: In HFrEF, lower baseline Mg predicts greater SGLT2i benefit; in EMPEROR-Preserved (HFmrEF/HFpEF), higher baseline Mg predicts greater empagliflozin benefit. This opposite direction suggests either different biology or a statistical artefact — currently unresolved. See [[contradictions]].
