# MyoMobile Primary Results: App-Based PA Coaching in HFpEF (Zeid 2026)

> MyoMobile primary results (N=185 HFpEF; 6-month, 3-arm EE2 RCT): app-based personalised physical activity coaching significantly increased daily step count vs. standard care — the first positive digital health RCT in HFpEF — with secondary improvements in KCCQ and 6MWT.

## Core Arguments

**Trial:** MyoMobile. EE2 (effectiveness-efficiency) three-arm parallel RCT. NCT04940312. N=**185** HFpEF patients (LVEF >40%, NYHA I–III). DZHK Rhine-Main collaborative. Duration: 6 months active intervention.

**Arms:**
1. **App-based personalised PA coaching** (most intensive): physician-supervised PA goal setting + real-time app feedback, gamification, personalised daily step targets, and motivational alerts
2. **Tracking only** (intermediate): wearable activity tracker providing step count data without coaching features
3. **Standard care** (control): usual HFpEF management, no activity tracker provided

**Primary endpoint:** Change in daily step count at 6 months (accelerometer-measured; objective physical activity assessment).

**Primary results:**
- Arm 1 (app coaching) vs. arm 3 (control): **statistically significant** increase in daily step count at 6 months
- Arm 2 (tracking only) vs. arm 3: intermediate effect, smaller than coaching arm
- Dose-response gradient consistent with coaching features as the active ingredient beyond mere self-monitoring

**Secondary results:**
- KCCQ total summary score: improved in coaching arm
- 6-minute walk test: improved
- NT-proBNP: trends toward reduction
- Exertional dyspnea: reduced

**Significance:** This is the **first positive RCT** establishing app-based digital coaching as a scalable physical activity intervention in HFpEF. Unlike supervised exercise training (requiring gym or clinic infrastructure), app-based coaching is accessible remotely — addressing the implementation gap identified by [[mirzai2025exercise]] and [[sachdev2023exercise]]. The DZHK Rhine-Main population overlaps with the [[myovasc]] registry, providing deep phenotypic characterisation of enrolled patients.

**Design paper:** [[zeid2025myomobile]] (EHJ Digital Health 2025) defined the trial; this paper (2026) delivers primary results. The entity [[myomobile]] previously noted "results pending" — now updated.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

MyoMobile extends the supervised exercise training evidence base to digital health delivery, demonstrating that coaching-enhanced app-based intervention achieves meaningful physical activity gains in HFpEF. Combined with [[alonso2022heartcamp]] (behavioural coaching by staff) and [[mirzai2025exercise]] (meta-analysis showing MICT best evidence), it supports adherence-focused digital coaching as a complementary strategy to in-person supervised exercise training.

The design features — remote delivery, gamification, personalised targets — align with the scalability gap in HFpEF exercise evidence identified in [[hfpef-treatment-gap]] and the digital health topic in [[ml-ai-hfpef]].
