# Yi 2025 — AI in HFpEF Review

> Systematic narrative review of 38 AI/ML studies in HFpEF across four domains (diagnosis, phenotyping, risk prediction, management); AI is particularly suited to HFpEF's heterogeneity; spironolactone ML responder analyses reframe TOPCAT as an enrichment failure; main gaps are prospective validation and regulatory readiness.

## Core Arguments

### Study selection
PubMed, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science; inception to May 2025; English; human subjects with separate HFpEF subgroup data. 719 identified → 445 after deduplication → 134 full-text assessed → **38 included** (14 diagnosis, 11 phenotyping, 10 risk prediction, 3 management).

### AI-based diagnosis (Table 1; 14 studies)

**Echocardiography:**
- Akerman 2023/2025 — 3D-CNN on single apical 4-chamber clip; AUC 0.79; sensitivity 0.87, specificity 0.79; outperformed H₂FPEF and HFA-PEFF scores in non-diagnostic outputs; external validation AUC 0.85/sensitivity 0.79/specificity 0.89
- Chiou 2021 — AI-assisted prescreening via intrabeat LA/LV dynamics from single 4AC view; accuracy 0.91, sensitivity 0.96, specificity 0.85; external validation AUC 0.85

**ECG:**
- Unterhuber 2021 — CNN on baseline 12-lead ECG; AUC 0.80, sensitivity 0.99, specificity 0.60; 11,955 patients; external validation AUC 0.79, NPV 0.98 — reliable screening rule-out
- Kwon 2021 — DLM on 32,671 12-lead ECGs from 20,169 patients; AUC 0.866/0.869 internal/external validation
- Gao 2025 — CNN + long short-term memory on 6,000 ECG amplitude values within 10s; accuracy 0.78, specificity 0.71, sensitivity 0.75; N=238 EHR encounters
- Lee 2024 — AI-enabled ECG; DD grades 1–2: AUC 0.84–0.91; grade 3: 0.94

**EHR/NLP:**
- Garan 2023 — NLP + AI inference on 4,288 EHR encounters; F1=0.91; found **75.4% additional undiagnosed HFpEF** cases meeting ESC criteria — large pool of underdiagnosis
- Wu 2024 (NLP) — NLP detection of HFpEF from EHR; AUC 0.88 (ESC criteria)
- Wu 2022 (AIM-HFpEF) — EHR-based AI prediction model; ROC 0.90; equitable (tested across demographic groups)
- Ahmad 2024 (MARKER-HF) — performance of risk models for mortality in HF from EHR; N=6,764; 7 labs + DBP
- Jain 2022 — subclinical pulmonary congestion + abnormal hemodynamics in HFpEF; mean lung density [−758 HU to −783 HU] vs [−787 to −828 HU], P=0.002; ensemble classifier accuracy 0.98

**Other modalities:**
- Özen Kavas 2023 — PPG-based heart rate variability; specificity 0.96, sensitivity 0.98
- Yang 2025 — oral hyperspectral imaging; novel non-invasive; AUC 0.81, accuracy 0.85 in external validation

### AI-based phenotyping (Table 2; 11 studies)

| Reference | Method | Population | Key finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shah 2015 | Hierarchical clustering | N=397 HFpEF | 3 phenotypes; highest-risk group HR **4.2** (2.0–9.1, P<0.001) for HF hosp |
| Pandey 2021 | Agglomerative + DeepNN | N=1,242/303/external | Elevated filling pressure subgroup; validated across TOPCAT, NEAT-HFpEF, RELAX |
| Chao 2022 | k-means (9 diastolic vars) | N=23,414 total | 3 groups: normal (N=8,312), impaired relaxation (N=11,779), elevated filling (N=4,323); differential long-term prognosis |
| Sabbah 2020 | Hierarchical clustering | N=301 (RELAX/NEAT/INDIE) | Obese-inflammatory phenotype; 13 inflammatory biomarkers |
| Cohen 2020 | Latent class | N=1,767 TOPCAT Americas | 49 protein markers; 3 phenogroups; differential spironolactone response |
| Uijl 2021 | Latent class | N=6,909 SwedeHF + N=2,153 CHECK-HF | Distinct phenotypic clusters with different prognosis; external validation |
| Gu 2021 | ML clustering | N=970 (validation N=290) | Phenogroup 3 (obesity/DM/T2DM/severe HF/high BNP) showed pronounced beta-blocker/ARB benefit — precision medicine signal |

**Common phenotype axes found across studies:** cardiometabolic (obesity, DM, CKD), inflammatory, ischaemic/fibrotic, and age/AF-dominant. Most studies identify 3–4 phenogroups with HR differences of 2–4× for adverse outcomes.

### AI-based risk prediction (Table 3; 10 studies)

| Reference | Model | N | Key metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Angraal 2020 | RF | TOPCAT N=1,767 | C-stat 0.72 (mortality), 0.76 (hospitalization); BUN, BMI, KCCQ top predictors |
| Gao 2021 | SVM | N=380 (hospitalized HFpEF) | NT-proBNP, hs-TnT, GDF-15, TNFα; 2-year death prediction |
| Zheng 2024 | XGBoost | N=746 | 90-day readmission AUC 0.896; LAD, LVEDD, nitrates, fall risk, edema |
| Hu 2025 | LightGBM | N=766 | 1-year readmission; MI history, AF, monocytes/lymphocytes ratio, BNP, EF, E/e', ACEi/ARB/ARNI |
| Wang 2024 | ML vertical visualization | N=294/118 | Ischaemic CMP + HFpEF post-CABG; C-index 0.768/0.633 |
| Wang 2025 | SVM | N=326 | AS + HFpEF post-TAVR; 58 metabolic biomarkers; AUC 0.756 |
| Zhou 2021 | Genetic Algorithm + KPLS | Framingham HFS | 116 DEGs; gene expression-based risk |

**Consistent top predictors across studies:** NT-proBNP/BNP, creatinine/eGFR, EF, E/e', age, BMI, AF, loop diuretic use, NYHA class.

### AI-based management (Table 4; 3 studies)

| Reference | Intervention | Design | Key finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bayes-Genis 2021 | Empagliflozin mechanism | In silico | Dominant mechanism = NHE1 (Na⁺/H⁺ exchanger 1) inhibition → cardiomyocyte oxidative stress modulation; first computational simulation for HFpEF pharmacology |
| Kresoja 2023 | Spironolactone responders | RCT (TOPCAT N=3,445 + Aldo-DHF N=422) | ML identifies responders: spironolactone significantly reduces CV events in responders (log-rank P=0.008) but not non-responders (P=0.52) |
| Desai 2024 | Spironolactone treatment effect | RCT (TOPCAT N=3,445) | Individualized treatment effect prediction; BMI highest contributor (33.7%), eGFR 27.3%, EF 15.1%, age 12.8% |

### Challenges and limitations
- Most studies are **retrospective and observational** — prospective validation rare
- **External validation** frequently absent; model performance tends to be overestimated
- **Black box opacity** — explainability tools (XAI) under active development; FDA/IMDRF regulatory frameworks evolving
- **Data heterogeneity** — HFpEF diagnostic criteria vary across studies in Table 1 (HFA-PEFF, H₂FPEF, ESC, echocardiographic diastolic dysfunction only)
- **Algorithmic bias** — limited representation of diverse populations; AIM-HFpEF explicitly addresses equity
- TRIPOD-AI reporting criteria not met in most included studies

### Future directions
- Multimodal AI (imaging + omics + biomarkers + EHR)
- Generative AI on unstructured EMR text
- NHLBI HeartShare program for federated HFpEF data infrastructure
- PASSION-HF "virtual doctor" — AI-assisted patient self-care; decision support based on real-time clinical standards

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

1. **Most comprehensive AI/ML survey in HFpEF to date:** 38 studies across 4 domains; covers diagnosis, phenotyping, prognosis, treatment response prediction. Provides single-source reference for the scope and maturity of the field.

2. **Spironolactone ML responders reframe TOPCAT:** Kresoja 2023 and Desai 2024 (both cited and summarised) show TOPCAT's null result is an enrichment failure — BMI, eGFR, EF, age predict who benefits. BMI is the top predictor (33.7%). This is directly relevant to [[hfpef-treatment-gap]] and [[spironolactone]].

3. **NLP reveals massive HFpEF underdiagnosis:** Garan 2023 found 75.4% of patients meeting ESC HFpEF criteria were undiagnosed. This is a critical epidemiological finding for [[hfpef-diagnosis]] and [[hfpef]] epidemiology section.

4. **ECG deep learning as scalable screening:** Unterhuber 2021 and Kwon 2021 show AUC ~0.87 for HFpEF from 12-lead ECG alone; NPV 0.98 makes ECG-AI useful for rule-out. Important for resource-limited settings without echocardiography.

5. **Spironolactone phenotype signal (Gu 2021):** Phenogroup 3 (obesity/DM) showed pronounced beta-blocker/ARB benefit vs. other phenogroups — concrete precision medicine outcome directly relevant to [[hfpef-treatment-gap]] and ongoing SGLT2i/MRA treatment selection debate.

## Contradictions
- ML spironolactone responder analyses (Kresoja 2023; Desai 2024) suggest TOPCAT failure is enrichment failure — BMI-rich subgroup would have benefited. TOPCAT overall (Pitt 2014: P=0.14) vs. Americas subgroup (post-hoc P=0.026): this contradiction predates ML but ML strengthens the responder hypothesis. See [[contradictions]] and [[pitt2014topcat]].
- ECG-AI AUC 0.87 for HFpEF detection contrasts with clinical ECG being non-diagnostic for HFpEF per guidelines. AI identifies non-obvious patterns in standard 12-lead data that clinicians cannot extract consciously. The discriminative signal is likely a composite of multiple subtle morphological features (LVH regression, LA enlargement, rate, axis).
