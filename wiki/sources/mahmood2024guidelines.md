# Mahmood 2024 — Systematic Review of HFpEF Clinical Practice Guidelines

> First systematic review of 7 rigorously developed HFpEF clinical practice guidelines: consensus on LVEF ≥50% definition + TTE/NP role; disagreement on diagnostic thresholds and RAAS/beta-blocker use; evidence gaps in combined SGLT2/1 inhibition, GLP-1RA, non-steroidal MRAs, and population screening.

## Core Arguments

### Study Overview
PRISMA-compliant systematic review of guidelines on HFpEF management in adults. MEDLINE + EMBASE searched 28 February 2024 for publications over last 10 years. 2,396 titles identified → 10 guidelines/recommendations retrieved → 7 met AGREE II rigour threshold (score ≥50%) and were retained for full analysis.

**Seven high-rigour guidelines analyzed:**

| Guideline | Organization | Region | Year (latest update) | AGREE II score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AHA/ACC/HFSA | American Heart Association | United States | 2022 | 85% |
| ESC | European Society of Cardiology | Europe | 2021 (update 2023) | 72% |
| NICE | National Institute for Health and Care Excellence | UK | 2018 (update 2023) | 88% |
| CCS/CHFS | Canadian Cardiovascular Society/Canadian HF Society | Canada | 2017 (update 2020) | 69% |
| SHA | Saudi Heart Association | Saudi Arabia | 2019 (update 2022) | 68% |
| NHFA/CSANZ | National Heart Foundation of Australia/Cardiac Society of Australia and New Zealand | Australia/NZ | 2018 | 66% |
| JCS/JHFS | Japanese Circulation Society/Japanese Heart Failure Society | Japan | 2017 (update 2021) | 55% |

- Population-based screening
- Efficacy of rehabilitation

## Detailed Findings

### Areas of Agreement

**Definition:** All 7 guidelines define HFpEF as clinical signs/symptoms + LVEF ≥50%; 6/7 additionally require echocardiographic or invasive evidence of diastolic dysfunction/elevated filling pressures; 5/7 include elevated NPs. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Diagnostic role of TTE and NPs:** Universal across all 7. If TTE non-diagnostic → CMR, CPET, or invasive haemodynamics recommended by ≥4 guidelines. RHC recommended by 4 guidelines; myocardial biopsy by 4 guidelines.

**SGLT2i:** 4/7 guidelines (those updated post-2021) recommend SGLT2i as pharmacological disease-modifying therapy for HFpEF — AHA/ACC/HFSA, ESC, NHFA/CSANZ, SHA, (and JCS/JHFS). All guidelines updated after EMPEROR-Preserved/DELIVER publication have incorporated SGLT2i. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Multidisciplinary HF programme:** All guidelines recommend MDT involvement, patient self-management education, exercise rehabilitation programmes. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Advanced treatment:** All 6 guidelines recommend cardiac transplantation consideration in advanced HF; all emphasise palliative care (patient-centered, early initiation alongside disease management). (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

### Areas of Disagreement

**NP thresholds (major discordance):**
- Standard: NT-proBNP >125 pg/mL (ambulatory) — adopted by ESC, AHA/ACC/HFSA, CCS/CHFS, SHA
- Age-adjusted: **NHFA/CSANZ** → NT-proBNP >450 (age <50y) or >900 (age 50–75y) or **>1800 (age >75y)** — substantially higher in elderly
- JCS/JHFS: BNP 40–100 pg/mL cannot be ruled out; similar age-adjusted thresholds for NT-proBNP
- Impact: Elderly patients (age >75y) may not qualify as HFpEF by NHFA/CSANZ criteria even with meaningful NP elevation below 1800 pg/mL. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**E/e' threshold:**
- ESC: E/e' **>9** = suggestive of diastolic dysfunction (mean, biplane); E/e' ≥13 + mean e' <9 cm/s = criterion for Table 9
- AHA/ACC/HFSA: Average E/e' **≥15** (lateral) or septal >15 as significant filling pressure elevation
- Other guidelines (NHFA/CSANZ, JCS/JHFS): septal E/e' **>14** or average E/e' >13 as indicative
- ESC used >9 as a diagnostic pivot point derived from outcome data; higher thresholds in other guidelines reflect echocardiographic operating points for invasively confirmed elevated filling pressures. See [[contradictions]] #17. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**LAVI threshold:**
- AHA/ACC/HFSA: LA volume index **≥29 mL/m²** (lower threshold, body surface area)
- ESC/NHFA/CSANZ/JCS/JHFS: LA volume index **>34 mL/m²**
- The AHA threshold is consistently lower, meaning more patients qualify for the structural HFpEF criterion under AHA criteria. See [[contradictions]] #18. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**RAAS inhibitors/beta-blockers in chronic HFpEF:**
- 4 guidelines recommend MRA and/or ARB at Class IIb
- 3 guidelines suggest ARNI consideration
- Only JCS/JHFS suggests ACEi (unlike all others which avoid ACEi in HFpEF)
- 2 guidelines support beta-blocker in HFpEF
- AHA/ACC/HFSA explicitly states stronger support for MRA, ARB, ARNI in patients with LVEF closer to 50%
- ESC alone recommends finerenone for T2DM + CKD (after ESC 2023 update). (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Surveillance frequency:**
- ESC + NICE: 6-monthly review for stable patients
- JCS/JHFS + CCS/CHFS: at least annual review
- Post-discharge review timing: AHA/ACC/HFSA within 7 days; ESC + NHFA/CSANZ within 1–2 weeks; NICE <2 weeks
- No consensus on constitution of monitoring or which specialist (HF nurse, cardiologist, PCP) should be responsible. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Acute HF — tolvaptan/acetazolamide:**
- Tolvaptan (V2 receptor antagonist, aquaretic): only CCS/CHFS and JCS/JHFS recommend in volume overload/hyponatraemia; ESC/NHFA/CSANZ discuss but do not formally endorse
- Acetazolamide (carbonic anhydrase inhibitor): only SHA recommends in resistant oedema/insufficient loop response; ESC 2023 emphasised need for further data before formal recommendation. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Ultrafiltration:**
- JCS/JHFS, NICE, SHA: recommend as last resort for refractory fluid overload
- ESC + NHFA/CSANZ: discuss potential benefits; do not formally recommend routine use. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

### Evidence Gaps

**GLP-1 receptor agonists:** No guidelines contain a recommendation for GLP-1RA use in HFpEF as of their review dates. Published results at time of Mahmood 2024:
- **STEP-HFpEF** (semaglutide in obese HFpEF): published NEJM 2023 (Kosiborod et al.) — improved symptoms, exercise function, weight loss; hospitalization/mortality not primary endpoint
- **STEP-HFpEF DM** (semaglutide in obese HFpEF with T2DM): published NEJM 2024 — consistent results
- **SUMMIT** (NCT04847557): ongoing at time of publication; will provide further prognostic data
- Lean muscle loss risk (tirzepatide): noted as caveat for GLP-1/GIP agents. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Combined SGLT2/1 inhibition (sotagliflozin):**
- SOLOIST-WHF: trial of sotagliflozin in recently worsening HFpEF; terminated early due to COVID-19; positive signal
- ESC and AHA/ACC/HFSA have recommended sotagliflozin in select patients with T2DM but benefits of widespread use remain unclear; adverse effects (diarrhoea, genital infections, DKA) more common vs. placebo
- **SOTA-P-CARDIA** (NCT05562063): ongoing trial to delineate sotagliflozin benefit in HFpEF without T2DM. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Spironolactone/eplerenone in HFpEF:**
- SPIRIT-HF (NCT04727073) and SPIRRIT (NCT02901184): ongoing to resolve the TOPCAT MRA question
- Evidence not definitive. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Non-steroidal MRAs in prevention:**
- Only ESC recommends finerenone for CKD+T2DM prevention
- FINEARTS-HF (NCT04435626): expected to publish results soon (as of Feb 2024). (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Population-based screening:**
- AHA/ACC/HFSA: recommends NP screening (BNP >50 and NT-proBNP >125 pg/mL) for at-risk patients
- No other guideline provides population screening guidance
- STOP-HF trial (BNP-guided general practice management) and PONTIAC trial (NT-proBNP screening in diabetics): reduced CV outcomes with NP-based screening; HFpEF-specific effects unknown. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

**Cardiac rehabilitation efficacy:**
- All 7 guidelines recommend cardiac rehab/exercise training but acknowledge evidence in HFpEF is significantly weaker than HFrEF
- **REHAB-HFpEF** (NCT05525663) and **REACH-HFpEF**: ongoing trials to generate HFpEF-specific cardiac rehab evidence. (source: 2024-ehj-qcco-Mahmood_systematic_review_practice_guidelines.pdf)

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

First systematic review to apply AGREE II methodology to HFpEF guidelines from 7 organisations — provides meta-level synthesis unavailable from reading individual guidelines. The agreement/disagreement/gap taxonomy (Figure 2) is directly actionable for clinical practice and research prioritisation. Key additions to wiki:
- Documents international guideline divergence on NP thresholds, E/e' thresholds, and LAVI — previously not captured
- Confirms 4/7 guidelines now recommend SGLT2i (confirms ESC 2023 and AHA 2022 findings at international level)
- Provides first structured synthesis of evidence gaps as of early 2024 (GLP-1RA, combined SGLT2/1, rehabilitation)
- Notes STEP-HFpEF/STEP-HFpEF DM published results and SOTA-P-CARDIA as new trials

## Contradictions

**New contradictions introduced by this review:**

- **E/e' threshold divergence (ESC >9 vs. AHA/ACC/HFSA ≥15 vs. others >14):** Same parameter, 6-point spread between guidelines. ESC threshold anchored to mortality risk data; other guidelines use echocardiographic operating points for invasively elevated filling pressures. See [[contradictions]] #17.
- **LAVI threshold divergence (AHA/ACC/HFSA ≥29 mL/m² vs. ESC/others >34 mL/m²):** AHA lower threshold means more patients qualify for the structural HFpEF criterion; diagnostic sensitivity vs. specificity trade-off. See [[contradictions]] #18.
- **NP age-adjustment: uniform vs. age-stratified thresholds:** NHFA/CSANZ uses NT-proBNP >1800 pg/mL for age >75y; standard guidelines use >125 pg/mL uniformly. Effect: many elderly HFpEF patients fail to meet NHFA/CSANZ criterion despite meaningful NP elevation. See [[contradictions]] #19.
