# Tamaki 2023 — NLR + PLR Prognostic in ADHF-HFpEF (PURSUIT-HFpEF)

> In 1,026 ADHF patients with LVEF ≥50% (PURSUIT-HFpEF registry, Japan), combined high NLR (>4.5) AND high PLR (>193) predicted cardiac death with HR 2.66 (P=0.0008) over 429-day follow-up; serial NLR+PLR (admission → discharge) strengthened prediction (HR 2.71); CRP was NOT independently associated with cardiac death.

## Key Findings

- **NLR cut-off: >4.5** (AUC 0.64; sensitivity 65%; specificity 61%; P<0.0001)
- **PLR cut-off: >193** (AUC 0.60; sensitivity 56%; specificity 62%; P=0.0009)
- **Combined high NLR+PLR: HR 2.66 (95% CI 1.51–4.70; P=0.0008) for cardiac death** — strongest predictor
- High NLR alone: HR 2.49 (1.51–4.10; P=0.0003)
- High PLR alone: HR 1.76 (1.09–2.84; P=0.0215)
- All-cause death: high NLR+PLR HR 1.90 (1.31–2.76; P=0.0007)
- **Serial NLR+PLR (admission + discharge; both high):** HR 2.71 (1.33–5.53; P=0.0061) for cardiac death — temporal persistence of high inflammation stronger predictor than single timepoint
- **CRP tertiles: NOT independently associated with cardiac death** in multivariable model (only weakly with all-cause death)
- Comorbidity burden (0–6+ of 8 comorbidities) progressively increased the proportion with high NLR, PLR, or both — CMD, DM, CKD, COPD, AF each additively increase inflammatory marker elevation

## Methods (brief)

**Population characteristics:**

| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| Median age | 83 years (very elderly) |
| Female | 55% |
| BMI | 24 kg/m² |
| LVEF | 62% |
| AF | 46% |
| Anemia | 72% |
| eGFR (median) | 45 mL/min/1.73m² (moderate CKD burden) |
| SGLT2i use | ~2% (effectively unexposed cohort) |

**NLR calculation:** Neutrophil count / lymphocyte count (admission blood draw).  
**PLR calculation:** Platelet count / lymphocyte count (admission blood draw).

**Multivariable adjustment:** Cox regression adjusted for age, sex, LVEF, eGFR, BNP, NYHA class, and comorbidities.

## Limitations

- Registry observational design — no adjustment for unmeasured confounders (e.g., SGLT2i exposure is essentially zero; comorbidities may drive both NLR/PLR and outcomes)
- Single urban Japanese registry (Osaka); very elderly cohort (median age 83) — may not generalise to younger or Western HFpEF populations
- NLR/PLR are non-specific inflammatory markers (elevated by infection, malignancy, haematologic conditions, drug effects) — not HFpEF-specific; confounding by active infection or other acute processes not fully excluded
- AUC of 0.60–0.64 indicates moderate discrimination only — NLR/PLR add modest incremental value over standard predictors
- CRP dissociation from outcomes (vs. NLR association) may reflect differences in what each marker captures (innate immune cellular response vs. hepatic acute-phase protein)

## Contradictions

CRP not independently associated with cardiac death (Tamaki 2023) despite CRP being a widely used systemic inflammation marker — parallels PROMIS-HFpEF (CRP not associated with CMD prevalence). However, NLR (neutrophil-lymphocyte ratio) IS strongly associated with cardiac death in the same cohort. This dissociation — NLR prognostic, CRP not — may reflect that neutrophil-mediated innate immune activation rather than hepatic acute-phase response is the mechanistically relevant inflammatory pathway in HFpEF. Or NLR/PLR may capture multi-system illness burden (frailty, infection, organ dysfunction) not fully captured by CRP. Noted in `wiki/contradictions.md`.
