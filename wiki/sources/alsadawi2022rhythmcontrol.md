# Al-Sadawi 2022 — Rhythm vs. Rate Control in HFpEF+AF

> Meta-analysis of 5 studies (N=16,825 HFpEF+AF) showed rhythm control was associated with significantly lower odds of mortality vs. rate control (OR 0.735; 95% CI 0.665–0.813; P<0.001; I²=0%), with 4 of 5 studies using catheter ablation.

## Key Findings

- **Rhythm control vs. rate control (all-cause/CV mortality and combined outcomes):** OR 0.735 (95% CI 0.665–0.813; P<0.001)
- **Heterogeneity:** I²=0% — results are highly consistent across 5 studies
- **Catheter ablation predominance:** 4 out of 5 studies used catheter ablation as primary rhythm control strategy
- Net relative risk reduction: ~26.5% lower odds of adverse outcome with rhythm control

## Methods

Systematic literature search for studies comparing rhythm vs. rate control in HFpEF+AF. Five studies meeting inclusion criteria (HFpEF defined by LVEF ≥50% or ≥40%; AF documented; outcome data available). Random-effects model; Mantel-Haenszel odds ratio. No significant publication bias detected. Predominantly retrospective/observational studies; one subgroup analysis from an RCT.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

This meta-analysis provides the largest aggregate evidence that rhythm control is superior to rate control in HFpEF+AF. The zero heterogeneity (I²=0%) across 5 independent studies is striking and strengthens confidence in the direction of effect despite observational methodology limitations. Predominantly catheter ablation-driven. The result is mechanistically plausible: in HFpEF, diastolic filling is especially dependent on the atrial contribution (atrial kick) and adequate heart rate control; restoring sinus rhythm via ablation preserves atrial function and reduces rapid rate-related diastolic impairment. Supports and is supported by [[rillig2021eastafnet4]].
