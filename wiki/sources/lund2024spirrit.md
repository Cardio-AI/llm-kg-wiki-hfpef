# Lund 2024 — SPIRRIT-HFpEF Design Paper

> SPIRRIT-HFpEF is the first registry-based randomized controlled trial (RRCT) in HF: a pragmatic PROBE design using SwedeHF (Sweden) + US Trial Innovation Network; primary endpoint total CV deaths + HF hospitalizations; ~2,200 enrolled mid-2024; results expected end 2025.

**Full citation:**
Lund LH, Faxén J, Hage C, et al.; on behalf of the SPIRRIT-HFpEF Investigators. Spironolactone in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction: rationale and design of the SPIRRIT-HFpEF trial. *Eur J Heart Fail.* 2024;26(11):2453–2463. doi:[10.1002/ejhf.3453](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejhf.3453)

## Summary

Design paper for SPIRRIT-HFpEF (NCT02901184), a multicentre PROBE-design RRCT of spironolactone/eplerenone plus usual care vs. usual care alone in HFpEF/HFmrEF. The trial uses the SwedeHF registry (Sweden) and the US Trial Innovation Network (TIN) as platforms, allowing pragmatic enrolment, streamlined consent, and administrative outcomes ascertainment — the first RRCT in HF for a chronic condition. (source: lund2024spirrit)

## Trial Design Summary

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **NCT** | NCT02901184; EudraCT 2016-002019-16 |
| **Platforms** | SwedeHF (Sweden; ~80% of enrolment); US TIN (US; ~20%) |
| **Design** | PROBE (Prospective, Randomized, Open-label, Blinded Endpoint) |
| **Intervention** | Spironolactone or eplerenone + usual care vs. usual care alone |
| **Dose** | 25 mg/day target dose; 50 mg/day if tolerated |
| **Population** | HFpEF/HFmrEF: LVEF ≥40%; NT-proBNP >300 ng/L (sinus rhythm) or >750 ng/L (AF); loop diuretics daily/most days; NYHA II–IV; age ≥50 |
| **Exclusion** | Known EF <40% ever; eGFR <30 mL/min; K+ >5.0 mmol/L; current dialysis; LVAD; CRT; severe primary cardiomyopathy; terminal illness |
| **Primary outcome** | **Total number of CV deaths + total HF hospitalisations** (incidence rate; counting recurrent events) |
| **Secondary outcomes** | Time to CV death or first HHF; individual components; others |
| **Power** | 85% power for HR 0.80, requiring 721 first composite events |
| **Target N** | ~2,400 (final sample size event-driven) |
| **Enrolment status** | ~2,200 enrolled as of Q2 2024; enrolment through 2024; follow-up through end 2025 |
| **Funding** | Swedish Heart-Lung Foundation; Swedish Research Council; Erling Persson Foundation; NHLBI/NIH (USA) |

## Key Design Innovations

**Registry-based RCT (RRCT) concept (Figure 1):**
- Conventional RCT strengths (randomisation, causal inference) + registry strengths (real-world enrolment, low cost, administrative outcomes)
- SwedeHF provides: automated pre-screening, baseline data, linked national outcome registries (death, hospitalisation via Swedish Board of Health and Welfare)
- No study-specific echocardiography required — pragmatic design avoids enrichment by structural criteria that may not be universally available
- No study-specific in-person follow-up visits; K+ and eGFR monitoring by local labs (weeks 1, 4, 8, 26, 52, and as needed)

**MRA as generic, cheap intervention:**
- Spironolactone costs approximately $0.10/day in the US (out-of-pocket) — contrast with finerenone (~$3.61/day)
- Generic drug, low regulatory barrier, widely familiar to clinicians — ideal RRCT target

**Protocol amendment — primary endpoint change:**
- Original primary endpoint was time to first CV death or HHF (time-to-event)
- Amended to total number of CV deaths + HF hospitalisations (incidence rate, counting recurrent events)
- Rationale: blinded interim analysis (2022) suggested fewer than expected events; recurrent event counting increases power by counting all HF hospitalisations, not just the first

**COVID-19 impact:**
- Enrolment delayed substantially during 2020–2021; staff redeployed; enrolment at dramatically reduced rates during pandemic
- Protocol allowed extended follow-up to achieve target events

## Clinical Significance

- SPIRRIT addresses whether the TOPCAT Americas subgroup benefit is reproducible in a broader, more generalisable population
- In the context of FINEARTS-HF (positive for finerenone), SPIRRIT results will determine whether MRA benefit is class-wide or finerenone-specific
- As the first RRCT in chronic HF, it tests the pragmatic trial methodology for future large-scale inexpensive HF trials
