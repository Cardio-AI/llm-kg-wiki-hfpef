# Shi 2022 — sST2 as Diagnostic and Prognostic Biomarker in HFpEF

> sST2 is a **poor diagnostic biomarker** for HFpEF vs. controls (AUC <0.7) but a **strong prognostic marker** for all-cause death (log sST2 HR 2.76; I²=0%; P=0.013) and a composite endpoint (HR 6.52; P=0.000) in HFpEF patients.

## Core Arguments

### Diagnostic Performance of sST2 in HFpEF
- AUC for diagnosing HFpEF vs. healthy controls: generally **<0.70** — poor diagnostic discriminator
- Substantially inferior to NT-proBNP for diagnosing HFpEF
- sST2 elevation is non-specific — also elevated in HFrEF, pulmonary hypertension, and other conditions
- Conclusion: sST2 should NOT replace NT-proBNP or HFA-PEFF diagnostic scoring for HFpEF diagnosis

### Prognostic Value in HFpEF
- **All-cause mortality (multivariable):** log sST2 HR 2.76 per unit (95% CI reported); **I²=0%** (no heterogeneity); P=0.013
- **Composite endpoint (mortality + hospitalisation):** HR 6.52 per unit; P<0.001
- Heterogeneity absent (I²=0%) for mortality endpoint — unusually consistent across studies
- sST2 retains independent prognostic value after adjustment for NT-proBNP in several studies

### Mechanism of sST2 in HFpEF
- sST2 (soluble ST2, IL-33 receptor decoy) reflects myocardial fibrosis and mechanical strain
- Elevated sST2 in HFpEF → increased myocardial fibrosis load, reduced IL-33 signalling → impaired cardioprotection
- sST2 levels correlate with markers of fibrosis (galectin-3) and diastolic dysfunction (E/e') in HFpEF

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

sST2 occupies a distinct niche as a prognostic marker in HFpEF — useful for risk stratification after diagnosis but not for diagnosis itself. The absence of heterogeneity (I²=0%) for the mortality endpoint is notable. Its mechanistic link to fibrosis makes it complementary to NT-proBNP (filling pressures) in the HFpEF biomarker panel. Relevant to the [[fu2024inflammation]] meta-analysis context and the broader fibrosis/inflammation biomarker landscape.
