# CMR Phenotyping Across HF Subtypes

> Cross-sectional CMR study (n=54 HF [22 HFpEF, 17 HFmrEF, 15 HFrEF] + 19 controls): HFpEF is characterised by impaired LA function (EDV 48.1 vs 38.1 mL; reservoir strain 28.9 vs 35.9; P<0.001) and impaired LV GLS (−15.0 vs −19.2%; P=0.001) with preserved LVEF and elevated native T1 (1012 vs 988 ms; P=0.003) — LA dysfunction is the primary structural mechanism in HFpEF, distinguishable from HFmrEF and HFrEF by CMR.

## Core Arguments

**Study type:** Comparative cross-sectional CMR study. N=**54 HF patients** (22 HFpEF, 17 HFmrEF, 15 HFrEF) + **19 healthy controls** (age/sex-matched; approximately 4 controls per patient). CMR at 1.5 Tesla (Philips Achieva). Feature-tracking (FT) strain analysis, T1 mapping, ECV, LGE.

**HFpEF diagnostic criteria:** LVEF ≥50%, plus E/e' >13 OR mean septal LAVI >34 mL/m².

**HFpEF vs controls — key quantitative findings:**

*LA structure/function (primary HFpEF signature):*
- LA end-diastolic volume (EDV): **48.1±9.4 vs 38.1±4.8 mL** (P<0.001) — LA enlargement
- LA reservoir strain (Es): **28.9±10.4 vs 35.9±6.0** (P=0.008) — impaired LA storage
- LA total reservoir strain: **48.4±14.3 vs 62.9±10.0** (P<0.001) — marked LA dysfunction
- LA conduit strain: **20.2±9.5 vs 27.3±5.9** (P=0.021) — impaired passive emptying
- LACI: significantly elevated (P=0.004) — LA/LV coupling mismatch
- LAEF: significantly reduced (P=0.004)

*LV structure/function:*
- LV volumes: **no significant difference from controls** — LVEF preserved, volumes not different
- LV GLS: **−15.0±3.0 vs −19.2±2.0%** (P=0.001) — subclinical systolic impairment despite preserved LVEF

*Fibrosis:*
- Native T1: **1012 ms vs 988 ms** (P=0.003) — diffuse myocardial fibrosis in HFpEF

*NT-proBNP — LA correlation:*
- NT-proBNP correlates with LA Ee ratio: **r=−0.41, P=0.008** — NT-proBNP reflects LA filling pressure

**HF phenotype hierarchy by CMR:**
- HFpEF: disproportionate LA dysfunction; preserved LV volumes; subclinical LV GLS impairment; moderate T1 elevation
- HFmrEF: intermediate; LV booster-pump compensation for impaired conduit; mixed LA/LV involvement
- HFrEF: global myocardial dysfunction; LA and LV both severely impaired; highest T1/fibrosis
- Progressive T1 prolongation: HFpEF (1012 ms) → HFmrEF → HFrEF — fibrosis gradient across phenotypes

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

Provides direct empirical evidence that LA dysfunction — not LV systolic failure — is the primary structural mechanism in HFpEF when assessed by CMR. The preserved LV volumes alongside impaired LV GLS (−15.0 vs −19.2%) demonstrate subclinical systolic dysfunction hidden by conventional LVEF measurement. Native T1 elevation confirms diffuse fibrosis in HFpEF myocardium (consistent with [[fayyaz2025pathophys]] tissue findings). CMR is superior to echocardiography for quantifying LA strain and detecting the HFpEF signature.

The NT-proBNP ↔ LA Ee correlation (r=−0.41) supports the interpretation that natriuretic peptide elevation in HFpEF reflects LA pressure load, not LV systolic stress — relevant to NT-proBNP interpretation and the diagnostic thresholds in H₂FPEF/HFA-PEFF.
