# MAGGIC

> Individual patient meta-analysis of ~39,000 patients with chronic HF showing that all-cause mortality is lower in HFpEF than HFrEF, but the relative advantage shrinks substantially after adjustment for clinical covariates; established the prognosis comparison benchmark.

**File:** `raw/[not yet ingested]` · **Authors:** Pocock SJ et al. · **Year:** 2013 · **Journal:** European Heart Journal  
**Study type:** Meta-analysis (individual patient data) · **N:** ~39,000 · **Population:** Chronic HF across all EF groups  
**Follow-up:** varies by cohort · **Intervention/Exposure:** EF group (preserved vs. reduced) · **Primary outcome:** All-cause mortality

---

## Key Findings

- All-cause mortality lower in HFpEF than HFrEF in unadjusted analyses
- After adjustment for comorbidities, the mortality difference narrows considerably
- HFpEF patients are older with more non-CV comorbidities — much of the apparent survival advantage is confounding
