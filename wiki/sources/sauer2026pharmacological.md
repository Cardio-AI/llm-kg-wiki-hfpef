# Established and emerging pharmacologic options and unmet needs in HFpEF and HFmrEF

> Comprehensive 2026 review of established and emerging pharmacotherapy for HFpEF and HFmrEF; provides the most current cross-society guideline comparison (ESC/AHA-ACC-HFSA/JCS-JHFS/iCARDIO Alliance) and a landscape of active emerging trials including balcinrenone (BALANCED-HF) and vicadrostat (EASi-HF).

**Full citation:**
Sauer AJ, ter Maaten JM, Savarese G, Ponikowski P, Anker SD, Butler J, Zannad F, Filippatos G, Böhm M, Bauersachs J, Pieske B, Lund LH; on behalf of the writing committee. Pharmacological options and needs in heart failure with mildly reduced and preserved ejection fraction. *ESC Heart Fail.* 2026. doi:[10.1093/eschf/xvag056](https://doi.org/10.1093/eschf/xvag056)

## Core Arguments

**1. Established therapies with positive evidence:**
- **SGLT2 inhibitors** are the only class with Class I recommendations across multiple societies for HFpEF (ESC Class I A; JCS/JHFS Class I A). AHA/ACC/HFSA maintains Class IIa B-R. iCARDIO "Strongly recommended."
- **Non-steroidal MRA (finerenone, FINEARTS-HF):** 16% reduction in total WHF + CV death (RR 0.84; P=0.007); approved in the USA for HFpEF and HFmrEF (LVEF ≥40%); JCS/JHFS Class IIa B-R; iCARDIO "Recommended"; ESC/AHA not yet recommended formally.
- **GLP-1 RA / dual GIP/GLP-1 RA (obesity phenotype):** iCARDIO "Strongly recommended" for obesity; JCS/JHFS "Suggested"; ESC/AHA/JCS no formal recommendation yet. STEP-HFpEF (semaglutide) and SUMMIT (tirzepatide, HR 0.62) provide evidence.
- **Steroidal MRAs (spironolactone/eplerenone):** AHA/ACC/HFSA Class IIb B-R for selected patients; JCS IIb B-NR; ESC no recommendation for HFpEF; iCARDIO "Suggested."

**2. No evidence of benefit in HFpEF:**
- RASi/ARBs/ARNIs: no Class I recommendation from any society; selected patients at lower end of LVEF spectrum may benefit (Class IIb in AHA/JCS).
- Beta blockers: **no recommendation from any society for HFpEF** (meta-analysis: no benefit in HF with EF ≥50%). May have indication in HFmrEF.
- IV iron supplementation: ESC Class IIa A for HFmrEF; **no recommendation for HFpEF** from any society (FAIR-HFpEF: 49 m 6MWD improvement, suggestive but N=39 only).

**5. Combination therapy and precision medicine:**
- Three-pillared strategy proposed: SGLT2i + nsMRA + GLP-1 RA simultaneously — parallel to quadruple therapy in HFrEF.
- Phenotype-specific approaches: RASi/ARNI for CKD comorbid HFpEF; GLP-1 RA for obesity; nsMRA for aldosterone-mediated phenotype.
- SGLT2i + nsMRA combination reduces CV death or first worsening HF by 31% (HR 0.69; 95% CI 0.59-0.81) in three-trial combined analysis (DELIVER + FINEARTS-HF + PARAGON-HF).
- Polypharmacy monitoring: electrolytes, BP, eGFR required; potassium binders, low-dose loop/thiazide diuretics to enable MRA use.

**6. Mechanistic pathway diagrams (Figures 2–5):**
- Neprilysin/RAS pathway: sacubitril/valsartan (ARNI), spironolactone (sMRA), finerenone (nsMRA), balcinrenone (MR modulator), vicadrostat (ASI)
- SNS pathway: nebivolol (beta blocker)
- GLP-1/GIP pathway: semaglutide (GLP-1 receptor), tirzepatide (GLP-1 + GIP receptor)
- SGLT2 pathway: dapagliflozin, empagliflozin
