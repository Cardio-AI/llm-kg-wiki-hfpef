# Albulushi 2025 — SGLT2i Myocardial Fibrosis in Diabetic HFpEF (CMR RCT)

> Dapagliflozin reduced myocardial ECV by 3.5% vs. 0.8% placebo (P<0.001) over 12 months in HFpEF+T2DM — first prospective serial CMR evidence for SGLT2i-mediated myocardial fibrosis regression in HFpEF; LVMI, LVEF, 6MWT, and NT-proBNP also improved.

**Full citation:**
Albulushi A, Askari KM, Al-Abedi AM, Al-Kulaibi MA, Hasan MS, Hosseini Z, Al-Rahman MT, Tanoh DB, Hasan AS, Al-Helli Y, Basouni A. Impact of SGLT2 inhibitors on myocardial fibrosis in diabetic HFpEF: a longitudinal study. *Eur J Med Res.* 2025;30:592. doi:[10.1186/s40001-025-02834-7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40001-025-02834-7)

---

## Summary

Multicenter double-blind placebo-controlled RCT at three tertiary care centres (Royal Hospital Muscat; Oman International Hospital; National Heart Center, Oman — with sites in Chicago IL and Cairo Egypt). N=100 (50 dapagliflozin, 50 placebo). HFpEF+T2DM with evidence of elevated myocardial fibrosis by ECV ≥27%. 12-month follow-up. Serial cardiac MRI (CMR) with T1 mapping and ECV quantification as primary endpoint. First prospective human trial quantifying longitudinal myocardial fibrosis changes with SGLT2i in HFpEF. (source: albulushi2025sglt2fibrosis)

## Design

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Design** | Multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT |
| **N** | 100 randomised (50 dapa, 50 placebo); 96 completed 12 months |
| **Intervention** | Dapagliflozin 10 mg/day vs. placebo |
| **Background therapy** | ACEi/ARB + beta-blocker + MRA for ≥3 months prior to enrolment (92%, 78–80%, 64–68% respectively) |
| **Population** | HFpEF: LVEF ≥50%, elevated filling pressures by echo/hemodynamic criteria; T2DM: HbA1c 7.0–10.0%; ECV ≥27% on CMR at baseline |
| **Age** | 65–66 years (mean); male 60% |
| **Exclusion** | eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m²; MRI contraindication; active malignancy |
| **Primary endpoint** | Change in ECV (%) from baseline to 12 months |
| **CMR protocol** | 1.5T Siemens MAGNETOM Avanto; T1 mapping (MOLLI 5(3)3); ECV calculated with hematocrit correction; Syngo.via VB30A software |
| **Imaging timepoints** | Baseline, 6 months, 12 months |
| **Randomisation** | Computer-generated; stratified by baseline ECV (<30 vs. ≥30%) |
| **IRB** | 0075-22-FB (Declaration of Helsinki) |

## Baseline Characteristics

Balanced groups: age 65–66y; BMI ~29 kg/m²; SBP ~135–138 mmHg; HbA1c 7.9–8.2%; eGFR 72–74 mL/min; NT-proBNP 764–786 pg/mL; ECV ~29.5–30%; LVMI ~93–95 g/m²; E/e' ~14–15; E/A ~1.1–1.2. No significant differences between groups at baseline.

## Results

| Outcome | Dapagliflozin | Placebo | P value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ΔECV (%, primary)** | **–3.5% (CI –4.2 to –2.8)** | –0.8% (CI –1.3 to –0.4) | **<0.001** |
| ΔLVMI (g/m²) | –8.2 (CI –9.5 to –7.0) | –2.1 (CI –3.0 to –1.2) | 0.002 |
| ΔLVEF (%) | +3.8 | +1.5 | 0.02 |
| Δ6MWT (meters) | +45 (CI 35–55) | +10 (CI 5–15) | 0.01 |
| ΔNT-proBNP (pg/mL) | –210 (IQR –320 to –170) | –50 (IQR –90 to +50) | 0.008 |
| ΔHbA1c (%) | –1.2 (CI –1.5 to –1.0) | –0.4 (CI –0.6 to –0.2) | 0.01 |
| HF hospitalisation | 4% | 12% | 0.03 |
| All-cause mortality | 2% | 6% | 0.18 |
| HR for HHF | 0.42 (95% CI 0.17–1.03) | — | 0.06 (trend) |

**ECV trajectory (Figure 2):** Dapagliflozin group showed progressive ECV decline from baseline through 6 and 12 months; placebo group remained stable. Separation apparent by 6 months.

**Correlation matrix (Figure 4):** ECV reduction correlated with:
- LVMI change (r=0.45; P=0.002)
- 6MWT improvement (r=0.38; P=0.01)
- HbA1c reduction (r=0.30; P=0.01)
- SBP reduction (r=0.35; P=0.03)

## Mechanistic Interpretation

Proposed SGLT2i antifibrotic mechanisms:
1. **TGF-β pathway inhibition** — reduces myofibroblast activation and collagen deposition
2. **Oxidative stress reduction** — decreases reactive oxygen species driving fibrosis
3. **Extracellular matrix remodelling** — modulates metalloproteinase and TIMP activity
4. **SGLT2 expressed in cardiomyocytes** — direct cardiac effects independent of glycaemia
5. **Synergy with background MRA therapy** — MRAs reduce collagen via aldosterone blockade; SGLT2i reduces via TGF-β/oxidative stress; parallel pathways

ECV >29% associated with significantly higher HF hospitalisation and mortality in prior HFpEF studies (cited reference in paper). ECV reduction of 3.5% may represent meaningful structural risk reversal.

## Clinical Implications

- First prospective serial CMR evidence that SGLT2i (dapagliflozin) reduces myocardial interstitial fibrosis in HFpEF+T2DM
- ECV as a surrogate endpoint for antifibrotic therapy trials — supports use in future mechanistic RCTs
- Diabetic phenotype is highest ECV burden — aligns with obesity/metabolic HFpEF as most responsive to SGLT2i
- ECV-guided treatment stratification: patients with ECV ≥27% may represent highest-yield SGLT2i targets
- Combination SGLT2i + MRA: background MRA use in 78–80% of patients; additional SGLT2i benefit is incremental and additive

## Limitations

- Small sample (N=100); single geographic region (predominantly Middle Eastern cohort)
- Short follow-up (12 months); underpowered for hard outcomes
- No tissue-based molecular markers (TGF-β, collagen turnover) to confirm mechanism
- Dapagliflozin only — results may not fully apply to empagliflozin despite shared class pharmacology
- No standardised serial diastolic echocardiography across sites
- Not powered for all-cause mortality
