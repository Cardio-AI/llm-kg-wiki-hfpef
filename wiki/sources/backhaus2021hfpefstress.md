# HFpEF-Stress Trial (Backhaus 2021)

> Real-time free-breathing cardiac MRI (RT-CMR) during exercise stress noninvasively identifies HFpEF with high accuracy — exercise left atrial (LA) long-axis strain (LAS) was the single best discriminator (AUC 0.93), outperforming echocardiographic E/e', NT-proBNP, and the H₂FPEF/HFA-PEFF scores — in a prospective single-centre trial referenced against invasive right heart catheterisation (RHC).

**Study type:** Prospective single-centre observational, invasive-reference-standard diagnostic accuracy study · **N:** 75 recruited, 68 analysed (34 HFpEF, 34 noncardiac dyspnea) · **Population:** Echocardiographic diastolic dysfunction (E/e' ≥8) + preserved LVEF (≥50%) + exertional dyspnea (NYHA ≥II), referred to University Hospital Göttingen 08/2017–09/2019 · **Follow-up:** None (cross-sectional diagnostic study) · **Intervention/Exposure:** Same-day (±2-day) RT-CMR at rest and during supine bicycle exercise stress, compared against invasive RHC at rest and exercise (reference standard: PCWP ≥15 mmHg rest or ≥25 mmHg exercise) · **Primary outcome:** Diagnostic accuracy (AUC) of RT-CMR-derived LV/LA functional parameters for RHC-defined HFpEF

## Key Findings

- **Baseline:** HFpEF patients (n=34) vs. noncardiac dyspnea (n=34): older (69 vs. 66y, P=0.034), more AF (16 vs. 5, P=0.004), higher NT-proBNP (255 vs. 75 ng/L, P<0.001), higher E/e' rest (12.5 vs. 9.15, P<0.001), higher LAVI (43.8 vs. 36.2 mL/m², P=0.001)
- **Invasive haemodynamics:** PCWP rest 13 vs. 8 mmHg (P<0.001); PCWP exercise 27 vs. 18 mmHg (P<0.001); cardiac index similar at rest (2.9 vs. 2.9) but lower during exercise in HFpEF (5.15 vs. 5.84, P=0.022)
- **RT-CMR LA long-axis strain (LAS) was the best discriminator overall:** AUC 0.82 (rest) → 0.93 (exercise) — the only parameter to show both high absolute accuracy and significant rest-to-exercise improvement (P=0.029)
- **Conventional CMR/echo parameters at rest showed weak discrimination:** LV volumetric parameters AUC 0.51–0.55; NT-proBNP AUC 0.83; E/e' AUC 0.80 (rest, falling to 0.61 at exercise); H₂FPEF score AUC 0.72; HFA-PEFF score AUC 0.77–0.78
- **No group difference in LV native T1 or ECV (myocardial fibrosis)** at rest — contrasts with other invasive/CMR fibrosis studies (see [[rommel2016stiffmap]]) and suggests LA functional impairment, not diffuse LV fibrosis, is the dominant discriminator in this earlier-stage HFpEF-referral cohort
- **LA emptying fraction (LAEF) impaired in HFpEF at rest and failed to increase with exercise** (patients with noncardiac dyspnea showed a compensatory LAEF increase during exercise; HFpEF patients did not) — AUC rest 0.73 → exercise 0.83 (P=0.002)
- **RV ejection fraction modestly higher in HFpEF (67.6 vs. 63.8%, P=0.034)** — an unexpected finding, discussed as possibly reflecting compensatory RV mechanics rather than dysfunction at this disease stage
- 44% of HFpEF (15/34) identified by RHC at rest; the remaining 56% (19/34) required exercise RHC for diagnosis — consistent with the broader exercise-unmasked-HFpEF literature (see [[landsteiner2025hemodynamics]])

## Methods (brief)

Single-centre, Göttingen (University Hospital), DZHK-funded. Screened 873 patients with LVEF ≥50% and E/e' ≥8 from echo database; final n=75 recruited after multi-step exclusion (873→499→75→68 after CMR-related exclusions: CAD n=4, amyloidosis n=1, HCM n=1, valvular disease n=1). RT-CMR: 3.0T Siemens Skyra, free-breathing strongly undersampled radial balanced SSFP sequence, 30 frames/s temporal resolution, 1.6×1.6mm in-plane resolution, 6mm slice thickness — enables true real-time acquisition during actual pedalling (distinct from prior studies that scanned immediately after exercise cessation). RHC via right internal jugular Swan-Ganz catheter with simultaneous exercise CMR-compatible ergometry (Lode). T1 mapping (MOLLI 5(3)3 pre-contrast, 4(1)3(1)2 post-contrast) for native T1/ECV. Feature-tracking (TomTec) for LV GLS/GCS/GRS and LA reservoir/conduit/booster (Es/Ee/Ea) function, plus manual long-axis strain (LAS) analysis (OsiriX).

## Limitations

Single centre; modest sample size (n=68 analysed) limits subgroup power. RHC and CMR performed on different days (1 day apart in all but one case) rather than simultaneously — introduces some temporal mismatch risk. RT-CMR LAS requires manual post-processing (not yet automated) — limits current scalability. No long-term outcome/prognostic follow-up — this is a cross-sectional diagnostic-accuracy study only. Cohort is a referral population with pre-existing echocardiographic diastolic dysfunction, not a general dyspnea population — may not generalise to primary-care screening.

## Contradictions
No native T1/ECV difference at rest between HFpEF and noncardiac dyspnea in this cohort — contrasts with the ECV-based separation reported in [[rommel2016stiffmap]] (STIFFMAP, same modality, different centre and control definition). Flagged as an open methodological question about diffuse-fibrosis CMR markers' sensitivity depending on cohort selection. See `wiki/contradictions.md`.
