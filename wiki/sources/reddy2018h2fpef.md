# H₂FPEF Score

> The H₂FPEF score — six simple clinical and echocardiographic variables scored 0–9 — discriminates HFpEF from noncardiac dyspnoea with AUC 0.841 (derivation) / 0.886 (validation), outperforming prior ESC consensus algorithms; its simplicity makes it practical for outpatient use without requiring NPs.

**Full citation:**
Reddy YNV, Carter RE, Obokata M, Redfield MM, Borlaug BA. A Simple, Evidence-Based Approach to Help Guide Diagnosis of Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction. *Circulation.* 2018;138(9):861–870. doi:[10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.118.034646](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.118.034646)
**DOI:** 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.118.034646  
**Reference standard:** Invasive haemodynamic exercise testing (right heart catheterisation)

## Core Arguments

### The H₂FPEF Score (0–9 points)

| Variable | Points |
|---|---|
| **H** — Heavy (BMI >30 kg/m²) | 2 |
| **²** — Hypertensive (≥2 antihypertensive drugs) | 1 |
| **F** — Atrial Fibrillation (any: paroxysmal or permanent) | 3 |
| **P** — Pulmonary Hypertension (PASP >35 mmHg on echo) | 1 |
| **E** — Elder (age >60 years) | 1 |
| **F** — Filling pressure elevated (E/e' >9) | 1 |
| **Total** | **0–9** |

**Interpretation:**
- Score 0–1: HFpEF unlikely; consider noncardiac dyspnoea
- Score 5–9: HFpEF likely; consider empirical treatment or confirmatory testing
- Score 2–4: Intermediate; invasive testing recommended

### Derivation and Validation
- Derivation cohort: 414 consecutive patients at Mayo Clinic (267 HFpEF, 147 noncardiac dyspnoea); HFpEF prevalence 64%
- Validation cohort: 100 patients (61 HFpEF, 39 controls); AUC 0.886
- Reference standard: invasive haemodynamic exercise testing (PCWP ≥15 mmHg at rest OR ≥25 mmHg on exercise)
- Odds of HFpEF doubled per 1-unit increase (OR 1.98, 95% CI 1.74–2.30)
- AUC improvement over 2016 ESC consensus algorithm: +0.169 (P<0.0001)

### Comparison to HFA-PEFF (Pieske 2019)
- H₂FPEF: simpler, 6 variables, no NP required, validated against invasive standard
- HFA-PEFF: more comprehensive, 3-domain composite score, NP integral, AF-stratified NP thresholds, 4-step algorithm
- NT-proBNP did not incrementally add diagnostic accuracy to the H₂FPEF score beyond the 6 variables

### Key Limitation
- Single-centre, retrospective (Mayo Clinic); external validation limited
- Patients referred for invasive testing — not representative of general HFpEF population (HFpEF prevalence 64% vs. community ~10–30%)
- Early-stage HFpEF (45% had elevated filling pressures only during exercise, not at rest)
