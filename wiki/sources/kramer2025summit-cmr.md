# SUMMIT CMR Substudy — Tirzepatide and Cardiac Structure in Obesity-Related HFpEF

> Tirzepatide reduced LV mass by 11 g and paracardiac adipose tissue by 45 mL over 52 weeks vs. placebo — the first RCT to demonstrate GIP/GLP-1 agonism reduces LV mass in HFpEF; semaglutide did not reduce LV mass in STEP-HFpEF by echocardiography.

**File:** `raw/2025-JACC-Kramer-SUMMIT-CMR_substudy.pdf` · **Authors:** Kramer CM, Borlaug BA, Zile MR, et al. · **Year:** 2025 · **Journal:** J Am Coll Cardiol  
**Study type:** Pre-specified imaging substudy of SUMMIT RCT (NCT04847557) · **N:** 175 enrolled; 106 completed with adequate images (50 tirzepatide, 56 placebo) · **Population:** HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%), obesity (BMI ≥30), NYHA II–IV  
**Intervention/Exposure:** Tirzepatide 2.5 mg → 15 mg SC weekly vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Change in LV mass by CMR at 52 weeks

---

## Key Findings

Tirzepatide reduced LV mass by 11 g (placebo-corrected; 95% CI −19 to −4 g; P=0.004) and paracardiac adipose tissue by 45 mL (95% CI −69 to −22 mL; P<0.001), driven by pericardial fat reduction (−43 g; P<0.001). Epicardial adipose tissue did not change significantly. No between-group differences in LVEF, LV strain, or LA volumes. LV mass change correlated with body weight loss (r=0.34; P<0.02).

This is the first study to demonstrate LV mass reduction by GIP/GLP-1 co-agonism in obesity-related HFpEF. Contrast: semaglutide (STEP-HFpEF echocardiographic substudy) did not reduce LV mass. (source: raw/2025-JACC-Kramer-SUMMIT-CMR_substudy.pdf)

## Methods (brief)

Pre-specified CMR substudy of SUMMIT. 175 patients (24% of trial) at CMR-capable sites underwent baseline CMR. 106 completed baseline and 52-week CMR with adequate image quality. Multiplanar cine imaging at 1.5T or 3T (same scanner for baseline and follow-up). Standard SSFP protocol; ECG-gated, breath-hold. Image analysis blinded to treatment group and time point (25-year experienced analyst; MEDIS software).

Paracardiac adipose tissue defined as epicardial fat (within visceral pericardium) + pericardial fat (beyond parietal pericardium). No T1 mapping, gadolinium, or right ventricular analysis.

**Baseline (N=106):** age 65y, BMI 38 kg/m², weight 98 kg, KCCQ-CSS 53, 6MWD 314m, LVEF 62%, LV mass 120g (tirzep) / 122g (placebo), 52% T2DM, 86% NYHA II. Geographic: 80% Latin America (Argentina 38%, Brazil 42%).

## Limitations

- 58/175 (33%) attrition due to inadequate CMR image quality (obesity-related ECG gating/breath-holding difficulties)
- Site expertise in CMR variable; non-expert CMR sites included
- No T1 mapping — cannot distinguish cardiomyocyte hypertrophy from interstitial fibrosis
- No gadolinium enhancement
- LA volumes from biplane long-axis images (not modified Simpson's short-axis stack) — lower reproducibility
- Geographic skew: 80% Latin America, 0 US completers; may not generalise to European/US HFpEF
- 52% T2DM in this substudy; metabolic contributions to LV mass change cannot be separated

## Contradictions

**Tirzepatide CMR vs. semaglutide echo:** SUMMIT CMR shows LV mass −11 g (P=0.004); STEP-HFpEF echocardiographic substudy shows no LV mass change with semaglutide. Whether this is a true pharmacological difference (GIP co-agonism) or a measurement sensitivity difference (CMR superior to echo for LV mass) is unresolved. See [[contradictions]].
