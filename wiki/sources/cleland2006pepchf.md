# PEP-CHF

> Perindopril failed to significantly reduce all-cause mortality/unplanned HF hospitalisation (HR 0.92, P=0.55) in elderly HF patients with preserved LVEF — the oldest RAAS trial in HFpEF; results confounded by high drug discontinuation.

**Full citation:**
Cleland JGF, Tendera M, Adamus J, et al.; PEP-CHF Investigators. The Perindopril in Elderly People with Chronic Heart Failure (PEP-CHF) study. *Eur Heart J.* 2006;27(19):2338–2345. doi:[10.1093/eurheartj/ehl250](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehl250)
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** 850 · **Population:** Age ≥70 years, LVEF >40%, echocardiographic diastolic dysfunction (LVH or dilated LA), clinical HF · **Mean follow-up:** 26.2 months  
**Intervention:** Perindopril 4 mg vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Composite of all-cause mortality + unplanned HF hospitalisation

---

## Key Findings

Primary composite: HR 0.92 (95% CI 0.70–1.21; P=0.55) — not significant. HF hospitalisation: HR 0.86 (P=0.38). Exploratory 1-year data: nominal (non-significant) reduction in HF hospitalisation. High drug discontinuation: ~36% had discontinued study drug by year 1 — diluted ITT effect.

## Methods (brief)

Double-blind, placebo-controlled. Enrolled elderly patients (age ≥70) with LVEF >40% and echocardiographic evidence of diastolic dysfunction (LVH or LA dilatation). Perindopril 4 mg. No NP-based enrichment — predates NP-threshold era of HFpEF trials.

## Results

Primary composite HR 0.92 (P=0.55). HF hospitalisation HR 0.86 (P=0.38). 1-year exploratory: nominal reduction in HF hospitalisation (exact values pending ingest). [Full results pending ingest.]

## Limitations

Pre-NP-enrichment era: echocardiographic criteria only may include patients without true HFpEF. High early drug discontinuation (~36% by year 1) — substantial dilution of ITT effect. Mean follow-up 26.2 months — shorter than most contemporary HFpEF trials. Small N (850) vs. subsequent trials (I-PRESERVE N=4,128). Perindopril 4 mg — dose adequacy uncertain.

PEP-CHF 1-year exploratory data (trending positive) vs. overall neutral 26-month result — whether early drug discontinuation confounds the ITT analysis or the drug genuinely lacks effect remains debated. High discontinuation is a recurring theme in early HFpEF RAAS trials. See [[contradictions]].
