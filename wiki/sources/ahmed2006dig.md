# DIG-Preserved

> Post-hoc analysis of the DIG trial in patients with HF and LVEF ≥45%; digoxin did not significantly reduce HF hospitalizations; neutral result contributing to the catalogue of failed HFpEF therapies.

**File:** `raw/[not yet ingested]` · **Authors:** Ahmed A et al. · **Year:** 2006 · **Journal:** Circulation  
**Study type:** RCT (post-hoc analysis of DIG ancillary trial) · **N:** ~988 · **Population:** HF with LVEF ≥45%  
**Follow-up:** ~37 months · **Intervention:** Digoxin vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** HF hospitalization

---

## Key Findings

- Digoxin **did not significantly reduce** HF hospitalizations in preserved EF HF
- Neutral result; no mortality benefit

## Methods (brief)

[Expand on paper ingest]

Analysis of the preserved-EF arm of the DIG trial (the main DIG trial included only reduced-EF patients). Post-hoc nature limits conclusions.

## Limitations

[Expand on paper ingest]

- Post-hoc analysis, not a prospective preserved-EF trial
- LVEF ≥45% inclusion; pre-dates modern HFpEF phenotyping
- Digoxin's mechanism (inotropy) is not directly relevant to diastolic dysfunction
