# Reddy 2017 — Arterial Stiffening With Exercise in HFpEF (Reddy/Borlaug)

> In 98 invasively characterized HFpEF subjects vs. 22 hypertensive controls, arterial stiffness indices were similar at rest but diverged dramatically with exercise — HFpEF subjects had higher arterial elastance, lower total arterial compliance, and increased wave reflections during exercise, directly correlated with higher filling pressures and lower CO; inorganic sodium nitrite partially reversed exertional arterial stiffening with hemodynamic benefit.

## Key Findings

### Resting State
- No significant differences in most arterial stiffness indices (Ea, TACI, aortic PP, wave reflections) between HFpEF and hypertensive controls at rest — arterial stiffness appears similar at rest
- PCWP and PA pressures significantly higher in HFpEF at rest (PCWP 18 vs. 8 mmHg; PA mean 28 vs. 16 mmHg; both P<0.0001)

### Submaximal Exercise (20W) — Divergence Unmasked
- **Ea indexed:** 3.26±0.92 (HFpEF) vs. 2.64±0.71 (control); adjusted P=0.04 — higher arterial elastance
- **TACI:** 0.50±0.18 (HFpEF) vs. 0.70±0.22 (control); adjusted P<0.0001 — lower total arterial compliance
- **Peripheral PPA (pulse pressure amplification):** lower in HFpEF (P=0.02) — inadequate vasodilation
- **PCWP at exercise:** 32±7 (HFpEF) vs. 14±5 (control) mmHg; P<0.0001
- **CO at exercise:** 3.4±0.9 vs. 4.2±0.8 L/min/m²; adjusted P=0.05
- All arterial stiffness differences persisted after adjusting for age and BMI (both P<0.001)
- Correlation: higher exercise PCWP with lower TACI (r=−0.42; P<0.0001) and higher Ea (r=0.30; P=0.002); lower CO with lower TACI (r=0.56; P<0.0001) and higher Ea (r=−0.66; P<0.0001)

### Peak Exercise (N=42 HFpEF + 22 controls)
- Peak VO₂: 8.6±2.3 (HFpEF) vs. 14.8±3.8 ml/min/kg; P<0.0001
- Higher Ea (3.16±0.92 vs. 2.45±0.71; P=0.003), higher AIx, lower TACI, lower PPA in HFpEF
- Wave reflection augmentation index (AIx) at peak correlated with: higher Ea (r=0.34; P=0.007), lower TACI (r=−0.52; P<0.0001), higher PCWP (r=0.29; P=0.02), lower CO (r=−0.54; P<0.0001)

### Nitrite Intervention (N=52, 1:1 double-blind randomised)
At 20-W exercise, compared with placebo:
- **PCWP: −8±1 mmHg (P<0.0001)** — large filling pressure reduction
- **CO: +0.8±0.3 L/min (P=0.01)** — improved cardiac output
- **TACI: +0.10±0.02 ml/mm Hg·m² (P=0.0005)** — improved arterial compliance
- **Ea indexed: −0.38±0.14 (P=0.008)** — reduced arterial elastance
- **SVRI: −204±75 (P=0.009)** — reduced systemic vascular resistance
- **Aortic AIx: −8±2% (P<0.0001)** — reduced wave reflections
- At rest: reduced Pb, RM, AIx (wave reflections); no effect on arterial elastance or compliance

## Methods (brief)

**Invasive protocol:** Supine cycle ergometry; right heart catheterization (9F jugular vein; 2F fluid-filled catheter for PCWP and PA pressures); radial artery cannulation (5/6F) for continuous arterial pressure. Central aortic waveform derived by transfer function (SphygmoCor). Simultaneous expired gas analysis for VO₂.

**Arterial waveform analysis:** Central aortic pressure waveforms analyzed for: Ea (effective arterial elastance), TACI (total arterial compliance index), PPA (peripheral pulse pressure amplification), AIx (augmentation index), RM (reflection magnitude), Pf (forward wave), Pb (backward/reflected wave).

## Limitations

- Supine cycling at 20W and ~40-50% lower VO₂ than upright treadmill — not directly comparable to upright exercise physiology; may underestimate true exercise capacity
- No randomisation for the primary comparison (HFpEF vs. controls) — observational; residual confounding by comorbidities possible despite BMI/age adjustment
- Nitrite substudy only 52 patients; no clinical outcome data — hemodynamic proof-of-concept, not efficacy trial
- Hypertensive controls without HF may not fully represent the comparator population relevant to HFpEF therapeutics

## Contradictions

Arterial stiffness indices (Ea, TACI, wave reflections) are similar at rest between HFpEF and hypertensive controls — yet diverge significantly with exercise. This challenges the notion that resting echocardiographic or non-invasive arterial measurements capture the true haemodynamic burden of HFpEF; exercise unmasking is required. Resting comparisons may systematically underestimate arterial disease burden in HFpEF. Noted in `wiki/contradictions.md`.
