# Ferreira 2023 — Spironolactone Echocardiographic IPD Meta-Analysis

> Spironolactone reduced LAVi, LVMi, IVS thickness, E/e' ratio, and increased LVEF across HOMAGE, Aldo-DHF, and TOPCAT (N=984); consistent beneficial structural remodelling provides mechanistic support for MRA use in HFpEF.

**Full citation:**
Ferreira JP, Cleland JG, Girerd N, Bozec E, Rossignol P, Pellicori P, Cosmi F, Mariottoni B, Solomon SD, Pitt B, Pfeffer MA, Shah AM, Petutschnigg J, Pieske B, Edelmann F, Zannad F. Spironolactone effect on cardiac structure and function of patients with heart failure and preserved ejection fraction: a pooled analysis of three randomized trials. *Eur J Heart Fail.* 2023;25(1):108–113. doi:[10.1002/ejhf.2726](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejhf.2726)

---

## Summary

Individual patient data (IPD) meta-analysis of three RCTs comparing spironolactone vs. placebo or usual care on echocardiographic endpoints in HFpEF. Enrolled 984 patients (452 HOMAGE, 398 Aldo-DHF, 134 TOPCAT-Americas). Spironolactone exposure 9–12 months. Analysis by ANCOVA. (source: raw/2023-ESC-Ferreira-spironolactone_effect_hfpef.pdf)

## Design

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Analysis type | IPD meta-analysis (individual patient data), 3 RCTs |
| Trials included | HOMAGE (N=452, stage B; 25–50 mg spiro vs. usual care); Aldo-DHF (N=398, 25 mg spiro vs. placebo); TOPCAT-Americas (N=134, 15–45 mg spiro vs. placebo) |
| Population | Stage B HFpEF (HOMAGE) or stage C HFpEF (Aldo-DHF, TOPCAT); LVEF ≥45–50% |
| Exposure duration | 9–12 months |
| Primary hypothesis | Spironolactone reduces LAVi |
| Analysis method | ANCOVA; outcome = change in echocardiographic parameter; covariate = baseline value; random-effects meta-analysis; interaction p-trend by trial |

## Results

| Parameter | Effect (spiro vs. control) | P value | Heterogeneity (P) |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAVi (ml/m²) | –1.1 (–2.0 to –0.1) | 0.03 | 0.23 |
| LVMi (g/m²) | –3.6 (–6.4 to –0.8) | 0.01 | 0.28 |
| IVS thickness (mm) | –0.2 (–0.3 to –0.1) | 0.01 | 0.74 |
| E/e' ratio | –1.3 (–2.4 to –0.2) | 0.02 | <0.01 |
| LVEF (%) | +1.7 (0.8–2.6) | <0.01 | 0.67 |

**E/e' heterogeneity:** Larger E/e' reduction in Aldo-DHF (–1.27) and TOPCAT (–2.41) vs. HOMAGE (–0.38). Explained by lower baseline E/e' in HOMAGE (less margin for reduction). No evidence of heterogeneity for all other parameters.

**Subgroup analysis:** No treatment-by-subgroup interactions for age, sex, diabetes status, CAD, or hypertension — benefit appears homogeneous across clinical phenotypes.

## Clinical Implications

- Mechanistic support for spironolactone as a cardiac structural remodelling agent in HFpEF: reduces LA volume (fibrillation risk reduction), LV hypertrophy, and filling pressures
- LAVi reduction is clinically meaningful: LA enlargement is an independent predictor of AF, HF worsening, and death in HFpEF
- LVMi and IVS reduction indicates reversal of concentric hypertrophy — the structural hallmark of HFpEF
- E/e' reduction by 1.3 units suggests genuine improvement in LV diastolic stiffness, not just afterload reduction
- LVEF increase (+1.7%) is modest but significant; patients with mildly reduced EF at lower end of inclusion range may have greater margin

## Relationship to Other Evidence

- Provides structural endpoint bridge between TOPCAT outcomes (neutral overall, positive Americas subgroup) and SPIRRIT/SPIRIT-HF outcome trials ([[spirrit]])
- FINEARTS-HF (finerenone, NEJM 2024) confirmed MRA class effect on outcomes → Ferreira 2023 echocardiographic data now sits within a class-wide mechanistic framework
- Turgeon 2025 Bayesian reanalysis of TOPCAT ([[turgeon2025finearts]]) posterior HR 0.87 — Ferreira 2023 structural data is consistent with a true but modest clinical benefit

## Limitations

- Post-hoc echocardiographic subsample of each trial — not pre-specified IPD meta-analysis
- Echocardiographic protocols differed across trials (harmonised but not identical)
- TOPCAT Americas subset only (N=134 of 1,282) — limited contribution
- No clinical outcome data within this analysis — structural changes assumed but not shown to translate to event reduction
