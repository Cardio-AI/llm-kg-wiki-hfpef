# CMR in HFpEF: Comprehensive Phenotyping Review

> Comprehensive review (Eur Heart J Cardiovasc Imaging 2024): multi-parametric CMR — combining volumetrics, strain, LGE, T1/T2 mapping, exercise CMR, and spectroscopy — enables phenotype-specific characterisation of HFpEF pathophysiology including diffuse fibrosis, metabolic derangements (↑myocardial triglycerides, ↓PCr/ATP), haemodynamic reserve limitation, and microvascular dysfunction not captured by standard echocardiography.

## Core Arguments

**Type:** Comprehensive review. No new primary data. CMR techniques systematically reviewed for HFpEF characterisation:

**1. Volumetrics and function**
- LV/RV/LA volumes, LVEF, LACI (LA-LV diastolic coupling index), LAEF
- LACI — ratio of LA volume to LV end-diastolic volume — quantifies LA/LV coupling mismatch; emerges as marker of HFpEF severity
- RV dysfunction present in ~20% of HFpEF patients (links to pulmonary hypertension phenotype)

**2. Strain analysis**
- CMR feature-tracking (FT-CMR): LV/LA global longitudinal, circumferential, radial strain
- LA reservoir, conduit, and booster-pump strain components
- Impaired LA strain precedes overt LV dysfunction in HFpEF

**3. Fibrosis assessment**
- LGE: aetiological distinction (cardiac amyloidosis: subendocardial/global; sarcoidosis: basal septal; hypertensive HFpEF: patchy mid-wall)
- Extracellular volume (ECV): diffuse interstitial fibrosis quantification
- Native T1: elevated in HFpEF; correlates with histological fibrosis ([[lange2024cmr]], [[fayyaz2025pathophys]])

**4. T2 mapping** — myocardial oedema/inflammation detection

**5. Perfusion and microvascular function**
- Coronary flow reserve (CFR) by CMR perfusion imaging
- Reduced CFR in HFpEF; associated with impaired exercise capacity

**6. Metabolic spectroscopy**
- ³¹P-MRS: PCr/ATP ratio — reduced in HFpEF, indicating impaired myocardial energetics
- ¹H-MRS: myocardial triglyceride content — elevated in HFpEF with obesity/T2DM; correlates with diastolic dysfunction
- Hyperpolarised ¹³C-MRS: real-time pyruvate-to-lactate flux (research tool)

**7. Exercise CMR**
- Real-time CMR during supine exercise: measures haemodynamic reserve, fills the diagnostic gap between rest and exertion
- Detects exertional PCWP elevation not captured at rest echo — relevant to occult HFpEF diagnosis

**8. Elastography** — myocardial stiffness quantification (research tool)

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

CMR is the most comprehensive single-modality phenotyping tool for HFpEF, capturing pathophysiology missed by echocardiography: diffuse fibrosis (ECV, T1), metabolic derangements (spectroscopy), haemodynamic reserve (exercise CMR), and microvascular function (CFR). The multi-parametric CMR approach enables mapping of HFpEF endotypes — metabolic, fibrotic, inflammatory, microvascular — which is prerequisite for phenotype-guided therapy. Exercise CMR also provides non-invasive haemodynamic assessment relevant to diagnosis in AF patients where H₂FPEF/HFA-PEFF are unreliable ([[reddy2024afhfpef]]).
