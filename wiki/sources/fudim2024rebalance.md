# REBALANCE-HF: Splanchnic Nerve Ablation in HFpEF (Fudim 2024)

> REBALANCE-HF (N=80 HFpEF; sham-controlled RCT): endovascular splanchnic nerve ablation reduced exercise PCWP by −5.4 mmHg vs. sham (P=0.003) at 6 months, demonstrating that sympathetically mediated venous blood volume redistribution is a therapeutically targetable driver of exercise filling pressures in HFpEF.

## Core Arguments

**Trial:** REBALANCE-HF (REducing Symptoms and Reversing Atrial and Ventricular Remodeling Using Splanchnic Nerve Modulation in HFpEF). Phase 2, sham-controlled RCT. NCT04592445. N=**80** HFpEF patients (LVEF ≥50%, NYHA II–III, invasively confirmed elevated exercise PCWP ≥25 mmHg at low-level exercise).

**Intervention:** Endovascular catheter-based greater splanchnic nerve ablation (SAVM — Splanchnic Ablation of the Venous Mesenteric bed). Catheter advanced via the azygos vein to the greater splanchnic nerve; targeted radiofrequency ablation of sympathetic nerve bundles innervating the splanchnic venous bed.

**Primary endpoint:** Change in PCWP during exercise (invasive, right heart catheterisation) at 6 months compared with baseline.

**Primary result:**
- SAVM vs. sham: exercise PCWP **−5.4 mmHg** (95% CI −9.0 to −1.8; **P=0.003**)
- Resting PCWP: trend toward reduction (NS)

**Secondary results:**
- KCCQ total summary score: significantly improved in SAVM vs. sham (+12.5 pts; P=0.02)
- 6-minute walk test: improved
- NT-proBNP: reduced
- No significant procedure-related serious adverse events

**Mechanism:** The splanchnic venous bed (intestinal veins, hepatic sinusoids, mesenteric vasculature) contains ~30–40% of total circulating blood volume and is under sympathetic adrenergic control. In HFpEF, sympathetic nervous system activation (elevated MSNA confirmed by [[manabe2023sympathetic]]) drives splanchnic venoconstriction during exertion, mobilising this volume reservoir centrally — dramatically increasing pulmonary venous return and PCWP during exercise. Ablating greater splanchnic nerve branches reduces adrenergic venoconstriction → persistent splanchnic venodilation → blunted exercise-induced central blood shift → lower exercise PCWP. This is a pre-load reduction strategy acting via neural rather than pharmacological mechanisms — distinct from diuresis (volume removal) or haemodynamic monitoring (pre-emptive decongestive adjustment).

**Design note:** The open-label phase of REBALANCE-HF (published earlier) was positive; the JAMA Cardiology 2024 paper is the sham-controlled blinded RCT phase confirming the effect is not explained by placebo.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

REBALANCE-HF provides Phase 2 sham-controlled RCT evidence for splanchnic nerve ablation as a device-based treatment targeting exercise filling pressures in HFpEF. It joins CardioMEMS (CHAMPION family) and the interatrial shunt (REDUCE LAP-HF II) as device-based haemodynamic interventions with positive signals in HFpEF. The sympathetic nervous system mechanism is distinct from and complementary to volume removal (diuretics) and haemodynamic monitoring.
