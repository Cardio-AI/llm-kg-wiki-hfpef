# EMPULSE — In-Hospital Empagliflozin in Acute HF (Voors 2022)

> Empagliflozin initiated in-hospital in haemodynamically stable acute HF achieved a win ratio of 1.36 (P=0.0054) over 90 days, establishing the safety and clinical benefit of early in-hospital SGLT2i initiation across the LVEF spectrum.

**Full citation:**
Voors AA, Angermann CE, Teerlink JR, et al. The SGLT2 inhibitor empagliflozin in patients hospitalized for acute heart failure: a multinational randomized trial. *Nat Med.* 2022;28:568–574. doi:[10.1038/s41591-021-01659-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-021-01659-1)
**Year:** 2022 · **Journal:** Nat Med 2022;28:568–574 · **DOI:** 10.1038/s41591-021-01659-1  
**ClinicalTrials.gov:** NCT04157751  
**Study type:** Multinational double-blind randomised placebo-controlled RCT · **N:** 530 randomised (empagliflozin 265; placebo 265); 524 received ≥1 dose (safety population)  
**Screened:** 566; enrolled at 118 centres in 15 countries  
**Population:** Acute de novo (33.2%) or decompensated chronic HF (66.8%), LVEF-unrestricted; haemodynamically stable (SBP ≥100 mmHg, no IV diuretics in preceding 6 h, no IV vasodilators in preceding 6 h, no IV inotropes in preceding 24 h); randomised ≥24 h and ≤5 days after admission; elevated NT-proBNP ≥1,600 pg/mL (non-AF) or ≥2,400 pg/mL (AF)  
**Follow-up:** 90 days  
**Intervention:** Empagliflozin 10 mg once daily vs. matched placebo, initiated in-hospital (median 3 days [IQR 2–4] from admission to randomisation)  
**Primary outcome:** Hierarchical win ratio composite: (1) all-cause death; (2) number of HF events; (3) time to first HF event; (4) KCCQ-TSS change ≥5 points at day 90  
**Funded by:** Boehringer Ingelheim and Eli Lilly

## Key Findings

- **Primary endpoint (stratified win ratio): 1.36 (95% CI 1.09–1.68; P=0.0054)** — significant; 53.9% empagliflozin wins vs 39.7% placebo wins; 6.4% ties
- Deaths: 11 (4.2%) empagliflozin vs 22 (8.3%) placebo; time-to-death win ratio component: 7.2% vs 4.0%
- HF events: 28 patients (10.6%) vs 39 (14.7%); total HF events 36 vs 52; HFE frequency win component: 10.6% vs 7.7%
- KCCQ-TSS change from baseline to day 90: adjusted mean 36.19 (95% CI 33.28–39.09) vs 31.73 (28.80–34.67); adjusted mean difference +4.45 (0.32–8.59)
- KCCQ-TSS ≥10-point improvement at day 90: 83.1% empagliflozin vs 76.3% placebo; odds ratio 1.52 (0.93–2.50)
- NT-proBNP AUC (baseline to day 30): adjusted geometric mean ratio 0.90 (0.82–0.98) — significant reduction
- Diuretic response: greater weight loss per loop diuretic dose at day 15 (−3.33 vs −1.02 kg/40 mg; difference −2.31) and day 30 (−3.80 vs −1.01; difference −2.79)
- CV death or HFE at end of trial: HR 0.69 (0.45–1.08) — not significant
- LVEF subgroup: HFrEF (LVEF ≤40%) win ratio 1.35 (1.04–1.75); HFpEF (LVEF >40%) win ratio 1.39 (0.95–2.03); interaction P=0.9008
- De novo vs decompensated chronic HF: win ratio 1.29 (0.89–1.89) vs 1.39 (1.07–1.81); interaction P=0.7590
- Diabetic vs non-diabetic: win ratio 1.47 (1.07–2.02) vs 1.30 (0.97–1.73); interaction P=0.5683
- Renal safety: eGFR <30 mL/min — 27 (10.2%) empagliflozin vs 24 (9.1%) placebo; acute renal failure 7.7% vs 12.1% (lower with empagliflozin); no ketoacidosis in either arm

## Methods (brief)

Double-blind placebo-controlled RCT across 118 centres in 15 countries. Empagliflozin initiated in-hospital once patient was haemodynamically stable. Median time from admission to randomisation: 3 days (IQR 2–4). LVEF-unrestricted — includes HFrEF, HFmrEF, and HFpEF. Primary composite tested hierarchically: (1) all-cause death; (2) number of HF events (frequency); (3) time to first HF event; (4) KCCQ-TSS change ≥5 points at day 90. Stratified win ratio calculated using non-parametric generalised pairwise comparison within HF status strata; weights analogous to Mantel-Haenszel approach.

## Limitations

- 90-day follow-up — long-term effects on mortality and HF hospitalisation unknown (only CV death or HFE at end of trial was a secondary endpoint; HR 0.69 but non-significant)
- Predominantly HFrEF (68.7%); HFpEF subgroup (28.7%) underpowered for definitive conclusion
- Win ratio: ordering of hierarchy (death > HF event frequency > time to first HFE > KCCQ) — KCCQ dominates numerically because it is the most common tier at which wins/losses are determined (35.9% vs 27.5% of comparisons); the trial was not powered for individual components
- Asia region win ratio 0.66 (0.34–1.30) — directionally reversed but non-significant; interaction P=0.0602 (near threshold); regional heterogeneity unexplained
- Initiated in-hospital — settings without adequate in-hospital infrastructure may have reduced feasibility; required ≥24 h hospitalisation with stabilisation criteria
- Short enrolment window (June 2020–February 2021); ARNI use low (13.6%), SGLT2i use at baseline not reported (de novo patients would not be on it)

## Contradictions

HFpEF subgroup win ratio 1.39 (0.95–2.03) is directionally consistent with HFrEF (1.35, 1.04–1.75) but crosses 1.0, reflecting insufficient statistical power. The nominal non-significance in HFpEF should not be interpreted as evidence against effect — EMPULSE was not powered for LVEF subgroups. EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER provide definitive chronic HFpEF benefit data for the same drug class.
