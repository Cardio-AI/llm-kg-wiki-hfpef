# Pulmonary Arterial Capacitance in HFpEF+PH (Al-Naamani 2015)

> PAC <1.1 mL/mmHg predicts ~5× greater mortality risk in HFpEF+PH-LHD (HR 4.9; 95% CI 1.9–12.4; P<0.001 by Kaplan-Meier analysis); outperforms PVR, DPG, and TPG as a prognostic marker (AUC 0.73 vs. 0.50, 0.45, 0.37 respectively). In multivariate Cox, PAC per 1 mL/mmHg increase: HR 0.48 (0.26–0.89; P=0.02).

**File:** `raw/2015-JACC-Al-Naamani-pulmonary-arterial-capacitance.pdf` · **Authors:** Al-Naamani N, Preston IR, Paulus JK, Hill NS, Roberts KE  
**Year:** 2015 · **Journal:** JACC Heart Failure 2015;3(6):467–474 · **DOI:** 10.1016/j.jchf.2015.01.013  
**Study type:** Prospective cohort · **N:** 73 (Iso-PH n=47; Comb-PH n=26) · **Population:** HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%) + PH due to left heart disease (PH-LHD); mean age 69±12 y; 74% female; 93% non-Hispanic White; BMI 33±10 kg/m² · **Follow-up:** Median 3.6 years (IQR 4.5 years); all followed until death or October 31, 2013; enrollment Jan 2004–Dec 2012  
**Intervention/Exposure:** Pulmonary arterial capacitance (PAC = stroke volume / pulmonary pulse pressure), DPG, PVR, TPG at right heart catheterization  
**Primary outcome:** All-cause mortality

---

## Key Findings

- PAC <1.1 mL/mmHg: sensitivity 91% (95% CI 84–98%), specificity 38% (95% CI 27–49%), PPV 74%, NPV 69%; AUC 0.73 (95% CI 0.60–0.86) for all-cause mortality
- DPG AUC 0.50, TPG AUC 0.45, PVR AUC 0.37 — PVR worse than chance; PAC significantly more discriminating than all three (P=0.004, 0.001, and 0.001 vs. PAC)
- PAC <1.1 mL/mmHg vs. ≥1.1 mL/mmHg (Kaplan-Meier): HR 4.9 (95% CI 1.9–12.4; P<0.001)
- **Multivariate Cox** (Table 4): only **age** (HR 1.78 per 10-yr increase, 95% CI 1.14–2.79; P=0.01) and **PAC** (HR 0.48 per 1 mL/mmHg increase, 95% CI 0.26–0.89; P=0.02) independently predicted mortality
- Comb-PH (DPG ≥7 mmHg): HR 1.15 (95% CI 0.51–2.62; P=0.73) — combined pre+post-capillary PH not associated with worse survival vs. Iso-PH
- Acute vasodilator response (iNO; 57/73 tested; 18% met Rich criteria, 4% met Sitbon criteria): not associated with improved survival (P=0.60 and 0.95 respectively)
- Cumulative incidence of death during study: 34% (25/73)

## Methods (brief)

Prospective single-centre cohort; Tufts Medical Center, Boston; January 2004–December 2012. All patients had HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%) with PH documented on RHC (mean PAP ≥25 mmHg, PCWP >15 mmHg). Excluded: PH Groups 1, 3, 4, 5; moderate-severe left-sided valvular disease; hypertrophic cardiomyopathy; pericardial disease; amyloidosis. Haemodynamic variables computed at RHC. PAC = stroke volume / pulmonary pulse pressure. Optimal PAC threshold identified by ROC analysis (sensitivity >90%, maximal achievable specificity). Multivariable Cox: backward stepwise elimination, entry p<0.05, removal p>0.10.

**Baseline hemodynamics (total population, Table 2):** Mean PAP 41±11 mmHg; PAWP 21±4 mmHg; PAC 2.1±1.2 mL/mmHg; PVR 4.9±3.7 WU; CO 4.7±1.5 L/min; SV 69±29 mL; BNP median 313±357 pg/mL (n=56). NYHA class II: 48%; III: 40%; IV: 9%. Comorbidities: AF 44%; HTN 75%; DM 30%; CAD 32%; OSA 38%.

## Results

**ROC analysis (AUC for mortality prediction):**

| Variable | AUC (95% CI) | P vs. PAC |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| PAC | 0.73 (0.60–0.86) | — |
| DPG | 0.50 (0.36–0.65) | 0.004 |
| TPG | 0.45 (0.30–0.60) | 0.001 |
| PVR | 0.37 (0.22–0.53) | 0.001 |

**Kaplan-Meier survival by PAC threshold:**

| PAC threshold | HR (95% CI) | P |
|--------------|-------------|---|
| PAC <1.1 vs. ≥1.1 mL/mmHg | 4.9 (1.9–12.4) | <0.001 |

PAC <1.1 mL/mmHg: sensitivity 91% (84–98%), specificity 38% (27–49%), PPV 74%, NPV 69%.

**Multivariate Cox regression (Table 4; final model):**

| Variable | HR (95% CI) | P |
|----------|-------------|---|
| Age per 10-yr increase | 1.78 (1.14–2.79) | 0.01 |
| PAC per 1 mL/mmHg increase | 0.48 (0.26–0.89) | 0.02 |

Comb-PH (DPG ≥7 mmHg) vs. Iso-PH: HR 1.15 (95% CI 0.51–2.62; P=0.73) — no survival difference between PH subtypes.

## Limitations

- Single centre; N=73 — small; event rate not explicitly reported
- No prospective validation cohort
- PAC threshold (1.1 mL/mmHg) derived and applied in the same cohort (overfitting risk)
- PCWP >15 mmHg criterion for PH-LHD excludes patients with purely exercise-induced PH
- DPG ≥7 mmHg as Comb-PH criterion differs from 2015 ESC definition (DPG ≥7 + PVR >3 WU); limits comparability
- Short follow-up window for some patients (enrolled Dec 2012)

## Contradictions

Current ESC/ACC guidelines classify PH by PVR and DPG; this study shows PVR (AUC 0.37) is a worse prognostic predictor than PAC (AUC 0.73) in HFpEF+PH-LHD. Update `wiki/contradictions.md` if a dedicated PH-HFpEF contradictions entry is created.
