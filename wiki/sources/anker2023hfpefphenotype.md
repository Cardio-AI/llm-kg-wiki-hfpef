# Patient Phenotype Profiling in HFpEF — Anker 2023 (HFA/ESC Scientific Statement)

> Two-layer model: SGLT2i universal for all HFpEF; additional therapy phenotype-guided across 18 comorbidity phenotypes using a structured treatment wheel (Figure 2).

**Full citation:**
Anker SD, Usman MS, Anker MS, Butler J, Böhm M, Abraham WT, et al. Patient phenotype profiling in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction to guide therapeutic decision making. A scientific statement of the Heart Failure Association, the European Heart Rhythm Association of the European Society of Cardiology, and the European Society of Hypertension. *Eur J Heart Fail.* 2023;25(7):936–955. doi:[10.1002/ejhf.2894](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejhf.2894)

Scientific statement of the Heart Failure Association (HFA) of the ESC, the European Heart Rhythm Association (EHRA), and the European Society of Hypertension (ESH). Based on a workshop held in Nice, October 2021.

## Core Arguments

### Universal Foundation: SGLT2i for All HFpEF

SGLT2 inhibitors (dapagliflozin, empagliflozin) are the only pharmacotherapy with convincingly demonstrated improvement in clinical outcomes across HFmrEF and HFpEF (LVEF >40%). Evidence base:

- **EMPEROR-Preserved:** 21% reduction in composite first HHF/CV death (HR 0.79; P<0.001). Benefit consistent in LVEF >50%, males/females, T2DM and non-T2DM subgroups. Also slowed eGFR decline. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)
- **DELIVER:** Significant reduction in first HHF/CV death. No EF heterogeneity across the LVEF range. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)
- **SOLOIST-WHF:** Sotagliflozin (SGLT2/SGLT1 combined inhibitor) in T2DM + worsening HF (N=1,222); reduction in total HHF/CV death; consistent in both HFrEF and HFpEF subgroups. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)
- **Meta-analysis (5 trials):** Significant reduction in HHF + CV death.

Adverse effects: urinary/genital infections (infrequent). Avoid in eGFR <20 ml/min/1.73 m². Euglycaemic ketoacidosis in rare cases — avoid in HFpEF patients with precipitating ketoacidosis risk factors. SGLT2i benefit stable across blood pressure range, including "decapitated hypertension" (decline of BP over time in HFpEF). (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

## Phenotype-Specific Key Content

### Sex Differences
Women are more prone to HFpEF than HFrEF. Mechanisms: mitochondria express oestrogen receptors → protection against ischaemia/reperfusion injury; smaller LV chambers, increased wall thickness, concentric remodelling, lower diastolic compliance and reserve, greater arterial stiffness, impaired pulmonary vascular remodelling. Predisposing conditions enriched in women: metabolic risk factors, autoimmune disease, preeclampsia, breast cancer treatment. Women with HFpEF have worse symptoms and QoL but lower mortality than men. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

SGLT2i: benefit similar in men and women regardless of LVEF (EMPEROR-Preserved, DAPA-HF). Sacubitril/valsartan (PARAGON-HF): women HR 0.73 vs. men HR 1.03 — sex-treatment interaction (post-hoc of negative trial). (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### LVEF 50–55% (Borderline Subgroup)
LVEF 50–55% is borderline reduced (male normal: 52–72%; female: 54–74%). TOPCAT: patients 50–<55% had worse outcomes than normal LVEF and showed spironolactone benefit. PARAGON-HF: sacubitril/valsartan benefit pronounced at LVEF ≤median 57%. EMPEROR-Preserved: empagliflozin benefit emerges at LVEF ≥50% to <64%. This group represents a distinct clinical subphenotype. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### Very High LVEF (>65%/>70%)
U-shaped mortality: lowest at LVEF 60–65%; higher at both lower and higher ends. LVEF >70% → consider secondary HFpEF (amyloidosis, HOCM, valvular). Need HFA-PEFF algorithm with CMR, Tc-PYP, endomyocardial biopsy, genetic testing. Specific treatments: mavacamten (HOCM), tafamidis (ATTR), enzyme substitution (Fabry), aortic valve intervention (paradoxical low-gradient aortic stenosis). (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### Iron Deficiency
Prevalence 50–75% in HFpEF. Associated with lower VO2, dyspnoea, reduced 6MWD, reduced HRQOL. FAIR-HFpEF (NCT03074591) and PREFER-HF (NCT03833336) are both ongoing RCTs of IV iron (ferric carboxymaltose vs. placebo) in HFpEF; primary endpoint 6MWT at 52 weeks or 24 weeks respectively. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### Coronary Artery Disease + Coronary Microvascular Dysfunction
CAD prevalence: 35–76%. CMD: PROMIS-HFpEF (N=202, prospective, non-invasive coronary flow reserve via adenosine stress transthoracic Doppler): 66% endothelium-independent CMD + 24% endothelium-dependent CMD = 91% total. Two previous studies: 71–72% CMD prevalence. CMD → more fibrosis at CMR, worse diastolic function, worse outcomes. CMD as a therapeutic target; no large RCT yet. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### Atrial Fibrillation
Prevalence up to 50% (including paroxysmal). Strong bidirectional relationship with HFpEF. HHF in HFpEF may result from AF episodes. EAST-AFNET4: early rhythm control (antiarrhythmic drugs or ablation) reduced CV death/stroke/HF hospitalisation — results extrapolated to HFpEF with caution (not dedicated HFpEF trial). **CABA-HFpEF** (NCT05508256): Phase III, DZHK, randomised catheter ablation vs. rate control specifically in HFpEF patients with AF — will provide direct evidence. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### High Heart Rate and Chronotropic Incompetence
High RHR (>80 bpm): 20–30% of HFpEF. Association with adverse outcomes in secondary analyses of CHARM and I-PRESERVE. Ivabradine (EDIFY trial): no anti-hypertrophic/anti-remodelling effect, no improvement in exercise tolerance or NT-proBNP in HFpEF. myPACE RCT: personalised accelerated heart rate setting improved NT-proBNP, physical activity, KCCQ. Beta-blockers: uncertain overall benefit; **beta-blocker withdrawal significantly increased functional capacity and VO2 uptake** in HFpEF with chronotropic incompetence — suggests these drugs may be harmful in this subphenotype. Rate-adaptive atrial pacing did not improve exercise capacity. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### Arterial Hypertension
Most prevalent CV risk factor in HFpEF: 56% in HFrEF, 64% in HFmrEF, 72% in HFpEF (Swedish HF registry). SBP-mortality is U-shaped in HFmrEF/HFpEF; ACC/AHA/HFSA recommend SBP <130 mmHg. SBP <120 mmHg may associate with worse outcomes (OPTIMIZE-HF registry). HYVET study: indapamide 64% reduction in HF occurrence in elderly hypertensives. SPRINT analysis: subgroups supporting SBP <130 mmHg benefit in HFpEF. RAAS inhibitors (CHARM, I-PRESERVE, PARAGON-HF) failed to improve outcomes despite antihypertensive effect — suggests BP-independent mechanism may explain HFpEF failures. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### MRA (Spironolactone)
TOPCAT overall neutral; Americas positive; Russia/Georgia compliance issue confirmed by spironolactone metabolites. **SPIRIT-HF** (NCT04727073) and **SPIRRIT** (NCT02901184) are both ongoing to further delineate MRA role in HFpEF. Current ESC position: MRA "may be considered" after weighing benefits vs. hyperkalaemia. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

**FINEARTS-HF** (NCT04435626): ongoing RCT of finerenone (non-steroidal, selective MRA) vs. placebo in HFpEF (LVEF ≥40%, eGFR ≥25 ml/min/1.73 m²) — evaluating effect on HHF/CV death. First trial of a novel MRA class in HFpEF. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### Type 2 Diabetes and Obesity
T2DM: 27–43% in HFpEF trials, 20% newly diagnosed. Mechanisms: AGEs → myocardial stiffness/fibrosis; free fatty acid lipotoxicity; hyperinsulinaemia → endothelial dysfunction; titin switch → maladaptive relaxation. SGLT2i: approved for T2DM + HFpEF (dual indication). GLP-1 RA (semaglutide, tirzepatide): excellent weight-loss efficacy; flagged in this statement for obesity treatment in HFpEF alongside SUMMIT (NCT04847557) and STEP-HFpEF (NCT04788511). Weight loss also reduces LV mass and mass/volume ratio, improves diastolic function. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### HFpEF in Cancer Patients
Cancer therapies excluded from all major HFpEF RCTs — no SGLT2i evidence in this subgroup. Doxorubicin → diastolic dysfunction in 60% at 1 year, 80% at 3 years (N=362, breast cancer). Radiation: microvascular endothelial damage and fibrosis. Monoclonal antibodies and TKIs: vascular stiffness, hypertension → diastolic dysfunction. Careful CV workup including echo recommended for all cancer patients receiving cardiotoxic agents. General HFpEF treatment principles apply (SGLT2i, diuretics), with caution given infection risk during cytotoxic chemotherapy. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

### Secondary HFpEF (Mimics — Table 1)
| Mimic | Key diagnostic tests |
|---|---|
| Restrictive cardiomyopathy (ATTR, AL) | Tc-PYP scintigraphy (ATTR); Bence-Jones protein; galactosidase (Fabry) |
| Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy | Echo, CMR, genetic testing |
| Constrictive pericarditis | Echo (respirophasic septal shift, hepatic vein flow reversal), CMR |
| Valvular heart disease | Echo (paradoxical low-gradient AS in elderly women with hypertension/T2DM) |

ATTRwt present in 13% of HFpEF with LVH; 5% without LVH. APPOLLO-B (NCT03997383): patisiran (RNA interference) for ATTR-CM ongoing. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

## Contradictions
- SGLT2i benefit at very high LVEF (>65%) uncertain; Anker 2023 notes this group may need individualized assessment (secondary HFpEF exclusion) before universal SGLT2i. This creates tension with the "universal for all HFpEF" framing.
- Beta-blockers in HFpEF: Anker 2023 highlights that withdrawal improves function in chronotropic incompetence subgroup; yet beta-blockers are recommended as add-on in hypertension and ischaemic heart disease subgroups — same drug contradictory across phenotypes.
