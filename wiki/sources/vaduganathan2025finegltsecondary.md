# Effects of Finerenone With and Without SGLT2 Inhibitor Use (Vaduganathan 2025 / FINEARTS-HF)

> Prespecified subgroup analysis of FINEARTS-HF (N=6001) by baseline SGLT2i use: finerenone reduced cardiovascular death and total worsening HF events similarly regardless of baseline SGLT2i use (RR 0.83 with SGLT2i vs. 0.85 without; P-interaction=0.76), with nearly double the absolute risk reduction in the higher-risk SGLT2i-treated subgroup — supporting complementary, additive use of the two drug classes rather than redundancy.

## Key Findings

- **817 participants (13.6%) treated with an SGLT2i at baseline** (8 different agents; dapagliflozin n=406 [48.7%], empagliflozin n=394 [47.2%], canagliflozin n=25 [3.0%] most frequent)
- **SGLT2i users at baseline had a substantially higher-risk profile**: more likely male, more recent HF events, higher natriuretic peptides; 75.4% had diabetes, 55.0% had eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m² (vs. lower rates in non-SGLT2i-users, Table 1)
- **Primary outcome (CV death + total worsening HF events) consistent regardless of baseline SGLT2i use**: RR 0.83 (95% CI 0.60–1.16) with SGLT2i vs. RR 0.85 (95% CI 0.74–0.98) without; P-interaction=0.76
- **Absolute risk reduction nearly double in the SGLT2i-treated subgroup** (4.7 vs. 2.5 total events per 100 patient-years) — because baseline event rates were much higher in that subgroup (24.2 vs. 15.2 per 100 patient-years with placebo)
- **Symptom benefit (KCCQ-TSS) consistent**: finerenone +7.6 pts (SGLT2i) vs. +8.0 pts (no SGLT2i); placebo +5.6 vs. +6.5; between-arm difference +2.0 vs. +1.5 points; P-interaction=0.66
- **Safety consistent regardless of SGLT2i use**: similar rates of serious adverse events, hyperkalemia, and hypokalemia in both arms irrespective of background SGLT2i; potassium rose by +0.19 mmol/L at 1 month in both subgroups (P-interaction=1.00); eGFR decline from baseline to 3 months greater with finerenone than placebo regardless of SGLT2i use (P-interaction=0.21)
- **SGLT2i initiation during the trial was less common with finerenone**: among 5184 participants without baseline SGLT2i use, 980 (18.9%) initiated one during follow-up — 17.7% in the finerenone arm vs. 20.1% in placebo (HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.76–0.97, P=0.02)
- **SGLT2i discontinuation was similar between arms** among the 817 baseline users (24.7% finerenone vs. 23.8% placebo; HR 1.02, 95% CI 0.77–1.35, P=0.89)
- **Time-updated analysis** (treating SGLT2i use as a time-varying covariate) did not meaningfully alter the finerenone treatment effect on the primary outcome (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.76–0.95, P=0.003)
- No new safety signals identified with combined finerenone + SGLT2i use

## Methods (brief)

Prespecified subgroup analysis of FINEARTS-HF (randomised 1:1, finerenone [target dose 20 mg/day if baseline eGFR ≤60 mL/min/1.73m², 40 mg/day if higher] vs. matching placebo; MRAs prohibited during the trial). Baseline SGLT2i use = any SGLT2i at any dose at randomisation, of any type, including in combination with other antihyperglycaemic therapy. Recurrent events (primary outcome and total worsening HF events) analysed by the semiparametric proportional rates method (Lin et al.); time-to-first-event outcomes by Cox proportional hazards models, stratified by geographic region and baseline LVEF (<60% vs. ≥60%); treatment-effect heterogeneity assessed via an interaction term for treatment randomisation × baseline SGLT2i use. Early eGFR change (baseline to 3 months) assessed via linear regression including baseline eGFR, treatment arm, concomitant SGLT2i use, and their interaction. Postbaseline SGLT2i initiation/discontinuation assessed using Cox proportional hazards models, as prespecified in the FINEARTS-HF Academic Statistical Analysis Plan.

## Limitations

Prespecified but not powered specifically to detect treatment-effect heterogeneity by SGLT2i use — within-subgroup findings should be interpreted cautiously (paper's own stated limitation). Baseline SGLT2i use was modest (13.6%), reflecting the trial's 2020–2023 enrolment period predating SGLT2i's more widespread contemporary uptake in HFmrEF/HFpEF — FINEARTS-HF nonetheless enrolled the most SGLT2i-background patients of any MRA trial in this population to date. No access to historical medication records — some participants classified as SGLT2i-nonusers may have previously used and discontinued an SGLT2i before randomisation. Specific reasons for SGLT2i discontinuation during the trial were not collected.
