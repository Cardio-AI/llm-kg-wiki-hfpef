# Kittleson 2024 — 2024 Update to 2020 ACC/AHA HF Performance & Quality Measures

> The 2024 update adds 3 new performance measures (GDMT at discharge for HFrEF, BP control in HFpEF, SGLT2 inhibitors for HFrEF) and 6 new quality measures (SGLT2i for HFmrEF/HFpEF, social determinants screening, pregnancy counselling, GDMT continuation in HFimpEF, GDMT optimisation before TEER, amyloid screening) — reflecting SGLT2i evidence and HFpEF-specific management priorities.

## Core Content

### New Performance Measures (3 total — Class 1 guideline recommendations)

| PM | Short Title | Key Specification |
|---|---|---|
| PM-1 | Start GDMT at Discharge From HF Hospitalisation (Inpatient) | ≥18y, LVEF ≤40%; prescribe ARNi, ACEi, ARB, beta-blockers, MRA, SGLT2i at discharge |
| PM-2 | BP Control in HFpEF With Hypertension | ≥18y, LVEF ≥50%; SBP <130 mmHg + DBP <80 mmHg; inpatient+outpatient |
| PM-3 | SGLT2 Inhibitor Therapy for HFrEF | ≥18y, LVEF ≤40%; SGLT2i prescribed in inpatient and outpatient settings |

### New Quality Measures (6 total — Class 1 or 2a recommendations, or exploratory)

| QM | Short Title | Key Specification |
|---|---|---|
| QM-1 | SGLT2 Inhibitor for HFmrEF or HFpEF | LVEF >40%; SGLT2i use documented (quality measure, not yet performance measure) |
| QM-2 | Screening for Social Determinants of Health | All HF patients; standardised SDOH screening tools |
| QM-3 | Counselling Regarding Pregnancy and CV Risk | Individuals with cardiomyopathy; patient-centred counselling |
| QM-4 | Continuation of GDMT in HFimpEF | LVEF recovery ≥40%; continue medications (do not discontinue) |
| QM-5 | Optimising GDMT Prior to TEER for Secondary MR | Optimise HF therapy before transcatheter mitral repair |
| QM-6 | Monoclonal Protein Screen for Bone Scintigraphy in Suspected CA | Exclude light-chain amyloidosis when interpreting bone scintigraphy for ATTR-CM |

### No Retired or Revised Measures
- All 2020 ACC/AHA measures retained as-is

### HFpEF-Specific Highlights
- **PM-2 (BP control):** SBP <130 mmHg + DBP <80 mmHg now a *performance measure* for HFpEF with hypertension — first HFpEF-specific performance measure
- **QM-1 (SGLT2i in HFpEF/HFmrEF):** SGLT2i use is a *quality measure* (not yet performance measure) based on Class 2a evidence in 2022 guideline; DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved support this; European ESC guidelines have since upgraded to Class 1
- **QM-6 (amyloid screening):** Signals growing importance of ATTR-CM differential in suspected HFpEF

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

This document formalises SGLT2i use and BP control as measurable standards for HFpEF care delivery in the US system. PM-2 is the first HFpEF-dedicated performance measure. QM-1 bridges the gap between guideline recommendation and formal quality tracking for SGLT2i in HFpEF. QM-6 reflects the importance of excluding ATTR-CM as HFpEF mimic/co-diagnosis. Complements the 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA guideline context.

## Contradictions

SGLT2i for HFpEF is a quality measure (Class 2a evidence) in this 2024 document, while ESC 2023 guidelines upgraded it to Class 1 performance measure — a transatlantic evidence-grading discrepancy. Update to performance measure expected in next AHA/ACC guideline cycle.
