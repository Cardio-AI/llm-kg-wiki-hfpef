# TORCH DZHK Registry Rationale (Seyler 2017)

> Design and rationale paper for the DZHK TranslatiOnal Registry for CardiomyopatHies (TORCH) — a German multi-centre registry and biobank for 2,300 patients with non-ischemic cardiomyopathies featuring deep molecular phenotyping and a multi-module structure linking clinical data to genomics, inflammation, and histopathology.

**File:** `raw/2017-ESC-HF-Seyler-TORCH_DZHK-1_rationale.pdf` · **Authors:** Seyler C et al. · **Year:** 2017 · **Journal:** ESC Heart Failure

---

## Core Arguments

TORCH addresses the gap between clinical phenotyping and molecular mechanisms in cardiomyopathy. Key objectives:

1. Establish a national multi-centre biobank of well-phenotyped non-ischemic CMP patients
2. Enable genotype-phenotype correlation across major CMP subtypes
3. Longitudinal follow-up to define prognostic biomarkers
4. Serve as infrastructure hub for investigator-initiated clinical trials
5. Link clinical-level phenotyping to molecular-level profiling (genomics, transcriptomics, inflammation, metabolomics)

Rationale: non-ischemic CMPs are genetically heterogeneous, their molecular drivers are incompletely understood, and existing registries lacked the combined depth of clinical + molecular phenotyping required for precision medicine. (source: 2017-ESC-HF-Seyler-TORCH_DZHK-1_rationale.pdf)

## Registry Structure

### Sites and Countries

- **19 DZHK (German Centre for Cardiovascular Research) university centres** in Germany
- Multi-site coordination hub: University Hospital Heidelberg (PI: Hugo Katus)
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT02187263 (TORCH Phase 1); followed by NCT04265040 (TORCH-Plus, Phase 2)

### Target Enrollment

- **2,300 patients** (Phase 1 TORCH, NCT02187263)
- Expanded to **4,340 patients** in TORCH-Plus (NCT04265040)
- Enrollment started December 2014

### Disease Definition / Eligibility

**Inclusion (primary non-ischemic CMPs):**
- Dilated Cardiomyopathy (DCM): familial/genetic, inflammatory/persistent myocarditis, or idiopathic (after exclusion of secondary cause); LV systolic dysfunction (EF ≤45%)
- Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM / HOCM): sarcomere-based
- Left Ventricular Non-Compaction (LVNC)
- Arrhythmogenic Right Ventricular Cardiomyopathy (ARVC/D)
- Acute myocarditis
- Cardiac amyloidosis (AL, TTR wild-type, TTR hereditary)
- Age 18–79 years

**Exclusion:**
- Ischemic heart disease / significant CAD
- Severe valvular disease (not secondary to LV dysfunction)
- Severe uncontrolled arterial hypertension; primary pulmonary arterial hypertension
- Cardiotoxic agent exposure; chest radiation history
- Drug/alcohol abuse; life expectancy <1 year (non-cardiac cause)
- Tachymyopathy (reversible CMP from persistent tachycardia)
- Age <18 or >79 years

(source: 2017-ESC-HF-Seyler-TORCH_DZHK-1_rationale.pdf)

### CMP Breakdown at Enrollment

| Subtype | ~% |
|---|---|
| Dilated Cardiomyopathy (DCM) | ~56% |
| Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM) | ~15% |
| ARVC | ~10% |
| LVNC | ~8% |

## Parameters Collected

### Obligatory (All Patients)

| Domain | Parameters |
|---|---|
| **Echocardiography (TTE)** | LVEF, LV volumes, LV mass, LA size, diastolic function parameters, RVEF |
| **ECG** | 12-lead; rhythm, QRS duration, QRS morphology |
| **Laboratory** | NT-proBNP, BNP, troponin T/I, creatinine, eGFR, CRP, electrolytes, HbA1c, lipids, CBC |
| **Clinical** | NYHA class, MACE events, HF hospitalization, medication history |

### Optional / Subgroup

| Domain | Parameters |
|---|---|
| **Cardiac MRI (CMR)** | LVEF (gold standard), LGE (fibrosis mapping), T1/T2 mapping, volumetry |
| **Endomyocardial biopsy** | Histology (Masson trichrome, Congo Red), immunohistology, viral load (PCR), electron microscopy |
| **Exercise testing** | Ergometry; in subset: CPET |

### Biobank (Blood)

| Fraction | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Serum | Protein biomarkers, inflammation markers (cytokines, galectin-3) |
| EDTA plasma | Metabolomics, cell-free DNA, circulating microRNAs |
| Citrate plasma | Coagulation markers |
| Urine | Renal biomarkers |
| RNA | Transcriptomics (RNA-seq), gene expression profiling |
| Genomic DNA | Whole exome sequencing, SNP arrays, genotype-phenotype correlation |
| PBMCs | Peripheral blood mononuclear cells; functional inflammatory assays |

(source: 2017-ESC-HF-Seyler-TORCH_DZHK-1_rationale.pdf)

## Registry Modules

TORCH was structured in four parallel modules operating on the same patient cohort:

| Module | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1. Clinical phenotyping + follow-up | Longitudinal outcomes; biosampling at predefined timepoints |
| 2. Genomics | Comprehensive next-generation sequencing; genotype-phenotype mapping |
| 3. Inflammation | Viral load, cytokine profiling, immune cell phenotyping; risk in inflammatory CMP |
| 4. Biomarker | Novel circulating biomarkers; prediction of outcomes and genotype |

---

## Follow-Up

- Minimum follow-up: 12 months (primary registry)
- Annual visits with clinical assessment, echo, laboratory, and biosampling
- Primary endpoint: all-cause mortality (TORCH-Plus: 4-year follow-up)

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

TORCH is primarily a non-HFpEF registry (focus: non-ischemic CMPs, many with reduced EF). Its relevance:
- Provides infrastructure and methodology for deep molecular phenotyping in HF
- Includes HCM and amyloidosis cohorts — both CMP subtypes can phenocopy HFpEF
- TORCH-Plus (NCT04265040) extends the registry with a larger amyloid and HCM arm, overlapping with HFpEF differential diagnosis
- Demonstrates the German DZHK model of registry-linked molecular research, paralleled by DECIPHER-HFpEF for HFpEF specifically
