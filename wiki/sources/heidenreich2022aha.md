# 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA Guideline for the Management of Heart Failure

> Comprehensive US guideline update incorporating EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER results; introduces HFimpEF as a fourth EF category, elevates SGLT2i to Class 2a for HFpEF, formalises an A–D staging system, and provides the first guideline-level HFpEF pharmacotherapy recommendations beyond diuretics.

## Core Arguments

### EF Classification (Table 4)

The AHA 2022 guideline retains the ESC 2021 three-way classification and adds a fourth category:

| Category | LVEF | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| HFrEF | ≤40% | Systolic HF; four-pillar therapy established |
| HFmrEF | 41–49% | Mildly reduced; heterogeneous; individualized treatment |
| HFpEF | ≥50% | Preserved; diagnosed by elevated LV filling pressures |
| **HFimpEF** | **≥40% (recovered from ≤40%)** | **New category; prior HFrEF now showing EF recovery; continuation of neurohormonal therapy recommended** |

HFimpEF is conceptually important: it clarifies that EF recovery does not equal cure and that HFrEF disease-modifying therapy should continue even after EF normalisation. (source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)

### HFpEF Definition

AHA 2022 requires **all three** for HFpEF diagnosis:
1. HF signs and symptoms (NYHA ≥II)
2. LVEF ≥50%
3. **Evidence of elevated LV filling pressures** — either by invasive measurement or by echocardiographic surrogates (E/e' ratio, PA systolic pressure, left atrial enlargement)

This is more hemodynamically explicit than ESC 2021, which formally requires a structural or functional abnormality (Table 9) but without mandating demonstrated filling pressure elevation. (source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)

### A–D Staging System (Table 3)

| Stage | Label | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| A | At risk | Risk factors (hypertension, diabetes, obesity, cardiotoxin exposure); no symptoms, no structural disease |
| B | Pre-HF | Structural disease (LVH, reduced LVEF, diastolic dysfunction) OR elevated NPs — but no current HF symptoms |
| C | Symptomatic HF | Current or prior symptoms of HF |
| D | Advanced HF | Severely reduced quality of life despite maximal therapy; recurrent hospitalizations; INTERMACS profiles 1–6 |

Stage A reflects primary prevention framing: aggressively treat hypertension, diabetes, and obesity before structural disease develops. Stage B introduces the concept of "pre-HF" which has no ESC 2021 analogue at the same granularity. (source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)

### HFpEF Pharmacotherapy (Section 7.7, Figure 12)

| Agent | Class | Level | Evidence basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin or dapagliflozin) | **2a** | B-R | EMPEROR-Preserved + DELIVER |
| MRA (spironolactone) | 2b | B-R | TOPCAT Americas subgroup |
| Sacubitril/valsartan (ARNI) | 2b | B-R | PARAGON-HF LVEF <57% subgroup; sex-stratified signal |
| ARB or ACEI | 2b | C-LD | No positive trial; congestion reduction rationale |
| Diuretics | I | C | Symptom relief only |
| Comorbidity treatment | I | C | Hypertension, AF, obesity, T2DM |

**SGLT2i Class 2a** is the headline change from ESC 2021. AHA 2022 incorporated EMPEROR-Preserved (2021) and DELIVER (2022) data, both of which were after ESC 2021's publication cutoff. (source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)

### H₂FPEF Diagnostic Score

Six-item probability score (0–9) endorsed for pre-test HFpEF likelihood. Full scoring table → [[hfpef-diagnosis]] (canonical). Items: BMI >30 (2 pts), AF (3 pts), age >60 (1 pt), ≥2 antihypertensives (1 pt), E/e' >9 (1 pt), PA systolic >35 mmHg (1 pt). Score ≤1: low; ≥6: high; 2–5: stress testing or invasive evaluation warranted. (source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)

### Biomarkers (Section 4.2)

- **BNP/NT-proBNP**: Class I for diagnosis and prognosis across all HF phenotypes. AF, obesity, and renal failure alter thresholds.
- **Natriuretic peptide-guided therapy**: Class 2a for outpatient HFrEF management (limited HFpEF data).
- **Soluble ST2 and galectin-3**: Class 2b — additive prognostic value in chronic HF; not routinely used diagnostically.
- **Biomarker panels**: No validated combination yet; under investigation.

### Imaging (Section 4.4)

- **Echocardiography (TTE)**: Class I for all patients with suspected new or worsening HF. Key parameters: LVEF, diastolic function grade, left atrial size/volume, PA systolic pressure, RV function.
- **Cardiac MRI (CMR)**: Class I when echo inadequate (poor windows, cardiomyopathy characterisation, infiltrative disease). Gold standard for LVEF, myocardial fibrosis (LGE), tissue characterisation.
- **Nuclear/CT**: Limited role; CT angiography for CAD exclusion when echo inconclusive.

### Cardiac Amyloidosis (Section 7.8)

- Tafamidis (transthyretin stabiliser): Class I for ATTR-CM with HF symptoms — significant mortality and hospitalization reduction in ATTR-AMYLOIDOSIS trial.
- Technetium PYP scintigraphy: non-invasive diagnosis of ATTR-CM (Class I).
- Emphasises the importance of ruling out amyloidosis before assuming generic HFpEF.

### Advanced HF / Stage D

- INTERMACS profiles 1–7 characterise severity (1 = crash and burn; 7 = advanced NYHA III).
- MCS (mechanical circulatory support) and transplantation discussions aligned with profile-based eligibility.
- Sodium restriction and fluid management: individualised.

### Exercise Training

- Class I, Level A for exercise training in symptomatic HF (EF-agnostic): reduces hospitalization and improves functional capacity. (source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

This guideline updates the ESC 2021 foundational source in four key ways:
1. **Closes part of the [[hfpef-treatment-gap]]**: SGLT2i becomes Class 2a (the first pharmacotherapy recommendation above Class 2b for HFpEF)
2. **Expands [[hf-phenotype-classification]]**: HFimpEF added as fourth category
3. **Sharpens [[hfpef-diagnosis]]**: Filling pressure elevation explicitly required; H₂FPEF score endorsed
4. **Extends staging depth**: A–D staging more clinically actionable than ESC's symptom-based NYHA framing alone

See [[guideline-comparison]] for systematic ESC 2021 vs. AHA 2022 contrast.

## Contradictions
- SGLT2i Class 2a (AHA 2022) vs. no recommendation (ESC 2021): driven purely by publication timeline; evidence is the same.
- HFpEF definition: AHA 2022 requires demonstrated elevated filling pressures; ESC 2021 requires structural/functional abnormality on Table 9 — different operational criteria for the same clinical syndrome.
- HFimpEF: no ESC 2021 analogue — borderline overlap with HFmrEF when LVEF recovers from 35–40% to 41–49% creates classification ambiguity between systems.
