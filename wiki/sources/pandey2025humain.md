# Effect of HU6 on Body Weight and Cardiac Function in Patients With Obese Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction: The HuMAIN-HFpEF Randomized Clinical Trial

> HU6 (a controlled mitochondrial uncoupler) produces modest fat-specific weight loss (−2.86 kg vs. placebo) in obese HFpEF without significant improvement in peak VO₂, 6MWD, KCCQ, or diastolic function at 19 weeks — proof-of-concept for fat-specific catabolism but limited functional benefit in this Phase 2A trial.

**Full citation:**
Pandey A, et al. Effect of HU6 on Body Weight and Cardiac Function in Patients With Obese Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction: The HuMAIN-HFpEF Randomized Clinical Trial. *JAMA Cardiol.* 2025;10(6):609–616. doi:[10.1001/jamacardio.2025.0103](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamacardio.2025.0103)

## Core Arguments

**Trial:** Phase 2A, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose escalation. NCT05284617. N=66 randomised (33 HU6, 33 placebo); 56 completed. Duration: **19 weeks**. Population: obese HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%, BMI ≥30, age >30). Published March 2025.

**Intervention:** HU6 — a controlled metabolic accelerator that promotes mitochondrial uncoupling (increases thermogenesis/fat oxidation without increasing oxygen demand). **NOT a bioartificial kidney or device.** [Note: trials-pending.md incorrectly described HuMAIN as a "Humacyte bioartificial kidney" — this is wrong; HU6 is a small-molecule mitochondrial uncoupler.]

**Primary outcome — body weight:**
- HU6: **−2.86 kg vs. placebo** (95% CI −4.68 to −1.04 kg; P=0.003) ✓
- Total fat mass: **−2.96 kg** (P<0.001) — fat-specific reduction
- Visceral fat: **−1.3%** (P=0.003)
- Lean muscle mass: **preserved** (no significant change) — important distinction from GLP-1RAs which cause ~30–40% lean mass loss

**Secondary outcomes — cardiac/functional:**
- Peak VO₂: No significant change (P NS)
- 6-Minute Walk Distance: No significant change (P NS)
- KCCQ total symptom score: No significant change (P NS)
- NT-proBNP: No significant change (P NS)
- hs-CRP: No significant change (P NS)
- Diastolic function (E/e', LAVI): No significant change (P NS)

**Secondary outcomes — haemodynamic:**
- Systolic blood pressure: **−8.7 mmHg** (P=0.006) — clinically meaningful BP reduction

**Safety:** Not detailed in agent extract; Phase 2A suggests dose-escalation safety monitoring was primary safety endpoint; drug generally tolerated.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

HuMAIN is the first trial of fat-specific catabolism via mitochondrial uncoupling in HFpEF. The fat/lean mass selectivity (lean mass preserved vs. GLP-1RA) is mechanistically important — GLP-1RA-induced lean mass loss is a concern for sarcopenia in older HFpEF patients. However, 19 weeks of modest weight loss (−2.86 kg) did not translate to functional improvement. Larger trials with longer duration and greater weight loss may be needed. This complements the SUMMIT (tirzepatide) and STEP-HFpEF (semaglutide) evidence base by testing a mechanistically distinct weight-loss pathway.

## Contradictions
Fat-specific weight loss (−2.86 kg, lean mass preserved) did not improve VO₂, 6MWD, or KCCQ — challenging the assumption that any weight loss (vs. GLP-1RA weight loss with ~30% lean mass loss) should produce functional benefit. The functional benefits of semaglutide/tirzepatide in STEP-HFpEF/SUMMIT may depend on mechanisms beyond weight loss (haemodynamic offloading, direct GLP-1 effects). Note in [[contradictions]].
