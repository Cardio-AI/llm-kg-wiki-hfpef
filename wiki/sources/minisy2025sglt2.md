# Minisy 2025 — SGLT2i in HFpEF: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

> 9 RCTs, >20,000 patients: SGLT2i reduced CV death or HHF (HR 0.83; 95% CI 0.76–0.90; GRADE high for HHF), reduced HHF alone (HR 0.75), improved KCCQ (+1.8 pts); all-cause mortality neutral (HR 0.92); no publication bias.

**Full citation:**
Minisy MM, Abdelaziz A. The role of SGLT2 inhibitors in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF): a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. *BMC Cardiovasc Disord.* 2025;25:765. doi:[10.1186/s12872-025-05127-3](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12872-025-05127-3)

---

## Summary

PRISMA 2020-compliant systematic review and meta-analysis of all RCTs testing SGLT2 inhibitors in HFpEF (LVEF ≥40%; searched PubMed, Scopus, Embase, CENTRAL, ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP; January 2015–June 2025). Nine RCTs included. Risk of bias assessed by Cochrane RoB 2.0; certainty of evidence by GRADE. (source: raw/2025-BMC-CardiovasDisease-Minisy-SGLT2-role_systematic_review.pdf)

## Design

- **Search window:** January 2015–June 2025
- **Databases:** PubMed, Scopus, Embase, CENTRAL, ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP
- **HFpEF definition:** LVEF ≥40% (deliberate broadening to include HFmrEF, aligning with EMPEROR-Preserved, DELIVER trial enrolment)
- **Trials included (N=9):** CHIEF-HF, DEFINE-HF, DELIVER, DETERMINE-Preserved, EMPEROR-Pooled, EMPEROR-Preserved, EMPULSE, PRESERVED-HF, SOLOIST-WHF
- **Total N:** >20,000 patients
- **Primary outcome:** Composite CV death or HHF
- **Secondary outcomes:** HHF alone, all-cause mortality, KCCQ score improvement ≥5 points

## Results

| Outcome | HR or Effect | 95% CI | P | I² | GRADE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CV death or HHF (composite) | **HR 0.83** | 0.76–0.90 | <0.0001 | 62% | High |
| HHF alone | **HR 0.75** | 0.68–0.84 | <0.001 | — | High |
| All-cause mortality | HR 0.92 | 0.85–1.01 | NS | — | Low |
| CV death alone | HR ~0.88 | 0.95 CI includes 1 | NS | — | Moderate |
| KCCQ improvement (6 trials) | **+1.8 points** vs. placebo | — | — | — | Moderate |

**Risk of bias (Cochrane RoB 2.0):**
- 3/9 trials: overall low risk (EMPEROR-Pooled, EMPEROR-Preserved, EMPULSE)
- 6/9 trials: "some concerns" (mainly incomplete outcome data [D3] or unblinded PRO assessment [D4])

**GRADE ratings:**
- HHF alone and composite: **High certainty** — multiple large trials, consistent effect, no heterogeneity in direction, no publication bias
- CV death and all-cause mortality: **Moderate/Low** — wide CI, inconsistency
- KCCQ: **Moderate** — risk of unblinded assessment bias, inconsistency

**Heterogeneity (I²=62%):** Moderate; not explained by single outlier trial; reflects true population/trial heterogeneity across drug, dose, EF range, and patient characteristics. No funnel plot asymmetry (Egger test) — no significant publication bias.

## Interpretation

17% relative risk reduction in the composite (HR 0.83) is robust and consistent across all 9 trials with overlapping CIs and no outlier studies. The HHF reduction (HR 0.75 = 25% RRR) is the most consistent finding — GRADE high. Mortality neutrality in HFpEF contrasts with the mortality benefits in large HFrEF trials (DAPA-HF, EMPEROR-Reduced); this likely reflects: (1) heterogeneity of HFpEF phenotypes with competing non-CV mortality; (2) metabolic/renal rather than direct hemodynamic mechanisms; (3) relative underpowering for CV death in the EF-preserved spectrum.

## Clinical Implications

- Meta-analysis provides the most comprehensive evidence base to date for SGLT2i class effect in HFpEF
- HHF reduction is clinically and statistically robust — GRADE high justifies Class I guideline endorsement (ESC 2023)
- Mortality neutrality should be interpreted as insufficient evidence rather than absence of effect — HR 0.92 is directionally favorable
- KCCQ +1.8 points is modest but consistent and relevant as a patient-centered outcome

## Relationship to Other Evidence

- Complements Requena-Ibanez 2022 ([[requenaibanez2022sglt2]]) mechanistic review
- Real-world implementation gap documented by Gonzalez 2024 ([[gonzalez2024sglt2trends]]) — this meta-analysis confirms robust evidence for implementation
- Albulushi 2025 ([[albulushi2025sglt2fibrosis]]): provides mechanistic CMR evidence for SGLT2i antifibrotic effect in T2DM+HFpEF

## Limitations

- Inclusion of HFmrEF (EF 40–49%) trials alongside classic HFpEF (EF ≥50%) — effect size may differ across this spectrum
- Moderate I² (62%) — unexplained residual heterogeneity; no patient-level meta-analysis performed
- No subgroup analysis by T2DM, individual SGLT2i agent, or geographic region
- Risk of unblinded PRO assessment in several trials potentially inflates KCCQ estimates
