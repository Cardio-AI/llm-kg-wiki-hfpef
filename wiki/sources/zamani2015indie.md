# INDIE-HFpEF

> Inorganic nitrate (potassium nitrate) did not improve peak VO₂ or exercise capacity in HFpEF; added to converging negative evidence for the nitrate/NO pathway as a treatment target.

**File:** `raw/[not yet ingested]` · **Authors:** Zamani P et al. · **Year:** 2015 · **Journal:** JACC: Heart Failure  
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** ~65 · **Population:** HFpEF with LVEF ≥50%  
**Follow-up:** ~6 weeks · **Intervention:** Inorganic nitrate (KNO₃) vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Peak VO₂ (exercise capacity)

---

## Key Findings

- Inorganic nitrate **did not improve** peak VO₂ or exercise capacity
- Neutral result across exercise endpoints
