# RALI-DHF: Ranolazine in Diastolic Heart Failure (Maier 2013)

> RALI-DHF (N=20 HFpEF; Phase 2 crossover): ranolazine reduced invasively measured exercise LVEDP by 4.7 mmHg (P=0.001) — proof-of-concept for the late Na⁺ current → Ca²⁺ overload → diastolic dysfunction therapeutic pathway, with no large Phase 3 RCT subsequently conducted.

**Full citation:**

## Core Arguments

**Trial:** RALI-DHF (Ranolazine in the Treatment of Diastolic Heart Failure). Phase 2, crossover RCT, double-blind, placebo-controlled. Single centre. N=**20** HFpEF patients (LVEF ≥50%, LVEDP >12 mmHg at rest or >15 mmHg at exercise on right heart catheterisation).

**Intervention:** Ranolazine — IV loading dose followed by oral 500→750 mg BID for 2 weeks vs. matched placebo (crossover; 2-week washout between periods).

**Primary endpoint:** LVEDP during exercise (invasive LV micromanometer at 20W supine exercise).

**Primary results:**
- Exercise LVEDP: ranolazine −**4.7 mmHg** (95% CI −6.7 to −2.7; **P=0.001**)
- Resting LVEDP: also reduced (−3.1 mmHg; P<0.001)
- NT-proBNP: reduced
- Mitral E/A ratio improved (better diastolic filling pattern)
- Peak VO₂: no significant change

**Mechanism:** Ranolazine selectively inhibits the late sodium current (late I_Na) in cardiomyocytes. In HFpEF, late I_Na is upregulated (as in HFrEF), leading to excessive diastolic intracellular Na⁺ accumulation. Elevated [Na⁺]i reduces NCX-mediated Ca²⁺ extrusion (or reverses NCX to Ca²⁺ entry mode), increasing diastolic intracellular Ca²⁺. Elevated diastolic Ca²⁺ maintains myofilament Ca²⁺ sensitisation, impairing active lusitropy (relaxation) → elevated LVEDP. Ranolazine blocks late I_Na → ↓[Na⁺]i → normalised NCX activity → ↓diastolic Ca²⁺ → improved relaxation → reduced filling pressures.

This is mechanistically distinct from the NO/cGMP pathway targeted by NEAT-HFpEF, INDIE-HFpEF, RELAX, VITALITY-HFpEF, and INABLE-Training (all negative in HFpEF). It addresses the Ca²⁺ dysregulation arm of diastolic dysfunction, one of 8 confirmed pathophysiological pathways in [[fayyaz2025pathophys]].

**Limitations:** N=20, single centre, crossover design without parallel-group statistical power for clinical outcomes. No functional exercise capacity improvement detected (VO₂ unchanged). No large Phase 3 RCT followed.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

RALI-DHF provides mechanistic proof-of-concept for the late I_Na → Ca²⁺ overload pathway as a druggable target in HFpEF. It is an important positive haemodynamic signal that has not been replicated in a definitive clinical outcomes trial. The contrast with the uniformly negative NO/cGMP pathway trials (RELAX, NEAT-HFpEF, INDIE-HFpEF, INABLE-Training) suggests that HFpEF pathophysiology is mechanistically heterogeneous and that the Ca²⁺/Na⁺ pathway represents a distinct intervention target.

The [[fayyaz2025pathophys]] tissue-level review confirms diastolic Ca²⁺ handling dysfunction (SERCA2a, NCX) as a confirmed HFpEF mechanism, providing post-hoc biological validation of the RALI-DHF rationale.
