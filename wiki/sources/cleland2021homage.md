# Cleland 2021 — HOMAGE Trial: Primary Results

> Primary-results publication of HOMAGE (NCT02556450; N=527 randomized, "Stage B" HF-risk population). Primary endpoint — interaction between baseline serum galectin-3 and change in serum PIIINP with spironolactone vs. control — was **negative** (P=0.947 for interaction; overall PIIINP mean difference -0.15 µg/L, P=0.323). Secondary endpoints were favourable: spironolactone reduced serum PICP, PICP/CITP ratio, systolic blood pressure, NT-proBNP, and left atrial volume, and increased LVEF, over up to 9 months.

## Summary

Primary-results publication of the HOMAGE trial. Multicentre, prospective, randomized, open-label, blinded-endpoint (PROBE) trial testing spironolactone 25–50 mg/day vs. usual care (control) for up to 9 months in people at increased risk of developing heart failure ("Stage B"). Primary hypothesis — that spironolactone's effect on serum collagen synthesis marker PIIINP would be greater in patients with higher baseline serum galectin-3 — was not supported. Secondary analyses showed spironolactone favourably affected type-I (but not type-III) collagen markers, blood pressure, natriuretic peptides, and echocardiographic structure/function. (source: cleland2021homage)

## Core Arguments

**Design:**
- Multicentre, prospective, randomized, open-label, blinded-endpoint (PROBE) trial (echocardiographic core-lab assessment blinded to treatment allocation; investigators not blinded)
- Registered NCT02556450; protocol and SAP at clinicaltrials.gov
- Funded by European Union 7th Framework Programme (grant 305507); sponsor ACS Biomarkers (Amsterdam); monitoring/data management by European Drug Development Hub (Nancy, France)
- Enrolment January 2016 – June 2018; follow-up visits at 1 week, 1, 2, 3, 6, and 9 months; final echocardiogram planned at 9 months but occurred between 3–8 months for some participants owing to fixed trial funding duration

**Population ("Stage B" heart failure — at increased risk, not yet diagnosed with HF):**
- Either sex, aged ≥65 years (protocol amended to ≥60 years) with established coronary artery disease or at high risk of coronary disease
- Eligible if plasma NT-proBNP 125–1000 ng/L or BNP 35–280 ng/L
- Main exclusions: eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m², serum potassium >5.0 mmol/L, LVEF <45%, atrial fibrillation, diagnosis of heart failure, or treatment with loop diuretics
- 877 patients assessed for eligibility; 350 excluded (main reason: NP out of range, n=253); 527 randomized (262 control, 265 spironolactone); 506 (96%) attended final visit (255 control analysed, 251 spironolactone analysed)
- Baseline: median age 73 (IQR 68–78), 26% women, 72% coronary disease, 42% diabetes, 22% eGFR <60; median NT-proBNP 214 ng/L (IQR 137–356); median galectin-3 16.1 µg/L (IQR 13.5–19.7) — both above published normal values

**Intervention:** Spironolactone initiated 25 mg/day, titratable to 50 mg/day or reduced/stopped per serum potassium and renal function, vs. control (no additional treatment; no MRA or other potassium-sparing diuretic permitted in either arm)

**Primary endpoint (pre-specified):** Interaction between baseline serum galectin-3 (above vs. below median) and change in serum procollagen type-III N-terminal pro-peptide (PIIINP) from baseline to final visit, in participants assigned spironolactone vs. control — i.e., a biomarker-interaction hypothesis test, not a clinical or functional outcome. Rationale: galectin-3 was hypothesized as a marker identifying patients with greater fibrotic/collagen-synthesis response to spironolactone.

**Primary result — NEGATIVE:** No interaction between baseline galectin-3 and treatment effect on PIIINP change (P=0.947 for interaction). Serum PIIINP changed little overall with spironolactone vs. control (mean difference -0.15 µg/L; 95% CI -0.44 to 0.15; P=0.323). Galectin-3 did not identify a subgroup with greater collagen-marker response to spironolactone.

**Secondary endpoints (positive, exploratory — no adjustment for multiple comparisons):**
| Endpoint | Effect (spironolactone vs. control) | P |
|---|---|---|
| Serum PICP (µg/L) | mean diff -8.1 (95% CI -11.9 to -4.3) | <0.0001 |
| PICP/CITP ratio | mean diff -2.9 (95% CI -4.3 to -1.5) | <0.0001 |
| Serum CITP | increased with spironolactone | <0.0001 |
| Serum galectin-3 | rose in both groups, more with spironolactone | 0.002 |
| Systolic blood pressure (mmHg) | mean diff -10 (95% CI -13 to -7) | <0.001 |
| NT-proBNP (ng/L) | mean diff -57 (95% CI -81 to -33) [abstract]; end-of-trial table diff -43 (-75 to -11), P=0.009 | <0.001 |
| Left atrial volume index (mL/m²) | mean diff -1 (95% CI -2 to 0) | 0.010 |
| LVEF (%) | mean diff +1.2 (95% CI +0.2 to +2.2) | 0.022 |
| E-wave velocity | mean diff -0.05 (95% CI -0.07 to -0.02) | <0.001 |
| Serum potassium | rose with spironolactone (~+0.2 mmol/L) | <0.001 |
| eGFR | declined more with spironolactone | <0.001 |

No interaction with baseline galectin-3 was found for any secondary marker. No treatment effect on serum PIIINP, MMP-1, symptoms, exercise capacity (shuttle walk), haemoglobin, troponin, or GDF-15.

**Safety/clinical events:** Composite of HF hospitalization/atrial fibrillation, non-fatal MI, stroke, or cardiovascular death: 14 events in 11 control participants vs. 12 events in 9 spironolactone participants (P=0.50). Two deaths in each group. Trial was not powered for clinical outcomes.

**Authors' interpretation:** Spironolactone did not reduce PIIINP or serum galectin-3, and galectin-3 did not identify a subgroup with greater collagen response — the trial's specific mechanistic hypothesis was not confirmed. However, spironolactone reduced serum PICP and the PICP/CITP ratio (suggesting an effect on type-I, but not type-III, collagen turnover), and produced early (within 1 month) reductions in blood pressure and NT-proBNP, sustained through to favourable echocardiographic remodelling (reduced LA volume, increased LVEF) by final visit — a pattern the authors describe as "favourable cardiac remodelling" (Take-home figure) potentially reflecting natriuretic/diuretic and antihypertensive effects as well as direct anti-fibrotic action. Authors explicitly state the trial "was neither large nor long enough to demonstrate" whether these effects translate into delayed/prevented progression to symptomatic heart failure, and that hypothesis-generating interpretation of secondary/neutral-primary-endpoint findings should apply.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

HOMAGE is not an HFpEF trial (LVEF <45% was an exclusion criterion, and patients had no HF diagnosis) — it enrolled a "Stage B" pre-HF population. Its relevance is (1) as the primary-results source that fills a prior sourcing gap on [[homage-trial]] (previously documented only via the downstream [[ferreira2023spironolactone]] pooled echocardiographic IPD meta-analysis, which did not report HOMAGE's own primary endpoint), and (2) as mechanistic groundwork for MRA use across the HF risk spectrum, including HFpEF (via [[aldo-dhf]], [[topcat]], [[spirrit]]). (source: cleland2021homage)
