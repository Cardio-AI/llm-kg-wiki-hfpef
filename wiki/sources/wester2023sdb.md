# Wester 2023 — Insights into HFpEF and Sleep-Disordered Breathing

> SDB affects up to 58–80% of HFpEF patients, shares pathomechanisms with HFpEF (intermittent hypoxia/reoxygenation → CaMKII overactivation → diastolic dysfunction/arrhythmia), and is especially prevalent in the "obese HFpEF phenotype," which may particularly benefit from PAP therapy and GLP-1 agonists.

## Core Arguments

### Epidemiology
- SDB prevalence in HFpEF: **58–80%** (vs. 10–20% in non-obese HFpEF)
- SDB in HFpEF predominantly OSA (obstructive); HFrEF more commonly CSA
- SDB prevalence is highest in the "obese HFpEF phenotype" (40–60%) vs. non-obese HFpEF (10–20%)

### HFpEF Phenotypes in SDB Context
Three phenotypic clusters (from Peters et al. / Shah et al. cluster analyses):

| Phenotype | ~% HFpEF | Key Features | SDB Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Older, vascular aging | ~45% | >75y, HTN, arterial stiffness, CKD, inflammation | Variable SDB; likely benefit from anti-inflammatory strategies |
| Metabolic, obese | ~30% | 60–70y, obesity, DM, EAT, inflammation | HIGH SDB; OSA predominates; may benefit from PAP therapy |
| Younger, NP-deficient | ~25% | ~60y, lower BNP/NP, no inflammation | Lower SDB prevalence; non-inflammatory |

### Mechanistic Pathways: SDB → HFpEF

1. **Increased cardiac afterload:** Intermittent hypoxia/reoxygenation → ↑ sympathetic tone + ↑ ROS → arterial hypertension → LV hypertrophy → diastolic dysfunction; intrathoracic pressure swings (OSA) → ↑ LV wall stress → fibrosis
2. **Systemic inflammation:** Intermittent hypoxia → NF-κB pathway → TNF-α, IL-6 → myocardial hypertrophy, fibrosis; CaMKII-mediated MMP2 upregulation → ↑ passive LV stiffness
3. **CaMKII overactivation (novel):** Intermittent hypoxia/reoxygenation → ↑ ROS → CaMKII oxidation → dysregulated Na⁺/Ca²⁺ homeostasis → impaired diastolic SR Ca²⁺ reuptake; late Na⁺ current activation → afterdepolarisations → atrial arrhythmias → HFpEF
4. **Insulin resistance:** OSA strongly associated with T2DM; insulin resistance → RAAS activation, inflammation → HFpEF development
5. **ACE2 reduction in women:** SDB → decreased myocardial ACE2 in women → ↑ angiotensin II → ↑ pathological remodelling → sex-specific HFpEF development

### Treatment Implications

- **PAP therapy (CPAP/BiPAP/ASV):** ASV contraindicated in HFrEF+CSA (SERVE-HF); small study in HFpEF+OSA (N=36): ASV improved diastolic function, reduced BNP, reduced composite HF endpoint
- **GLP-1 agonists:** Weight loss → OSA severity reduction; semaglutide data (STEP-HFpEF) supports metabolic obese HFpEF phenotype; clinical trials for OSA+HFpEF warranted
- **SGLT2 inhibitors:** Reduce visceral/subcutaneous fat + CaMKII activity + renal function → may reduce SDB severity indirectly
- **CaMKII inhibition:** Preclinical promising; clinical translation limited by specificity/bioavailability issues
