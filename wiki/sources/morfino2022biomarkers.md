# Morfino 2022 — HFpEF Biomarkers Review

> Comprehensive narrative review of HFpEF biomarkers across six pathophysiological pathways; Galectin-3 AUC 0.927 for diagnosis; GDF-15+Gal-3+sST2 panel best for prognosis in acute HFpEF; hs-TnT stronger predictor in men than women.

**Full citation:**

## Summary

Open-access review (Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna + Fondazione Toscana Gabriele Monasterio, Pisa) mapping HFpEF biomarkers across six mechanistic pathways: myocardial stretch, inflammation/endothelial dysfunction, myocardial injury, fibrotic pathways, and obesity/senescence/iron deficiency/kidney. Covers NPs, hs-TnT, sST2, Galectin-3, GDF-15, and emerging markers. (source: morfino2022biomarkers)

## Natriuretic Peptides

- Diagnostic AUC across 51 studies: ~0.80 (moderate)
- MR-proANP: HR 2.56 (1.64–4.00) for outcomes in T2DM+HFpEF; important because it is **not influenced by neprilysin** → relevant in patients on ARNi (sacubitril/valsartan)
- NT-proBNP-guided therapy inconclusive in HFpEF (unlike HFrEF)
- NT-proBNP change >1,000 ng/L over 6 months → HR ~2 for CV death/HF hospitalisation
- NPs are **lower in obesity** — recognised diagnostic gap (validated against invasive hemodynamics)

## High-Sensitivity Troponin T (hs-TnT)

- hs-TnT higher in HFrEF than HFpEF overall, but prognostically significant in both
- **Sex difference:** hs-TnT is a stronger outcome predictor in HFpEF men (HR 3.33) than women (HR 1.35) — mechanism unclear; possibly reflects higher rates of ischaemic injury in men
- PARAGON-HF secondary analysis ([[gori2021paragon]]): sacubitril/valsartan reduces hs-TnT 9% vs. valsartan at week 16 and 10% at week 48 (P<0.001); patients with hs-TnT ≤17 ng/L by week 16 had better outcomes (P=0.046)
- Threshold 17 ng/L (PARAGON-HF median) separates higher vs. lower outcome risk in that cohort; general clinical ULN = 14 ng/L

## Soluble ST2 (sST2)

- Not cardiac-specific — can be elevated in infections, malignancy, kidney disease, liver failure
- HFpEF-specific: associated with **RV pressure overload, congestion, and comorbidities** but NOT LV geometry
- TIME-CHF sST2 HR 12.18 for 18-month survival (HFpEF subgroup) — high prognostic signal but driven by comorbidity confounding
- Acute decompensated HFpEF: prognostic HR 6.62–10.04 per SD increase
- Limited role in isolated HFpEF diagnosis due to lack of cardiac specificity

## Galectin-3

- Fibrosis biomarker (β-galactoside-binding lectin secreted by activated macrophages)
- **AUC 0.927** for HFpEF vs. controls — highest diagnostic AUC of non-NP biomarkers reviewed
- Cut-off 10.1 ng/mL → 78% sensitivity, 95% specificity for HFpEF diagnosis
- Prognostic: HR 2.33 per SD (P=0.009) for CV death/HF hospitalisation
- **Stronger predictor in HFpEF than in HFrEF** — inverse of the NP and hs-TnT pattern; aligns with the fibrosis-dominant pathophysiology of HFpEF
- Not influenced by NP-lowering therapies (ARNi) — complementary biomarker panel

## GDF-15 (Growth Differentiation Factor-15)

- TGF-β superfamily cytokine; elevated by systemic inflammation, oxidative stress, and cardiorenal stress
- Elevated in HFpEF vs. controls in DIAST-CHF subgroup (N=142); independent prognostic value
- **Not influenced by atrial fibrillation** — contrast with NPs (AF substantially elevates NPs) → GDF-15 more stable in AF-prevalent HFpEF populations
- Multi-biomarker panel: GDF-15 + Galectin-3 + sST2 best combination for predicting 2-year death in acute HFpEF
- Clinical limitation: no assay standardisation; not commercially widely available

## Clinical Synthesis

No single biomarker achieves both high diagnostic and prognostic accuracy in HFpEF. The six-pathway framework suggests a multi-biomarker panel is likely necessary for HFpEF precision medicine. NPs remain first-line due to commercial availability; Galectin-3 and GDF-15 offer additive prognostic information; sST2 adds RV congestion signal. MR-proANP is uniquely valuable in ARNi-treated patients.

- hs-TnT sex difference (stronger in men) contrasts with the general HFpEF female predominance literature — see [[beale2018sex]]; see [[contradictions]]
