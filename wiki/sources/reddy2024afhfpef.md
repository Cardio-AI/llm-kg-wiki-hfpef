# Unrecognized Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction in Atrial Fibrillation

> Pooled data from 6 screening studies show 83% occult HFpEF prevalence in symptomatic AF patients by exercise RHC, and ~82% occult AF in HFpEF patients at 1 year; the two conditions are deeply intertwined diagnostic and prognostic partners.

## Core Arguments

**Type:** Narrative review synthesising 6 exercise RHC screening studies and ambulatory ECG monitoring data; no original patient dataset (n=N/A).

**Key findings:**

**HFpEF prevalence in AF:**
- Pooled from 6 screening studies using exercise RHC: **~83% of symptomatic AF patients** meet haemodynamic criteria for HFpEF (exercise PCWP ≥25 mmHg or PCWP/CO slope >2 mmHg/L/min)
- Noninvasive diagnostic criteria (H₂FPEF, HFA-PEFF) have very limited sensitivity in AF patients; H₂FPEF score is automatically intermediate (2 points) in all AF patients regardless of haemodynamics
- Exercise RHC is the only reliable method for HFpEF diagnosis in AF

**AF prevalence in HFpEF:**
- ~82% of HFpEF patients have documented AF at 1 year of ambulatory ECG monitoring
- Paroxysmal AF is frequently unrecognised at HFpEF diagnosis; short AF episodes (>6 minutes) are clinically significant

**Anticoagulation in AF + HFpEF:**
- Pooled ARTESIA + NOAH-AFNET 6 trials: anticoagulation for AF >6 minutes reduces stroke by **~32%** but increases clinically significant bleeding
- HFpEF patients may have disproportionate stroke risk from subclinical AF given elevated LA pressures and atrial remodelling

**Diagnostic interplay:**
- "Satisfaction of search" — clinicians diagnosing AF may stop searching for underlying HFpEF, leading to systematic underdiagnosis
- LA remodelling from elevated HFpEF filling pressures promotes AF substrate; AF in turn worsens filling pressures (loss of atrial kick, tachycardia-mediated cardiomyopathy) — bidirectional relationship

**AI-ECG:** ECG-AI AF probability score (each 10% increase) associated with 31% increased risk of new AF — potential screening tool referenced from Attia 2019 (see [[attia2019ecgaf]]).

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

Establishes the scale of diagnostic overlap between HFpEF and AF. This is critical for HFpEF diagnosis (many patients diagnosed with "AF" alone have underlying HFpEF), for trial interpretation (AF enriches for HFpEF but may exclude patients via NT-proBNP thresholds), and for treatment (anticoagulation strategy, CABA-HFpEF ablation trial). Also highlights the limitation of noninvasive HFpEF diagnostic algorithms in AF patients.
