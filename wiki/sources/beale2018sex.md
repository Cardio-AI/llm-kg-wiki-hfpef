# Sex Differences in Cardiovascular Pathophysiology: Why Women Are Overrepresented in Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction

> Women's predisposition to HFpEF arises from sex-specific cardiac structure, vascular aging, immune biology, and unique exposures including pregnancy and preeclampsia.

**File:** `raw/2018-CirculationAHA-Beale-sex_differences_HFpEF.pdf` · **Authors:** Beale AL, Meyer P, Marwick TH, Lam CSP, Kaye DM · **Year:** 2018 · **Journal:** Circulation 138:198–205

---

## Core Arguments

- Women are the majority of HFpEF patients; female sex is one of the strongest epidemiological discriminators of HFpEF vs. HFrEF.
- **Cardiac structure:** Women have smaller LV chambers, higher systolic and diastolic LV elastance (stiffness), more concentric (not eccentric) remodeling with pressure overload; LVEF increases more steeply with aging in women.
- **Vascular:** Women have greater arterial elastance, higher pulse pressure, smaller stiffer aorta, earlier pulse wave reflection, and declining ventricular-arterial coupling with age (preserved in men); these abnormalities amplify diastolic dysfunction.
- **Pulmonary hypertension:** 82% of HFpEF patients with PH are female (vs. 58% without PH); women show a steeper MPAP-to-saline-volume slope, indicating smaller pulmonary vasodilatory reserve.
- **Inflammation and immune biology:** Women express more proinflammatory genes in cardiac tissue; higher CRP; autoimmune diseases (predominantly female) are associated with diastolic dysfunction; female myocardium shows sex-differentially expressed genes involving NF-κB and KLF4 pathways.
- **Comorbidities amplified in women:** Diabetes increases HF risk 5× in women vs. 2.4× in men; hypertension increases HF risk 3× in women vs. 2× in men; iron deficiency more prevalent in women both globally and in HF.
- **Unique exposures:** Parity correlates with diastolic dysfunction ~1 decade after last delivery; preeclampsia confers 4× future HF risk and persistent LV remodeling at 6–12 months postpartum.
- **Molecular mechanisms:** Menopause-associated low estrogen activates RAAS → reactive oxygen species → collagen synthesis → fibrosis; titin phosphorylation state influenced by sex-linked kinase activity; sex differences in calcium handling and fatty acid metabolism.
- **Open question:** Whether HFpEF preponderance in women is partly artifactual due to sex-neutral LVEF ≥50% cutoff (elderly women normally have higher LVEF, so 50% may be relatively reduced in some women).

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

Synthesises evidence across cardiac structure, vascular biology, immune system, and social/reproductive exposures. Directly informs phenotype profiling (sex as a phenotyping dimension), diagnostic criteria debates, and the open question of sex-specific LVEF thresholds.
