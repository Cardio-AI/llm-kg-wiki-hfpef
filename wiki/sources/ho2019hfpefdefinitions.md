# Ho 2019 — Competing HFpEF Definitions: Prognostic Implications

> Seven guideline HFpEF definitions applied to the same cohort enrolled 12–90% of patients; only 53% had invasively confirmed physiologic HFpEF (elevated PCWP), and HFpEF_phys independently predicted CV events regardless of guideline label.

**Full citation:**
Ho JE, Zern EK, Wooster L, et al. Differential Clinical Profiles, Exercise Responses, and Outcomes Associated With Distinct HFpEF Definitions. *Circulation.* 2019;140(5):353–365. doi:[10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.118.039451](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.118.039451)
**Study type:** Prospective observational · **N:** 461 · **Population:** LVEF ≥50%, NYHA II–IV, referred for CPET at MGH · **Follow-up:** Median [not specified in extraction; events-based]  
**Intervention/Exposure:** Application of 7 HFpEF diagnostic definitions with invasive CPET as reference standard  
**Primary outcome:** Proportion enrolled by each definition; sensitivity/specificity for HFpEF_phys; CV event incidence by definition

## Key Findings

- **Definition heterogeneity:** Enrollment ranged from 12% (HFSA) to 90% (ACC/AHA) of the same 461-patient cohort depending on which guideline definition was applied
- **Reference standard:** 243/461 (53%) had "physiologic HFpEF" (HFpEF_phys) — invasively elevated PCWP at rest or during exercise by [[cardiopulmonary-exercise-testing]]
- **Sensitivity/specificity trade-off by definition:**
  - ACC/AHA: sensitivity 91%, specificity 11% for HFpEF_phys
  - HFSA: sensitivity 17%, specificity 94%
  - Echo structural abnormalities alone: sensitivity 71%, specificity 51%
  - NT-proBNP ≥125 pg/mL: sensitivity 48%, specificity 74%
- **CV event rates:** Varied 75–298 per 1000 person-years across definitions (same cohort)
- **Prognostic finding:** HFpEF_phys was an independent predictor of CV events (HR 1.62, p=0.01) even in patients not meeting any guideline criteria — physiologic diagnosis carries prognostic weight beyond label assignment

## Methods (brief)

Single-centre prospective cohort at MGH. Inclusion: LVEF ≥50%, NYHA II–IV symptoms, clinical referral for CPET. All patients underwent invasive CPET (intra-arterial line + pulmonary artery catheter). Seven definitions applied post-hoc: ACC/AHA, ESC, HFSA, TOPCAT trial criteria, PARAGON trial criteria, I-PRESERVE trial criteria, RELAX trial criteria. HFpEF_phys defined as PCWP ≥15 mmHg at rest or ≥25 mmHg during exercise.

HFpEF_phys (invasive PCWP) HR for CV events: 1.62 (P=0.01), independent of guideline classification.

## Limitations

- Single-centre (MGH); high-referral tertiary population — may not represent community HFpEF
- Definitions applied retrospectively/post-hoc to a clinical cohort not designed for all criteria
- Invasive CPET itself carries procedural risk; limits generalisability of reference standard to routine practice
- Follow-up duration for CV events not fully characterised in available extraction

## Contradictions

- ACC/AHA and HFSA definitions enroll radically different proportions of the same cohort (90% vs. 12%) — HFpEF trial populations are not interchangeable. See [[contradictions]].
- NT-proBNP ≥125 has only 48% sensitivity for invasively confirmed HFpEF — reinforces that NP-negative patients can have true physiologic HFpEF. Consistent with ESC 2021 obesity caveat but extends to broader population.
