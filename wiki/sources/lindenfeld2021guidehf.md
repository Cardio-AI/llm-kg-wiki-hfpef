# Lindenfeld 2021 — GUIDE-HF: Haemodynamic-Guided HF Management (CardioMEMS)

> GUIDE-HF, which enrolled 1,000 HF patients across all EF groups (including 47% HFpEF), found that haemodynamic-guided management with CardioMEMS did not significantly reduce the primary composite of all-cause mortality + HF events overall (HR 0.88; P=0.16), though a pre-COVID sensitivity analysis supported benefit (HR 0.81; P=0.049).

## Key Findings

### Primary Outcome
- **All-cause mortality + total HF events (composite):** HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.74–1.05; P=0.16) — **NOT SIGNIFICANT**
- **Pre-COVID sensitivity analysis (patients enrolled/events before March 2020):** HR 0.81 (95% CI 0.66–1.00; P=0.049) — borderline significant

### COVID-19 Impact
- COVID-19 pandemic disrupted usual care in control group (telemedicine more common → better remote management → reduced event rate in control group), attenuating apparent benefit of sensor-guided therapy
- Pre-COVID analysis is considered the more clinically meaningful estimate

### EF Subgroup Analysis
- **HFpEF subgroup (LVEF >40%, N=469):** HR 0.85 (95% CI 0.66–1.09; P=0.28) — directionally consistent but not significant in this subgroup
- Effect size similar across EF subgroups (no significant interaction)
- LVEF did not significantly modify treatment response

### Haemodynamic Benefit
- PA pressure-guided therapy reduced PA pressures in the active arm
- More medication adjustments (diuretics, vasodilators) in active arm

## Methods

All patients implanted with CardioMEMS PA sensor; randomised to haemodynamic-guided management (PA pressure data transmitted to clinical team, who adjusted medications) vs. sham (data collected but not transmitted). Primary composite: all-cause death + total HF hospitalisations + urgent HF visits (rate ratio). 12-month follow-up. COVID sensitivity analysis excluded events and patient-time from March 2020 onward.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

GUIDE-HF provides the broadest EF-inclusive evidence for CardioMEMS PA pressure monitoring. The COVID-19 pandemic made interpretation complex — the pre-COVID signal suggests benefit consistent with CHAMPION trial. The HFpEF subgroup (N=469) was adequately sized to show a directional trend but not statistical significance. Complements [[horiuchi2022npguided]] (NP-guided therapy generally neutral in HFpEF). The CardioMEMS device was approved for HFpEF by the FDA based on accumulated data.

## Contradictions

The pre-COVID analysis (supporting benefit) vs. the overall analysis (not significant) reflects the challenge of conducting RCTs during a pandemic rather than a true biological contradiction. CHAMPION demonstrated benefit in HFrEF; GUIDE-HF extension to HFpEF directionally consistent but underpowered.
