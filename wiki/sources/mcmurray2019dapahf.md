# DAPA-HF

> Dapagliflozin 10 mg/day reduced the primary composite of worsening HF or CV death by 26% in HFrEF regardless of diabetes status; established SGLT2 inhibitors as a fourth pillar of HFrEF therapy.

**File:** `raw/[not yet ingested]` · **Authors:** McMurray JJV et al. · **Year:** 2019 · **Journal:** NEJM  
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** ~4,744 · **Population:** HFrEF with LVEF ≤40%, NYHA II–IV, eGFR ≥25  
**Follow-up:** ~18.2 months · **Intervention:** Dapagliflozin 10 mg/day vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Worsening HF (hospitalization or urgent IV therapy) + CV death

---

## Key Findings

- Dapagliflozin reduced primary composite by **~26% relative risk reduction**
- Benefit consistent in patients **with and without T2DM**
- Significant reductions in HF hospitalizations, CV death, and all-cause mortality
- Also improved symptoms (KCCQ score) and slowed eGFR decline

## Methods (brief)

[Expand on paper ingest]

Included patients with LVEF ≤40%, NYHA II–IV, on optimised background HF therapy (RAAS blockade + beta-blocker). Both diabetic and non-diabetic patients included.

## Limitations

[Expand on paper ingest]

- ~45% had T2DM; glycaemic benefits may have contributed in diabetic patients

- Background therapy included MRA in ~71% — real-world patients often less optimised
