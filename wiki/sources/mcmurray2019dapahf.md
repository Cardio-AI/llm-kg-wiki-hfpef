# DAPA-HF

> Dapagliflozin 10 mg/day reduced primary composite of worsening HF or CV death by 26% (HR 0.74 [0.65–0.85] P<0.001) in HFrEF regardless of diabetes status (DM HR 0.75; non-DM HR 0.73); KCCQ win ratio 1.18 P<0.001; renal AEs fewer on dapagliflozin (6.5% vs 7.2%); creatinine rise only +0.02 mg/dL; NT-proBNP −301 pg/mL P<0.001; hematocrit +2.41% P<0.001; NNT=21; established SGLT2 inhibitors as fourth pillar of HFrEF therapy beyond glucose lowering; directly motivated DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved in HFpEF.

**Full citation:**
McMurray JJV, Solomon SD, Inzucchi SE, et al.; DAPA-HF Trial Committees and Investigators. Dapagliflozin in Patients with Heart Failure and Reduced Ejection Fraction. *N Engl J Med.* 2019;381(21):1995–2008. doi:[10.1056/NEJMoa1911303](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1911303)
**Study type:** RCT (placebo-controlled) · **N:** 4,744 randomised (dapagliflozin 2,373; placebo 2,371) · **Population:** HFrEF, LVEF ≤40%, NYHA II–IV, eGFR ≥25, on optimised background therapy  
**Follow-up:** Median 18.2 months · **Intervention:** Dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Worsening HF (hospitalisation or urgent IV therapy visit) or CV death  
**NCT:** NCT03036124 · **Enrollment:** Feb 15, 2017–Aug 17, 2018 · **Sites:** 410 centres, 20 countries  
**Sponsor:** AstraZeneca (provided study drug/placebo, funded trial; steering committee had final decisions on publication)

## Key Findings

- Primary composite (worsening HF or CV death): HR **0.74 (0.65–0.85)**, **P<0.001**; 386 (16.3%) vs 502 (21.2%); NNT=21
- HF hospitalisation: HR **0.70 (0.59–0.83)**, **P<0.001**; 237 (10.0%) vs 326 (13.7%)
- CV death + HF hospitalisation: HR **0.75 (0.65–0.85)**, **P<0.001**
- CV death: HR **0.82 (0.69–0.98)**; 227 (9.6%) vs 273 (11.5%)
- All-cause death: HR **0.83 (0.71–0.97)**; 276 (11.6%) vs 329 (13.9%)
- Total (recurrent) HF hosp + CV deaths: rate ratio **0.75 (0.65–0.88)**, **P<0.001**; 567 vs 742 events
- KCCQ total symptom score: win ratio **1.18 (1.11–1.26)**, **P<0.001**; LS change: +6.1±18.6 (dapa) vs +3.3±19.2 (placebo)
- **Non-diabetics (55% of cohort): HR 0.73 (0.60–0.88)** — consistent with DM HR 0.75 (0.63–0.90); first major SGLT2i HF trial establishing non-glucose mechanism
- NYHA subgroup: class II HR 0.63 (0.52–0.75) vs class III/IV HR 0.90 (0.74–1.09) — possible attenuation in advanced HF (post-hoc, no formal interaction P reported)

## Methods

**Design:** Double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled, multicenter RCT. ITT primary analysis. Independent endpoint adjudication committee. Hierarchical testing of primary and secondary outcomes.

**Dose:** Dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily throughout (no titration). Same dose used across HFrEF and HFpEF trials (DELIVER).

**Eligibility:**
- Age ≥18, LVEF ≤40%, NYHA II–IV
- eGFR ≥25 mL/min/1.73m²
- NT-proBNP ≥600 pg/mL (SR) or ≥900 pg/mL (AF); OR prior HF hospitalisation within 12 months with NT-proBNP ≥300 (SR) or ≥450 (AF)
- On stable RAAS blocker + beta-blocker for ≥4 weeks (unless contraindicated/intolerant)

**Exclusion:** Type 1 DM; eGFR <25; SBP <95 mmHg; symptomatic hypotension; prior SGLT2i; LVEF >40%; ACS/stroke within 3 months; planned revascularisation

**Statistical power:** 90% power to detect 17% RRR in primary composite; 1,390 events required; ~18 months median follow-up; annual event rate ~12.6% assumed for placebo.

## Limitations

- ~45% had T2DM; non-DM subgroup HR consistent (0.73) but mechanistic separation from glucose lowering requires mechanistic studies
- NYHA III/IV apparent attenuation: HR 0.90 (0.74–1.09) — inconsistent with other advanced-disease markers showing consistent benefit; post-hoc, no formal interaction P; likely chance finding but acknowledged
- Median follow-up 18.2 months — shorter than PARADIGM-HF (27 months) or I-PRESERVE (49.5 months); cumulative mortality curves still diverging at end
- ~24% female — underrepresents HFpEF-typical sex distribution; female subgroup HR 0.79 (0.59–1.06) includes 1.0 in CI
- Sacubitril-valsartan used in 10.5% — novel at time; HR consistent in this subgroup (0.75) but this is a subset of already-optimised patients
- 71% on MRA — higher than real-world; HR consistent in non-MRA subgroup (0.74), so no interaction, but population highly optimised
- eGFR ≥25 threshold — more permissive than prior HF trials; enables CKD patients but limits generalisation to eGFR 25–30 range where SGLT2 renal effects uncertain

## HFpEF Wiki Relevance

DAPA-HF is an HFrEF trial foundational to this wiki for four reasons:

1. **Non-DM benefit establishes mechanism:** Non-diabetic HR 0.73 (consistent with DM 0.75) proves SGLT2 inhibition improves HF outcomes independent of glucose lowering. This directly motivated testing in HFpEF (DELIVER [[solomon2022deliver]], EMPEROR-Preserved [[anker2021emperor]]) where most patients have metabolic comorbidities but the primary question was always whether HF — not DM — was being treated.

2. **Renal safety template:** Creatinine +0.02 mg/dL only; renal AEs fewer on dapagliflozin (6.5% vs 7.2%); same pattern reproduced in DELIVER (HR for renal composite ~0.71). SGLT2 inhibitors cause initial GFR dip (hemodynamic; tubuloglomerular feedback) but long-term renoprotection — same profile across HFrEF and HFpEF.

3. **NT-proBNP −301 pg/mL:** Neurohormonal unloading demonstrated despite short follow-up. Same direction (though different magnitude) in DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved. Consistent with haemodynamic offloading, not purely glycaemic effect.

4. **Hematocrit +2.41%:** Erythropoiesis stimulation or haemoconcentration — mechanistic candidate for exercise capacity improvement downstream in HFpEF. DELIVER/EMPEROR-Preserved share this signal.

## Contradictions
- NYHA III/IV attenuation: HR 0.90 (0.74–1.09) in DAPA-HF vs NYHA II 0.63 (0.52–0.75). Authors note this is inconsistent with other advanced-disease markers (lower EF, higher NT-proBNP, lower eGFR) all showing consistent benefit. In EMPEROR-Reduced (NYHA III/IV HR ~0.77) and DELIVER (NYHA III consistent), this attenuation was not reproduced. Likely a chance finding in this single trial post-hoc analysis. See [[contradictions]].
- Creatinine +0.02 mg/dL (P=0.007 — slightly higher in dapa group) vs renal AEs fewer (6.5% vs 7.2% NS): short-term haemodynamic eGFR dip from SGLT2 tubuloglomerular feedback versus long-term nephroprotection. The composite renal adverse event endpoint (more clinically meaningful) is lower on dapagliflozin despite the slightly higher creatinine. Both findings are real; they measure different timeframes of renal biology.
