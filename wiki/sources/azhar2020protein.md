# Dietary Protein + Exercise in Obese Elderly HFpEF (Azhar 2020)

> Open-label pilot RCT (N=16 analysed, 3 arms) in obese elderly HFpEF showing that whey protein supplementation alone did not improve physical performance and increased body fat, while protein supplementation combined with multimodal exercise (2×/week hydrotherapy + 1×/week gym) significantly improved 6MWD (+36.6 m), walk speed, quadriceps strength, and haemodynamic parameters over 12 weeks.

**Full citation:**
Azhar G, Raza S, Pangle A, Coleman K, Dawson A, Schrader A, Wolfe RR, Wei JY. Potential Beneficial Effects of Dietary Protein Supplementation and Exercise on Functional Capacity in a Pilot Study of Individuals with Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction. *Gerontol Geriatr Med.* 2020;6:1–9. doi:[10.1177/2333721420982808](https://doi.org/10.1177/2333721420982808)
**Study type:** Open-label pilot 3-arm RCT · **N:** 23 randomised, 16 analysed (C n=6, PS n=5, PS+E n=5) · **Population:** Obese elderly HFpEF (LVEF >50%, grade 1–2 diastolic dysfunction), BMI 28–45, age 55–85, mean age 70 · **Follow-up:** 12 weeks  
**Intervention:** Control (C): usual care; Protein Supplement (PS): whey protein to achieve 1.2 g/kg/day total intake; Protein+Exercise (PS+E): same protein + 2×/week hydrotherapy + 1×/week gym

---

## Key Findings

1. **Protein supplementation alone did not improve physical performance and increased body fat.** PS group showed no significant improvement in 6MWD, walk speed, or quadriceps strength vs control; body fat percentage increased (P<0.05 vs control). (source: Azhar2020Protein)

2. **Combined protein + exercise significantly improved functional outcomes.** PS+E group: 6MWD +36.6 m vs control (P<0.05); 10 m walk speed +0.13 m/s; quadriceps strength +21.5 kg vs control (P<0.05). (source: Azhar2020Protein)

3. **Haemodynamic improvements in PS+E arm.** Reduced systolic BP, diastolic BP, pulse pressure, and heart rate vs other groups at 12 weeks. (source: Azhar2020Protein)

4. **Protein alone is insufficient; exercise is the active ingredient.** The protein-only arm demonstrates that nutritional supplementation without physical training does not produce functional gains in this population — and may even worsen body composition. (source: Azhar2020Protein)

## Limitations

- Very small sample (N=16 analysed, N=5 per active arm); severely underpowered; results are exploratory
- Open-label design; blinding not possible for protein supplement or exercise
- Pilot study — no power calculation; results require replication
- Water-based exercise may not be representative of land-based cardiac rehabilitation programmes
- No cardiac functional assessments (echocardiographic data not reported)
