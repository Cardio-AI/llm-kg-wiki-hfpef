# Paulus & Tschöpe 2013 — Novel HFpEF Paradigm

> Proposes a paradigm shift for HFpEF: from LV afterload excess → LV hypertrophy to comorbidities → systemic inflammation → coronary microvascular endothelial inflammation → ↓NO bioavailability → ↓cGMP → ↓PKG → titin hypophosphorylation + cardiomyocyte hypertrophy and stiffness → diastolic LV dysfunction.

**Full citation:**
Paulus WJ, Tschöpe C. A Novel Paradigm for Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction: Comorbidities Drive Myocardial Dysfunction and Remodeling Through Coronary Microvascular Endothelial Inflammation. *J Am Coll Cardiol.* 2013;62(4):263–271. doi:[10.1016/j.jacc.2013.02.092](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2013.02.092)
**Study type:** State-of-the-Art Paper and Commentary (mechanistic review)  
**Supported by:** European Commission (FP7-Health-2010; MEDIA-261409)

## Key Findings

- **New paradigm**: HFpEF development driven by comorbidity-induced systemic inflammation → coronary microvascular endothelial inflammation → ↓NO → ↓cGMP → ↓PKG
- **Old paradigm rejected**: LV afterload excess alone cannot explain HFpEF; LV operates at favourable Laplace relationship in HFpEF (small cavity + preserved systolic function → low wall stress despite high SBP)
- **Cardiomyocyte stiffness mechanism**: ↓PKG → hypophosphorylation of titin N2B segment → increased Fpassive (resting tension); PKG phosphorylation of N2B reduces stiffness; PKA also reduces stiffness; PKC increases stiffness
- **Fibrosis mechanism**: ↓NO → TGF-β release → fibroblast → myofibroblast differentiation → collagen deposition → interstitial fibrosis → additional contribution to diastolic stiffness
- **Statin data (Figure 4)**: Statin-treated HFpEF patients show ↑PKG activity, ↓nitrotyrosine content, smaller cardiomyocytes, lower Fpassive (all P<0.001 to P<0.01) — supporting anti-inflammatory/endothelial mechanism as therapeutic target
- **HFpEF vs. HFrEF distinction**: HFpEF remodeling driven by endothelial oxidative stress (outside → in); HFrEF remodeling driven by cardiomyocyte oxidative stress from ischemia/infection/toxicity (inside → out)

## The Paulus-Tschöpe Cascade (Figure 1)

```
Comorbidities (obesity, DM, HTN, COPD, iron deficiency)
  ↓
Systemic proinflammatory state (↑IL-6, ↑TNF-α, ↑sST2, ↑pentraxin 3)
  ↓
Coronary microvascular endothelial inflammation
(↑VCAM, ↑E-selectin → leukocyte migration → subendothelial monocytes)
  ↓
↑ROS production (NADPH oxidase, nitrotyrosine formation)
↓NO bioavailability (O₂⁻ + NO → ONOO⁻)
  ↓
↓soluble guanylate cyclase (sGC) activity in cardiomyocytes
↓cGMP concentration
↓PKG activity
  ↓
Two parallel consequences:
1. Cardiomyocyte hypertrophy (PKG removes brake on prohypertrophic stimuli)
2. ↑Fpassive (hypophosphorylation of titin N2B segment → stiff titin spring)
  ↓
High diastolic LV stiffness → HFpEF
(also: TGF-β → myofibroblasts → collagen → interstitial fibrosis → additional stiffness)
```

## Methods (brief)

Narrative/mechanistic review synthesizing: myocardial biopsy studies; animal models; epidemiological data from HFPEF registries and outcome trials (I-PRESERVE, ALL-HAT, MONICA); prior mechanistic work by van Heerebeek, Borbély, Falcão-Pires, and Westermann groups; statin observational data; PDE5i clinical data; echocardiographic studies.

No primary data; all evidence cited from 97 references.

## Key Mechanistic Evidence Cited

### Comorbidities as HFpEF drivers (not arterial hypertension alone)
- In all major registries/trials, HTN in HFpEF = elevated SBP ±148 mm Hg but normal DBP ±83 mm Hg — LV wall stress low
- ALL-HAT (40,000 patients): BMI (not SBP/DBP) discriminated HFrEF vs. HFpEF development
- MONICA registry: LA enlargement related to obesity (not HTN) — supports metabolic not pressure mechanism
- Pulmonary hypertension secondary to HFpEF (PH-HFpEF): right atrial dilatation excess vs. primary PH despite similar mean PAP — attributed to high obesity prevalence, not pressure load alone

### Microvascular endothelial inflammation
- VCAM and E-selectin abundantly expressed in myocardial biopsies of HFPEF patients
- Vasodilator response to acetylcholine reduced in HFpEF (coronary microvascular dysfunction demonstrated)
- Peripheral endothelial dysfunction = independent predictor of outcome in HFpEF

### ↓NO, ↓cGMP, ↓PKG in cardiomyocytes
- Low myocardial PKG activity AND low cGMP demonstrated in HFPEF myocardial homogenates (van Heerebeek et al.)
- BNP inability to normalise cGMP in HFpEF myocardium — consistent with low concentrically remodelled LV wall stress (which normally stimulates BNP-cGMP signalling)

### Titin stiffness
- HFpEF cardiomyocytes: characteristically high resting tension attributed to N2B titin hypophosphorylation
- PKG corrects this in vitro
- N2BA → N2B isoform shift in HFpEF vs. normal
- PKA (less stiff), PKG (less stiff), PKC (more stiff) — differential phosphorylation sites

### Statin observation (Figure 4)
| Marker | HFPEF stat- | HFPEF stat+ | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| pVASP/VASP (PKG activity) | ~0.62 | ~0.82 | <0.01 |
| Nitrotyrosine % | ~4.3 | ~2.8 | <0.001 |
| Myocyte diameter (μm) | ~28 | ~25 | <0.001 |
| Fpassive (kN/m²) | ~7.7 | ~6.0 | <0.001 |

### RAAS neutral in HFpEF — consistent with paradigm
- ACEi/ARBs exert vasculoprotective effect on macrovasculature (↓MI in EUROPA/PERFECT) but do not restore endothelial microvascular function in inflammation+comorbidity setting
- PERFECT substudy: perindopril NS for flow-mediated brachial artery dilation (P=0.23) despite ↓MI (P=0.001) — macrovascular vs. microvascular dissociation

## Diagnostic Implications

Proposed additions to HFpEF diagnostic algorithms:
- Anthropometric measures (waist circumference) — captures central obesity/visceral fat
- Plasma inflammatory markers (IL-6, TNF-α, sST2, pentraxin 3)
- Vascular hyperemic responses (vasodilator capacity)
- Aim to move beyond purely structural/filling-pressure criteria

## Therapeutic Implications

Targets consistent with the paradigm:
- ↑NO bioavailability: NO donors, PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil), antioxidants, resveratrol
- ↑cGMP/PKG: PDE5i; PDE9i (preclinical); sGC stimulators (note: VITALITY-HFpEF later negative)
- Endothelial function: statins (anti-inflammatory + direct endothelial effects)
- Anti-inflammatory: theoretically MRA (Zannad data on collagen crosslinks); IL-1 blockade (theoretical, referenced)
- Comorbidity correction: obesity, DM, HTN management

Note: PDE5i (RELAX trial, published same year) subsequently demonstrated neutral in outcome trial. sGC stimulator (VITALITY-HFpEF) also neutral. Statin benefit remains observational only.

## Limitations

- Narrative review; no primary data; no systematic literature search; evidence synthesis limited to expert selection
- Evidence base primarily from myocardial biopsy studies (small N, selected centres, potential selection bias)
- Paradigm does not fully account for right heart involvement, renal contribution, or phenotypic heterogeneity beyond obesity/DM/HTN
- Therapeutic implications based on preclinical and observational data; subsequent RCTs (RELAX, VITALITY-HFpEF) did not support cGMP pathway as therapeutic target in clinical HFpEF
- Does not integrate the phenomapping/heterogeneity concept that emerges from Shah2015Phenomapping
