# Gori 2021 — PARAGON-HF hs-TnT

> Sacubitril/valsartan reduced hs-TnT by ~10% vs. valsartan in HFpEF; patients whose hs-TnT declined to ≤17 ng/L by week 16 had better subsequent outcomes; higher baseline hs-TnT may predict greater Sac/Val benefit (P interaction = 0.07).

**Full citation:**
Gori M, Senni M, Claggett B, Liu J, Maggioni AP, Zile M, Prescott MF, Van Veldhuisen DJ, Zannad F, Pieske B, Lam CSP, Rouleau J, Jhund P, Packer M, Pfeffer MA, Lefkowitz M, Shi V, McMurray JJV, Solomon SD. Integrating High-Sensitivity Troponin T and Sacubitril/Valsartan Treatment in HFpEF: The PARAGON-HF Trial. *JACC Heart Fail.* 2021;9(9):627–635. doi:[10.1016/j.jchf.2021.04.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jchf.2021.04.009)

## Summary

Secondary biomarker analysis of [[paragon-hf]] (LVEF ≥45%, N=4,822 total; hs-TnT subsample: N=1,260 at randomisation; N=1,120 at week 16; N=1,205 at week 48). Assessed whether: (1) baseline hs-TnT predicts outcomes; (2) sacubitril/valsartan modifies hs-TnT trajectory; (3) hs-TnT changes predict or modify treatment benefit.

## Design

- **Parent trial:** PARAGON-HF (sacubitril/valsartan vs. valsartan, LVEF ≥45%, HFpEF)
- **Biomarker subsample:** 1,260 with baseline hs-TnT measurement (~26% of biomarker cohort)
- **Assay:** Elecsys (Roche) hs-TnT; upper limit of normal 14 ng/L
- **Assessment timepoints:** Randomisation, week 16, week 48
- **Outcome:** Composite CV death + total HF hospitalisation

## Key Findings

**Prevalence and prognosis:**
- 58.3% had baseline hs-TnT >14 ng/L (above ULN)
- Higher hs-TnT independently predicted primary composite: adjusted HR 1.38 (95% CI 1.19–1.59; P<0.001) per doubling
- Association not modified by sex

**Treatment effect on hs-TnT:**
- Sacubitril/valsartan reduced hs-TnT by **9% at week 16** (P<0.001) vs. valsartan
- Sacubitril/valsartan reduced hs-TnT by **10% at week 48** (P<0.001) vs. valsartan
- Effect consistent regardless of baseline hs-TnT level

**hs-TnT trajectory and outcomes:**
- Patients whose hs-TnT decreased to ≤17 ng/L (median) by week 16 had lower subsequent composite risk (P=0.046)
- On-treatment hs-TnT reduction is therefore prognostically meaningful, not merely a pharmacodynamic marker

**Treatment effect heterogeneity by hs-TnT:**
- Weak signal: patients with baseline hs-TnT >17 ng/L (above median) had a numerically greater benefit from Sac/Val (P interaction = 0.07 — not significant)
- Insufficient to define a biomarker-enriched population prospectively

## Clinical Implications

- hs-TnT may complement NP-based patient selection for Sac/Val in HFpEF — high baseline hs-TnT identifies patients with ongoing myocardial injury who may derive greater benefit
- hs-TnT reduction under Sac/Val is biologically plausible: ARNi reduces wall stress and neurohormonal activation → less cardiomyocyte stretch-related injury
- 17 ng/L threshold (median of this cohort): context-dependent; general clinical ULN is 14 ng/L
- Residual confounding from non-cardiac sources of hs-TnT elevation (skeletal muscle, CKD) not addressed

## Relationship to Other Evidence

- Morfino 2022 ([[morfino2022biomarkers]]) synthesises this finding in the context of all HFpEF troponin literature: stronger prognostic value in HFpEF men (HR 3.33) vs. women (HR 1.35) from other cohorts; hs-TnT 17 ng/L threshold from this paper
- Sacubitril/valsartan entity: [[sacubitril-valsartan]]; parent trial entity: [[paragon-hf]]

## Limitations

- Post-hoc biomarker analysis — not powered for interaction testing
- hs-TnT subsample (26%) may not represent full PARAGON-HF population
- Single assay (Elecsys); hs-TnI results may differ
- P interaction = 0.07 for treatment effect modification — suggestive only, not definitive
- Non-cardiac sources of elevated hs-TnT not accounted for
