# Beale 2019 — Iron Deficiency in HFpEF: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

> Iron deficiency is highly prevalent in HFpEF (59%, 95% CI 52–65%) and is associated with worse exercise capacity and quality of life in observational studies; however, as of 2019, no RCT evidence existed for the benefit of iron supplementation in HFpEF specifically.

## Core Arguments

### Iron Deficiency Prevalence in HFpEF
- **Overall iron deficiency:** 59% (95% CI 52–65%)
- **Functional iron deficiency:** 34% (ferritin 100–299 ng/mL + TSAT <20%)
- **Absolute iron deficiency:** 30% (ferritin <100 ng/mL)
- Prevalence substantially higher than in the general population and comparable to HFrEF

### Impact on Exercise Capacity and Quality of Life
- **3 out of 4 studies** showed iron deficiency associated with worse peak VO₂ (mL/kg/min)
- Iron deficiency associated with worse 6-minute walk test distance and poorer KCCQ/MLHFQ quality of life scores in observational data
- Mechanism: impaired mitochondrial function, reduced oxidative phosphorylation, skeletal muscle dysfunction (independent of haemoglobin)

### Treatment Evidence Gap (2019)
- No RCTs of IV iron supplementation in HFpEF available at time of publication
- FAIR-HF and CONFIRM-HF demonstrated benefits in HFrEF — hypothesis that similar benefits would extend to HFpEF
- Review explicitly anticipates need for an HFpEF-specific iron trial (subsequently conducted as FAIR-HFpEF; see [[vonhaehling2024fair]])

### Methodological Notes
- High heterogeneity across studies in iron deficiency definition; most used WHO criteria (ferritin <100 or ferritin 100-299 + TSAT <20%) but variation exists
- Study populations included ambulatory outpatients predominantly; very elderly or severe HFpEF underrepresented

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

This review established the epidemiological basis for iron deficiency as a clinically important comorbidity in HFpEF and the rationale for therapeutic trials. The 59% prevalence figure is widely cited. Directly anticipates FAIR-HFpEF (2024). Complements [[vonhaehling2024fair]] (RCT) and [[ponikowski2020affirm]] (AFFIRM-AHF in HFrEF context).
