# SOCRATES-PRESERVED — Vericiguat Phase 2b in HFpEF (Pieske 2017)

> Phase 2b dose-finding RCT of vericiguat (sGC stimulator) in HFpEF: co-primary NT-proBNP and LA volume endpoints both negative (P>0.2), but exploratory KCCQ-CSS improvement at 10 mg (+9.2 pts, P=0.016) motivated VITALITY-HFpEF phase 3 — which was also neutral, illustrating the surrogate-to-outcome translation problem.

**Study type:** Phase 2b multicenter double-blind RCT (dose-finding) · **N:** 477 · **Sites:** 145 sites  
**Population:** HFpEF; LVEF ≥45%; worsening HF (hospitalisation or IV diuretics within 4 weeks of clinical stabilization)  
**Follow-up:** 12 weeks  
**Intervention:** Vericiguat 1.25 mg (n=96), 2.5 mg (n=96), 5 mg (n=96), 10 mg (n=96) vs. placebo (n=93)  
**Primary outcome:** Change in log NT-proBNP + change in left atrial volume (co-primary)

## Key Findings

- **Co-primary endpoints (NT-proBNP + LAV at 12 weeks): NEGATIVE** — NT-proBNP P=0.2017; LAV P=0.3688 (both two-sided, across all doses)
- **Exploratory KCCQ-CSS at 10 mg:** +9.2 pts vs placebo (P=0.016); difference weeks 4–12: 5.7 pts (P=0.0465)
- Echo signals at 10 mg: E wave +9 cm/s (P<0.05); medial e' slope +0.08 cm/s (P<0.05)
- Heart rate at 10 mg: −5.9 bpm vs placebo (P=0.0045); no blood pressure change
- Protocol violation: 48 patients (IxRS error) received lower doses than randomised — excluded from exploratory analyses
- KCCQ signal motivated VITALITY-HFpEF (phase 3, N=789) — which was neutral (KCCQ-PLS P=0.47 at 15 mg, P=0.80 at 10 mg)

## Methods (brief)

Parallel-group dose-ranging design; 5 arms (4 active doses + placebo), N=96 per active arm, N=93 placebo. Enriched for worsening HF (hospitalisation or IV diuretic use within 4 weeks of clinical stabilization). Vericiguat = soluble guanylate cyclase (sGC) stimulator; acts via NO-independent pathway to elevate cGMP. Distinguished from PDE5 inhibitors by direct sGC stimulation rather than preventing cGMP degradation. Co-primary endpoints: change from baseline in log-transformed NT-proBNP and left atrial volume (LAV) at 12 weeks, tested hierarchically (NT-proBNP first, then LAV if NT-proBNP significant). Exploratory endpoints included KCCQ-CSS, echocardiographic parameters, 6MWD, BNP.

## Results

### Co-Primary Endpoints

| Endpoint | Direction | P (two-sided, across doses) |
|----------|-----------|---------------------------|
| Log NT-proBNP change at 12 wk | No dose-dependent effect | 0.2017 |
| LAV change at 12 wk | No dose-dependent effect | 0.3688 |

### Exploratory Endpoints (10 mg arm vs placebo)

| Endpoint | Effect | P |
|----------|--------|---|
| KCCQ-CSS change | +9.2 pts | 0.016 |
| KCCQ-CSS diff (wk 4–12) | +5.7 pts | 0.0465 |
| E wave velocity | +9 cm/s | <0.05 |
| Medial e' slope | +0.08 cm/s | <0.05 |
| Heart rate | −5.9 bpm | 0.0045 |
| Systolic/diastolic BP | No change | NS |

### Phase 3 Outcome (VITALITY-HFpEF, Armstrong 2020)

| Dose | KCCQ-PLS change | P |
|------|----------------|---|
| Vericiguat 15 mg | Neutral | 0.47 |
| Vericiguat 10 mg | Neutral | 0.80 |

Phase 2b KCCQ signal did not replicate in phase 3.

## Limitations

- Phase 2b; not powered for clinical outcomes
- 12-week follow-up — long-term signal uncertain
- NT-proBNP and LAV are surrogate endpoints; their failure as co-primaries is informative
- IxRS protocol violation: 48 patients received lower doses (compromises dose-response interpretation)
- KCCQ-CSS is exploratory (not pre-specified primary); P=0.016 in a multiple-comparison context
- High AF prevalence (40%) may confound echocardiographic measurements (E wave, e')
- Phase 3 dose selection (10 mg and 15 mg) based on limited exploratory signal
