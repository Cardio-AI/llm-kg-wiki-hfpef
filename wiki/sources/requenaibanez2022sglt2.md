# Requena-Ibanez 2022 — SGLT2i EF Spectrum Review

> SGLT2i benefit in HFpEF may be attenuated at LVEF >60%; EF measurement variability, EAT reduction, and antifibrotic mechanisms argue for phenotype-based rather than EF-based patient selection.

**Full citation:**
Requena-Ibáñez JA, Santos-Gallego CG, Requena-Ibanez J, Badimón JJ. Mechanistic insights of SGLT2 inhibitors in heart failure: prevention of epicardic adipose tissue accumulation and renal-cardiac crosstalk. *Cardiovasc Drugs Ther.* 2023;37(5):989–996. doi:[10.1007/s10557-022-07371-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10557-022-07371-7)

---

## Summary

Narrative review from Mount Sinai, NY, examining the mechanistic rationale for SGLT2 inhibitors across the ejection fraction spectrum, with particular focus on the EMPEROR-Preserved EF >60% attenuation signal. Argues for moving beyond EF classification toward phenotype-based patient selection.

## Key Content

**EMPEROR-Preserved EF subgroup attenuation:**
- In EMPEROR-Preserved, the primary benefit of empagliflozin was attenuated in patients with LVEF >60%
- HR in LVEF >60% subgroup: approximately 0.87 (0.69–1.10; non-significant); this subgroup represented <10% of the trial population
- Whether this attenuation represents a true biological boundary, EF measurement noise, or an underpowered subgroup finding is unresolved
- DELIVER (dapagliflozin, broader EF range ≥40%) did not show the same EF >60% attenuation pattern to the same degree

**EF measurement variability:**
- LVEF as a continuous variable has substantial intra- and inter-reader variability (±5–10 percentage points)
- Patients near EF boundaries (e.g., 55–65%) may be enrolled in different EF categories across assessments
- CMR (cardiac MRI) provides lower EF variability than echocardiography and should be considered in borderline cases
- Dynamic EF changes (improvement from hypertension treatment, worsening from volume overload) challenge a static EF cut-off

**SGLT2i mechanisms in HFpEF beyond glycaemia:**
- **EAT reduction:** Epicardial adipose tissue (EAT) reduction by SGLT2i demonstrated in EmpaTropism model (porcine); EAT is a reservoir of pro-inflammatory adipokines driving cardiac fibrosis
- **Myocardial fibrosis reduction:** Antifibrotic effects via TGF-β pathway modulation; relevant to HFpEF pathophysiology
- **Endothelial NO restoration:** SGLT2i improves endothelial nitric oxide availability → reduces microvascular dysfunction
- **Reverse LV remodelling:** SGLT2i reduces LV mass and improves LV geometry in non-diabetic HF

**Phenotype-based selection over EF:**
- Patients with obese/metabolic-inflammatory phenotype are proposed as most responsive (high EAT, T2DM, high CRP)
- EF >60% patients tend to have less left ventricular remodelling target — but may still benefit from EAT reduction and diuretic-like haemodynamic effects
- Trial enrolment Table 1 lists CHIEF-HF (NCT04252287), PRESERVE-HF (NCT03030235), DELIVER, EMPEROR-Preserved as contemporary HFpEF SGLT2i trial landscape

## Clinical Implications

- EF >60% attenuation in EMPEROR-Preserved should not prompt automatic SGLT2i exclusion — it was a small subgroup and DELIVER showed less attenuation
- Phenotypic enrichment (obese, metabolic, high inflammatory markers) may identify patients most likely to benefit regardless of EF
- CMR-based EF measurement for borderline EF patients avoids misclassification

## Relationship to Other Evidence

- Minisy 2025 ([[minisy2025sglt2]]) meta-analysis confirms overall HR 0.83 across 9 trials without restricting by EF
- Gonzalez 2024 ([[gonzalez2024sglt2trends]]) documents real-world implementation gap, especially in non-T2DM HFpEF
- Albulushi 2025 ([[albulushi2025sglt2fibrosis]]) provides prospective CMR evidence for SGLT2i antifibrotic mechanism in diabetic HFpEF

## Limitations

- Narrative review without systematic methodology or meta-analysis
- EmpaTropism model is porcine — human translation of EAT reduction findings requires further validation
- EMPEROR-Preserved LVEF >60% subgroup was small (<10%) and post-hoc
