# PROMIS-HFpEF — CMD Prevalence in HFpEF (Shah 2018)

> First prospective multi-centre, multinational study demonstrating CMD prevalence of 75% (151/202) in guideline-defined HFpEF patients without unrevascularised macrovascular CAD, using adenosine stress transthoracic Doppler echocardiography; CMD correlated with microalbuminuria, NT-proBNP, and impaired RV function — but not with CRP or symptoms.

**Study type:** Prospective multicentre multinational observational cohort · **N:** 202 HFpEF patients  
**Sites:** 5 centres — Stockholm + Gothenburg (Sweden), Turku (Finland), Chicago (USA), Singapore  
**Population:** Guideline-defined HFpEF (HFA-PEFF score ≥5 in 82% of CMD patients); LVEF ≥50%; no unrevascularised macrovascular CAD  
**CMD measurement:** Coronary flow reserve (CFR) by adenosine stress transthoracic Doppler echocardiography of LAD; CMD defined as CFR <2.5

## Key Findings

- **CMD prevalence: 75% (151/202; 95% CI 69–81%)** — high CMD burden in guideline-defined HFpEF without macrovascular CAD
- Mean CFR overall: 2.13 ± 0.51, median 2.08; CMD group mean 1.90 ± 0.30; no-CMD group mean 2.80 ± 0.30
- CMD patients more likely to have AF (58% vs 25%; P=0.004) and smoking history (70% vs 43%; P=0.0006)
- BMI higher in no-CMD group (32.5 vs 29.0; P=0.017) — the obese HFpEF phenotype may be CMD-spared
- **Multivariable correlates of lower CFR (continuous):**
  - UACR (microalbuminuria): β=−0.34, P=0.002 → 4.1 per SD decrease in CFR, P=0.028
  - NT-proBNP: β=−0.27, P=0.0001 → 543 per SD decrease, P=0.010
  - RHI (reactive hyperaemia index — endothelial function): β=0.21, P=0.004
  - TAPSE: β=0.26, P=0.0002 — impaired RV function associated with worse CMD
  - RV free wall strain: β=−0.8, P=0.022
- **CRP NOT associated with CMD** (P=0.95) — systemic inflammation (as measured by CRP) dissociates from CMD
- **KCCQ and 6MWT NOT independently associated with CMD** — CMD does not directly explain symptom burden or functional impairment in cross-sectional analysis
- HFA-PEFF score ≥5 (definite HFpEF) in 82% of CMD patients

## Methods (brief)

**CMD measurement protocol:** Adenosine stress echocardiography with transthoracic Doppler of LAD (diastolic peak velocity ratio stress/rest). CFR <2.5 defined CMD. No invasive coronary angiography required.

**Inclusion criteria:** Clinical HFpEF (ESC 2016 criteria); LVEF ≥50%; no obstructive epicardial CAD by angiography or CT within past 5 years, or no clinical reason to suspect CAD; stable outpatient status.

**Baseline characteristics (CMD vs. no-CMD):** Age ~70 years in both groups; LVEF ~60%; female majority.

## Contradictions

CMD prevalence 75% by non-invasive Doppler CFR — but CMD method (Doppler echo of LAD) only reflects LAD territory flow; microvascular resistance is not directly measured. DIAMOND-HFpEF (CMR MPR, different method) finds 70% CMD prevalence — concordant estimate but via structurally different measurement. PROMIS-HFpEF's CRP dissociation (no correlation with CMD) conflicts with the general inflammation-as-mechanism narrative in HFpEF — CRP may not be the right systemic inflammation marker for CMD. Noted in `wiki/contradictions.md`.
