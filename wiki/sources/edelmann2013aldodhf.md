# ALDO-DHF (Edelmann 2013)

> Spironolactone 25 mg significantly improved echocardiographic diastolic function (E/e' −1.5; P<0.001) and induced reverse cardiac remodelling (LV mass −6 g/m²; P=0.009) in HFpEF, but did not improve maximal exercise capacity (peak VO₂ P=0.81), symptoms, or quality of life.

**Study type:** Multicenter, parallel-group, double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT · **N:** 422 (213 spironolactone; 209 placebo) · **Population:** Ambulatory HFpEF, age ≥50y, NYHA II–III, LVEF ≥50%, diastolic dysfunction grade ≥I, peak VO₂ ≤25 mL/kg/min · **Follow-up:** 12 months (mean 11.6 months)

## Key Findings

- **E/e' improved significantly** (adjusted mean difference −1.5; 95% CI −2.0 to −0.9; P<0.001) — one of two co-primary endpoints MET
- **Peak VO₂ unchanged** (+0.1 mL/min/kg; 95% CI −0.6 to +0.8; P=0.81) — co-primary endpoint NOT MET
- **Reverse cardiac remodelling demonstrated:** LV mass −6 g/m² (P=0.009); LV end-diastolic diameter −1.4 mm (P=0.01); LV EF +1.6% (P=0.04)
- **NT-proBNP reduced:** geometric mean ratio 0.86 (95% CI 0.75–0.99); P=0.03
- **6-minute walk distance decreased** −15 m (95% CI −27 to −2; P=0.02) — paradoxical worsening of submaximal exercise
- **No improvement in symptoms or QoL** (NYHA, MLHFQ, SF-36, PHQ-9 all NS)
- **Safety:** Potassium +0.2 mmol/L; eGFR −5 mL/min/1.73m²; no hyperkalemia hospitalisations; gynecomastia in some patients

## Methods (brief)

**Population:** Ambulatory patients ≥50y; chronic NYHA class II or III; LVEF ≥50% on echocardiography; echocardiographic diastolic dysfunction grade ≥I (by ESC 2007 criteria) or AF; peak VO₂ ≤25 mL/kg/min; adequate organ function (creatinine <1.8 mg/dL; eGFR ≥30; K <5.1 mmol/L; Hgb ≥11 g/dL)

**Key exclusions:** LVEF ≤40%; significant CAD (angina, >50% stenosis, prior CABG ≤3 months); COPD (VC <80% or FEV₁/VC <80%); significant lab abnormalities; contraindication to spironolactone; concomitant potassium-sparing diuretic (triamterene, amiloride); prior potassium supplement

**Dose:** Spironolactone 25 mg once daily (mean achieved dose 21.6 mg, 95% CI 20.8–22.3); dose could be reduced to 25 mg every other day for K >5.2 mmol/L or reversible adverse effects; stopped for K >5.5 mmol/L or serious adverse effects; rechallenge encouraged

**Assessments:** Echocardiography (blinded core laboratory), CPET, 6-minute walk test, QoL questionnaires, lab samples — all at baseline, 6 months, 12 months

**Statistical analysis:** ANCOVA with follow-up value as dependent variable, treatment as factor, baseline as covariate; intention-to-treat; no multiplicity adjustment for co-primary endpoints (global Type I error 0.05 split 0.025/0.025 each)

**Baseline characteristics (combined group):**
- Mean age: 67 (SD 8) y; 52% female; BMI 28.9 (3.6) kg/m²
- Mean LVEF: 67 (8)% (notably higher than TOPCAT/FINEARTS populations)
- Median NT-proBNP: 158 (IQR 83–299) ng/L (very low compared to outcomes trials)
- E/e' medial: 12.8 (4.0); peak VO₂: 16.4 (3.5) mL/min/kg; 6MWD: 530 (87) m
- 92% hypertension; 40% CAD; 65% hyperlipidemia; 17% diabetes mellitus; 5% AF; 3% COPD
- 77% on ACEi/ARB; 72% beta-blockers; 54% diuretics; 25% calcium antagonists; 25% statins

## Limitations

- Fixed low dose (25 mg/day; no up-titration): below the dose proven effective in HFrEF (RALES used up to 50 mg); may underestimate achievable remodelling and functional benefit
- Low-risk, ambulatory, German/Austrian population: mean NT-proBNP 158 ng/L vs. 750 pg/mL (TOPCAT) vs. 1040 pg/mL (FINEARTS-HF) — results may not apply to higher-risk patients with more severe HF
- LVEF ≥50% only (mean 67%): excludes HFmrEF range; more "pure" HFpEF than TOPCAT or FINEARTS-HF
- 12-month follow-up only — no mortality or HF hospitalisation data; underpowered for clinical events
- 52% female: adequate representation but subgroup analyses underpowered
- Peak VO₂ ≤25 mL/kg/min inclusion criterion: ambulatory patients with mild-to-moderate exercise limitation only; excludes most severely limited patients
- Surrogate endpoint (E/e') improved without functional benefit — questions validity of E/e' as a surrogate for clinical outcomes

## Contradictions

E/e' improved (P<0.001) and LV mass decreased (P=0.009) despite no change in peak VO₂, 6MWD, NYHA class, or QoL. This structural-functional dissociation suggests: (a) structural remodelling may precede functional recovery with longer treatment; (b) E/e' does not reliably predict exercise-related outcomes; (c) 12-month follow-up insufficient; or (d) spironolactone dose (25 mg) inadequate for functional benefit.
