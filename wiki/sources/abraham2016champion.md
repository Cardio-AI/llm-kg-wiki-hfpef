# CHAMPION Trial: Complete Follow-Up and Open-Access Results

> CHAMPION complete follow-up (n=550): haemodynamic-guided therapy (CardioMEMS PA pressure monitoring) reduced HF admissions by 33% during randomised-access period (HR 0.67, P<0.0001); former control patients given open access achieved 48% reduction (HR 0.52, P<0.0001) — sustained, dose-dependent benefit confirms haemodynamic monitoring as an effective intervention targeting the HFpEF substrate of elevated LA/PA pressures.

## Core Arguments

**Trial:** CHAMPION (CardioMEMS Heart Sensor Allows Monitoring of Pressure to Improve Outcomes in NYHA Class III Heart Failure Patients). Complete follow-up paper including open-access extension. NCT00531661. N=**550** (280 active, 270 control at original randomisation). Population: NYHA class III HF including HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%) patients. FDA approved CardioMEMS for NYHA class III HF in 2014.

**Device:** CardioMEMS PA pressure sensor — wireless implantable haemodynamic monitoring device measuring mean PAP, systolic PAP, diastolic PAP, and heart rate. Clinicians use daily transmitted PAP data to pre-emptively adjust diuretics, vasodilators, and other medications before clinical decompensation occurs.

**Results — randomised-access period:**
- HF admissions: **HR 0.67**, **P<0.0001**
- **33% reduction** in HF hospitalisations with haemodynamic-guided management vs control

**Results — open-access extension (former control group given monitoring):**
- HF admissions: **HR 0.52**, **P<0.0001**
- **48% reduction** — larger than original active group effect, possibly reflecting physician learning curve and optimised PAP-guided management protocols over time

**Mechanism:** Daily PAP data detects subclinical haemodynamic congestion (rising PA diastolic pressure) 1–2 weeks before symptomatic decompensation. Pre-emptive treatment (outpatient diuretic adjustment, vasodilator titration) prevents escalation to hospitalisation. Treats the elevated LA/PA pressure state that is the defining haemodynamic feature of HFpEF.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

CHAMPION establishes haemodynamic monitoring as a device intervention that succeeds in HFpEF where most pharmacological strategies have failed. The mechanism is disease-mechanism-targeted: HFpEF is characterised by dynamic LA/PA pressure elevation (especially on exertion), and direct haemodynamic guidance enables personalised diuretic management that static clinical assessment cannot. The 33–48% hospitalisation reduction is larger than any pharmacological intervention in HFpEF to date.

This evidence base underpins the LA pressure offloading hypothesis also tested by the atrial shunt (REDUCE LAP-HF II, [[patel2024reducelaphf]]) and informs the GUIDE-HF study (broader EF population).
