# PARAGON-HF

> Sacubitril/valsartan marginally missed its primary endpoint in HFpEF (RR 0.87, P=0.06); significant signals in women (RR 0.73) and LVEF ≤57% (RR 0.78); significant renal protection (HR 0.50) and NYHA improvement; FDA label extended to "LVEF below normal"; remains the definitive near-miss ARNI trial in HFpEF.

**Full citation:**
Solomon SD, McMurray JJV, Anand IS, et al.; PARAGON-HF Investigators and Committees. Angiotensin–Neprilysin Inhibition in Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction. *N Engl J Med.* 2019;381(17):1609–1620. doi:[10.1056/NEJMoa1908655](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1908655)
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** 4,822 randomised (4,796 efficacy) · **Population:** HF, LVEF ≥45%, NYHA II–IV, elevated NPs, structural heart disease  
**Follow-up:** Median 35 months (IQR 30–41) · **Intervention:** Sacubitril/valsartan 97/103 mg BID vs. valsartan 160 mg BID  
**Primary outcome:** Composite of total HF hospitalisations + CV death  
**NCT:** NCT01920711 · **Enrollment:** Jul 18, 2014–Dec 16, 2016 · **Sites:** 848 centres, 43 countries  
**Sponsor:** Novartis

---

## Key Findings

- Primary composite RR **0.87** (95% CI 0.75–1.01), **P=0.06** — **narrowly missed significance**
- Total HF hospitalisations: RR 0.85 (0.72–1.00)
- CV death: HR 0.95 (0.79–1.16) — not significant
- **NYHA improvement** (secondary): OR 1.45 (1.13–1.86) — **significant**
- **Renal protection** (secondary): HR 0.50 (0.33–0.77) — **significant**; sacubitril/valsartan halved renal composite events
- KCCQ at 8 months: mean difference +1.0 point (0.0–2.1) — **not significant**
- **Sex subgroup:** women RR 0.73 (0.59–0.90) vs. men 1.03 (0.85–1.25); interaction P=0.018
- **LVEF subgroup:** ≤median (57%) RR 0.78 (0.64–0.95) vs. >median 1.00 (0.81–1.23); interaction P=0.026
- Only 2/12 prespecified subgroups showed heterogeneity — both exploratory
- FDA extended label to "HFpEF with LVEF below normal" based on these signals

## Methods

**Design:** Randomised, double-blind, active-comparator RCT. Two sequential run-in phases: valsartan (median 15 days) → sacubitril/valsartan (median 19 days) — patients tolerating both randomised 1:1. ITT. Primary analysis: semiparametric proportional rates (Lin et al.) + joint gamma frailty model stratified by region.

**Eligibility:**
- Age ≥50, NYHA II–IV
- LVEF ≥45% within 6 months
- Elevated NPs: NT-proBNP ≥300 pg/mL (SR) / ≥900 pg/mL (AF); BNP equivalents
- Structural heart disease: LA enlargement or LV hypertrophy
- Diuretic use required
- ACEi/ARB (non-MRA) discontinued before run-in

**Statistical analysis:** Target 1,847 primary events; 95% power to detect 22% lower rate; adjusted alpha 0.048 after one interim analysis. Secondary outcomes hierarchically tested; CIs for secondary/exploratory outcomes not adjusted for multiplicity.

**Efficacy population:** 26 patients excluded from analysis (GCP violation at one site closed June 27, 2017) → N=4,796.

**Key safety pattern:** Sacubitril/valsartan had more hypotension and angioedema, but **unexpectedly lower** creatinine elevation and hyperkalemia — opposite of typical RAAS safety concern. Renal haemodynamic effect of neprilysin inhibition may be protective.

## Limitations

- Active comparator (valsartan, not placebo) — RAAS-attributable baseline effect suppressed; true placebo-controlled benefit likely larger
- Sequential run-in excluded ~900 patients (~16% of those entering run-in) — enriched tolerable, stable population
- Subgroup analyses (sex, LVEF) were exploratory secondary analyses; only 2/12 prespecified subgroups showed heterogeneity — not pre-specified primary; no multiple comparison correction
- Mean LVEF 57.6% — 51% of patients at LVEF ≤median (57%), overlapping HFmrEF territory; not pure HFpEF ≥50%
- 848 centres in 43 countries — pronounced heterogeneity in practice patterns and background therapy
- Run-in enrichment may explain smaller-than-anticipated treatment difference

## Contradictions
- FDA label (LVEF below normal) based on exploratory subgroup vs. ESC 2021 no recommendation vs. AHA 2022 Class 2b — three regulatory/guideline bodies, three conclusions from same trial. See [[contradictions]] #3.
- Sex interaction (women benefit, men do not): if real, represents first sex-specific pharmacotherapy signal in HFpEF. If false positive (2/12 subgroup tests), may mislead clinical practice.
- Renal protection (HR 0.50) is the strongest secondary endpoint — stronger than the primary outcome direction — yet is rarely highlighted in the HFpEF narrative.
- KCCQ decline in both arms (sacubitril/valsartan −1.6; valsartan −2.6) — both groups deteriorated symptomatically; difference of 1.0 point is NS. Contrast with DELIVER (KCCQ win ratio positive). [[contradictions]] — symptom measurement sensitivity.
