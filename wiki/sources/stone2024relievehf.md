# RELIEVE-HF Trial (Stone 2024)

> Randomized, double-blind, placebo-procedure-controlled trial of the V-Wave transcatheter interatrial shunt in symptomatic heart failure of any LVEF (N=508). The device was safe and the primary effectiveness endpoint was neutral overall, but outcomes diverged sharply by baseline LVEF: patients with **reduced** LVEF (≤40%) had fewer cardiovascular events with shunt treatment, while patients with **preserved** LVEF (>40%) — the HFpEF-relevant stratum — had *more* adverse cardiovascular events, including higher all-cause mortality (16.4% vs. 5.2%, HR 3.24) and more HF hospitalisations, with shunt treatment.

## Key Findings

- **Primary safety endpoint met:** 0/250 shunt-group events (upper 97.5% CI 1.5%; P<0.0001 for noninferiority vs. an 11% performance goal)
- **Primary effectiveness endpoint neutral in the full population:** win ratio 0.86 (95% CI 0.61–1.22, P=0.20)
- **Sharp effect-modification by baseline LVEF (P-interaction=0.03 for the primary win-ratio endpoint):**
  - **Reduced LVEF (≤40%, n=206):** win ratio 1.40 (95% CI 0.80–2.46, P=0.23) — directionally favourable; the composite of death/transplant/LVAD/HFH annualised rate was 49.0% (shunt) vs. 88.6% (placebo), RR 0.55 (95% CI 0.42–0.73, P<0.0001); HFH rate 26.0% vs. 52.0%/yr, HR 0.52 (0.31–0.86, P=0.01)
  - **Preserved LVEF (>40%, n=302 — the HFpEF-relevant stratum):** win ratio 0.61 (95% CI 0.39–0.98, P=0.009) — **significantly worse with shunt treatment.** Composite CV-event annualised rate 60.2% (shunt) vs. 35.9% (placebo), RR 1.68 (95% CI 1.29–2.19, P=0.0001). **All-cause death at 2 years: 16.4% (shunt) vs. 5.2% (placebo), HR 3.24 (95% CI 1.38–7.59, P=0.004).** HF hospitalisation rate 37.0% vs. 19.0%/yr, HR 2.05 (95% CI 1.35–3.10, P=0.0008)
  - Outcomes tracked continuously by baseline LVEF as a continuous variable: shunt effect transitioned from harm to benefit at approximately LVEF 40%
- **No between-group difference in quality of life (KCCQ-OSS) change during follow-up, in the whole population or in either LVEF stratum** — the divergent clinical-event findings did not correspond to a divergent symptom/QoL signal
- **The trial was originally powered at N=400 but expanded to ~500 (with FDA agreement) specifically to allow separate assessment of the pre-specified reduced- and preserved-LVEF strata** — the stratified analyses, while pre-specified, remain formally hypothesis-generating (not powered to a primary comparison)

## Methods (brief)

Randomised 1:1, stratified by baseline LVEF (≤40% vs. >40%, echocardiographic core lab) and by site. Blinded via sham transseptal catheterisation with identical sedation/procedure script for controls (no device placement). Post-procedure: aspirin 75–100mg + masked clopidogrel/placebo for 6 months (unless already anticoagulated, in which case antiplatelet only). Primary effectiveness analysis used the unmatched win ratio (Finkelstein-Schoenfeld method) across the 5-component hierarchy (death > transplant/LVAD > HFH > outpatient worsening HF > ΔKCCQ-OSS). Protocol amended May 2020 to substitute ΔKCCQ-OSS for Δ6MWT as the fifth hierarchy component (COVID-19 access disruption). Intention-to-treat primary analysis; per-protocol sensitivity analysis concordant.

## Limitations

The LVEF-stratified analyses, though pre-specified, were not independently powered — the trial's overall sample size was set for the full-population primary endpoint, and the strikingly divergent stratum results are explicitly labelled hypothesis-generating by the authors, not confirmatory. Mechanism for preserved-LVEF harm is not established by this trial (a companion mechanistic paper, Zile et al. 2026 JACC Cardiovasc Imaging, addresses this — not yet ingested). Device-specific (V-Wave/Ventura) findings may not generalise to other interatrial shunt devices (e.g. Corvia's device tested in [[reduce-lap-hf-ii]]), which use different orifice sizes and patient-selection criteria (notably, REDUCE LAP-HF II required elevated invasively-measured exercise PCWP for enrolment; RELIEVE-HF did not). Open-label antiplatelet/anticoagulation management differed by randomisation arm in a small minority of patients per site discretion.

## Contradictions
**HFpEF-specific harm from interatrial shunt therapy** is a striking counterpoint to the wiki's other positive HFpEF device-trial evidence. All-cause mortality more than tripled (HR 3.24) in the shunt arm among preserved-LVEF patients, with no offsetting QoL benefit. This does not contradict REDUCE LAP-HF II (a different device, different — invasively-enriched — population), but it is a direct caution against generalising "device-based preload/LA-pressure reduction is beneficial in HFpEF" as a class statement.
