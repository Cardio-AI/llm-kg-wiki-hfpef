# CAPACITY-HFpEF

> Praliciguat (sGC stimulator) did not improve peak VO₂ (P=0.37); KCCQ overall score significantly worse on active drug (P=0.007); 6-MWT responders fewer on praliciguat (37% vs. 59%, P=0.007); significant harm signal in patients ≥70y (P interaction=0.004) — the primary enrichment target group; confirms cGMP pathway negative in metabolic-phenotype HFpEF alongside simultaneous VITALITY-HFpEF publication.

**File:** `raw/2020-JAMA-Udelson-CAPACITY-HFpEF_study.pdf` · **Authors:** Udelson JE, Lewis GD, Shah SJ et al. · **Year:** 2020 · **Journal:** JAMA 324(15):1522–1531  
**Study type:** RCT (phase 2) · **N:** 196 randomised; primary analysis n=143 (65 vs. 78) · **Population:** HFpEF, LVEF ≥40%, metabolic phenotype enrichment (≥2 of: DM, HTN, obesity, age ≥70)  
**Follow-up:** 12 weeks · **Intervention:** Praliciguat 40 mg/day vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Change in peak VO₂ from baseline to week 12  
**NCT:** NCT03254485 · **Enrollment:** Nov 15, 2017–Apr 30, 2019 · **Sites:** 59 centres, US and Canada only  
**Sponsor:** Cycllerion Therapeutics (sponsor sat on publications committee but did not have veto rights)

## Key Findings

- Peak VO₂ (primary): adjusted difference **−0.30 mL/kg/min** [95% CI −0.95 to 0.35], **P=0.37** — neutral
- **KCCQ overall summary score:** change −7.2 [95% CI −12.3 to −2.0], **P=0.007** — significantly **worse on praliciguat**
- KCCQ responders (≥5-point improvement): 41% praliciguat vs. 63% placebo; OR 0.43 [0.22–0.84]; **P=0.01**
- 6-MWT: adjusted difference −16.7m [−47.4 to 13.9]; P=0.28 — neutral; placebo improved +27.0m vs. +6.4m praliciguat
- 6-MWT responders (≥15m): 37% vs. 59%; OR 0.39 [0.19–0.77]; **P=0.007** — significantly fewer on active drug
- VE/VCO₂ slope: P=0.65 — neutral
- CPET responders (≥1.5 mL/kg/min): 20.0% vs. 21.8%; OR 0.9 (0.4–2.1); P=0.82 — neutral
- NT-proBNP change: +6.9 ng/L [−12.0 to 29.9]; P=0.50 — no biomarker effect
- Echocardiography: no significant differences in any structural or functional parameter
- **Age interaction (Figure 2):** patients ≥70y — praliciguat treatment difference **−1.22 [−2.44 to −0.46]** (harm); patients <70y — +0.46 [−0.39 to 1.32] (trend to benefit); **P interaction=0.004**
- **Sex interaction:** men −0.89 [−1.74 to −0.04] (harm); women +0.49 [−0.51 to 1.48] (neutral/trend); P interaction=0.04

## Methods

**Design:** Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2 trial. Initially 1:1:1:1 ratio (praliciguat 40, 20, 10 mg vs. placebo); protocol amended June 27, 2018 to refocus on 40 mg vs. placebo 1:1 (10 and 20 mg arms discontinued; data not included in efficacy analysis). Stratified by AF status (yes/no) and baseline peak VO₂ (<60% vs. ≥60% of normal). AF limited to <20% of population. ITT and primary analytic populations defined separately.

**Dose:** Weeks 1–2: twice daily; weeks 3–12: once daily at 40 mg. Oral tablets.

**Eligibility:**
- Age ≥45, LVEF ≥40%
- Impaired peak VO₂ (<80% age- and sex-adjusted normal; RER ≥1.0 on CPET)
- NYHA II–IV symptoms
- ≥1 objective HF evidence criterion: HF hospitalisation within 12 months; NT-proBNP >300 pg/mL or BNP ≥100 pg/mL within 6 months; echo evidence (LV hypertrophy, LA enlargement, E/e' ≥15); elevated PCWP ≥15 mmHg rest or ≥25 mmHg exercise, or LVEDP ≥15 mmHg rest
- **Metabolic enrichment:** ≥2 of 4 NO-deficiency conditions: type 2 diabetes, hypertension, obesity (BMI ≥30), age ≥70
- AF limited: persistent/permanent AF capped at ~20% of sample

**Primary analytic population:** Patients completing ≥8 weeks dosing + ≥1 evaluable postbaseline assessment + no major protocol deviation: 40-mg group n=65, placebo n=78 (n=143 total).

**Statistical power:** 90% power to detect 1.3 mL/kg/min difference in peak VO₂ (observed favourable effects in obese HFpEF exercise trials); SD 2.5 mL/kg/min; 10% attrition; N=184 target. ANCOVA primary analysis.

**Age interaction (P=0.004) interpretation:** Praliciguat reduced SBP −6.3 mmHg. In older patients with HFpEF who may be preload-dependent, this BP reduction may have been hemodynamically detrimental. The enrichment criterion (age ≥70) targeted for mechanistic benefit was the very group harmed — suggests sGC-cGMP pathway is not the primary impairment driver, or that BP-lowering confounds benefit.

## Limitations

- Phase 2 trial; N=143 primary analysis — insufficient power for clinical outcomes; designed for signal detection only
- Primary analytic population only n=65 vs. n=78 — high attrition from randomised N=196; differential attrition (praliciguat worse compliance)
- Median NT-proBNP ~244 pg/mL; 57% had ≤300 pg/mL — well below PARAGON-HF (≥300 SR) and DELIVER (≥300 SR) entry thresholds; significant proportion may not have confirmed HFpEF by modern NP criteria
- Loop diuretic use only 19% — much lower than all other major HFpEF RCTs; selects for milder congestion phenotype
- 12-week duration — too short to detect structural remodelling benefits; sGC stimulation may require longer to affect myocardial fibrosis
- Large placebo responder effect on 6-MWT (+27.0m) may reflect test-retest learning effect; diminishes drug detection sensitivity
- KCCQ worsening (P=0.007): may reflect drug tolerability issues (dizziness, hypotension) rather than true HF worsening; but PR symptoms are distinct from KCCQ domains
- Age interaction (P=0.004) is exploratory; multiple subgroup testing; but mechanistically plausible given BP lowering effect
- US/Canada only — limits generalisability; VITALITY-HFpEF had similar geographic restriction
- Protocol amendment mid-trial (dropping 20mg/10mg arms) — reduces dose-response characterisation; original design had broader dose ranging

## Contradictions
- KCCQ worse on active drug (P=0.007) despite mechanistic enrichment for NO-deficiency phenotype. The drug targeted what was hypothesised as the primary impairment mechanism, yet made symptoms significantly worse. Metabolic phenotype ≠ NO-deficient phenotype, or BP lowering drove harm. See [[contradictions]].
- Age interaction: patients ≥70 harmed (P interaction=0.004), yet age ≥70 was one of the 4 enrichment criteria hypothesised to select for NO-deficiency phenotype responders. The enrichment strategy backfired in the most enriched subgroup.
- 6-MWT: placebo improved +27m, praliciguat only +6.4m (P=0.28). Massive placebo responder effect means peak VO₂ direction (both near 0) may be the more reliable measure; however KCCQ favoured placebo directionally and significantly.
- Companion VITALITY-HFpEF (vericiguat, same drug class, same day): both neutral on primary, both no significant QoL benefit. Together close the sGC stimulator class for HFpEF. Yet VICTORIA (vericiguat in HFrEF) was positive — EF-specific biology or HFpEF patient selection problem. See [[contradictions]].
