# EMPEROR-Reduced

> Empagliflozin 10 mg/day reduced CV death + HF hospitalization by ~25% in HFrEF regardless of diabetes status, and slowed eGFR decline; part of the evidence base establishing SGLT2i as a fourth HFrEF pillar.

**File:** `raw/[not yet ingested]` · **Authors:** Packer M et al. · **Year:** 2020 · **Journal:** NEJM  
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** ~3,730 · **Population:** HFrEF with LVEF ≤40%, NYHA II–IV, eGFR ≥20  
**Follow-up:** ~16 months · **Intervention:** Empagliflozin 10 mg/day vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** CV death + HF hospitalization

---

## Key Findings

- Empagliflozin reduced primary composite by **~25% relative risk reduction**
- Benefit consistent regardless of diabetes status
- Significant slowing of eGFR decline (renal protection)
- Meta-analysis with DAPA-HF: no heterogeneity in CV mortality benefit

## Methods (brief)

[Expand on paper ingest]

Included LVEF ≤40% but with lower eGFR threshold (≥20) than DAPA-HF (≥25). Background HFrEF therapy required.
