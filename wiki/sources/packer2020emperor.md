# EMPEROR-Reduced

> Empagliflozin 10 mg/day reduced CV death + HF hospitalisation by 25% (HR 0.75 [0.65–0.86] P<0.001) in HFrEF regardless of diabetes status (DM HR 0.72; non-DM HR 0.78); eGFR decline slowed by +1.73 mL/min/1.73m²/yr (P<0.001) with structural renal protection confirmed by discontinuation analysis; composite renal outcome HR 0.50 (0.32–0.77); CV death HR 0.92 NS; NNT=19; enriched for severe systolic dysfunction (71.8% LVEF ≤30%, median NT-proBNP 1887 pg/mL); LVEF >30% subgroup HR 0.99 — possible EF-gradient even within HFrEF spectrum.

**Full citation:**
Packer M, Anker SD, Butler J, et al.; EMPEROR-Reduced Trial Investigators. Cardiovascular and Renal Outcomes with Empagliflozin in Heart Failure. *N Engl J Med.* 2020;383(15):1413–1424. doi:[10.1056/NEJMoa2022190](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2022190)
**Study type:** RCT (placebo-controlled) · **N:** 3,730 randomised (empagliflozin 1,863; placebo 1,867) · **Population:** HFrEF, LVEF ≤40%, NYHA II–IV, eGFR ≥20, enriched for low LVEF + high NT-proBNP  
**Follow-up:** Median 16 months (data cutoff April 29, 2020) · **Intervention:** Empagliflozin 10 mg once daily vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** CV death or hospitalisation for worsening HF (time to first event)  
**NCT:** NCT03057977 · **Enrollment:** April 2017–November 2019 · **Sites:** 520 centres, 20 countries  
**Sponsors:** Boehringer Ingelheim and Eli Lilly (co-funded; academic executive committee; independent statistician verified analyses)

## Key Findings

- Primary (CV death + HF hosp): HR **0.75 (0.65–0.86)**, **P<0.001**; 361 (19.4%) vs 462 (24.7%); NNT=19
- First HF hospitalisation: HR **0.69 (0.59–0.81)** — 31% relative reduction
- Total HF hosp (first + recurrent): HR **0.70 (0.58–0.85)**, **P<0.001**; 388 vs 553 events
- CV death: HR **0.92 (0.75–1.12)** — NS
- All-cause death: HR **0.92 (0.77–1.10)** — NS
- eGFR slope (primary renal secondary): −0.55 vs −2.28 mL/min/1.73m²/yr; between-group difference **+1.73 (1.10–2.37)** mL/min/1.73m²/yr, **P<0.001**
- Composite renal outcome: HR **0.50 (0.32–0.77)** — significant 50% reduction
- KCCQ at 52 weeks: +5.8±0.4 (empa) vs +4.1±0.4 (placebo); difference **+1.7 (0.5–3.0)**
- Non-diabetics (50% of cohort): HR **0.78 (0.64–0.97)** — consistent with DM HR 0.72
- LVEF >30% subgroup: HR **0.99 (0.76–1.31)** — possible EF-dependent attenuation within HFrEF
- Sacubitril-valsartan users (~19%): HR **0.64 (0.45–0.89)** — consistent/possibly enhanced

## Methods

**Design:** Double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled, event-driven, multicenter RCT. ITT primary analysis. Clinical events committee adjudicated all outcomes blinded. One prespecified interim analysis (one-sided alpha ~0.001 stopping boundary). Two hierarchically tested secondary outcomes (total HF hosp recurrent; then eGFR slope at one-sided alpha 0.0496).

**Enrichment:** To enrich for severe HF, patients with LVEF 31–40% required prior HF hospitalisation within 12 months OR NT-proBNP ≥1000 pg/mL; patients with LVEF 36–40% required NT-proBNP ≥2500 pg/mL (doubled in AF). Patients with LVEF ≤30% had lower NT-proBNP thresholds (≥600 pg/mL SR). This enrichment selectively concentrated the most severely ill HFrEF patients — contrast with DAPA-HF's broader enrolment.

**Dose:** Empagliflozin 10 mg once daily throughout (same dose as EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER).

**Eligibility:**
- Age ≥18, LVEF ≤40%, NYHA II–IV
- eGFR ≥20 mL/min/1.73m² (lower threshold than DAPA-HF ≥25)
- NT-proBNP enrichment thresholds as above
- On stable recommended HF therapy (diuretics, RAASi, beta-blocker; MRA when indicated)

**Statistical power:** 90% power to detect 20% RRR; 841 primary outcome events needed; annual event rate ~15% in placebo assumed; one interim analysis.

### Safety

- **Genital tract infection** (uncomplicated): more frequent in empagliflozin — only safety signal
- Hypoglycaemia, lower limb amputation, bone fracture: **no between-group difference**
- Hypotension, volume depletion, renal dysfunction, bradycardia, hyperkalemia: **no difference**
- Drug discontinuation (not death): 303 (16.3%) empagliflozin vs 335 (18.0%) placebo

**Discontinuation eGFR analysis (unique):** In 966 patients with measurements 23–45 days after stopping both study drugs, eGFR declined −0.93 (−1.97 to 0.11) mL/min/1.73m²/yr (empagliflozin) vs −4.21 (−5.26 to −3.17) mL/min/1.73m²/yr (placebo). Confirms the renal benefit is structural — persists after SGLT2 haemodynamic effect withdrawal — not just tubuloglomerular feedback mediated.

## Limitations

- Median follow-up 16 months — shorter than DAPA-HF (18.2 mo) and far shorter than PARAGON-HF or I-PRESERVE; CV death HR 0.92 NS likely underpowered for mortality (NNT for CV death would require ~3 years)
- Enrichment criteria concentrated LVEF ≤30% and high NT-proBNP — less generalisable to LVEF 31–40% range; LVEF >30% subgroup HR 0.99 raises EF-gradient question
- Higher sacubitril-valsartan use (~19%) than DAPA-HF (10.5%) — more optimised background; patients not on sacubitril-valsartan had consistent HR 0.77
- 23.5% female — same limitation as DAPA-HF; female HR 0.59 appears more favourable than male 0.80 but small numbers and no formal interaction
- eGFR threshold ≥20 (vs DAPA-HF ≥25) — extends to more advanced CKD but limits comparison; eGFR <60 subgroup HR 0.83 (0.69–1.00) at border of significance
- COVID-19 operational impact: 42 patients lost to follow-up, 21 unknown vital status — minor; sensitivity analyses showed no material effect

## HFpEF Wiki Relevance

EMPEROR-Reduced is an HFrEF trial foundational to this wiki for four reasons:

1. **EMPEROR programme context:** EMPEROR-Reduced (LVEF ≤40%) and EMPEROR-Preserved ([[anker2021emperor]], LVEF ≥40%) share identical design, drug, dose, and enrichment philosophy. Direct comparison confirms class effect across EF spectrum. The eGFR renal finding is reproduced in EMPEROR-Preserved (HR 0.50).

2. **LVEF >30% EF-gradient:** Subgroup HR 0.99 (0.76–1.31) in LVEF >30% patients (vs 0.65–0.70 in LVEF ≤30%) hints at a continuous EF-dependent benefit even within the HFrEF range — consistent with PARADIGM-HF + PARAGON-HF pooled analysis showing benefit declining with rising LVEF, and EMPEROR-Preserved being positive in HFpEF.

3. **Renal mechanism proved beyond haemodynamics:** Discontinuation analysis (eGFR −0.93 vs −4.21 after drug stop) confirms structural nephroprotection. Same finding repeated in DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved — mechanistically important for HFpEF with concurrent CKD.

4. **Hematocrit +2.36%:** Erythropoiesis signal matched across DAPA-HF (+2.41%), DELIVER, EMPEROR-Preserved — candidate mechanism for exercise capacity improvement downstream.

## Contradictions
- CV death HR 0.92 NS (EMPEROR-Reduced) vs HR 0.82 NS (DAPA-HF): both neutral individually; pooled analyses show significant CV death reduction. Underpowered individually — short follow-up (16 months). Not a true contradiction; both directionally consistent and numerically similar to PARADIGM-HF's 0.80 VS enalapril.
- LVEF >30% subgroup HR 0.99: possible attenuation even within HFrEF vs consistent benefit in LVEF ≤30%. If real, implies the SGLT2i benefit gradient begins within HFrEF, not just at the HFrEF/HFpEF boundary. However, this is a post-hoc subgroup in a trial not powered for this distinction. See [[contradictions]].
- EMPEROR-Reduced sacubitril-valsartan HR 0.64 vs non-users 0.77: numerically more benefit on background ARNI. Same direction in DAPA-HF (0.75 on ARNi vs 0.74 without). Not a contradiction but a signal worth tracking: if neprilysin inhibition + SGLT2 inhibition are synergistic, this has implications for HFpEF combination therapy ([[solomon2019paragon]] + [[solomon2022deliver]]).
