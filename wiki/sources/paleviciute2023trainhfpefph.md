# Paleviciuté 2023 — TRAIN-HFpEF-PH Design Paper

> Study protocol for TRAIN-HFpEF-PH (NCT05464238), a randomised controlled multicentre trial testing whether a standardised low-intensity exercise and respiratory rehabilitation programme is safe and improves exercise capacity, quality of life, haemodynamics, and diastolic function in patients with invasively confirmed pulmonary hypertension associated with HFpEF (HFpEF-PH) — the first randomised exercise trial designed specifically for this population.

**Full citation:**
Paleviciuté E, Čelutkienė J, Šimbelytė T, Gumbienė L, Jurevičienė E, Zakarkaitė D, Česna S, Eichstaedt CA, Benjamin N, Grünig E. Safety and effectiveness of standardized exercise training in patients with pulmonary hypertension associated with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (TRAIN-HFpEF-PH): study protocol for a randomized controlled multicenter trial. *Trials.* 2023;24:281. doi:[10.1186/s13063-023-07297-x](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-023-07297-x)

## Summary

Design paper for TRAIN-HFpEF-PH (NCT05464238), registered 2022-07-19, sponsored by Heidelberg University (PI Ekkehard Grünig; co-investigator/corresponding author Eglė Paleviciuté). A multicentre RCT across German and Lithuanian pulmonary hypertension centres testing a standardised low-intensity exercise and respiratory rehabilitation programme — already validated for safety/efficacy in PAH and CTEPH — in patients with HFpEF-PH specifically, a population no prior exercise trial has focused on. (source: Paleviciute2023TRAINHFpEFPH)

## Trial Design Summary

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **NCT** | NCT05464238; registered 2022-07-19 |
| **Sponsor / sites** | Heidelberg University; Thoraxklinik Heidelberg (core/coordinating centre); Germany + Lithuania |
| **Design** | Randomised (1:1, block randomisation), controlled, parallel-group with crossover of the control arm (waiting-group design); single-blind (outcomes assessor blinded) |
| **Population** | WHO/NYHA FC II–IV; PH confirmed by RHC (mPAP >20 mmHg rest; mPAWP ≥15 mmHg or LVEDP ≥16 mmHg at rest and/or PAWP ≥25 mmHg during exercise/PAWP >18 mmHg after fluid challenge); LVEF ≥50%; receiving optimised, ≥1-month-stable diuretic therapy |
| **Exclusion** | Pre-capillary PH (groups I, III, IV, V per ESC/ERS classification); severe valvular disease; walking disability; concurrent interventional study; severe lung disease (FEV1/FVC <0.5, TLC <60% predicted); active myocarditis, unstable angina, exercise-induced ventricular arrhythmias; haemoglobin <75% LLN; systolic BP <85 mmHg; pregnancy/lactation |
| **Intervention** | Standardised low-intensity rehabilitation: interval ergometer training + dumbbell training + respiratory therapy + mental/guided-walk training; 3-week in-hospital phase (intensity 40–50%→60–80% peak HR) + 12-week home continuation |
| **Control** | Standard treatment/daily routine for 15 weeks, then offered the training programme (waiting-group crossover) |
| **Target N** | 90 (45 per arm; powered for a 35 m 6MWD difference, SD 50 m, 85% power, α 0.05, 15% dropout allowance) |
| **Primary endpoint** | Change in 6-minute walk distance (6MWD), baseline → 15 weeks |
| **Secondary endpoints** | WHO functional class, SF-36 QoL, HADS anxiety/depression, Borg dyspnoea scale, clinical worsening, echocardiography (LVEF, RV function, sPAP, TAPSE), NT-proBNP, CPET parameters; optional invasive RHC and cardiac MRI at rest/exercise |
| **Status at publication** | Not yet recruiting (protocol v1.0, 2019-12-05); estimated recruitment start 2023 Q1; estimated primary completion 2025 Q4; total planned study duration 3 years (2023–2025) |
| **Funding** | No specific grant (public/commercial/not-for-profit) reported |

## Rationale

Pulmonary hypertension associated with left heart disease (LHD-PH) affects an estimated 52–83% of HFpEF patients by echocardiographic criteria, and 57.4% by invasive haemodynamic assessment in the cited cohort data (355/618 HFpEF patients had PH; 75/618 had combined pre-/post-capillary PH). No prior randomised exercise trial has focused specifically on HFpEF-PH — existing PH exercise-training evidence comes from PAH/CTEPH trials that explicitly excluded LHD-PH (pathognomonic for HFpEF-PH), and HFpEF exercise trials generally have not focused on right-heart/pulmonary-circulation involvement. Treatment options for HFpEF-PH are non-specific and limited (SGLT2i is the one pharmacological exception cited); this protocol positions structured exercise/respiratory rehabilitation as a candidate non-pharmacological intervention specifically for this phenotype.

## Clinical Significance

- Fills a population-specific gap: HFpEF-PH patients differ from HFpEF-without-PH in haemodynamics and exercise intolerance, so extrapolating general HFpEF or PAH/CTEPH exercise evidence to this subgroup is not established
- Primary endpoint (6MWD) and instrument battery (SF-36, HADS, CPET, optional invasive RHC/CMR) allow direct comparison against the broader HFpEF exercise-trial literature ([[optimex-clin]], [[rehab-hf]], [[ex-dhf]])
- Waiting-group crossover design means every enrolled patient eventually receives the intervention — ethically favourable but limits the ability to assess durability of control-arm untreated natural history beyond 15 weeks
