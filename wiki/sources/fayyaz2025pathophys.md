# HFpEF Pathophysiology from Human Cardiac Tissue (Nat Rev Cardiol 2025)

> Systematic review of 56 human myocardial tissue studies in HFpEF (Nat Rev Cardiol 2025;22:90–104): identifies 8 major pathological mechanisms — fibrosis, cardiomyocyte hypertrophy, microvascular rarefaction, diastolic dysfunction (titin/Ca²⁺), metabolic derangements (↓ATP/mitochondria), inflammation/oxidative stress, impaired cGMP-PKG signalling, and ER stress/DNA damage — with pronounced inter-study heterogeneity confirming HFpEF as a pathophysiologically diverse syndrome requiring endotype-targeted therapy.

## Core Arguments

**Type:** Scoping/systematic review of human myocardial tissue studies. **56 studies** published 2004–2023. Tissue sources: endomyocardial biopsy (EMB, 54%), surgical LV biopsy (39%), autopsy/donor hearts (7%). Mean cohort n≈30 patients/study. HFpEF LVEF criteria: 68% used ≥50%, 20% used >40%. Comparators: non-failing donors (38%), HFrEF (32%), normal function (11%).

**8 key pathophysiological pathways:**

**1. Myocardial fibrosis — most consistent finding**
- Diffuse interstitial, perivascular, and replacement collagen deposition
- Variable severity; amplified by obesity, T2DM, hypertension
- CMR-ECV and native T1 correlate with histological fibrosis density ([[ipek2024cmr]], [[lange2024cmr]])
- Proteomics: 599 differentially expressed proteins; ECM and inflammatory proteins upregulated

**2. Cardiomyocyte hypertrophy**
- Predominantly concentric pattern; variable correlation with LV mass and diastolic stiffness
- Comorbidity-dependent: hypertension-driven vs obesity-driven hypertrophy may differ mechanistically

**3. Microvascular rarefaction**
- Reduced capillary density vs non-failing controls
- Associated with impaired coronary flow reserve (CFR) and exercise intolerance
- Endothelial ICAM/VCAM/E-selectin upregulation; reduced NO bioavailability

**4. Diastolic dysfunction — molecular mechanisms**
- **Titin**: elevated passive stiffness (oxidative modifications, N2B isoform upregulation, hypophosphorylation)
- **T-tubule disruption**: impaired sarcoplasmic reticulum Ca²⁺ handling → slowed relaxation
- **SERCA2a dysfunction**: reduced Ca²⁺ reuptake rate → impaired lusitropy

**5. Metabolic derangements**
- Reduced mitochondrial ATP synthesis and oxidative phosphorylation capacity
- Shift toward glycolysis (less ATP-efficient); impaired β-oxidation
- NAD⁺ deficiency; reduced OGDH (oxoglutarate dehydrogenase) expression
- Elevated myocardial triglycerides (correlates with ¹H-MRS findings, [[ipek2024cmr]])
- Transcriptomics (n=2 studies): 743 differentially expressed genes; metabolic genes downregulated

**6. Inflammation and oxidative stress**
- Elevated circulating and myocardial cytokines (IL-1, IL-6, TNF-α)
- Varied macrophage/T-cell infiltration — no single consistent inflammatory signature
- Pathophysiological heterogeneity argues against universal inflammatory phenotype; CRP-independent semaglutide benefit consistent with this ([[verma2024inflammation]])

**7. Impaired cGMP-PKG signalling (strong mechanistic consensus)**
- Downregulation of sGC pathway; reduced myocardial cGMP concentrations
- Loss of PKG-mediated anti-fibrotic, anti-stiffness, and calcium-sensitising effects
- SGLT2i benefit may partly operate via cGMP-PKG enhancement
- Note: vericiguat (sGC stimulator) showed minimal clinical benefit in HFpEF (SOCRATES-PRESERVED)

**8. ER stress and DNA damage**
- Elevated ER stress markers (IRE1, ATF4, CHOP) — unfolded protein response activation
- Increased DNA damage markers (phosphorylated H2AX, 53BP1); p53/CHK2 activation
- Potential cellular senescence pathway; NAD⁺ depletion link

**Comorbidity-dependent heterogeneity:** Obesity, T2DM, and hypertension each drive distinct molecular signatures — a major reason why no single drug has demonstrated universal HFpEF benefit. Different patients have different dominant pathways.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

The most comprehensive synthesis of HFpEF tissue pathophysiology to date. The 8-pathway framework is the mechanistic underpinning for why pharmacological monotherapy has failed: TOPCAT, CHARM-Preserved, I-PRESERVE, PARAGON-HF, RELAX, NEAT-HFpEF, CAPACITY all target single mechanisms in a heterogeneous syndrome. Each pathway represents a potential endotype-specific target:
- Fibrosis → MRAs (spironolactone), TGF-β inhibitors
- Energy deficit → SGLT2i, NAD⁺ precursors
- Titin/calcium → PKG activators, novel approaches
- Inflammation → anti-IL-1, anti-IL-6 (in inflammatory endotype)
- Microvascular → PDE5 inhibitors, NO donors (RELAX failed — possibly in wrong phenotype)
- cGMP-PKG → vericiguat, riociguat (clinical evidence weak so far)

This positions precision phenotyping (see [[hfpef-phenotype-profiling]], [[akerman2025ai]]) as the prerequisite for future trial success.
