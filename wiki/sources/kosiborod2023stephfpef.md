# STEP-HFpEF

> Semaglutide 2.4 mg improved HF symptoms (KCCQ-CSS diff +7.8 pts, P<0.001) and body weight (diff −10.7 pp, P<0.001) in obese HFpEF without T2DM — the first large GLP-1RA RCT in HFpEF, with consistent improvements in 6MWD, CRP, and a hierarchical composite.

**Full citation:**
Kosiborod MN, Abildstrom SZ, Borlaug BA, et al.; STEP-HFpEF Trial Committees and Investigators. Semaglutide in Patients with Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction and Obesity. *N Engl J Med.* 2023;389(12):1069–1084. doi:[10.1056/NEJMoa2306963](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2306963)
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** 529 (263 semaglutide, 266 placebo) · **Population:** HFpEF LVEF ≥45%, BMI ≥30 kg/m², no T2DM (HbA1c <6.5%), NYHA II–IV · **Duration:** 52 weeks + 5-week follow-up  
**Intervention:** Semaglutide 2.4 mg SC once weekly (dose escalation: 0.25 mg → 2.4 mg over 16 weeks) vs. placebo  
**Dual primary outcomes:** (1) KCCQ-CSS change at 52 weeks; (2) body weight % change at 52 weeks  
**NCT:** NCT04788511 · **Enrollment:** March 2021–March 2022 · **Sites:** 96 centres, 13 countries · **Funding:** Novo Nordisk

---

## Key Findings

Both dual primary endpoints met:
- **KCCQ-CSS:** sema +16.6 pts vs. placebo +8.7 pts; estimated difference **+7.8 pts** (95% CI 4.8–10.9; P<0.001)
- **Body weight:** sema −13.3% vs. placebo −2.6%; estimated difference **−10.7 pp** (95% CI −11.9 to −9.4; P<0.001)

All confirmatory secondary endpoints met:
- **6MWD:** diff +20.3m (95% CI 8.6–32.1; P<0.001) — sema +21.5m vs. placebo +1.2m
- **CRP:** treatment ratio 0.61 (95% CI 0.51–0.72; P<0.001) — sema −43.5% vs. placebo −7.3%
- **Hierarchical composite (win ratio):** 1.72 (95% CI 1.37–2.15; P<0.001) — includes CV death, HF events, KCCQ-CSS, 6MWD

Exploratory HF events (not powered): 1 sema vs. 12 placebo; HR 0.08 (95% CI 0.00–0.42).

## Methods (brief)

Phase 3 double-blind placebo-controlled RCT. Eligibility: LVEF ≥45%, BMI ≥30 kg/m², NYHA II–IV, KCCQ-CSS <90, 6MWD ≥100m, elevated filling pressures confirmed by one of: (a) invasive haemodynamics, (b) NT-proBNP + echocardiographic criteria, or (c) prior HF hospitalization within 12 months. Key exclusion: HbA1c ≥6.5% (i.e., no T2DM — the DM population was enrolled in the companion STEP-HFpEF DM trial, NCT04916470). Dose escalation: 0.25 mg × 4 wks → 0.5 mg × 4 wks → 1.0 mg × 4 wks → 1.7 mg × 4 wks → 2.4 mg (target, weeks 17–52). Dual primary endpoint structure with hierarchical confirmatory secondary testing at controlled α.

## Results

**Baseline characteristics (key):** Age 69 years, 56.1% female, 95.8% White, LVEF 57%, BMI 37.0 kg/m², KCCQ-CSS 58.9, 6MWD 320m, NT-proBNP 450.8 pg/mL, CRP 3.8 mg/L, 52.0% AF, 81.9% hypertension, 3.6% on SGLT2i at baseline.

**Dual primary:**
| Endpoint | Sema | Placebo | Diff (95% CI) | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KCCQ-CSS (pts) | +16.6 | +8.7 | +7.8 (4.8–10.9) | <0.001 |
| Body weight (%) | −13.3% | −2.6% | −10.7 pp (−11.9 to −9.4) | <0.001 |

**Confirmatory secondary:**
| Endpoint | Diff | 95% CI | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6MWD (m) | +20.3 | 8.6–32.1 | <0.001 |
| CRP ratio | 0.61 | 0.51–0.72 | <0.001 |
| Win ratio (hierarchical composite) | 1.72 | 1.37–2.15 | <0.001 |

**Safety:** SAEs 13.3% sema vs. 26.7% placebo. Cardiac disorders: 7 (2.7%) vs. 30 (11.3%). GI events were the primary cause of semaglutide discontinuation.

## Limitations

- Not powered for CV mortality or HF hospitalisation (hard outcomes); exploratory HR 0.08 is based on 1 vs. 12 events
- T2DM excluded — ~35% of HFpEF has T2DM (addressed by STEP-HFpEF DM)
- 95.8% White population limits generalisability
- Low baseline SGLT2i use (3.6%) — underrepresents contemporary background therapy
- Lean mass loss alongside fat mass reduction: sarcopenic obesity concern not addressed in primary analysis
- KCCQ and 6MWD are surrogates/PROs — whether symptom improvement translates to outcomes benefit is uncertain pending SUMMIT

## Contradictions

Symptomatic benefit (KCCQ +7.8 pts, 6MWD +20.3m, win ratio 1.72) without powered hard-outcome evidence — same pattern as CAPACITY-HFpEF (surrogate positive) but with far larger and more consistent effect. Whether the functional improvement translates to reduced CV mortality/hospitalisations remains to be shown by SUMMIT. Additionally, low SGLT2i background use (3.6%) leaves open the question of additive vs. redundant benefit with SGLT2i in contemporary practice. See [[contradictions]].
