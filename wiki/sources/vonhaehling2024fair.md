# von Haehling 2024 — FAIR-HFpEF Trial: IV Iron in HFpEF with Iron Deficiency

> FAIR-HFpEF, the first RCT of ferric carboxymaltose (FCM) in HFpEF with iron deficiency, showed improved 6-minute walk distance at 24 weeks (+49m vs. −8m; difference 49m, 95% CI 5–93; P=0.029) but was stopped early after enrolling only 40 patients; secondary QoL endpoints were not significantly different.

**Year:** 2024 · **Journal:** Eur Heart J 2024;45:3789–3800 · **DOI:** 10.1093/eurheartj/ehae479  
**Study type:** Randomised double-blind placebo-controlled trial · **NCT:** NCT03074591  
**N:** 74 screened; 40 randomised (18 FCM, 22 placebo); 39 analysed (modified ITT)  
**Sites:** 6 German centres  
**Population:** Chronic HFpEF (LVEF ≥45%), NYHA II–III, iron-deficient (ferritin <100 ng/mL or ferritin 100–299 + TSAT <20%), 6MWT <450m; Hb 9.0–14.0 g/dL; median age 80y, 62% women

## Key Findings

### Primary Endpoint (6MWTD Change Baseline to Week 24)

| Group | Baseline | Week 24 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| FCM | 308m (IQR 198–378) | 403m (IQR 306–416) | **+65±22m** (at peak week 32) |
| Placebo | 325m (IQR 250–342) | 308m (IQR 285–354) | **−8±61m** |
| **Difference at Week 24** | — | — | **49m (95% CI 5–93; P=0.029)** |

- Effect greatest at week 32 (FCM +65±22m vs. placebo −8±61m; P=0.005); partially attenuated by week 52
- 31 of 39 patients completed the 6MWT assessment at week 24

### Secondary Endpoints
- NYHA class: no significant difference
- Patient global assessment (PGA): no significant difference (5/17 FCM vs. 3/18 placebo "much/moderate improvement"; P=0.20)
- KCCQ overall summary score: non-significant improvement (+6.5±5.1 points; P=0.21)
- EQ-5D-3L: no significant difference

### Safety
- Total adverse events: 76 (FCM) vs. 114 (placebo) — fewer with FCM
- Serious adverse events: 5 (FCM) vs. 19 (placebo); rate ratio 0.27 (95% CI 0.07–0.96; P=0.043)
- CV hospitalisations: 2 (FCM) vs. 8 (placebo) — numerically fewer
- No severe allergic reactions; no treatment-related deaths

### Stopping
- Trial stopped prematurely after recruiting 40 of 200 planned patients; slow recruitment due to COVID, budget constraints, and regulatory issues at non-German sites
- This severely underpowers all endpoints; results are preliminary

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

FAIR-HFpEF is the first and to date only RCT of IV iron in HFpEF with iron deficiency. The 6MWT benefit at week 24 is clinically meaningful (+49m) and consistent with effect sizes in HFrEF trials (FAIR-HF, CONFIRM-HF). However, the early stopping after just 40 patients makes all conclusions preliminary. The trial is severely underpowered for QoL and hard outcomes. The safety signal (fewer serious adverse events with FCM) is promising. This trial needs replication in a larger cohort. Directly complements [[beale2019iron]] (systematic review establishing ID prevalence in HFpEF) and [[ponikowski2020affirm]] (AFFIRM-AHF in HFrEF context).
