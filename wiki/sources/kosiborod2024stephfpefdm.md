# STEP-HFpEF DM — Semaglutide in Obese HFpEF with T2DM (Kosiborod 2024)

> Semaglutide 2.4 mg improved HF symptoms (KCCQ-CSS +7.3 pts difference vs. placebo; P<0.001), reduced body weight (−6.4 percentage points; P<0.001), improved 6MWD (+14.3 m; P=0.008), reduced CRP (ratio 0.67; P<0.001), and improved hierarchical composite (win ratio 1.58, 1.29–1.94; P<0.001) in obese HFpEF patients with T2DM — the companion trial to STEP-HFpEF (non-DM), together establishing GLP-1RA as effective across the obese HFpEF spectrum regardless of T2DM status.

**Full citation:**
Kosiborod MN, Petrie MC, Borlaug BA, et al.; STEP-HFpEF DM Trial Committees and Investigators. Semaglutide in Patients with Obesity-Related Heart Failure and Type 2 Diabetes. *N Engl J Med.* 2024;390(15):1394–1407. doi:[10.1056/NEJMoa2313307](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2313307)
**Year:** 2024 · **Journal:** N Engl J Med 2024;390:1394–1407 · **DOI:** 10.1056/NEJMoa2313917 · **Published:** April 6, 2024  
**ClinicalTrials.gov:** NCT04916470 · **Funded by:** Novo Nordisk  
**Study type:** Phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomised RCT · **N:** 616 randomised (semaglutide 310; placebo 306); enrolled at 108 sites in 16 countries  
**Population:** HFpEF (LVEF ≥45%), BMI ≥30 kg/m², confirmed T2DM (diagnosis ≥90 days pre-screening, HbA1c ≤10%); NYHA II–IV; at least one of: elevated LV filling pressures; elevated NPs + echo abnormalities; or HF hospitalisation within 12 months + echo abnormalities + diuretics  
**Duration:** 52 weeks treatment + 5-week follow-up  
**Randomisation:** Stratified by BMI (<35 vs ≥35 kg/m²)  
**Intervention:** Semaglutide 2.4 mg subcutaneous once weekly (dose-escalated from 0.25 mg at 4-week intervals to 2.4 mg maintenance by week 16) vs. matched placebo; added to background glucose-lowering medications (non-GLP-1RA)  
**Dual primary outcomes:** (1) Change in KCCQ-CSS at week 52; (2) Percentage change in body weight at week 52  
**Confirmatory secondary endpoints (hierarchical):** (3) Change in 6MWD at week 52; (4) Hierarchical composite end point (death, HF events, KCCQ-CSS, 6MWD); (5) Change in CRP level

## Key Findings

- **KCCQ-CSS (dual primary): sema +13.7 pts vs. placebo +6.4 pts; difference +7.3 pts (95% CI 4.1–10.4; P<0.001)** — significant
- **Body weight (dual primary): sema −9.8% vs. placebo −3.4%; difference −6.4% (−7.6 to −5.2; P<0.001)** — significant
- **6MWD (confirmatory secondary): sema +12.7 m vs. placebo −1.6 m; difference +14.3 m (3.7–24.9; P=0.008)** — significant
- **Hierarchical composite (confirmatory secondary): win ratio 1.58 (95% CI 1.29–1.94; P<0.001)** — significant
- **CRP (confirmatory secondary): geometric mean ratio 0.67 (0.55–0.80; P<0.001)** — 42% reduction in CRP; significant anti-inflammatory signal
- KCCQ-OSS change: sema +13.5 pts vs. placebo +6.2 pts; difference +7.3 pts (4.2–10.4)
- NT-proBNP change: −23.2% (sema) vs. −4.6% (placebo); estimated treatment ratio 0.8 (0.7–0.9) — supportive
- HbA1c change: sema −0.7% vs. placebo +0.1%; difference −0.8% (−1.0 to −0.6)
- Adjudicated HF event (hospitalisation or urgent visit): 7 (2.3%) sema vs. 18 (5.9%) placebo; HR 0.40 (0.15–0.92) — nominally significant, exploratory; trial not powered for this endpoint
- Serious adverse events: 55 (17.7%) sema vs. 88 (28.8%) placebo; P=0.002 — semaglutide associated with significantly fewer SAEs
- All-cause death: 6 (sema) vs. 10 (placebo) — small numbers, not powered
- SGLT2i use at baseline: 34.5% (sema) vs. 31.0% (placebo); similar effect in both subgroups
- See companion trial **[[kosiborod2023stephfpef]]** for non-DM population (NEJM 2023): KCCQ-CSS +6.4, 6MWD +20.5m, weight −10.7%

## Methods (brief)

Phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. Entry: LVEF ≥45%, BMI ≥30 kg/m², T2DM (confirmed ≥90 days pre-screening), HbA1c ≤10%, NYHA II–IV; plus ≥1 of: elevated LV filling pressures; elevated NPs + echo abnormalities; HF hospitalisation within 12 months + echo + diuretics. GLP-1RA use in 90 days prior excluded. Semaglutide dose-escalated every 4 weeks from 0.25 mg to 2.4 mg target (maintenance reached at week 16). Dual primary endpoints analysed via ANCOVA. Hierarchical composite (confirmatory secondary) uses win-ratio approach. Two estimands: treatment policy (ITT) and trial product (on-treatment).

**Enrolment:** June 15, 2021–August 19, 2022; 108 sites; 16 countries (Asia, Europe, North America, South America).

## Limitations

- Not powered for HF hospitalisations or mortality
- 1-year follow-up — durability beyond 52 weeks unknown
- 26% Black participants (US sites) but overall non-White representation low
- Lean mass loss alongside fat mass not assessed in primary analysis — sarcopenic obesity concern
- KCCQ and 6MWD are surrogate/functional outcomes; whether they predict CV events in HFpEF remains uncertain; SUMMIT (NCT04847557) with peak VO₂ primary will provide more objective evidence

## Contradictions

HF hospitalisation HR 0.40 (0.15–0.92) is nominally significant here but the trial was not powered for this endpoint. This apparent clinical benefit should be confirmed in a properly powered outcomes trial (SUMMIT: peak VO₂ primary; additional HF outcome data from pooled analyses). The non-DM STEP-HFpEF did not report a significant HF event reduction. Directional consistency across both trials is reassuring but event-driven confirmation is needed.
