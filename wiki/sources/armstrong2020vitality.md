# VITALITY-HFpEF

> Vericiguat (sGC stimulator) neutral on KCCQ Physical Limitation Scale (LS diff −1.5 pts, P=0.47 at 15mg; −0.5 pts, P=0.80 at 10mg) and 6MWD in HFpEF after recent decompensation; large placebo response (+6.9 KCCQ PLS pts, exceeding MCID) driven by natural recovery post-decompensation; numerically more CV deaths in vericiguat 10 mg arm (4.6% vs. 1.5% placebo); closes sGC stimulator class for HFpEF alongside simultaneous CAPACITY-HFpEF publication.

**File:** `raw/2020-JAMA-Armstrong-VITALITY-HFpEF.pdf` · **Authors:** Armstrong PW, Lam CSP, Anstrom KJ et al. · **Year:** 2020 · **Journal:** JAMA 324(15):1512–1521  
**Study type:** RCT (phase 2b) · **N:** 789 randomised · **Population:** HFpEF, LVEF ≥45%, NYHA II–III, recent HF decompensation within 6 months, elevated NPs, structural disease  
**Follow-up:** 24 weeks · **Intervention:** Vericiguat 15 mg/day or 10 mg/day vs. placebo (1:1:1)  
**Primary outcome:** Change in KCCQ Physical Limitation Scale (PLS) from baseline to week 24  
**NCT:** NCT03547583 · **Enrollment:** Jun 15, 2018–Apr 23, 2019 · **Sites:** 168 centres, 21 countries  
**Sponsors:** Bayer and Merck Sharp & Dohme Corp (co-funded; participated in design, conduct, and data analysis; steering committee had final decisions on submission)

---

## Key Findings

- KCCQ-PLS (primary): LS difference vs. placebo **−1.5 pts [95% CI −5.5 to 2.5], P=0.47** (15 mg); **−0.5 [−4.6 to 3.5], P=0.80** (10 mg) — both neutral
- 6MWD (secondary): **−5.5 m [−19.7 to 8.8m], P=0.45** (15 mg); **−1.8 m [−16.2 to 12.6m], P=0.81** (10 mg) — neutral
- **Placebo KCCQ PLS response: +6.9 points** — exceeds MCID of 5 points; natural recovery after recent decompensation inflated placebo arm substantially
- No subgroup showed KCCQ PLS heterogeneity (including baseline NYHA, baseline KCCQ score)
- **All-cause death:** 10 (3.8%) / 15 (5.7%) / 7 (2.7%) for 15mg/10mg/placebo — numerically more deaths in 10mg arm (trial not powered for this)
- **CV death:** 8 (3.0%) / 12 (4.6%) / 4 (1.5%) — pattern similar; small numbers preclude conclusions
- Symptomatic hypotension: 6.4% (15 mg) vs. 3.4% (placebo)

## Methods

**Design:** Phase 2b, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter trial. Three-arm 1:1:1 randomisation. Stratified by region and heart rhythm. Primary analysis population: ≥1 dose + ≥1 baseline and post-baseline KCCQ PLS assessment (255/254/252 per group). Independent clinical events committee adjudicated CV hospitalisations and deaths. Worst-case imputation for deaths (KCCQ scored 0 from death onward).

**Dose:** Both vericiguat groups started at 2.5 mg once daily; up-titrated over 6 weeks to target 15 or 10 mg. Target dose achieved by week 18: 81.1% (15 mg), 88.9% (10 mg); by week 24: 83.0% (15 mg), 89.3% (10 mg). Dose modification based on mean SBP and hypotension symptoms.

**Eligibility:**
- Age ≥45, LVEF ≥45%, NYHA II–III
- HF decompensation within 6 months: HF hospitalisation OR IV diuretics without hospitalisation
- Elevated NPs: NT-proBNP ≥300 or BNP ≥100 pg/mL (SR); NT-proBNP ≥600 or BNP ≥200 pg/mL (AF); within 30 days of randomisation
- Echocardiographic structural evidence: LV hypertrophy or LA enlargement within 12 months

**Exclusion:** SBP ≥160 or <110 mmHg; DBP <40 mmHg; symptomatic hypotension; HR <50 or ≥100/min; IV treatment within 24 hours; LVEF <40%; concurrent nitrates, PDE5i, or sGC stimulators; ACS within 60 days; eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m²; hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy, pericardial disease, amyloidosis, sarcoidosis, primary valvular disease requiring surgery

**Statistical power:** 80% power to detect 5-point difference in KCCQ PLS (SD 21 points); N=735 (245/group); two-sided α=0.05; Bonferroni primary + secondary at 0.025 each.

**Predecessor:** SOCRATES-PRESERVED (Pieske B, Eur Heart J 2017) — phase 2a dose-finding; suggested NT-proBNP signal at 10 mg vericiguat — motivated VITALITY-HFpEF design with larger N, longer follow-up, and higher dose range to enhance detection. VITALITY failed to confirm.

## Limitations

- Phase 2b — not powered for clinical events; KCCQ PLS as primary endpoint; no mortality efficacy conclusion possible
- Large placebo response (+6.9 KCCQ PLS pts) driven by natural recovery after decompensation; enrolled within 30 days of event — captures acute-phase recovery trajectory
- No echocardiographic NP or structural confirmation required beyond standard clinical diagnosis; phenotypic heterogeneity not controlled
- 72.6% completed ≥24 weeks — meaningful dropout rate; worst-case imputation for deaths may overestimate adverse signal in active arm (authors acknowledge)
- Titration to target over 6 weeks — if early period determines QoL trajectory, delayed full-dose exposure may underestimate potential benefit
- AF prevalence ~35% — higher than most RAAS trials; AF patients require higher NP thresholds for enrolment (≥600 pg/mL NT-proBNP) — differential selection
- LVEF range 45–88%+ — includes HFmrEF (45–50%: ~20%); not restricted to true HFpEF ≥50%
- 21-country geographic distribution: 71% European centres — may not represent global HFpEF burden

## Contradictions
- Vericiguat neutral in HFpEF (VITALITY) vs. positive in HFrEF (VICTORIA, HR 0.90). Same drug, same mechanism, different EF phenotype. EF-dependent NO/cGMP pathway responsiveness or different underlying biology. See [[contradictions]].
- Large placebo response (+6.9 KCCQ PLS) exceeds MCID — the trial design using post-decompensation enrolment creates acute-recovery placebo inflation that may mask drug benefit; yet DELIVER (dapagliflozin) had similar enrolment criteria and showed positive KCCQ. Placebo inflation may be KCCQ-measure-specific (symptom recovery) rather than structural endpoint specific.
- Numerically more CV deaths in vericiguat 10mg arm (4.6% vs. 1.5% placebo). If real, paradoxical harm from sGC stimulation in vulnerable post-decompensation HFpEF patients — consistent with hypotension-harm signal in CAPACITY-HFpEF elderly. If chance (N too small), no conclusion. Direction of harm is consistent across CAPACITY age interaction and VITALITY 10mg mortality. See [[contradictions]].
- KCCQ PLS: placebo +6.9 pts (exceeds MCID), vericiguat 15mg +5.5 pts (within MCID range), 10mg +6.4 pts — all three groups reached clinically meaningful improvement; the drug numerically performed slightly worse than placebo. "Meaningful" improvement vs. "meaningful difference" are distinct concepts.
