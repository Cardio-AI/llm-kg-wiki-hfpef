# VITALITY-HFpEF

> Vericiguat (soluble guanylate cyclase stimulator) did not improve exercise capacity in HFpEF; one of several neutral cGMP-pathway trials.

**File:** `raw/[not yet ingested]` · **Authors:** Armstrong PW et al. · **Year:** 2020 · **Journal:** JAMA  
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** ~789 · **Population:** HFpEF with LVEF ≥45%  
**Follow-up:** ~24 weeks · **Intervention:** Vericiguat (1.5 mg or 15 mg) vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire-Physical Limitation score + 6-minute walk distance

---

## Key Findings

- Neither dose of vericiguat improved the primary endpoint vs. placebo
- Neutral result for exercise capacity and QOL endpoints

## Methods (brief)

[Expand on paper ingest]

Tested sGC stimulator (upstream from PDE5 inhibitors in cGMP pathway). Two dose groups vs. placebo. Functional capacity primary endpoint rather than outcomes.
