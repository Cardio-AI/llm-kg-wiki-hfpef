# Personalized App-Based Coaching for Improving Physical Activity in HFpEF: Rationale and Design of the MyoMobile Study

> Study design paper for the MyoMobile trial — first prospective RCT of personalized app-based physical activity coaching in HFpEF; three-arm EE2 design (standard care vs. wearable tracking vs. tracking + coaching) with step count as primary endpoint; N=185. **Primary results now published:** [[zeid2026myomobile]] (JACC Heart Fail 2026;14(5):102845) — app-based coaching significantly increased step count and KCCQ vs. standard care.

**Full citation:**
Zeid S, Prochaska JH, Schuch A, Tröbs SO, Schulz A, Münzel T, Pies T, Dinh W, Michal M, Simon P, Wild PS. Personalized app-based coaching for improving physical activity in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction patients compared with standard care: rationale and design of the MyoMobile Study. *Eur Heart J Digit Health.* 2025;6:298–309. doi:[10.1093/ehjdh/ztae096](https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjdh/ztae096)

## Core Arguments

### Rationale

Physical inactivity is a primary driver of HFpEF worsening, yet Class Ia exercise recommendations (≥30 min moderate-intensity PA, ≥5 days/week) are poorly implemented in the real world — supervised cardiac rehabilitation programmes have low participation and are unsuitable for a diverse HFpEF population. App-based coaching provides a personalized, home-based, flexible alternative focused on daily step counts — a metric familiar to patients and not requiring supervised facility attendance. (source: Zeid2025MyoMobile)

### Study Design

**Design type:** EE2 (Efficacy and Effectiveness) trial — simultaneously assesses both controlled-condition efficacy AND real-world effectiveness in a single design (Selker et al. 2019). Single-centre (Mainz), randomized, controlled, three-armed parallel group.

**Three arms (1:0.75:1 allocation):**
1. Standard care (PA counselling by trained physician; booklet with PA recommendations)
2. App-based PA tracking without coaching (fitness wristband, no active coaching)
3. App-based PA tracking + personalized coaching via MyoMobile app

**Duration:** 12-week intervention; 5 visits (screening/day −7, baseline/day 0, follow-up 1/week 6, follow-up 2/week 11, follow-up 3/week 12). Enrollment: November 2020 – January 2023, Rhine-Main, Germany.

**NCT:** NCT04940312

### Eligibility

**Key inclusion criteria:**
- Age ≥45 years; LVEF >40% (any imaging); NYHA Class I–III; stable HF ≥4 weeks
- Objective HF evidence (at least one of): NT-proBNP ≥300 pg/mL; HF hospitalisation within 12 months; diuretic treatment for HF symptoms ≥30 days prior
- Baseline daily step count ≥1,000 and <10,000 (to select ambulatory, activity-capable but sedentary patients)

**Key exclusion criteria:**
- Acute decompensated HF; non-ambulatory status; ACS/cardiac surgery within 3 months; active cancer treatment; alternative diagnoses explaining dyspnoea; PA limitation from non-HF conditions

Note: LVEF >40% inclusion threshold captures both HFpEF (≥50%) and HFmrEF (41–49%).

### Intervention: MyoMobile App Algorithm

All participants received a Vivosmart 4 fitness wristband (Garmin, USA) for continuous step count measurement, synced via Bluetooth to the MyoMobile app (Samsung Galaxy A20e). The coaching algorithm (Figure 3):
- Classifies participants into three activity levels: low (<2,500 steps/day), normal (2,500–10,000), high (>10,000)
- Sets a personalised weekly step goal based on the 5 most active days of the preceding week
- Adjusts goal up/down each week based on performance + willingness to increase (patient-reported)
- Provides daily motivational feedback and weekly performance summaries via push notifications
- Goal structure: +250 steps for slight increase; +median×0.05 for moderate increase (see Figure 3 algorithm)

Coaching-arm participants also received motivational interviewing for underperformers + motivational messages on Days 3 and 5 of each study week.

### Primary Endpoint

Change in average daily step count from baseline (7-day mean, Move Monitor 3D accelerometer, McRoberts, Netherlands) to end of 12-week intervention (7-day mean, week 12). Primary comparison: standard care vs. app-based coaching arm.

Linear regression analysis, ITT population.

### Sample Size

- Coaching vs. standard: N=62 per arm (power 90%, α=0.05); minimum detectable difference 1,400 steps/day; SD=2,400; correlation 0.75 between baseline and 12-week count; N=83 randomized per arm accounting for 25% dropout
- Tracking vs. standard: N=47 per arm (power 80%); N=63 randomized accounting for 25% dropout
- ITT total: N=185

### Secondary and Exploratory Endpoints

- Daily step count at week 6 (intermediate)
- Echocardiography: LVEF, E/e' ratio (cardiac structure/function)
- CPET: peak VO₂, ventilatory efficiency (VE/VCO₂)
- 6-minute walk test
- Heart rate variability (24h Holter ECG, Move Monitor)
- Autonomic function (COMPASS-31 questionnaire)
- Quality of life (KCCQ)
- PHQ-9 (depression), GAD-7 (anxiety)
- 100+ digital biomarkers / digital mobility outcomes (DMOs) from wearable accelerometers
- Multi-omics profiling (genomics, gene expression, inflammation, biomarker panel) — biobank samples at Visit 1 and Visit 4

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

- **First prospective RCT of app-based PA coaching specifically in HFpEF** — introduces digital health/mHealth as a distinct intervention paradigm alternative to facility-based supervised exercise training ([[supervised-exercise-training]]); lower access barrier, home-based, scalable
- **EE2 trial design** — novel design paradigm addressing both efficacy and real-world effectiveness simultaneously; relevant to implementation science for HFpEF exercise interventions
- **Step count as primary endpoint** — pragmatic metric; connects to accelerometry vs. VO₂peak vs. 6MWD discussion already in wiki ([[docherty2025determine]]); step count is more feasible at scale than supervised CPET
- **DZHK context** — same DZHK network as [[torch]] registry (Rhine-Main site); institutional biobank and multi-omics infrastructure shared; deep phenotyping enables molecular-mechanism exploration alongside intervention
- **Baseline data (N=185 pragmatic HFpEF sample):** median E/e' 9.42 (below many guideline cut-offs), 51.9% AF, 40.5% obesity, 54.6% CAD — representative real-world HFpEF cohort with high comorbidity burden; differs from clinical trial populations (EMPEROR-Preserved, DELIVER excluded unstable comorbidities)
- **LVEF ≥40% threshold:** includes HFmrEF (41–49%) — same as EMPEROR-Preserved/DELIVER; complicates interpretation for pure HFpEF questions
- **Primary results published:** [[zeid2026myomobile]] (JACC Heart Fail 2026;14(5):102845) — app-based coaching significantly increased daily step count vs. standard care; secondary improvements in KCCQ and 6MWT confirmed; first positive digital health RCT in HFpEF
