# HFA-PEFF Diagnostic Algorithm

> The HFA-PEFF algorithm provides a stepwise, four-stage diagnostic approach to HFpEF — P (pre-test), E (echo + NP score), F1 (functional testing if uncertain), F2 (final aetiology) — incorporating a composite point-based score that classifies patients as definite HFpEF (≥5 points), uncertain (2–4), or unlikely (≤1).

## Core Arguments

### The Four-Step Algorithm (P–E–F1–F2)

**Step 1 (P): Pre-test Assessment**
- History, ECG, standard echo, NPs, ergometry/6MWT or CPET
- NP screening cut-offs: NT-proBNP ≥125 pg/mL or BNP ≥35 pg/mL (high sensitivity, low threshold)
- Rule out overt non-cardiac causes and other cardiac causes (HFrEF, valve disease, ischaemia)
- Patients with elevated NPs or typical demographics proceed to Step E

**Step 2 (E): Echo + NP Score (HFA-PEFF Score)**
Composite score with three domains (Functional, Morphological, Biomarker). Major criteria = 2 pts; Minor criteria = 1 pt. Each domain contributes max 2 pts regardless of number of positives.

| Domain | Major Criteria (2 pts) | Minor Criteria (1 pt) |
|---|---|---|
| Functional | Septal e' <7 cm/s OR lateral e' <10 cm/s OR avg E/e' ≥15 OR TR velocity ≥2.8 m/s (PASP >35 mmHg) | Average E/e' 9–14 OR GLS <16% |
| Morphological | LAVI >34 mL/m² (SR) OR >40 mL/m² (AF) OR [LVMI ≥149/122 g/m² (m/w) AND RWT >0.42] | LAVI 29–34 mL/m² (SR) / 34–40 (AF) OR LVMI ≥115/95 g/m² (m/w) OR RWT >0.42 OR LV wall ≥12 mm |
| Biomarker (SR) | NT-proBNP >220 pg/mL OR BNP >80 pg/mL | NT-proBNP 125–220 pg/mL OR BNP 35–80 pg/mL |
| Biomarker (AF) | NT-proBNP >660 pg/mL OR BNP >240 pg/mL | NT-proBNP 365–660 pg/mL OR BNP 105–240 pg/mL |

**Interpretation:** ≥5 points = HFpEF confirmed; 2–4 = uncertain → proceed to Step F1; ≤1 = HFpEF unlikely

**Step 3 (F1): Functional Testing in Diagnostic Uncertainty**
- Non-invasive: Exercise stress echocardiography; E/e' ≥15 at peak stress → +2 pts; E/e' ≥15 AND TR >3.4 m/s → +3 pts
- Invasive at rest: LVEDP ≥16 mmHg OR PCWP ≥15 mmHg → HFpEF confirmed
- Invasive during exercise: PCWP ≥25 mmHg → HFpEF confirmed

**Step 4 (F2): Final Aetiology**
- CMR, biopsies, scintigraphy, genetic testing, specific lab tests
- Identify specific HFpEF subtypes: amyloidosis, HCM, infiltrative cardiomyopathy, storage diseases, metabolic/endocrine, iatrogenic

### Key Departures from Prior Recommendations
- Moves from single-parameter diagnosis to composite score
- AF-adjusted NP thresholds (3× higher than sinus rhythm)
- Separates "diagnostic uncertainty" (2–4 pts) as a formal category requiring functional testing
- Emphasises NPs can be normal in invasively confirmed HFpEF (~20% of patients)
- Step F2 stresses aetiological investigation, anticipating phenotype-targeted therapies (e.g., tafamidis for ATTR-CM)
