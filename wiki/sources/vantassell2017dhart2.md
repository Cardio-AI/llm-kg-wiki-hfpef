# D-HART2 Trial Design: Anakinra (IL-1 Blockade) in HFpEF (Van Tassell 2017)

> D-HART2 design paper: Phase 2b double-blind RCT of anakinra (IL-1 receptor antagonist, 100 mg/day SC × 24 weeks) vs. placebo in N=31 elevated-CRP HFpEF patients; primary CRP area-under-curve endpoint; rationale is IL-1 blockade to interrupt the Paulus–Tschöpe inflammation-to-diastolic-dysfunction cascade.

**Full citation:**
Van Tassell BW, Buckley LF, Carbone S, Trankle CR, Canada JM, Dixon DL, Abouzaki N, Oddi-Erdle C, Biondi-Zoccai G, Arena R, Abbate A. Interleukin-1 Blockade in Patients with Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction: Rationale and Design of the Diastolic Heart Failure Anakinra Response Trial 2 (D-HART2). *Clin Cardiol.* 2017;40(9):626–632. doi:[10.1002/clc.22719](https://doi.org/10.1002/clc.22719)

## Core Arguments

**Trial:** D-HART2 (Diastolic Heart Failure Anakinra Response Trial 2). Phase 2b, double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled. NCT02173548. N=**31** HFpEF patients (LVEF ≥50%, NYHA II–III, hs-CRP >2 mg/L to enrich for systemic inflammation). Duration: 24 weeks.

**Intervention:** Anakinra 100 mg/day SC (recombinant human IL-1 receptor antagonist) vs. matched placebo.

**Primary endpoint:** Area under the hs-CRP–time curve at 2 weeks — direct biomarker of target engagement and inflammation suppression rate.

**Secondary endpoints:** Peak VO₂, KCCQ, 6-minute walk test, NT-proBNP, echocardiographic diastolic parameters (E/e', LA volume).

**Mechanistic rationale:**
1. The [[paulus2013novelparadigm]] paradigm: systemic inflammation (elevated IL-1β, TNF-α) → coronary microvascular endothelial dysfunction → ↓NO → ↓cGMP → ↓PKG → myofilament Ca²⁺ sensitisation, titin hyperphosphorylation, and fibrosis → diastolic stiffness → HFpEF
2. IL-1β is a master pro-inflammatory cytokine; anakinra competitively inhibits the IL-1 receptor (blocks both IL-1α and IL-1β)
3. **D-HART1 pilot** (N=10, open-label, 2015): anakinra reduced hs-CRP by 60%, reduced IL-6, and increased peak VO₂ +0.7 mL/kg/min (P=0.048) — first signal of functional improvement with anti-inflammatory therapy in HFpEF
4. D-HART2 is a powered Phase 2b test of the pilot finding

**Patient enrichment:** Requirement for hs-CRP >2 mg/L selects patients with active systemic inflammation — the subphenotype where anti-inflammatory therapy is most biologically plausible. Without enrichment, effect size would be diluted if inflammation drives HFpEF only in a subset.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

This design paper documents the anti-inflammatory therapeutic rationale in HFpEF. The results paper ([[vantassell2018dhart2]]) reveals that inflammation suppression (CRP reduced) does not translate to improved exercise capacity — a key null result constraining the Paulus–Tschöpe mechanistic claim as a direct therapeutic pathway for exercise intolerance. This motivates the distinction between inflammation as biomarker vs. inflammation as causal driver of HFpEF symptoms.

## Contradictions
D-HART1 pilot showed VO₂ +0.7 mL/kg/min (P=0.048); D-HART2 results did not replicate (P=0.54). Small-sample pilot signal was likely a false positive. Document in [[contradictions]].
