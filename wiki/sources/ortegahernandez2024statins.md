# Ortega-Hernández 2024 — Prognostic Impact of Statins in HFpEF (RICA Registry)

> In 2,788 HFpEF patients from the Spanish RICA registry, statin therapy was independently associated with lower 1-year all-cause mortality (HR 0.74; 95% CI 0.61–0.89; P=0.002); the benefit was restricted to patients **without ischaemic heart disease** (HR 0.69; P<0.001) and was not significant in those with IHD (P=0.110).

## Key Findings

### Statin Use
- 1,121 (40.2%) receiving statins; 1,667 (59.8%) not
- Statin patients: younger (79.4 vs. 80.5y), more ischaemic HF (24.8% vs. 9.6%), more HTN/DM/dyslipidaemia, better functional status (Barthel, NYHA)

### Primary Outcome: 1-Year All-Cause Mortality
| Group | Mortality | P |
|---|---|---|
| Statin-treated | 14.7% | — |
| Non-statin | 20.9% | <0.001 |

### Multivariable Cox Regression (adjusted)
- **Statin use:** HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.61–0.89; P=0.002)
- **Female sex:** HR 0.72 (0.59–0.87; P<0.001)
- **Obesity (BMI >30):** HR 0.76 (0.64–0.94; P=0.008)
- **Dementia:** HR 1.53 (1.11–2.11; P=0.009) — worse
- **Anaemia:** HR 1.28 (1.06–1.55; P=0.011) — worse
- **eGFR <60:** HR 1.57 (1.30–1.91; P<0.001) — worse
- **Aldosterone antagonists:** HR 1.34 (1.10–1.62; P=0.004) — worse
- **Digoxin:** HR 1.34 (1.08–1.66; P=0.007) — worse

### Subgroup by IHD

| Subgroup | Statin HR (multivariable) | P |
|---|---|---|
| **Without IHD** | **0.69 (95% CI 0.56–0.86)** | **<0.001** |
| With IHD | 0.69 (95% CI 0.43–1.11) | 0.110 (NS) |

- The ischaemic subgroup analysis has smaller N (N=438 with IHD), explaining the wider CI and loss of significance
- Both CIs are wide and overlapping — the interaction term P-value is not reported, so a formal test of heterogeneity between groups is unavailable

## Methods

RICA registry: prospective, multicentre cohort. All HFpEF (ESC criteria; LVEF ≥50%; echo required). Statin use at discharge. Primary outcome: all-cause mortality at 1 year. Cox regression model including variables significant in univariable analysis (P<0.05). IHD subgroup analysis: separate Cox regressions for patients with and without IHD at baseline.

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

This is the largest single-registry analysis of statin use in HFpEF with IHD subgroup stratification. The non-IHD benefit is consistent with a TOPCAT post-hoc analysis that found statins beneficial only in HFpEF without IHD. Mechanistic rationale for non-IHD benefit: statins reduce EAT (see [[timoteo2024eat]]), reduce systemic inflammation/CRP, reduce LV fibrosis and hypertrophy, reduce QT variability (less sudden death) — all relevant in non-ischaemic HFpEF driven by hypertensive/metabolic disease. This pleiotropic benefit hypothesis supports statin use beyond conventional lipid-lowering indications in HFpEF without IHD.

## Contradictions

Observational data cannot confirm causality; statin patients had better baseline characteristics. GISSI trial (only RCT including HFpEF) showed no mortality benefit with rosuvastatin — though only 10% of patients had HFpEF and the HFpEF threshold was LVEF ≥40% (not ≥50%). No completed HFpEF-specific statin RCT exists.
