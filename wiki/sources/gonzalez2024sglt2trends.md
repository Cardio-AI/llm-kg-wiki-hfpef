# Gonzalez 2024 — SGLT2i Prescribing Trends 2013–2021

> By 2021, only 1 in 5 HFpEF patients with T2DM and 1 in 85 without T2DM were on an SGLT2i in US commercial/Medicare claims — a major implementation gap even after landmark trial publications and guideline updates.

**Full citation:**
Gonzalez J, Dave CV. Prescribing trends of SGLT2 inhibitors among HFrEF and HFpEF patients with and without T2DM, 2013–2021. *BMC Cardiovasc Disord.* 2024;24:285. doi:[10.1186/s12872-024-03961-5](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12872-024-03961-5)

## Summary

Serial cross-sectional study using US MarketScan commercial and Medicare claims (2013–2021). Enumerates SGLT2i prescribing prevalence in HFrEF and HFpEF cohorts annually, stratified by T2DM comorbidity status. (source: gonzalez2024sglt2trends)

## Design

- **Database:** MarketScan Commercial and Medicare, 2013–2021
- **Cohorts:** HFrEF (N=218,066; ICD I50.2/428.2×) and HFpEF (N=150,437; ICD I50.3/428.3×)
- **Exposure:** Any SGLT2i prescription (canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, ertugliflozin)
- **Stratification:** T2DM (ICD 250.X/E11); calendar year; age (<65 vs. ≥65)
- **IRB:** Rutgers University non-human subjects research waiver
- **Exclusion:** CKD stage 5/eGFR<15, dialysis, concurrent HFrEF+HFpEF codes

## Results

**HFpEF overall:**
- SGLT2i prevalence: 0.5% (2013) → 9.9% (2021); p-trend <0.001
- Sharp inflection after EMPEROR-Preserved (2021) and guideline updates

**HFpEF by T2DM status:**
- With T2DM: 0.5% → ~20.6% (2021)
- Without T2DM: 0.5% → ~1.2% (2021)
- T2DM:no-T2DM use ratio: 83.1 in 2018 → 17.5 in 2021 (decreasing but still 17-fold disparity)

**HFrEF overall:**
- SGLT2i prevalence: 0.3% (2013) → 18.6% (2021)
- Largest increases after DAPA-HF (2019) and EMPEROR-Reduced (2020) publications

**Market share shifts:**
- Canagliflozin: 100% (2013) → 2.5% (2021) — market collapse after CREDENCE/safety data
- Empagliflozin and dapagliflozin dominated by end of period; empagliflozin slightly leading in HFpEF

**Key inflection points (Figure 1 arrows):**
1. FDA approval of canagliflozin (2013)
2. FDA approval of dapagliflozin Q1 and empagliflozin Q3 (2014)
3. FDA approval of ertugliflozin (2017)
4. EMPEROR-REDUCED (2020) + ACC algorithm update
5. EMPEROR-PRESERVED (2021) — sharpest HFpEF inflection

**Sensitivity:** Trends similar across age strata; younger patients and T2DM patients had higher utilisation rates.

## Clinical Implications

- By 2021 approximately 1 in 5 HFpEF+T2DM patients but only 1 in 85 without T2DM were on SGLT2i — **large unmet prescribing need in non-diabetic HFpEF**
- The gap persisted despite publication of EMPEROR-Preserved, suggesting lag between evidence and practice
- Proposed barriers: prescribing inertia; formulary restrictions (50–60% commercial plan coverage without prior authorization); perception as antidiabetic rather than cardiovascular agents; adverse effect concerns (DKA, UTI, genital infections); frailty and BMI concerns
- The study ended in 2021 — DELIVER (2022) and ESC 2023 Class I upgrade would be expected to further increase uptake in subsequent years

## Limitations

- Commercial/Medicare claims only (~55% US population) — may not generalise to Medicaid, uninsured
- HF classification by ICD codes without echocardiographic confirmation (PPV ~90% for HFrEF, ~21% for HFpEF by prior validation)
- No race/ethnicity data (private insurers typically do not collect this)
- Study ended 2021; does not capture post-DELIVER uptake surge
- Cannot distinguish patient vs. provider refusals or contraindications
