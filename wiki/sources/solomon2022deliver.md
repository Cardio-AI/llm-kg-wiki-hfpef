# DELIVER

> Dapagliflozin significantly reduced worsening HF + CV death in HFpEF (LVEF >40%); together with EMPEROR-Preserved, established SGLT2 inhibitors as the first class with proven benefit across the full HF EF spectrum.

**File:** `raw/[not yet ingested]` · **Authors:** Solomon SD et al. · **Year:** 2022 · **Journal:** NEJM  
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** ~6,263 · **Population:** HFpEF with LVEF >40%, symptomatic HF, elevated NPs  
**Follow-up:** ~2.3 years · **Intervention:** Dapagliflozin 10 mg/day vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Worsening HF + CV death

---

## Key Findings

[Expand on paper ingest — published 2022; after ESC 2021 cutoff]

- Dapagliflozin significantly reduced primary composite of worsening HF + CV death
- Benefit seen across the LVEF spectrum (>40%), including LVEF ≥60%
- Also included patients with previously impaired LVEF that recovered (HF with improved EF)

## Methods (brief)

[Expand on paper ingest]

LVEF >40% (includes HFmrEF and HFpEF). Also included patients with prior LVEF ≤40% who had recovered — broadening beyond pure HFpEF. Companion trial to EMPEROR-Preserved.
