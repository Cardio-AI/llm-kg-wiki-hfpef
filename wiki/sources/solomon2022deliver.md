# DELIVER

> Dapagliflozin significantly reduced worsening HF and CV death in HF with LVEF >40% (HR 0.82, P<0.001); benefit consistent across all LVEF subgroups including ≥60%; dapagliflozin also improved KCCQ symptoms (win ratio 1.11, P=0.009); together with EMPEROR-Preserved, established SGLT2i across the full HF EF spectrum.

**File:** `raw/2022-NEJM-Solomon-Deliver_study.pdf` · **Authors:** Solomon SD et al. · **Year:** 2022 · **Journal:** NEJM 387:1089–1098  
**Study type:** RCT · **N:** 6,263 · **Population:** HF with LVEF >40%, evidence of structural heart disease, elevated NPs  
**Follow-up:** Median 2.3 years (IQR 1.7–2.8) · **Intervention:** Dapagliflozin 10 mg/day vs. placebo  
**Primary outcome:** Composite of worsening HF (hospitalisation or urgent visit) or CV death  
**NCT:** NCT03619213 · **Enrollment:** Aug 27, 2018–Dec 30, 2020 · **Sites:** 353 centres, 20 countries  
**Sponsor:** AstraZeneca

---

## Key Findings

- Primary composite HR **0.82** (95% CI 0.73–0.92), **P<0.001** — significant
- HF hospitalisation/urgent visit: HR 0.79 (0.69–0.91)
- CV death: HR 0.88 (0.74–1.05) — not significant in isolation
- All-cause death: HR 0.94 (0.83–1.07) — not significant
- Total recurrent events (worsening HF + CV deaths): rate ratio **0.77** (0.67–0.89), P<0.001
- **KCCQ-TSS benefit (secondary):** win ratio 1.11 (1.03–1.21), P=0.009; mean placebo-corrected difference +2.4 points (95% CI 1.5–3.4) at 8 months
- No EF heterogeneity: benefit consistent across LVEF ≤49%, 50–59%, ≥60%
- Benefit consistent regardless of: diabetes status, prior HF hospitalisation, prior LVEF ≤40% (HFimpEF), sex
- Safety profile similar to placebo; DKA 2 vs. 0; no Fournier's gangrene

## Methods

**Design:** Phase 3, international, multicenter, parallel-group, event-driven, double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT. ITT analysis. Cox proportional hazards model stratified by diabetes status.

**Statistical design:** Dual primary analysis with split alpha: overall population (α=0.024) + LVEF <60% subpopulation (α=0.038). KCCQ-TSS analysed as composite rank outcome with win ratio (pre-specified hierarchical secondary).

**Eligibility:**
- Age ≥40, stabilised HF
- LVEF >40% at enrolment (includes HFmrEF 41–49% and HFpEF ≥50%)
- Evidence of structural heart disease (LVH, LAE) + elevated NPs
- Prior LVEF ≤40% with recovery to >40% eligible (HFimpEF group included)
- Enrolment as outpatient or during/after HF hospitalisation
- Exclusions: eGFR <25, SBP <95 mmHg, T1DM

## Limitations

- LVEF >40% population includes HFmrEF; not restricted to strict HFpEF ≥50%
- 18% of patients had prior LVEF ≤40% (HFimpEF) — heterogeneous population
- <5% Black patients enrolled (proportional regionally, but limits generalisability)
- COVID-19 pandemic limited KCCQ assessment at 8 months to pre-March 2020 cohort (sensitivity analysis confirmed similar results)
- All subgroups underpowered; within-subgroup results should be interpreted cautiously
- Not powered to assess CV death alone

## Contradictions
- ESC 2021: no SGLT2i recommendation for HFpEF; AHA 2022: Class 2a. Timing discordance. See [[contradictions]] #9.
- DELIVER KCCQ positive (win ratio 1.11, P=0.009 at 8 months) vs. DETERMINE-Preserved KCCQ neutral (P=0.079 at 16 weeks). Same drug (dapagliflozin), both HFpEF. Explanation likely: different analytical approach (win ratio vs. change score) + longer follow-up in DELIVER. See [[contradictions]] #16.
- CV mortality NS in DELIVER (HR 0.88, P NS) — hospitalisation-driven benefit; mortality gap persists. See [[contradictions]] #9.
