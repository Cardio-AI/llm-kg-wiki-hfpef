# 2021 ESC Guidelines for Heart Failure

> Comprehensive ESC clinical practice guidelines covering all HF phenotypes; landmark for adding SGLT2 inhibitors as a fourth pillar of HFrEF therapy and establishing the current diagnostic and therapeutic framework for HFpEF.

**Full citation:**
McDonagh TA, Metra M, Adamo M, et al.; ESC Scientific Document Group. 2021 ESC Guidelines for the diagnosis and treatment of acute and chronic heart failure. *Eur Heart J.* 2021;42(36):3599–3726. doi:[10.1093/eurheartj/ehab368](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehab368)

---

## Core Arguments

The 2021 ESC Guidelines update the 2016 version with several landmark changes:

1. **SGLT2 inhibitors** (dapagliflozin, empagliflozin) added as Class I, Level A therapy for all patients with [[hfref]], regardless of diabetes status — based on DAPA-HF and EMPEROR-Reduced
2. **[[hfmref]]** renamed from 'mid-range' to 'mildly reduced' ejection fraction
3. Simplified diagnostic and treatment algorithm for [[hfpef]]; pragmatic three-criterion approach formalized
4. Updated comorbidity management (diabetes, iron deficiency, cancer)
5. Updated cardiomyopathy section including genetic testing guidance

**What is NOT new:** The fundamental position that no pharmacological therapy has proven mortality/morbidity reduction across the full [[hfpef]] population — see [[hfpef-treatment-gap]].

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

This is the foundational source for the wiki. It establishes:
- The three-way [[hf-phenotype-classification]] (HFrEF/HFmrEF/HFpEF)
- The [[hfpef-diagnosis]] framework (Table 9 markers, HFA-PEFF, H₂FPEF, invasive testing)
- The complete landscape of failed HFpEF trials
- The emerging signals (sacubitril/valsartan FDA endorsement, TOPCAT Americas, SGLT2i knowledge gap)
- The only two Class I HFpEF recommendations: comorbidity treatment + diuretics

Key knowledge gaps identified in this source: SGLT2i in HFpEF (EMPEROR-Preserved, DELIVER pending); HFpEF sub-phenotyping; sex-specific diagnostic thresholds.

## Contradictions
- LVEF ≥50% cut-off for HFpEF is acknowledged as arbitrary; EACVI defines LV systolic dysfunction as <52% (M)/<54% (F), creating a definitional overlap zone.
- Guidelines state "no treatment has convincingly reduced mortality in HFpEF" yet simultaneously note FDA endorsement of sacubitril/valsartan for LVEF below normal — reflects publication lag and jurisdictional differences.
- TOPCAT: Americas vs. non-Americas results diverge; suspected enrollment contamination in Eastern Europe.
