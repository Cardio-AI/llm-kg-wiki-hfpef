## Question

What are the common inclusion criteria across HFpEF clinical trials?

---

## Answer

Trial entry criteria for HFpEF are **not standardized** — see [[hfpef-diagnostic-definitions]] for the separate issue of diagnostic algorithms (H2FPEF, HFA-PEFF) vs. trial enrollment criteria, which are distinct. Across the pharmacological RCTs tracked in [[trials]], four dimensions recur:

### 1. LVEF threshold — the one near-universal criterion, but the cutoff has drifted

| Threshold | Trials |
|---|---|
| ≥45% | I-PRESERVE (source: Massie2008IPreserve), TOPCAT (source: Pitt2014TOPCAT), PARAGON-HF (source: Solomon2019PARAGON) |
| >40% | CHARM-Preserved (source: Yusuf2003CHARM), EMPEROR-Preserved (source: Anker2021EMPEROR), DELIVER (source: Solomon2022DELIVER), DETERMINE-Preserved (source: McMurray2024DETERMINE), FINEARTS-HF (source: Solomon2024FINEARTS), SPIRRIT-HFpEF (source: lund2024spirrit) |
| ≥50% | STEP-HFpEF (source: Kosiborod2023STEPHFPEF), NEAT-HFpEF, INDIE-HFpEF, RELAX, ALDO-DHF, SUMMIT — see [[trials]] |

The trend over time: earlier trials (CHARM-Preserved 2003, I-PRESERVE 2008, TOPCAT 2013) set the floor at 45–50%; from EMPEROR-Preserved/DELIVER (2021–2022) onward, most pivotal RCTs lowered it to >40% specifically to capture HFmrEF alongside HFpEF, reflecting the "class effect across the EF spectrum" finding (see [[sglt2-inhibitors-hfpef-rcts]]). Mechanistic/mechanistic-hemodynamic trials (RALI-DHF, D-HART2, REBALANCE-HF, DECIPHER-HFpEF) more often kept the stricter ≥50% floor.

### 2. NYHA functional class — near-universal, usually II–IV

TOPCAT (NYHA II–IV, source: Pitt2014TOPCAT), PARAGON-HF (NYHA II–IV, source: Solomon2019PARAGON), and EMPEROR-Preserved (NYHA II–IV, source: Anker2021EMPEROR) all required symptomatic HF. A narrower II–III band appears in some phase 2/mechanistic trials (PARAMOUNT, source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT; ALDO-DHF).

### 3. Natriuretic peptide (NT-proBNP/BNP) threshold — increasingly required, not universal

Older large outcome trials (TOPCAT, I-PRESERVE, CHARM-Preserved, PARAGON-HF as tabulated in [[trials]]) are not recorded in the registry as having a hard NP entry threshold. Beginning with PARAMOUNT (NT-proBNP >400 pg/mL, source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT), NP-gating becomes progressively more common: DETERMINE-Preserved (NT-proBNP ≥250, or ≥500 if AF), SPIRRIT-HFpEF (>300 ng/L sinus rhythm / >750 ng/L AF, source: lund2024spirrit). This reflects a broader shift toward biomarker-confirmed elevated filling pressures as an enrollment safeguard — see [[natriuretic-peptides]].

### 4. Age

Not universal, but present in some trials: I-PRESERVE required age ≥60 (source: Massie2008IPreserve); SPIRRIT-HFpEF required age ≥50 (source: lund2024spirrit). PEP-CHF (an older ACEi trial) required age ≥70. Most contemporary pivotal RCTs (EMPEROR-Preserved, DELIVER, FINEARTS-HF) do not impose an age floor in the registry record.

### 5. Population-enrichment criteria — mechanism/hypothesis-specific, not shared

Recent GLP-1/GIP-agonist and metabolic-phenotype trials add obesity/diabetes gating not seen in the earlier RAAS/ARNI trials: STEP-HFpEF requires BMI ≥30 and excludes T2DM (source: Kosiborod2023STEPHFPEF), while STEP-HFpEF DM and SUMMIT require BMI ≥30 with T2DM permitted or required. Mechanistic/invasive-hemodynamic trials (RALI-DHF, REBALANCE-HF, DECIPHER-HFpEF) instead gate on invasively-measured filling pressure (e.g., exercise PCWP ≥25 mmHg) rather than biomarkers or comorbidity.

## Limitations

- This synthesis draws on registry-level entry criteria captured in [[trials]] at time of ingest; full protocol inclusion/exclusion lists (comorbidity exclusions, prior HF hospitalization requirements, renal/hepatic exclusions) are not exhaustively captured here and would need per-trial protocol review.
- Absence of a criterion in the table (e.g., no NP threshold recorded for TOPCAT) reflects what the wiki has recorded from ingested sources, not a verified negative — flagged rather than asserted.
- HFmrEF-inclusive trials (EMPEROR-Preserved, DELIVER, FINEARTS-HF, SPIRRIT-HFpEF; LVEF >40%) are not "pure" HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%) trials — see [[hfmref]] for the boundary distinction.
