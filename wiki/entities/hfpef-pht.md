# HFpEF-PHT

> Heart Centre Leipzig prospective observational cohort study of right ventricular function and pulmonary hypertension in HFpEF; its left-ventricular endomyocardial-biopsy sub-cohort provided the tissue for Capone 2026's integrated multi-omics characterisation of cardiometabolic HFpEF.

## Description

**Full title (per source paper):** Right Ventricular Function and Pulmonary Hypertension in HFpEF (registered as NCT05055180)
**Site:** Heart Centre Leipzig, University of Leipzig — outpatient clinics
**Design:** Prospective observational cohort, recruited 2019–2021
**Population:** Outpatients with suspected HFpEF per 2016 ESC guidelines (NYHA ≥II, LVEF ≥50%, structural/functional LV diastolic dysfunction, BMI ≥25 kg/m²), confirmed invasively (LVEDP >15 mmHg rest and/or ≥25 mmHg exercise); non-failing overweight/obese (NFO) comparator group recruited and excluded for suspected HFpEF; cardiac amyloidosis excluded (CMR + LV EMB when suspected)
**Biopsy sub-cohort N:** 23 (19 HFpEF, 4 NFO) underwent left-ventricular endomyocardial biopsy for multi-omics analysis — a subset of the wider HFpEF-PHT cohort

## Role in HFpEF

Provides tissue-level (not just circulating-biomarker) evidence for the cardiometabolic HFpEF phenotype: proximal glycolytic blockade, succinate/TCA-cycle accumulation, and EFEMP1-led ECM/fibrosis protein upregulation, distinguished from obesity-driven changes by comparing HFpEF against obese (not lean) controls. Sits alongside [[stiffmap]] and [[decipher-hfpef]] as part of the same Leipzig Heart Center invasive-phenotyping research programme (shared investigators Karl-Philipp Rommel and Philipp Lurz), though it is a distinct cohort/study, not a formal substudy.

## Evidence

Results reported in [[capone2026hfpefpht]]: HFpEF hearts (n=19) vs. NFO (n=4) showed diminished proximal glycolysis (↓GPI, ↓PFKM1 protein, accumulated G6P, reduced pyruvate) with preserved glucose oxidation (PDH/PDK4 unchanged — obesity-driven, not HFpEF-specific); TCA-cycle succinate accumulation with reduced fumarate hydratase, consistent with a pseudohypoxia signature; energy-deprivation signature (↑AMP, ↑ADP); largely preserved fatty-acid oxidation with selective very-long-chain acylcarnitine remodelling; upregulated ECM/fibrosis proteins (LTBP1, IGFBP2, EFEMP1, FBLN5, POSTN), with EFEMP1 most strongly correlated with fibrosis%, LVEF, E/e′, and NT-proBNP.
