# SOCRATES-PRESERVED

> Phase 2b trial of vericiguat in HFpEF: NT-proBNP reduction and LA volume signal at higher doses motivated the phase 3 VITALITY-HFpEF, which ultimately found no benefit — a cautionary example of surrogate endpoint prediction failure.

## Description

**Full title:** Soluble Guanylate Cyclase Stimulator in Heart Failure Patients with Preserved Ejection Fraction  
**NCT:** [verify on ingest]  
**Intervention:** Vericiguat (multiple doses tested: 1.25, 2.5, 5, 10 mg) vs. placebo  
**Population:** HFpEF, LVEF ≥45%, worsening HF (recent hospitalisation or IV diuretics)  
**N:** ~477  
**Follow-up:** 12 weeks  
**Primary endpoint:** Change in NT-proBNP and LA volume (co-primary)  
**Published:** Eur Heart J. 2017;38(15):1127–1138 (Pieske B, Maggioni AP, Lam CSP, et al.)

SOCRATES-PRESERVED was the dose-finding and signal-finding phase 2b trial for vericiguat in HFpEF. The trial design was directly parallel to SOCRATES-REDUCED (same drug in HFrEF). NT-proBNP and left atrial volume were chosen as surrogates because they had predicted outcomes in prior HFpEF trials.

## Role in HFpEF

SOCRATES-PRESERVED is the mechanistic predecessor to VITALITY-HFpEF. Its NT-proBNP signal at higher doses justified dose selection for the phase 3 trial. The ultimate failure of VITALITY-HFpEF to replicate clinical benefit illustrates:

1. NT-proBNP reduction as surrogate endpoint in HFpEF does not reliably predict symptom or outcome benefit
2. Phase 2b HFpEF signals require cautious interpretation given heterogeneity of the population

This is consistent with the broader pattern of HFpEF surrogate-to-outcome translation failures: PARAGON-HF showed NT-proBNP and LA volume improvement but narrowly missed the primary composite endpoint. (source: [[solomon2019paragon]])

The trial also provides context for the Pieske group's engagement with sGC stimulation in HFpEF — Burkert Pieske was involved in both SOCRATES-PRESERVED and the HFA-PEFF diagnostic algorithm. (source: [[pieske2019hfapeff]])

## Evidence

Co-primary endpoints: NT-proBNP and LA volume. At higher doses (10 mg), a signal for NT-proBNP reduction was observed. LA volume showed trend at higher doses. The signal was modest and dose-dependent, not statistically definitive across all dose groups. Phase 2b, not adequately powered for hard outcomes. [Source: Pieske2017SOCRATES — pending full ingest.]

## Status

**Published:** EHJ 2017. Phase 3 successor VITALITY-HFpEF (NCT03547583, N=789) neutral on KCCQ-PLS (P=0.47 for 15 mg and P=0.80 for 10 mg). The vericiguat programme in HFpEF is now inactive. (source: [[armstrong2020vitality]])

## Contradictions

SOCRATES-PRESERVED signal (NT-proBNP reduction at high dose) vs. VITALITY-HFpEF neutral (KCCQ-PLS, no outcomes benefit) — the signal did not translate to phase 3. This is a core example in [[contradictions]] of surrogate endpoint failure in HFpEF.
