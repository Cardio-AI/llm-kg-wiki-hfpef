# Spironolactone

> Mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (MRA); Class I for HFrEF; TOPCAT Americas subgroup positive in HFpEF but overall trial neutral; no ESC recommendation for HFpEF.

---

## Description

Spironolactone is a mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (MRA). It blocks aldosterone, reducing sodium retention, sympathetic activation, and myocardial fibrosis. Eplerenone is a more selective MRA that causes less gynaecomastia; both are used as MRAs in HF. Starting dose: 25 mg o.d.; target: 50 mg o.d. Caution when eGFR is impaired or serum potassium >5.0 mmol/L. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

## Role in HFpEF

Spironolactone was the leading pharmacological candidate for HFpEF given its anti-fibrotic and anti-aldosterone mechanisms, both relevant to [[diastolic-dysfunction]]. [[topcat]] was the definitive trial. Its overall neutral result combined with a positive Americas subgroup is one of the most contested findings in HFpEF pharmacotherapy. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

## Evidence

**HFrEF:** Class I, Level A for all patients to reduce mortality and HF hospitalizations. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

**HFpEF — [[topcat]]:**
- Overall: Neutral primary composite endpoint (CV death, resuscitated cardiac arrest, HF hospitalization)
- Americas subgroup: Significant reduction in primary endpoint
- Post-hoc EF analysis: Significant benefit for patients with LVEF <55%
- Suspected contamination: Very low urinary spironolactone metabolite levels in non-Americas (Russia/Georgia) controls suggest patients may not have received placebo — diluting a true treatment effect in the overall analysis

**HFmrEF:** Retrospective analysis of TOPCAT patients with LVEF 45–55% showed reduced HF hospitalizations with spironolactone. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

## Status

| Guideline | HFrEF | HFpEF |
|---|---|---|
| ESC 2021 | Class I, Level A | No recommendation |
| AHA 2022 | Class I, Level A | Class 2b, B-R |

**ESC 2021:** No recommendation for spironolactone specifically in [[hfpef]] or [[hfmref]]. Overall TOPCAT result insufficient for a positive recommendation. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

**AHA 2022:** **Class 2b, B-R** — MRA may be considered in HFpEF to decrease hospitalizations, based primarily on the TOPCAT Americas subgroup. Applicable to patients with LVEF ≥50% who tolerate the drug (adequate renal function and potassium). (source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)
