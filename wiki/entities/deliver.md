# DELIVER

> Dapagliflozin significantly reduced worsening HF and CV death in HF with LVEF >40% (HR 0.82, 95% CI 0.73–0.92, P<0.001); benefit consistent across all LVEF subgroups including ≥60%, in contrast to the attenuation seen at high LVEF in EMPEROR-Preserved; also improved KCCQ-TSS (win ratio 1.11, P=0.009).

## Description

- **Intervention:** Dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily vs. placebo
- **Population:** HF with LVEF >40% (includes HFmrEF 41–49% and HFpEF ≥50%, plus HFimpEF with prior LVEF ≤40% recovered to >40%), evidence of structural heart disease, elevated natriuretic peptides, age ≥40 (source: solomon2022deliver)
- **N:** 6,263; 353 centres, 20 countries; enrolled Aug 27, 2018–Dec 30, 2020; median follow-up 2.3 years (IQR 1.7–2.8); sponsor AstraZeneca (source: solomon2022deliver)
- **Primary endpoint:** Composite of worsening HF (hospitalisation or urgent visit) or CV death
- **Full paper stub:** [[solomon2022deliver]]

## Role in HFpEF

DELIVER is the second major positive [[sglt2-inhibitors]] outcomes trial in [[hfpef]]/[[hfmref]], published in 2022 as the direct companion to [[emperor-preserved]] (empagliflozin). Where EMPEROR-Preserved showed benefit attenuating at LVEF ≥60% (HR 0.87, CI crossing 1.0), DELIVER found **no evidence of EF heterogeneity** — benefit was consistent from LVEF ≤49% (HR 0.87) through 50–59% (HR 0.79) to ≥60% (HR 0.78). This resolved much of the uncertainty left by EMPEROR-Preserved about whether elevated-EF HFpEF truly benefits from SGLT2 inhibition, and together the two trials established the class effect across the full HF EF spectrum, incorporated into AHA/ACC 2022 guidelines at Class 2a. (source: solomon2022deliver)

## Evidence

- **Primary composite: POSITIVE** — HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.73–0.92), P<0.001; dapagliflozin 512/3,131 (16.4%) vs. placebo 610/3,132 (19.5%) (source: solomon2022deliver)
- **HF hospitalisation/urgent visit:** HR 0.79 (95% CI 0.69–0.91); total recurrent events rate ratio 0.77 (95% CI 0.67–0.89), P<0.001 (source: solomon2022deliver)
- **CV death:** HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.74–1.05) — not significant in isolation; all-cause death HR 0.94 (95% CI 0.83–1.07) — not significant; trial not powered for mortality alone (source: solomon2022deliver)
- **KCCQ-TSS at 8 months:** win ratio 1.11 (95% CI 1.03–1.21), P=0.009; mean placebo-corrected difference +2.4 points (95% CI 1.5–3.4) (source: solomon2022deliver)
- **No EF heterogeneity:** LVEF ≤49% HR 0.87 (0.72–1.04); 50–59% HR 0.79 (0.65–0.97); ≥60% HR 0.78 (0.62–0.98) — "the concern from EMPEROR-Preserved about attenuated benefit at LVEF ≥65% was not replicated" (source: solomon2022deliver)
- **Consistent across:** diabetes status, prior HF hospitalisation, prior LVEF ≤40% (HFimpEF, HR 0.74), sex (source: solomon2022deliver)
- **Safety:** profile similar to placebo; DKA 2 vs. 0; no Fournier's gangrene in either arm (source: solomon2022deliver)

## Status

- **AHA/ACC 2022:** Class 2a recommendation for SGLT2 inhibitors in HFpEF/HFmrEF, based on DELIVER + EMPEROR-Preserved together (source: solomon2022deliver)
- **ESC 2021:** No recommendation — DELIVER published in 2022, after the ESC 2021 guideline cutoff (source: solomon2022deliver)

## Contradictions
- DELIVER's KCCQ-TSS benefit (win ratio 1.11, P=0.009 at 8 months) contrasts with the neutral KCCQ result in DETERMINE-Preserved (same drug, dapagliflozin, P=0.079 at 16 weeks) — likely explained by different analytic approach (win ratio vs. change score) and longer follow-up in DELIVER. (source: solomon2022deliver)
- CV death alone remained non-significant (HR 0.88) as in EMPEROR-Preserved — the mortality benefit gap in HFpEF persists despite two positive composite-endpoint trials. (source: solomon2022deliver)
- ESC 2021 carries no recommendation while AHA 2022 endorses Class 2a on DELIVER + EMPEROR-Preserved data — guideline-timing discordance. (source: solomon2022deliver)
