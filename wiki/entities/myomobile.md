# MyoMobile

> MyoMobile (NCT04940312; N=185 HFpEF): app-based personalised PA coaching significantly increased daily step count vs. standard care at 6 months, with secondary KCCQ and 6MWT improvements — first positive digital health RCT in HFpEF. Primary results: JACC Heart Fail 2026 ([[zeid2026myomobile]]).

## Description

**Full title:** Personalized App-Based Coaching for Improving Physical Activity in HFpEF Patients Compared With Standard Care  
**NCT:** NCT04940312  
**Intervention:** App-based PA coaching (wearable step counter + personalised coaching app) vs. step tracking alone vs. standard care  
**Population:** HFpEF (LVEF >40%), NYHA I–III, age ≥45 years, baseline daily step count 1,000–10,000  
**N:** 185 (ITT); randomised 1:0.75:1  
**Duration:** 12 weeks  
**Design:** EE2 (Efficacy and Effectiveness simultaneously assessed in single trial)  
**Primary endpoint:** Change in average daily step count  
**Published:** Design paper + baseline characteristics: Zeid 2025 (Eur Heart J Digit Health)  
**Primary results:** Published — JACC Heart Fail 2026;14(5):102845 ([[zeid2026myomobile]]). App-based personalised PA coaching (arm 1) significantly increased daily step count vs. standard care; secondary improvements in KCCQ and 6MWT confirmed. First positive digital health RCT in HFpEF.  
**Centre:** Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz, DZHK Rhine-Main (PI: Philipp Wild)

Baseline characteristics (N=185): mean age 69.7y, LVEF 53.5%, NT-proBNP 418 pg/mL, E/e' 9.42, AF 51.9%, obesity 40.5%, T2DM 36.8%, CKD 10.8%, NYHA II 65.4%. (source: [[zeid2025myomobile]])

**Step-goal algorithm (Figure 3):** 5 most active days in prior week used to compute baseline. Stratified targets: Low (<2,500 steps/day): +500 steps; Normal (2,500–10,000): +10%; High (>10,000): maintain. Goals updated weekly.

## Role in HFpEF

MyoMobile addresses the implementation gap for supervised exercise training (SET): evidence-based SET programmes improve peak VO₂ (+2.8 mL/kg/min, Sachdev 2023 AHA Statement meta-analysis), but participation rates in supervised cardiac rehabilitation are <10% of eligible HF patients. App-based PA coaching is scalable to primary care and home settings. (source: [[sachdev2023exercise]])

Mechanistic hypothesis: even modest increases in daily step count can activate skeletal muscle mitochondrial adaptations, partially reversing the skeletal muscle myopathy that accounts for >50% of the VO₂ deficit in HFpEF. The IMT and caloric restriction data from Bohmke 2022 suggest that peripheral — not central — mechanisms drive most of the exercise limitation, supporting home-based peripheral interventions. (source: [[bohmke2022nonpharm]])

MyoMobile is the digital health companion to the [[myovasc]] registry and part of the same DZHK Rhine-Main infrastructure. The 100+ digital biomarkers (DMOs) and multi-omics biobank collected will extend beyond the primary step count endpoint.

## Evidence

Design paper: [[zeid2025myomobile]] (Eur Heart J Digit Health 2025;6:298–309). Primary results: [[zeid2026myomobile]] (JACC Heart Fail 2026;14(5):102845). App-based coaching significantly increased daily step count vs. standard care; secondary KCCQ and 6MWT improvements confirmed. Step count and KCCQ both improved — no dissociation (contrast with [[docherty2025determine]] concern).

## Contradictions

None registered. Complements [[docherty2025determine]] (DETERMINE accelerometry substudy) on whether step count captures functional status — Docherty 2025 found weak cross-correlation between accelerometry and KCCQ/6MWD, suggesting step count and functional status measure distinct dimensions. If MyoMobile improves step count without KCCQ improvement, this dissociation will require mechanistic explanation. See [[contradictions]].
