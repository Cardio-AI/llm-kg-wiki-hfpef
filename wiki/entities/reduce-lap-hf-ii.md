## Description

**Full title:** Reduce Elevated Left Atrial Pressure in Patients With Heart Failure — Phase II  
**NCT:** NCT03088033  
**Intervention:** Implantable interatrial shunt device (creates a controlled left-to-right atrial shunt to decompress elevated LA pressure) vs. sham  
**Population:** HFpEF/HFmrEF with elevated LA pressure, LVEF ≥40%  
**Status:** Published (approximate year 2022–2023); full details pending ingest

The mechanistic hypothesis: HFpEF is characterised by chronically elevated left atrial pressure → pulmonary venous hypertension → dyspnoea and exercise intolerance. An interatrial shunt creates a controlled opening between the atria, allowing excess LA pressure to decompress into the right atrium → right ventricle → pulmonary circulation → reduced LA pressure.

The device targets the haemodynamic consequence of diastolic stiffness rather than the underlying myocardial mechanism — a pressure relief valve rather than a disease-modifying approach.

**Note on result:** Subsequent to being referenced in Pfeffer 2019 as an ongoing trial, REDUCE LAP-HF II has been published with a neutral overall result. A pre-specified subgroup analysis found benefit in patients with lower pulmonary vascular resistance (PVR) — suggesting the shunt decompresses effectively in patients without established pulmonary hypertension, but is harmful or neutral when the right heart is already unable to handle the shunted volume. This is a clinically important phenotypic subgroup distinction. [Verify exact result details on ingest.]

## Role in HFpEF

REDUCE LAP-HF II is the most advanced device-based trial specifically targeting the haemodynamic mechanism of HFpEF. Referenced in Pfeffer 2019 (alongside the minimally invasive pericardial modification trial) as one of two device approaches to elevated filling pressures. (source: [[pfeffer2019hfpef]])

If the PVR subgroup result holds in confirmatory trials, it would represent the first device therapy with a defined responder phenotype in HFpEF — establishing pulmonary vascular resistance as a treatment-relevant biomarker for shunt device selection.

## Evidence

Overall primary endpoint: neutral (not significantly improved in full population). PVR subgroup: signal favouring shunt device in lower PVR patients (benefit/harm interaction). [Verify on ingest — approximate result from post-Pfeffer 2019 reports.]

## Status

**Published** (approximate 2022–2023; full reference pending ingest). The neutral overall result limits guideline incorporation but has motivated a refined responder trial targeting lower-PVR HFpEF.

## Contradictions

Neutral overall result vs. positive PVR-defined subgroup — a pattern of subgroup heterogeneity that characterises HFpEF more broadly. If the PVR subgroup result is confirmed, it would reframe REDUCE LAP-HF II as an enrichment failure rather than a mechanistic failure. See [[contradictions]].
