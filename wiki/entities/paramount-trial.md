# PARAMOUNT

> Phase 2 double-blind RCT (N=301) showing LCZ696 (sacubitril/valsartan precursor) reduced NT-proBNP 23% more than valsartan at 12 weeks (P=0.005) and produced significant left atrial reverse remodelling at 36 weeks in HFpEF; the biomarker/mechanistic bridge that directly motivated PARAGON-HF.

## Description

- **Intervention:** LCZ696 (sacubitril/valsartan; initially 50 mg BID, uptitrated to 200 mg BID) vs. valsartan (initially 40 mg BID, uptitrated to 160 mg BID) (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **Population:** HFpEF; LVEF ≥45%; NT-proBNP >400 pg/mL; NYHA II–III; sinus rhythm; N=301 (LCZ696 n=149; valsartan n=152) across 65 centres in 13 countries (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **Baseline characteristics:** Age 71 years; 57% female; LVEF 58% (79% ≥50%); AF 28%; hypertension 95%; diabetes 38–41%; NYHA II 82%, NYHA III 18% (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **Primary endpoint:** Ratio of NT-proBNP at 12 weeks (LCZ696/valsartan, adjusted for baseline) (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **Follow-up:** 36 weeks total (primary endpoint assessed at 12 weeks) (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **Full paper stub:** [[solomon2012paramount]]

## Role in HFpEF

PARAMOUNT is the Phase 2 predecessor trial that directly motivated [[paragon-hf]] — it uses the same drug (LCZ696/sacubitril-valsartan) at lower dose and shorter follow-up, established against valsartan (not placebo) as the active comparator. It was the first RCT evidence that an ARNi reduces NT-proBNP and improves left atrial (LA) remodelling in HFpEF, providing the mechanistic rationale for taking sacubitril/valsartan into the larger, clinical-outcomes-powered PARAGON-HF trial (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT). Because PARAMOUNT was underpowered for clinical outcomes and used a biomarker surrogate as its primary endpoint, its "positive" result functions as proof-of-concept rather than proof of clinical benefit — a distinction that became directly relevant when PARAGON-HF later missed its overall primary clinical endpoint (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT).

## Evidence

- **Primary endpoint MET:** NT-proBNP ratio LCZ696/valsartan = 0.77 (95% CI 0.64–0.92; P=0.005) at 12 weeks — 23% greater reduction with LCZ696 (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **36-week NT-proBNP:** ratio 0.85 (0.65–1.09; P=0.20) — difference no longer significant, suggesting an early biomarker effect that attenuates over time (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **LA reverse remodelling (36 weeks):** LA volume change −4.6 mL (LCZ696) vs. +0.37 mL (valsartan); P=0.003 — a structural benefit that persists beyond the point where the NT-proBNP effect attenuates (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **NYHA class:** significantly improved at 36 weeks (P=0.05) (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **Blood pressure:** SBP −9.3 mmHg (LCZ696) vs. −2.9 mmHg (valsartan; P=0.001); the NT-proBNP benefit at 12 weeks was independent of this BP difference in sensitivity analysis (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **Renal function:** eGFR decline smaller with LCZ696 (−1.6 vs. −5.2 mL/min/1.73m²; P=0.007) — a cardiorenal signal (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **No significant improvement** in diastolic function parameters (E/e', E/A ratio, e') at either timepoint, despite the NT-proBNP and LA volume benefits — suggesting these endpoints capture different domains of effect (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **Subgroup:** Diabetes subgroup showed significantly greater NT-proBNP reduction with LCZ696 (interaction P=0.02) (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)
- **Safety:** SAE rate 15% (LCZ696) vs. 20% (valsartan; P=0.32); 1 mild angioedema case (LCZ696, no hospitalisation), 0 (valsartan) (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT)

## Status

Phase 2 mechanistic/biomarker trial — not a regulatory or guideline-defining trial in its own right. Its clinical relevance is entirely as the bridge to [[paragon-hf]], which tested the same drug for clinical outcomes at higher dose in a larger, longer trial (source: Solomon2012PARAMOUNT).

## Contradictions
- NT-proBNP benefit was significant at 12 weeks (P=0.005) but not at 36 weeks (P=0.20), while LA volume structural benefit persisted at 36 weeks — a temporal dissociation between biomarker and structural endpoints. See [[contradictions]] and [[solomon2012paramount]].
