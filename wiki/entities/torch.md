# TORCH / TORCH-Plus Registry

> German national multi-centre registry and biobank for non-ischemic cardiomyopathies, operated under the DZHK (German Centre for Cardiovascular Research); provides a deeply phenotyped cohort linking clinical, genomic, and molecular data across two phases (TORCH: 2,300 patients; TORCH-Plus: 4,340 patients).

## Description

The DZHK **TranslatiOnal Registry for CardiomyopatHies (TORCH)** was established in December 2014 as a multi-centre, prospective registry and biobank for patients with primary non-ischemic cardiomyopathies (CMP) at 19 German university hospitals participating in the DZHK network. (source: Seyler2017TORCH)

**Primary goal:** Link comprehensive clinical phenotyping with molecular-level profiling (genomics, transcriptomics, inflammation, metabolomics) to understand disease mechanisms and identify prognostic biomarkers and precision therapy targets.

**Two phases:**
- **TORCH / Phase 1** (NCT02187263): n=2,300; DCM, HCM, ARVC, LVNC, myocarditis; 12-month follow-up; completed recruitment ~2018–2019
- **TORCH-Plus / Phase 2** (NCT04265040): n=4,340; expanded amyloid, LVNC, and familial DCM arms; 4-year follow-up; ongoing

---

## Sites and Countries

- **19 DZHK university hospital centres** in Germany
- Coordinating centre: University Hospital Heidelberg (PI: Prof. Hugo Katus; admin: Benjamin Meder)
- All sites are German DZHK partner sites — no international enrolment in Phase 1; Germany-only in both phases
- (source: Seyler2017TORCH)

## CMP Subtypes Enrolled

| Subtype | ~% (Phase 1) |
|---|---|
| Dilated Cardiomyopathy (DCM) | ~56% |
| Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM / HOCM) | ~15% |
| ARVC/D | ~10% |
| LVNC | ~8% |
| Acute myocarditis / inflammatory CMP | ~7% |
| Cardiac amyloidosis (AL, TTR) | ~4% |

---

## Eligibility Summary

**Inclusion:** Non-ischemic structural CMP; age 18–79 (Phase 1), 18–80 (Phase 2); able to consent; ≥1 of the listed CMP diagnoses.

**Exclusion:** Ischemic heart disease; significant valvular disease; uncontrolled hypertension; primary pulmonary hypertension; cardiotoxin/radiation exposure; drug/alcohol abuse; tachymyopathy; life expectancy <1 year from non-cardiac cause.

---

## Parameters Collected

**Obligatory (all patients):**
- TTE (echocardiography): LVEF, volumes, mass, diastolic parameters, RVEF
- 12-lead ECG
- Laboratory: NT-proBNP, troponin, creatinine/eGFR, CRP, electrolytes, HbA1c, lipids, CBC
- NYHA class, medications, MACE events

**Optional/subgroup:**
- Cardiac MRI (CMR): LVEF, LGE, T1/T2 mapping
- Endomyocardial biopsy: histology, viral PCR, immunohistology
- CPET

**Biobank:**
- Serum, EDTA-plasma, citrate-plasma, urine, RNA, genomic DNA, PBMCs

## Registry Modules

| Module | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1. Clinical phenotyping + follow-up | Longitudinal outcomes; biosampling |
| 2. Genomics | NGS, WES, genotype-phenotype; rare variant detection |
| 3. Inflammation | Viral load, cytokine profiling, immune cell phenotyping |
| 4. Biomarker | Novel circulating markers; outcome and genotype prediction |

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

TORCH is not an HFpEF-specific registry — most patients have reduced EF (DCM) or hypertrophic/infiltrative disease. Relevance:
- HCM and amyloid cohorts overlap with HFpEF differential diagnosis
- Demonstrates deep molecular phenotyping methodology applicable to future HFpEF sub-phenotyping studies
- DECIPHER-HFpEF (see [[decipher-hfpef]]) is the parallel DZHK initiative specifically targeting HFpEF diagnosis
- TORCH-Plus amyloid arm directly relevant to distinguishing ATTR-CM from HFpEF
