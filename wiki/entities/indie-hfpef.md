# INDIE-HFpEF

> A single acute dose of concentrated inorganic nitrate (beetroot juice, 12.9 mmol NO₃⁻) was neutral on its primary endpoint of exercise efficiency (P=0.64) but significantly improved peak VO₂ (+1.0 mL/min/kg, P=0.005) and total work (+6.5 kJ, P=0.04) via peripheral haemodynamic augmentation (↑cardiac output +32.5%, ↓SVR −10.6%) — improving performance through the periphery rather than through cardiac oxygen efficiency.

## Description

- **Intervention:** A single dose of concentrated NO₃⁻-rich beetroot juice (12.9 mmol NO₃⁻, 140 mL; BEET IT Sport) vs. identical nitrate-depleted placebo, given 3 hours before maximal-effort supine-cycle CPET, in a randomised double-blind crossover design with ≥5-day washout (source: zamani2015indie)
- **Population:** HFpEF, LVEF >50%, symptomatic HF with elevated filling pressures (E/e' >8 plus ≥1 of: enlarged LA, elevated NT-proBNP, or PCWP >12 mmHg on prior catheterisation) (source: zamani2015indie)
- **N:** 17 (modified ITT; 20 entered); single centre, University of Pennsylvania; NIH-sponsored (source: zamani2015indie)
- **Primary endpoint:** Exercise efficiency (total work performed / total O₂ consumed) during maximal-effort CPET
- **Full paper stub:** [[zamani2015indie]]

## Role in HFpEF

INDIE-HFpEF sits in the same NO/cGMP-pathway trial cluster as [[neat-hfpef]] (isosorbide mononitrate), [[relax]] (sildenafil/PDE5i), [[vitality-hfpef]] (vericiguat), and [[capacity-hfpef]] (praliciguat), but tests a mechanistically distinct intervention: a single acute dose of dietary inorganic nitrate (NO₃⁻ → NO₂⁻ → NO via oral bacteria/gut), rather than a chronic organic nitrate or sGC-pathway drug. Its primary endpoint was neutral, but the significant peak VO₂ and haemodynamic improvements — driven by peripheral vasodilation and increased cardiac output rather than improved oxygen efficiency — distinguish it from the other pathway trials and place the exercise-intolerance bottleneck at the peripheral/vascular level for this acute intervention, complementing the central diastolic-filling-limitation hypothesis. (source: zamani2015indie)

## Evidence

- **Primary (exercise efficiency): NEUTRAL** — 4.5±0.8 vs. 4.6±1.1 kJ/L O₂; difference −0.1±1.0, P=0.64 (source: zamani2015indie)
- **Peak VO₂: SIGNIFICANT** — 12.6±3.7 vs. 11.6±3.1 mL O₂/min/kg; difference +1.0±1.2, P=0.005 (source: zamani2015indie)
- **Total work performed:** +6.5±11.9 kJ, P=0.04; exercise duration +0.8 min, P=0.02 (source: zamani2015indie)
- **Cardiac output at peak exercise:** +32.5±41.0% vs. placebo, P=0.006; **SVR:** −10.6±16.9%, P=0.03 — confirms peripheral haemodynamic mechanism (source: zamani2015indie)
- **Aortic augmentation index:** −9.1±15.4%, P=0.03 — reduced arterial wave reflections (source: zamani2015indie)
- **Steady-state VO₂ (25W constant intensity):** no difference, P=0.70 — benefit specific to peak/maximal exertion, not submaximal steady state (source: zamani2015indie)
- **Plasma NOₓ confirmed bioavailability:** 326 vs. 10 µmol/L, P=0.0003 (source: zamani2015indie)
- Baseline: 88% male, 82% Black, 100% hypertensive, 94% obese, 71% diabetic; only 53% met ESC HFpEF criteria; median NT-proBNP 144 pg/mL — below most modern HFpEF entry thresholds (source: zamani2015indie)

## Status

- No guideline recommendation; INDIE-HFpEF is a small (N=17), single-dose mechanistic pilot, not a therapeutic outcomes trial (source: zamani2015indie)
- Interpreted as mechanistic evidence that acute inorganic nitrate augments peripheral haemodynamics in HFpEF, but whether benefit persists with chronic dosing is untested (source: zamani2015indie)

## Contradictions
- INDIE-HFpEF (single-dose inorganic NO₃⁻: peak VO₂ improved, P=0.005) contrasts with [[neat-hfpef]] (chronic organic nitrate: activity worsened, dose-dependently). Same NO-pathway target, opposite direction. Possible explanations: acute vs. chronic exposure (nitrate tolerance develops rapidly with continuous organic nitrate); inorganic (dietary, oral-bacteria-dependent) vs. organic nitrate donor pathways; supine (INDIE) vs. ambulatory/upright exercise (NEAT). Neither study is definitive; together they suggest NO-pathway manipulation is not a simple "more NO = better" relationship. (source: zamani2015indie; redfield2015neat)
- Primary endpoint neutral (P=0.64) while multiple secondary endpoints were positive, without correction for multiple comparisons — the positive secondaries may partly represent Type I error, though the internal consistency across haemodynamic measures (cardiac output, SVR, heart rate, augmentation index) strengthens the mechanistic narrative. (source: zamani2015indie)
