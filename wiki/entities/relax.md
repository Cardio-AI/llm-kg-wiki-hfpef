# RELAX

> Phosphodiesterase-5 inhibition with sildenafil failed to improve peak VO₂ or clinical status in HFpEF — a key null result in the NO/cGMP therapeutic hypothesis.

## Description

**Full title:** Phosphodiesterase-5 Inhibition to Improve Clinical Status and Exercise Capacity in Diastolic Heart Failure  
**NCT:** NCT00763867  
**Intervention:** Sildenafil (titrated to 60–120 mg/day) vs. placebo  
**Population:** HFpEF, LVEF ≥50%, exertional limitation (NYHA II–III), stable on therapy  
**N:** 216  
**Duration:** 24 weeks  
**Primary endpoint:** Peak VO₂  
**Published:** JAMA 2013 (Redfield MM, Chen HH, Borlaug BA, et al.)

RELAX was a rigorously conducted NHLBI-sponsored trial testing whether PDE5 inhibition — which increases cGMP by preventing its degradation — would improve exercise tolerance in HFpEF. The mechanistic rationale: impaired cGMP/PKG signalling leads to titin hypophosphorylation → increased myocardial stiffness (a core HFpEF mechanism). Sildenafil was known to increase cGMP via PDE5 inhibition and had shown benefit in pulmonary arterial hypertension. The HFpEF hypothesis was that increased cGMP would reduce myocardial stiffness, improve diastolic function, and reduce filling pressures at rest and exercise.

## Role in HFpEF

RELAX is one of 4 NO/cGMP pathway trials that collectively show the pathway cannot be rescued by substrate-level or degradation-level interventions in the full HFpEF population:

| Trial | Drug | Mechanism | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| RELAX | Sildenafil | PDE5i (↑cGMP by ↓degradation) | Neutral |
| NEAT-HFpEF | Isosorbide mononitrate | Organic nitrate (↑NO → cGMP) | Neutral + harm |
| INDIE-HFpEF | Inorganic nitrate (KNO₃) | Inorganic nitrate (↑NO → cGMP) | Neutral |
| VITALITY-HFpEF | Vericiguat | sGC stimulator (↑cGMP) | Neutral |
| SOCRATES-PRESERVED | Vericiguat | sGC stimulator | Phase 2b signal (NT-proBNP) |

The convergent failure of these trials in the full HFpEF population suggests either: (1) the cGMP/PKG pathway deficit is not the principal driver of symptoms in most HFpEF patients; (2) phenotypic heterogeneity dilutes any responder subgroup; or (3) exercise intolerance in HFpEF is primarily peripheral (skeletal muscle myopathy) not cardiac. (source: [[sachdev2023exercise]], [[pfeffer2019hfpef]])

## Evidence

| Endpoint | Placebo | Sildenafil | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peak VO₂ change at 24 wk (mL/kg/min) | −0.20 | −0.20 | 0.90 |
| 6MWD change (m) | +15.0 | +5.0 | 0.92 |
| Clinical rank score | 95.8 | 94.2 | 0.85 |
| MLHFQ change | −8 | −8 | 0.44 |

**Safety signal (sildenafil vs. placebo):** creatinine +0.05 mg/dL (P=0.047); cystatin C increased (P=0.01); NT-proBNP +15 pg/mL (P=0.03); endothelin-1 +0.38 (P=0.046). **cGMP increased** with sildenafil (P=0.006) — target engagement confirmed but no functional benefit.

Baseline: median age 69y, 48% women, BMI 32.9 kg/m², peak VO₂ 11.7 mL/kg/min (41% predicted), 6MWD 308m, NT-proBNP 700 pg/mL, chronotropic incompetence 77%. (source: [[redfield2013relax]])

Hypothesis 7 in Ho 2019: RELAX criteria defined one of 7 HFpEF enrollment definitions applied to the same cohort — enrolled ~50% of the reference cohort (sensitivity ~50%, specificity ~70% for invasive HFpEF). (source: [[ho2019hfpefdefinitions]])

## Status

**Published:** JAMA 2013. No guideline recommendation for sildenafil or PDE5 inhibitors in HFpEF (ESC 2021/2023, AHA 2022). RELAX result contributed to the failed NO/cGMP hypothesis in HFpEF.

## Contradictions

SOCRATES-PRESERVED (vericiguat phase 2b) showed a signal for NT-proBNP reduction at higher doses, motivating VITALITY-HFpEF phase 3 which was then neutral. The SOCRATES signal may reflect dose-response relationship noise or NT-proBNP as a poor surrogate for clinical outcomes in this context. See [[contradictions]].
