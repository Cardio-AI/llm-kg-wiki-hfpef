# EMPEROR-Preserved

> Empagliflozin significantly reduced CV death + HF hospitalization vs. placebo in HFpEF/HFmrEF (HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.69–0.90, P<0.001) — the first major positive outcomes RCT in HFpEF; benefit driven primarily by reduced HF hospitalizations and attenuated at LVEF ≥60%.

## Description

- **Intervention:** Empagliflozin 10 mg once daily vs. placebo
- **Population:** HFpEF/HFmrEF, LVEF >40%, NYHA II–IV; NT-proBNP >300 pg/mL (no AF) or >900 pg/mL (AF); ≥1 echocardiographic criterion (LA volume index >34 mL/m², LA diameter >3.8 cm, LV wall thickness ≥1.1 cm, or E/e' ≥10); eGFR ≥20 mL/min/1.73m² (source: anker2021emperor)
- **N:** 5,988 (empagliflozin 2,997; placebo 2,991); 622 centers, 23 countries; enrolled March 2017–April 2020; median follow-up 26.2 months (source: anker2021emperor)
- **Primary endpoint:** CV death + HF hospitalization (time to first event)
- **Full paper stub:** [[anker2021emperor]]; secondary analysis: [[ferreira2026emperor]]

## Role in HFpEF

EMPEROR-Preserved is the first major positive outcomes RCT in [[hfpef]], breaking a long catalogue of neutral pharmacological trials ([[topcat]], [[i-preserve]], [[charm-preserved]]). Published in August 2021, after the ESC 2021 guideline cutoff, it established [[sglt2-inhibitors]] as a disease-modifying class across the EF spectrum once paired with its companion trial [[deliver]] (dapagliflozin). Both were incorporated into the AHA/ACC 2022 guideline update at Class 2a. (source: anker2021emperor)

The trial's central limitation is that benefit attenuates with rising LVEF (HR 0.71 at LVEF <50% vs. HR 0.87, CI crossing 1.0, at LVEF ≥60%), leaving open whether truly elevated-EF HFpEF derives meaningful benefit — a question [[deliver]] subsequently addressed with a broader, more consistent EF-response profile. (source: anker2021emperor)

## Evidence

- **Primary endpoint: POSITIVE** — HR 0.79 (95% CI 0.69–0.90; P<0.001); empagliflozin 415/2997 (13.8%) vs. placebo 511/2991 (17.1%) (source: anker2021emperor)
- **HF hospitalization:** HR 0.71 (95% CI 0.60–0.83); total (recurrent) HF hospitalizations HR 0.73 (95% CI 0.61–0.88), P<0.001 — the dominant driver of the composite benefit (source: anker2021emperor)
- **CV death:** HR 0.91 (95% CI 0.76–1.09) — numerically lower, not statistically significant; all-cause mortality HR 1.00 (95% CI 0.87–1.15) (source: anker2021emperor)
- **eGFR slope:** −1.25 vs. −2.62 mL/min/1.73m²/yr; difference +1.36 (95% CI 1.06–1.66), P<0.001 — renal-protective signal (source: anker2021emperor)
- **KCCQ-CSS at 52 weeks:** +4.51 vs. +3.18 pts; difference +1.32 (95% CI 0.45–2.19) (source: anker2021emperor)
- **LVEF-stratified benefit:** LVEF <50% HR 0.71; ≥50–<60% HR 0.80; ≥60% HR 0.87 (95% CI 0.69–1.10) — attenuation at the highest EF stratum (source: anker2021emperor)
- **Consistent across T2DM status:** T2DM present HR 0.79; T2DM absent HR 0.78; interaction not significant (source: anker2021emperor)
- **Serum magnesium secondary analysis (Ferreira 2026):** lower baseline Mg predicted worse outcomes on placebo; empagliflozin raised serum Mg (~0.05–0.08 mmol/L above placebo by 3 months); paradoxically, patients in the *highest* baseline Mg quintiles derived the *greatest* relative benefit (HR ~0.65–0.70) vs. the lowest quintile (HR ~0.90–0.95) — a direction opposite to reported HFrEF Mg-SGLT2i interactions (source: ferreira2026emperor)

## Status

- **AHA/ACC 2022:** Class 2a recommendation for SGLT2 inhibitors in HFpEF, based on EMPEROR-Preserved + DELIVER (source: anker2021emperor)
- **ESC 2021:** No recommendation — EMPEROR-Preserved was published after the guideline's evidence cutoff (source: anker2021emperor)
- Serum Mg findings (Ferreira 2026) are hypothesis-generating; no guideline impact — Mg is not a treatment-selection biomarker at present (source: ferreira2026emperor)

## Contradictions
- Published after the ESC 2021 cutoff, so ESC 2021 carries no recommendation while AHA 2022 endorses Class 2a on the same data — a guideline-timing discordance. (source: anker2021emperor)
- Benefit is strongest at LVEF <50% (HR 0.71) and weakest/non-significant at LVEF ≥60% (HR 0.87, CI crosses 1.0) — whether the highest-EF HFpEF stratum truly benefits remains open; [[deliver]] partially addresses this with a more EF-consistent effect. (source: anker2021emperor)
- Serum Mg-treatment interaction direction is reversed relative to reported HFrEF SGLT2i data (higher Mg → more benefit in HFpEF vs. lower Mg → more benefit in HFrEF) — unresolved whether this reflects true phenotype-specific biology or statistical noise. (source: ferreira2026emperor)
