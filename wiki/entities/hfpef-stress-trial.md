# HFpEF-Stress Trial

> Single-centre German diagnostic trial (Göttingen, DZHK-17) validating real-time exercise-stress cardiac MRI against invasive right heart catheterisation in HFpEF; exercise left atrial long-axis strain achieved AUC 0.93, the best-performing noninvasive marker tested.

## Description

**Full title:** Cardiovascular Magnetic Resonance Real-time Exercise Stress Testing in Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction
**NCT:** NCT03260621 · **DZHK ID:** DZHK-17
**Sponsor/site:** University Hospital Göttingen (single centre)
**PI:** Andreas Schuster, MD, PhD, MBA, University of Göttingen
**Funding:** Deutsches Zentrum für Herz-Kreislauf-Forschung (DZHK)
**N (actual):** 75 recruited (68 analysed: 34 HFpEF, 34 noncardiac dyspnea) — enrolled 08/2017–09/2019
**Status:** Completed; results published

A prospective, single-centre diagnostic-accuracy trial: patients with echocardiographic diastolic dysfunction (E/e' ≥8) and preserved LVEF (≥50%) referred for right heart catheterisation underwent additional real-time free-breathing cardiac MRI (RT-CMR) at rest and during supine exercise stress. HFpEF status was defined by the RHC reference standard (PCWP ≥15 mmHg rest or ≥25 mmHg exercise); patients not meeting this were classified as noncardiac dyspnea.

## Role in HFpEF

Tests whether RT-CMR — a fully noninvasive modality — can replace or supplement invasive RHC (the diagnostic gold standard, see [[hfpef-diagnosis]]) by measuring cardiac mechanics during actual physiological exercise rather than post-exercise. This directly targets the invasiveness barrier that limits RHC to specialist centres. Distinct from [[decipher-hfpef]] and [[stiffmap]] (both also CMR-vs-invasive validation studies at other German DZHK sites) in using real-time (not gated/breath-hold) acquisition specifically to capture cardiac mechanics *during* exercise stress rather than only at rest.

## Evidence

Exercise left atrial long-axis strain (LAS) was the single best-performing parameter: AUC 0.82 at rest, improving to 0.93 during exercise (P=0.029 for the rest-to-exercise improvement) — outperforming NT-proBNP (AUC 0.83), echocardiographic E/e' (AUC 0.80 rest, falling to 0.61 exercise), and both the H₂FPEF (AUC 0.72) and HFA-PEFF (AUC 0.77–0.78) clinical scores. LA emptying fraction was also a strong discriminator (AUC 0.73 rest → 0.83 exercise). Conventional LV volumetric CMR parameters performed poorly (AUC 0.51–0.60). No difference was found in native T1/ECV (diffuse fibrosis markers) between groups at rest in this cohort. See [[backhaus2021hfpefstress]] for full results.
