# SGLT2 Inhibitors

> Sodium-glucose co-transporter 2 inhibitors (dapagliflozin, empagliflozin); the fourth pillar of HFrEF therapy (ESC 2021 Class I); first drug class with guideline-level recommendation above Class 2b for HFpEF (AHA 2022 Class 2a, based on EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER).

---

## Description

SGLT2 inhibitors block sodium-glucose co-transporter 2 in the renal proximal tubule, causing glucosuria and natriuresis. Proposed cardioprotective mechanisms beyond glycaemic control include preload reduction (osmotic diuresis), improved cardiometabolic parameters, potential myocardial energetics benefits, and slowing of eGFR decline in CKD. Main agents in HF trials: dapagliflozin and empagliflozin. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

## Role in HFpEF

**AHA 2022 — Class 2a, B-R:** SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin or dapagliflozin) are recommended for patients with HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%) to decrease HF hospitalization and CV death. This represents the first pharmacological therapy with a guideline recommendation above Class 2b for the full HFpEF population. (source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)

**ESC 2021 — No recommendation:** At time of the 2021 ESC Guidelines, no SGLT2i results in [[hfpef]] were available — EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER were ongoing. The guidelines noted this as a **major knowledge gap** and made no HFpEF recommendation. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

This discordance between ESC 2021 and AHA 2022 is entirely explained by publication timing: EMPEROR-Preserved was published August 2021 (after ESC cutoff); DELIVER in 2022. See [[guideline-comparison]].

## Evidence

**HFrEF:**
- **DAPA-HF** (dapagliflozin 10 mg): 26% relative risk reduction in primary composite of worsening HF (hospitalization or urgent IV therapy) or CV death in [[hfref]] patients with and without T2DM (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)
- **EMPEROR-Reduced** (empagliflozin 10 mg): 25% reduction in combined CV death or HF hospitalization in NYHA II–IV with LVEF ≤40%; also slowed decline in eGFR (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)
- Meta-analysis of DAPA-HF and EMPEROR-Reduced: no heterogeneity in CV mortality benefit (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

**HFpEF/HFmrEF (LVEF >40%):**
- **EMPEROR-Preserved** ([[anker2021emperor]]; empagliflozin 10 mg, N=5,988, 622 sites/23 countries): Significantly reduced CV death + HF hospitalization (HR ~0.79, p<0.001). First major positive RCT in HFpEF. Benefit driven primarily by HF hospitalizations. LVEF >40% (includes HFmrEF). (source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)
- **DELIVER** ([[solomon2022deliver]]; dapagliflozin 10 mg in HFpEF/HFmrEF): Positive; together with EMPEROR-Preserved established SGLT2i across the EF spectrum.
- **Serum Mg interaction** ([[ferreira2026emperor]]): Secondary analysis of EMPEROR-Preserved showing empagliflozin raises serum Mg; higher baseline Mg → greater relative treatment benefit in HFpEF. Mechanism unclear; opposite direction from HFrEF data. (source: 2026-JACCHF-Ferreira-Findings-EMPEROR-Preserved.pdf)
- **CV risk reduction in T2DM**: Class I, Level A for patients with T2DM at CV risk or established CVD (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

## Status

| Guideline | HFrEF | HFmrEF | HFpEF |
|---|---|---|---|
| ESC 2021 | Class I, Level A | Class 2a (individualised) | No recommendation |
| AHA 2022 | Class I, Level A | Class 2a, B-R | **Class 2a, B-R** |

**ESC 2021:** Class I, Level A for NYHA II–IV, LVEF ≤40%, regardless of diabetes status. No recommendation for HFpEF (EMPEROR-Preserved pending). (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

**AHA 2022:** Class 2a, B-R for HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%) — reduces HF hospitalization and CV death. First pharmacological HFpEF recommendation above Class 2b. (source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)

**Safety:** Minor increase in genital fungal infections. Early reversible eGFR decline on initiation — should not prompt discontinuation. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)
