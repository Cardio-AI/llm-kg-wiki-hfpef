# BalanceD-HF

> Phase III AstraZeneca trial testing balcinrenone/dapagliflozin combination against dapagliflozin alone on cardiovascular death and heart failure events in patients with chronic heart failure and impaired kidney function who have had a recent HF event.

## Description

**Full title:** A Phase III, Randomised, Double-blind Study to Evaluate the Effect of Balcinrenone/Dapagliflozin, Compared With Dapagliflozin, on the Risk of Heart Failure Events and Cardiovascular Death in Patients With Heart Failure and Impaired Kidney Function
**Registration source:** ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT06307652, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06307652, accessed 2026-09-21
**Sponsor:** AstraZeneca
**Status:** Recruiting
**Design:** Phase III, international, multicentre, randomised, double-blind, parallel-group, double-dummy, active-controlled, event-driven; ~700 sites in ~40 countries
**N (estimated):** 3,850, randomised 1:1:1

**Arms:** (1) Balcinrenone/dapagliflozin 15mg/10mg + matching dapagliflozin placebo; (2) Balcinrenone/dapagliflozin 40mg/10mg + matching dapagliflozin placebo; (3) Dapagliflozin 10mg + matching balcinrenone/dapagliflozin placebo. All once daily on top of standard-of-care background therapy.

**Population:** Chronic heart failure, impaired kidney function, recent HF event. (The registry page as read does not state an explicit LVEF eligibility threshold — [needs source] if this needs confirming for HFpEF-relevance scoping.)

**Purpose (per registry page):** Evaluate whether balcinrenone/dapagliflozin reduces CV death and HF events (with and without hospitalisation) compared with dapagliflozin alone, on top of other standard-of-care drug classes.
