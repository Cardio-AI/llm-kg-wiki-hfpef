# ALDO-DHF

> RCT of spironolactone in ambulatory HFpEF: improved diastolic filling (E/e') and LV mass but did not improve maximal exercise capacity (peak VO₂) or symptoms. First large multicenter trial demonstrating structural reverse remodelling in HFpEF.

## Description

**Full title:** Aldosterone Receptor Blockade in Diastolic Heart Failure (Aldo-DHF)  
**Trial registration:** ISRCTN94726526; Eudra-CT No: 2006-002605-31 (European registration; no NCT)  
**Intervention:** Spironolactone 25 mg once daily vs. matching placebo (no up-titration)  
**Population:** Ambulatory HFpEF; age ≥50y; NYHA II–III; LVEF ≥50%; diastolic dysfunction grade ≥I; peak VO₂ ≤25 mL/kg/min  
**N:** 422 (213 spiro; 209 placebo)  
**Duration:** 12 months (mean 11.6 months); March 2007 – April 2012  
**Sites:** 10 in Germany and Austria  
**Published:** JAMA 2013;309(8):781–791 (Edelmann F, Wachter R, Schmidt AG, Pieske B, et al.)

## Key Results

**Co-primary endpoints (equally ranked):**
1. E/e' medial velocity ratio: −1.5 (95% CI −2.0 to −0.9); P<0.001 ✓ MET
2. Peak VO₂: +0.1 mL/min/kg (−0.6 to +0.8); P=0.81 ✗ NOT MET

**Structural remodelling (spiro vs. placebo):**
- LV mass index: −6 g/m² (−10 to −1); P=0.009
- LV end-diastolic diameter: −1.4 mm (−2.5 to −0.3); P=0.01
- NT-proBNP: geometric mean ratio 0.86 (0.75–0.99); P=0.03

**Unchanged with spironolactone:**
- Peak VO₂, 6-minute walk (−15 m; P=0.02 — paradoxically slightly worse), NYHA class, QoL (MLHFQ, SF-36, PHQ-9)

**Safety:** K +0.2 mmol/L; eGFR −5 mL/min/1.73m²; no hyperkalemia hospitalisations; gynecomastia requiring dose reduction in some patients

## Population Context

ALDO-DHF enrolled a lower-risk, "purer" HFpEF population than TOPCAT or FINEARTS-HF:
- Mean LVEF 67% (vs. TOPCAT ~59%; FINEARTS-HF 52.6%)
- Median NT-proBNP 158 ng/L (vs. TOPCAT ~750 pg/mL; FINEARTS-HF 1040 pg/mL)
- Ambulatory only; 86% NYHA II; exclusively LVEF ≥50%
- Shorter follow-up (12 months) — no mortality or hospitalisation data

## Significance

First large multicenter RCT to demonstrate **structural reverse cardiac remodelling** (LV mass, diastolic filling parameters) in HFpEF. The disconnect between structural improvement and functional/symptomatic outcomes is a central finding: it either reflects (a) insufficient dose/duration for functional recovery, (b) a floor effect in a low-risk population, or (c) E/e' as an inadequate surrogate for patient-relevant outcomes.

The study preceded [[topcat]] (published 2014) and [[finearts-hf]] (published 2024) and provided biological plausibility for MRA trials in HFpEF.
