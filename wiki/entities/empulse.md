# EMPULSE

> RCT demonstrating that empagliflozin initiated in-hospital during acute HF is safe and beneficial (win ratio 1.36) across the LVEF spectrum, including HFpEF.

## Description

**Full title:** Empagliflozin in Patients Hospitalized for Acute Heart Failure  
**NCT:** NCT04157751  
**Intervention:** Empagliflozin 10 mg vs. placebo, initiated in-hospital in acute/decompensated HF  
**Population:** Acute/decompensated HF across LVEF spectrum (HFrEF, HFmrEF, HFpEF); required haemodynamic stability  
**N:** ~530  
**Follow-up:** 90 days  
**Primary endpoint:** Hierarchical composite win ratio (death, HF event, change in KCCQ-TSS)  
**Published:** Lancet 2022 (Voors AA, Angermann CE, Teerlink JR, et al.)

EMPULSE addresses the practical clinical question of whether SGLT2i can and should be started during an acute HF admission, rather than waiting for outpatient stabilisation. Prior to this trial, SGLT2i were typically initiated or continued at discharge but rarely started de novo during acute decompensation due to concerns about renal function, blood pressure, and haemodynamic instability.

## Role in HFpEF

EMPULSE enrolled HFpEF patients and found benefit consistent across LVEF groups. This is clinically critical: the ESC 2023 Class I recommendation for SGLT2i in HFpEF creates a practical gap — when should initiation occur if the patient presents acutely decompensated? EMPULSE provides the evidence base for in-hospital initiation across all EF groups.

Mechanistically, empagliflozin in acute HF may provide haemoconcentration and osmotic diuresis complementary to loop diuretic therapy, with potential cardiorenal protective effects. (source: [[anker2021emperor]], [[packer2020emperor]])

## Evidence

Primary endpoint win ratio: 1.36 (95% CI 1.09–1.68; P=0.0054) — empagliflozin superior to placebo. Benefits observed across LVEF strata including HFpEF. No excess of renal adverse events or hypotension in the in-hospital initiation group. [Source: Voors2022EMPULSE — pending full ingest for exact subgroup breakdown.]

## Status

**Published:** Lancet 2022. Supports the ESC 2023 Class I recommendation for SGLT2i in HFpEF and the broader adoption of in-hospital SGLT2i initiation. (source: [[mcdonagh2023escupdate]])
