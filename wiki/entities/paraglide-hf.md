# PARAGLIDE-HF

> Sacubitril/valsartan (Sac/Val) vs. valsartan in 467 patients with LVEF >40% and recent worsening HF (WHF) event: significant time-averaged NT-proBNP reduction with Sac/Val overall (ratio 0.85, 95% CI 0.73–0.999), driven by the LVEF ≤60% subgroup; diverse population (52% women, 22% Black); Sac/Val excess symptomatic hypotension (24.0% vs 15.5%) especially in women and LVEF >60%.

## Trial Overview

**Full title:** Prospective Comparison of ARNI with ARB Given following stabilization In DEcompensated HFpEF (PARAGLIDE-HF)
**Registration:** NCT03988634
**Design:** Multicentre, double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled trial
**Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals
**N:** 467 randomised (233 Sac/Val, 234 Valsartan); 466 in safety analyses
**Duration:** Up to 20 months of treatment; primary endpoint assessed at Weeks 4 and 8

**Population:** LVEF >40% (includes HFmrEF and HFpEF), recent worsening HF (WHF) event within 30 days (HF hospitalisation, emergency department visit, or ambulatory urgent HF visit requiring IV diuretics). Post-stabilisation initiation: SBP ≥100 mmHg for ≥6h, no IV diuretics/vasodilators for 6h, no inotropes for 24h.

**Baseline characteristics:**
- Women: **52%**; Black patients: **22%** — more diverse than most prior HFpEF trials
- Median age: 70 years; median LVEF: 55%; median NT-proBNP: 2,009 pg/mL (high-risk WHF population)
- 69.5% enrolled in-hospital; 30.5% out-of-hospital

**Intervention:** Sacubitril/valsartan titrated to 97/103 mg BID vs. Valsartan 160 mg BID

**Primary endpoint:** Time-averaged proportional change in NT-proBNP from baseline to Weeks 4 and 8

---

## Primary Results

**Overall:** Sac/Val ratio of change = **0.85 (95% CI 0.73–0.999)** — statistically significant overall NT-proBNP reduction (McMurray JACC 2023)

**LVEF subgroup (pre-specified):**
- LVEF ≤60%: stronger NT-proBNP reduction (driven this subgroup)
- LVEF >60%: minimal to no benefit; higher symptomatic hypotension risk

**Secondary hierarchical endpoint:** favoured Sac/Val but did not reach statistical significance

## Clinical Significance

PARAGLIDE-HF is the first RCT of Sac/Val in the post-WHF transition population with LVEF >40%, filling the gap between PARAGON-HF (stable chronic HFpEF) and acute HF trials. The diverse enrollment (52% women, 22% Black) addresses a persistent representativeness gap. The NT-proBNP reduction is biologically meaningful — similar to patterns preceding hard outcome benefit in HFrEF — but PARAGLIDE-HF was not powered for clinical events. Subgroup data suggest benefit is concentrated in LVEF ≤60%, consistent with PARAGON-HF and the LVEF spectrum hypothesis ([[bozkurt2020sex]]).

The symptomatic hypotension signal (particularly in women with LVEF >60%) is clinically actionable for practice.
