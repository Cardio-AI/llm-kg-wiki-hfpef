# Balcinrenone

> Selective MR modulator — distinct from steroidal MRAs (spironolactone/eplerenone) and non-steroidal MRAs (finerenone) — currently in Phase 3 BALANCED-HF trial in combination with dapagliflozin for HF with recent HF event and eGFR 20–60 mL/min/1.73m².

## Description

**Class:** Selective mineralocorticoid receptor (MR) modulator — classified separately from both steroidal MRAs (spironolactone, eplerenone) and non-steroidal MRAs (finerenone) in the pharmacological hierarchy.

**Mechanism:** Acts as a partial agonist/antagonist at the MR; the modulator classification implies tissue-selective activity potentially different from pure antagonists. In the RAS pathway, balcinrenone acts downstream of aldosterone synthesis at the MR level (compared to vicadrostat/ASIs which act upstream at the synthesis step).

**Combination strategy:** BALANCED-HF evaluates balcinrenone in combination with dapagliflozin, reflecting the emerging combination therapy paradigm for HFpEF/HFmrEF (SGLT2i + MR-targeted agent).

---

## Role in HFpEF

MR hyperactivation drives myocardial fibrosis, inflammation, and sodium retention in HFpEF. Steroidal MRAs (spironolactone/eplerenone) are limited by hyperkalaemia risk, particularly in CKD (prevalent in ~60% of HFpEF patients). Non-steroidal finerenone (FINEARTS-HF) demonstrated 16% event reduction with a better electrolyte profile. Balcinrenone represents a third MR-targeted class aiming to refine the benefit/risk profile further with potential tissue selectivity.

The MIRACLE Phase 2 trial (N=133, HF+CKD, EF <60%) tested balcinrenone + dapagliflozin vs. dapagliflozin alone on UACR change at Week 12 — a renal endpoint proxy for MR activity. None of three doses (15 mg, 50 mg, 150 mg) significantly reduced UACR (p values: 0.1588, 0.6846, 0.1398). This does not exclude HF endpoint benefit, as UACR was the mechanistic endpoint, not a direct HF outcome measure. (source: [[sauer2026pharmacological]])

## Status

**Investigational.** No regulatory approval. Phase 3 BALANCED-HF ongoing. Phase 2 MIRACLE was neutral on primary renal endpoint but was not designed to assess HF outcomes.

**Guideline status:** No recommendation from any society. Under evaluation pending BALANCED-HF results.

## Contradictions

MIRACLE neutral on UACR does not necessarily predict BALANCED-HF outcome — UACR is a mechanistic surrogate, not a HF endpoint. The negative Phase 2 UACR result is a concern but does not refute ongoing Phase 3 evaluation.
