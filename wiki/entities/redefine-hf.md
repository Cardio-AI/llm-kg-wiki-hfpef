## Description

**Full title:** Randomized Trial to Determine the Efficacy and Safety of Finerenone on Morbidity and Mortality Among Heart Failure Patients With Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction Greater Than or Equal to 40% Hospitalized Due to an Episode of Acute Decompensated Heart Failure
**Registration source:** ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT06008197, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06008197, accessed 2026-09-21
**Sponsor:** Colorado Prevention Center · **Contact/PI:** Marc Bonaca, MD
**Status:** Recruiting
**Design:** International, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, event-driven, Phase III
**N (estimated):** 5,200

**Arms:** Finerenone vs. placebo, added to standard therapy.

**Population:** Heart failure with LVEF ≥40% (mildly reduced or preserved), hospitalised for an episode of acute decompensated heart failure.

**Purpose (per registry page):** Determine the efficacy and safety of finerenone on morbidity and mortality among hospitalised HF patients with LVEF ≥40%.

## Role in HFpEF

Directly HFpEF/HFmrEF-relevant and tests finerenone — already established in HFpEF via [[finearts-hf]] — specifically in the acute/post-hospitalisation setting rather than FINEARTS-HF's predominantly ambulatory population. Referenced alongside [[spirrit]] and CONFIRMATION-HF in the SPIRRIT-HFpEF design paper's Table 1 ([[lund2024spirrit]]) and discussed in [[khidihir2026finerenone]]'s review of ongoing finerenone trials.
