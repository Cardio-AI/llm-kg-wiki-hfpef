## Description

**Full name:** MyoVasc — Epidemiological Cohort to Investigate Development and Progression of Heart Failure  
**NCT:** NCT04064450  
**Design:** Prospective observational cohort  
**Population:** N=3,289 HF patients (all phenotypes: HFrEF, HFmrEF, HFpEF) plus healthy controls; age 35–84  
**Follow-up:** 10 years  
**Centre:** Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz, DZHK (German Centre for Cardiovascular Research) Rhine-Main  
**PI:** Philipp Wild  
**Start:** 2019; ongoing  
**Design published:** Gobel S et al. *Eur J Prev Cardiol.* 2021. doi:10.1093/eurjpc/zwab168

MyoVasc provides the observational infrastructure underpinning the DZHK Rhine-Main HF research program. Deep phenotyping includes echocardiography, cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET), vascular assessments, and multi-omics (genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics). The 10-year follow-up enables incident HF event capture, disease progression modelling, and biomarker validation.

## Role in HFpEF

MyoVasc serves as the biobank and deep phenotyping backbone for [[myomobile]] (NCT04940312, app-based PA coaching RCT in HFpEF, same site, same PI). The multi-omics infrastructure collected in MyoVasc enables exploratory mechanistic endpoints beyond the primary intervention trial endpoints. The registry also provides the HFpEF population denominator and phenotypic characterisation that contextualises MyoMobile's intervention cohort (AF 51.9%, obesity 40.5%, median NT-proBNP 418 pg/mL at MyoMobile baseline). (source: [[zeid2025myomobile]])

The AF and metabolic comorbidity burden in this registry cohort is broadly representative of typical European HFpEF phenotypes as described in Anker 2023 (HFA/ESC phenotyping consensus). (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]])

## Status

**Active** (ongoing enrolment/follow-up). Primary design paper published 2021 (pending ingest). MyoMobile primary trial results pending (12-week intervention; see [[myomobile]]).
