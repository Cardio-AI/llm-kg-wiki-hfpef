# CONFIRMATION-HF

> Phase III, international, randomised, open-label trial testing an early, intensive combination strategy of finerenone plus empagliflozin against usual care in patients hospitalized with heart failure, evaluated by a hierarchical win-ratio composite of mortality, HF events, and symptom score.

## Description

**Full title:** Combined Efficacy and Safety of an Early, Intensive, Management Strategy With Finerenone and SGLT2 Inhibitor in Patients Hospitalized With Heart Failure (CONFIRMATION-HF)
**Registration source:** ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT06024746, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06024746, accessed 2026-09-21
**Sponsor:** Colorado Prevention Center · **Contact/PI:** Marc Bonaca, MD (same contact as [[redefine-hf]]) · **Collaborators:** Saint Luke's Mid America Heart Institute, Bayer Healthcare Pharmaceuticals
**Status:** Recruiting · **Protocol ID:** 202303CPC
**Design:** International, randomised, controlled, **open-label** (no placebo/blinding — distinct from [[redefine-hf]]'s double-blind design), Phase III; 140 locations (US + Brazil)
**N (estimated):** 1,500

**Arms:** Finerenone + empagliflozin (experimental) vs. usual care (no intervention arm), both on top of standard-of-care background therapy.

**Population:** Current hospitalization or recent discharge with a primary diagnosis of heart failure, HF signs/symptoms at admission, elevated NT-proBNP (≥500 pg/mL sinus rhythm / ≥1500 pg/mL AF) or BNP (≥125 / ≥375 pg/mL), and treatment during the index hospitalization with ≥1 IV dose of a loop diuretic. **The eligibility criteria as read from the registry contain no LVEF/EF threshold at all — this is an any-EF trial, not restricted to HFpEF/HFmrEF.** (An earlier `trials-pending.md` entry for this trial, derived from a secondary review source, described it as "HFpEF; LVEF ≥45%" — that restriction is not present in the trial's own ClinicalTrials.gov registration and has been corrected here.) Key exclusions: eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m² and/or K⁺ >5.0 mmol/L, prior severe hyperkalemia on MRA, prior MRA/SGLT2i use, type 1 diabetes, recent (30-day) MI/revascularization/valve surgery/CRT implant, several structural-cardiomyopathy exclusions.

**Primary outcome:** Hierarchical win-ratio composite — time to all-cause mortality, total HF events, time to first HF event, and ≥5-point KCCQ-TSS difference — at 6 months. **Secondary outcomes:** time to first death/HF event; mean KCCQ-TSS change to 6 months; total HF events to day 90; serious adverse events and treatment-discontinuing adverse events.

## Role in HFpEF

Tests the finerenone + SGLT2i combination — both drug classes independently established as HFpEF/HFmrEF-effective ([[finearts-hf]] for finerenone, [[sglt2-inhibitors]] for empagliflozin/dapagliflozin) — in the acute post-hospitalisation setting, but in an **any-EF population**, so its HFpEF-specific relevance depends on subgroup results by EF stratum once reported, not on population-level restriction. Referenced alongside [[redefine-hf]] and [[spirrit]] in the SPIRRIT-HFpEF design paper's Table 1 ([[lund2024spirrit]]) and discussed in [[khidihir2026finerenone]]'s review of ongoing finerenone combination trials.

## Status
