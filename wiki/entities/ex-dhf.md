# Ex-DHF (Exercise Training in HFpEF)

> Largest and longest completed exercise RCT in HFpEF (n=322, 12 months): the Packer hierarchical composite primary endpoint was not met (Kendall's tau-b −0.073, P=0.17), yet VO₂peak improved by +1.3 mL/kg/min (P=0.003) and NYHA functional class improved substantially (OR 5.89, P<0.001) — a dissociation attributed largely to ~53% adherence.

## Description

- **Intervention:** Supervised combined endurance (aerobic) + resistance training vs. usual care
- **Population:** HFpEF patients
- **Duration:** 12 months
- **N:** 322
- **Primary endpoint:** Packer hierarchical composite score — ranked hierarchy of all-cause mortality > HF hospitalisation > KCCQ > 6-minute walk distance (6MWD)
- **Full paper stub:** [[edelmann2025exdhf]]

(source: edelmann2025exdhf)

## Role in HFpEF

Ex-DHF is the largest and longest exercise RCT completed in HFpEF to date, extending the follow-up horizon beyond earlier positive exercise trials (HF-ACTION, SECRET) to test whether exercise benefit translates into a hard, hierarchically-weighted composite endpoint. (source: edelmann2025exdhf)

An earlier, smaller interim dataset from the same trial was cited in the 2023 ACC Expert Consensus Decision Pathway on HFpEF management: a 3-month, N=64 subset showed peak VO₂ +3.3 mL/kg/min, improved quality of life, and improved E/e′ (source: Kittleson2023ACC) — consistent in direction with the full trial's eventual VO₂ finding, though at a much earlier stage (3 months, N=64) than the completed 12-month, N=322 result reported in edelmann2025exdhf.

The trial sits alongside pharmacological HFpEF trials as another example of the field's composite-endpoint problem: [[hfpef-treatment-gap]] and [[exercise-intolerance]] both note that clinically meaningful physiological or symptomatic improvement does not guarantee a positive hierarchical/composite trial result.

## Evidence

- **Primary outcome (Packer composite):** Kendall's tau-b = **−0.073**, P=**0.17** — not statistically significant; primary endpoint not met (source: edelmann2025exdhf)
- **VO₂peak:** **+1.3 mL/kg/min** at 12 months (P=0.003) — clinically meaningful aerobic benefit (source: edelmann2025exdhf)
- **NYHA functional class:** improvement OR **5.89** (P<0.001) — the largest symptomatic treatment effect reported for any HFpEF intervention trial in this wiki (source: edelmann2025exdhf)
- **Adherence:** ~**53%** — substantially below anticipated, and identified as the likely critical confounder attenuating the composite endpoint result; per-protocol analyses would likely show larger effects (source: edelmann2025exdhf)
- Composite endpoint insensitivity may also reflect the relatively short (12-month) follow-up for detecting hospitalisation-driven signals within a hierarchical composite (source: edelmann2025exdhf)

## Status

**Published (2025, Nature Medicine).** Current ESC 2023 guidance maintains exercise training as Class I, Level A recommended in HFpEF based on VO₂ and quality-of-life evidence; Ex-DHF supports the physiological rationale for exercise but, given its primary-endpoint failure, raises the methodological bar for what future HFpEF exercise trials should be powered to demonstrate. (source: edelmann2025exdhf)

## Contradictions
Primary endpoint failure (Packer composite P=0.17) conflicts with the large symptomatic benefit (NYHA OR 5.89) and significant VO₂ gain (P=0.003) — the same dissociation pattern (surrogate/symptomatic improvement without composite-endpoint significance) recurs across HFpEF pharmacological trials.
