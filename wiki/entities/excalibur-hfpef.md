# EXCALIBUR-HFpEF

> German Clinical Trials Register entry for a 2-centre (Heidelberg, Mainz) open-label RCT of 12-week app-based personalised exercise coaching added to standard HFpEF care vs. standard care alone; N=200 target; recruiting since January 2026.

## Description

**Full title:** A Multi-Arm, Multi-Center Study Investigating The Effects Of EXercise Training On Dimensions Of CArdiovascular HeaLth In PatIents BURdened With HFpEF
**Registration source:** German Clinical Trials Register, DRKS00039892, https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00039892/details, accessed 2026-09-21
**Sponsor:** Medizinische Fakultät Heidelberg / Universitätsklinikum Heidelberg (Prof. Dr. Sandy Engelhardt)
**PIs:** Prof. Dr. med. Norbert Frey (Innere Medizin III, Universitätsklinikum Heidelberg); Prof. Dr. med. M.Sc. Philipp Wild (Universitätsmedizin Mainz)
**Funding:** Carl-Zeiss-Stiftung
**Design:** 2-centre, 2-arm, randomised, open-label; minimisation-based allocation balancing age, sex, VO2peak, step count, LVEF, NT-proBNP
**N (target):** 200
**Actual start:** 2026-01-27 · **Planned completion:** 2028-01-31

**Arms:** (1) Standard treatment for HFpEF (consultation + medical exam); (2) Standard treatment + 12-week app-based personalised exercise coaching.

**Primary endpoints:** Intervention effect on at least one of — (a) change in daily step count, or (b) change in peak VO2 — baseline to end of intervention.

**Secondary endpoints:** Cardiac/pulmonary function (CMR, echocardiography, spirometry), muscle function, metabolism, neurohumoral activation, heart rate variability, 6-minute walking distance, CPET, accelerometry-measured non-sedentary activity, mental health (PHQ-9, GAD-7), fatigue (MFI-20), quality of life (KCCQ).

**Eligibility (inclusion):** HFpEF diagnosis — LVEF >40% by any imaging modality within 4 months; NYHA I–III dyspnoea; stable HF treatment ≥4 weeks; at least one of NT-proBNP ≥300 pg/mL, HF hospitalisation within 12 months, or diuretic-requiring HF symptoms ≥30 days; able to perform CPET and spirometry; average daily step count 1,000–10,000; age ≥45; capacity to consent.

## Role in HFpEF

Tests a digital-health/app-based coaching intervention in HFpEF, in the same design space as [[myomobile]] (also app-based physical activity coaching, also DZHK-adjacent) but with a dual step-count/peak-VO2 primary endpoint rather than step count alone.
