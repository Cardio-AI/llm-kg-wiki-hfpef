# SOTA-P-CARDIA

> RCT of sotagliflozin (SGLT2+SGLT1 dual inhibitor) in non-diabetic HFpEF — will determine whether intestinal SGLT1 inhibition adds to SGLT2 inhibition benefit in the majority HFpEF population without T2DM.

## Description

**Full title:** Sotagliflozin in Patients with Cardiometabolic Disease and Heart Failure  
**NCT:** NCT05562063  
**Intervention:** Sotagliflozin (combined SGLT2 + SGLT1 inhibitor) vs. placebo  
**Population:** HFpEF without T2DM  
**Status:** Ongoing (referenced in Mahmood 2024 guideline review)

Sotagliflozin inhibits both SGLT2 (renal glucose reabsorption, the target of dapagliflozin/empagliflozin) and SGLT1 (intestinal glucose absorption + cardiac glucose uptake). This dual mechanism may provide:
1. Additional osmotic diuresis and natriuresis via SGLT1
2. Reduced post-prandial glucose excursions (relevant to cardiometabolic inflammation)
3. Possible direct cardiac effects via SGLT1 in cardiomyocytes

Background: SOLOIST-WHF tested sotagliflozin in worsening HF across LVEF spectrum (including HFpEF) and showed a positive primary composite endpoint (CV death + HF hospitalisation + urgent HF visits) despite early termination due to COVID-19 pandemic funding withdrawal. The truncated result was statistically positive. SOTA-P-CARDIA extends this to non-diabetic HFpEF specifically. (source: [[mahmood2024guidelines]])

## Role in HFpEF

Identified in the Mahmood 2024 systematic review as addressing a gap: combined SGLT2/1 inhibition in HFpEF, particularly in the non-diabetic majority. Most HFpEF patients do not have T2DM; EMPEROR-Preserved (empagliflozin) and DELIVER (dapagliflozin) enrolled both diabetic and non-diabetic patients and found consistent benefit across T2DM status. SOTA-P-CARDIA specifically addresses the question of whether adding SGLT1 inhibition to SGLT2 inhibition improves outcomes in this majority population. (source: [[mahmood2024guidelines]])
