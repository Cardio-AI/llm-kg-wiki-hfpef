# DECIPHER-HFpEF

> German multi-centre diagnostic study validating cardiovascular magnetic resonance (CMR) against invasive pressure-volume (PV) loop haemodynamics in patients with HFpEF; aims to establish non-invasive CMR parameters as a reliable substitute for invasive diagnosis; n=185, 7 German centres, NCT03251183.

## Study Design

**Type:** Observational, cross-sectional diagnostic validation study — not a randomised treatment trial.

**Core comparison:** CMR parameters (novel quantitative parameters including T1-mapping, fully quantitative perfusion imaging, LGE) vs. invasive pressure-volume (PV) loops as the gold standard reference.

**Cohorts:**
| Cohort | Description |
|---|---|
| Main group | HFpEF patients: comprehensive CMR + invasive PV loops + echo + biopsy + blood sampling |
| Reproducibility group | HFpEF subgroup: repeated CMR for inter/intra-site reproducibility assessment |
| Age/gender matched controls | Matched non-HF patients: CMR + echo + blood sampling (no PV loops) |
| Healthy volunteers | CMR + echo + blood sampling; LVEF ≥50%, no structural disease |

## Disease Definition for Enrollment

**Main group (HFpEF) — Inclusion:**
1. Ability to provide informed consent
2. Typical HF symptoms (NYHA II–III) within the last 6 months
3. LVEF >45%, absence of structural heart disease on echo (except LVH or LA enlargement)
4. Echocardiographic evidence of elevated LV filling pressures: E/E'sep >15 OR E/E'lat >12 OR average E/E' >13 OR (E/E' >9 AND LA volume >34 mL/m² OR PA systolic >35 mmHg)
5. Indication for invasive haemodynamic work-up
6. Unclear aetiology of heart failure
7. Age >18 years

**Main group — Exclusion:**
- Atrial fibrillation or PVC rate >10/min (diagnostic quality concern for PV loops and CMR)
- Body weight >150 kg
- Inability to lie flat or still
- Contrast allergy or severe renal insufficiency (eGFR <30) — contraindication for invasive work-up
- Non-MR-conditional pacemaker; severe claustrophobia — contraindication for CMR
- Previous documented LVEF <45%
- Confirmed specific myocardial diagnosis (amyloid, ischaemic disease, valvular disease) — excludes mimics

## Parameters Collected

**Imaging:**
- **CMR (primary):** LV/RV volumes and function; LGE (fibrosis); T1-mapping (native and post-contrast); quantitative perfusion imaging (stress and rest); ventricular strain
- **Echocardiography (TTE) with ergometric stress (EchoErgo):** Cavity dimensions, flow velocities, tissue Doppler (myocardial motion velocity and strain), stress parameters at peak exercise

**Invasive:**
- **Pressure-volume (PV) loops:** EDPVR, ESPVR, dp/dt min, Tau (relaxation time constant), arterial elastance (Ea); simultaneous LV pressure and volume measurement at rest and with load changes
- **Swan-Ganz catheter (right heart):** RV pressures, PA systolic/diastolic pressure, pulmonary capillary wedge pressure (PCWP), pulmonary vascular resistance — via right femoral venous approach

**Biopsy:**
- **LV endomyocardial biopsy (main group):**
  - Masson trichrome: collagen volume fraction (myocardial fibrosis quantification)
  - Red oil staining: fat droplets
  - Congo Red: amyloid exclusion
  - Immunohistology: CD45 (total leukocytes), CD3 (T-cells), CD68 (monocytes/macrophages)
  - Western blot: titin isoform composition and phosphorylation status (myofilament remodelling)
  - Frozen samples at -80°C for future molecular analyses

**Biomarkers (blood):**
- BNP / NT-proBNP
- Cytokine profiling (myocardial inflammation)
- Galectin-3
- Procollagen Type I and III (fibrosis markers)
- hsCRP
- Whole blood → genomic DNA (genome analysis)
- PBMCs (Ficoll isolation in subset) → RNA isolation → RNA-seq or RT-PCR

## Primary Outcome

Significant influence of CMR imaging parameters on a multivariate model describing the invasive pressure-volume relation (EDPVR). Assessed by multivariable regression and F-test. Time frame: up to 4 weeks (no follow-up planned).

---

## Secondary Outcomes

- Association between CMR T1-mapping and biopsy collagen volume fraction
- CMR-derived diastolic function model vs. echocardiographic diastolic model
- CMR flow vs. echocardiographic flow parameters
- Discriminatory capacity: invasive vs. non-invasive multivariate models (ROC analysis, DeLong test)
- Reproducibility: intra-class correlations at one site; variability between sites

---

## Relevance to HFpEF Wiki

DECIPHER-HFpEF is the primary source validating non-invasive (CMR) tools against the gold-standard invasive haemodynamic reference for HFpEF diagnosis. Key implications:
- If CMR can reliably predict EDPVR and ESPVR, it could replace invasive PV loops in clinical HFpEF diagnosis
- T1-mapping linked to biopsy fibrosis: would validate CMR as a non-invasive fibrosis biomarker
- Diagnostic algorithms endorsed in [[heidenreich2022aha]] and [[mcdonagh2021esc]] rely on echocardiographic surrogates of filling pressure — DECIPHER-HFpEF tests their validity against true haemodynamic measures
- Biopsy + PV loop combination provides the most mechanistic HFpEF phenotyping available in any study
