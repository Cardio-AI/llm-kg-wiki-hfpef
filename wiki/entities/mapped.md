# MAPPED

> Milan single-centre observational study using non-contrast stress-perfusion cardiac MRI to compare coronary microvascular function and diffuse myocardial fibrosis between early- and advanced-stage HFpEF phenogroups and healthy controls. **Registry status is "Unknown" — last confirmed status was Recruiting as of 2024-03, and the record has not been updated since.**

## Description

**Full title:** MAgnetic Resonance Non-contrast Assessment of Cardiac Microvascular Dysfunction in Patients With Heart Failure With PrEserveD Ejection Fraction (MAPPED)
**Registration source:** ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT06316661, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06316661, accessed 2026-09-21
**Sponsor:** Istituto Auxologico Italiano · **Contact:** Camilla Torlasco (PI-equivalent contact), Luca Grappiolo (backup contact)
**Protocol ID:** 09C921
**Design:** Single-centre, observational, cross-sectional; 1 site (IRCCS Istituto Auxologico Italiano, Milan, Italy)
**N (estimated):** 60 — 40 HFpEF (20 early-stage, "Shah phenogroup 1"; 20 advanced-stage with pulmonary hypertension/RV dysfunction, "Shah phenogroup 3") + 20 healthy age/sex-matched controls

**Interventions:** Stress-perfusion cardiac MRI (diagnostic test, non-contrast T1-mapping) and cardiopulmonary exercise testing, applied to both HFpEF and control groups (observational, no therapeutic intervention).

**Population:** Age ≥18, HFpEF diagnosed per 2016 ESC guidelines (HFpEF group only); exclusions include pregnancy, CMR contraindications (including eGFR <30), atrial fibrillation, prior chemo/mediastinal radiotherapy, known CAD, diabetes mellitus, systemic inflammatory disease; controls additionally excluded for any cardiac/pulmonary/haematological/neoplastic condition.

**Outcome measures (both cross-sectional, at recruitment):** (1) Peak stress perfusion — T1-mapping reactivity (delta T1m) as a measure of impaired coronary microvascular function, HFpEF vs. controls; (2) Extracellular volume (ECV) — T1-mapping-derived diffuse fibrosis, HFpEF vs. controls.

## Registry status — "Unknown" (not the same as Group B's "actively recruiting, results pending")

Per ClinicalTrials.gov convention, "Unknown status" means the record has not been re-verified/updated by the sponsor within the required window — it does not confirm the trial is still enrolling, completed, or abandoned. Study registration/update dates on the registry page (2024-03-05, 2024-03-14, 2024-03-18) are all clustered in March 2024 with no update since, over a year stale relative to this wiki's current date.

Because status is unconfirmed rather than "actively progressing," this trial is tracked separately from the ordinary registry-only ("Group B") pattern — see [[trials-pending]] Group C. ## Role in HFpEF

If completed, would add microvascular-function and diffuse-fibrosis CMR data stratified by HFpEF phenogroup (early vs. advanced, per Shah's phenomapping framework) — directly relevant to [[coronary-microvascular-dysfunction]] and [[myocardial-fibrosis]], and comparable in modality to [[stiffmap]] and [[decipher-hfpef]] (both CMR T1-mapping/ECV vs. invasive reference standards, though MAPPED's own design compares HFpEF vs. controls only, not against an invasive haemodynamic reference).
