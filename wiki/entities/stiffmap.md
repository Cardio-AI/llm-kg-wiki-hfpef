# STIFFMAP

> Leipzig Heart Center single-centre study comparing CMR T1-mapping-derived extracellular volume fraction (ECV) against invasive pressure-volume loop haemodynamics in HFpEF; ECV independently predicts LV stiffness and identifies two mechanistic subphenotypes.

## Description

**Full title:** Comparison of Non-invasive Assessment of Cardiac Fibrosis by MRI Derived T1-Mapping and Ventricular Intrinsic Diastolic Function by Pressure-Volume-Analysis in Patients With Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction
**NCT:** NCT02459626
**Sponsor/site:** Heart Center Leipzig – University Hospital (single centre)
**PI:** Philipp Lurz, PhD, University Heart Center Leipzig
**N (actual):** 36 (24 HFpEF, 12 controls) — enrolled 09/2014–12/2018
**Status:** Completed; results published

Investigator-initiated observational single-centre study evaluating whether CMR-derived fibrosis quantification (T1-mapping/ECV) correlates with directly measured left and right ventricular stiffness from conductance-catheter pressure-volume analysis, testing whether CMR can serve as a valid noninvasive substitute for invasive diastolic function assessment in HFpEF.

## Role in HFpEF

Provides direct invasive-haemodynamic ground truth for a noninvasive imaging biomarker (ECV) that is otherwise validated mainly against histology or against other imaging modalities. Complementary to [[decipher-hfpef]] (also CMR-vs-PV-loop, larger N, 7 centres, includes biopsy) and to [[hfpef-stress-trial]] (CMR-vs-RHC, different modality focus — exercise LA mechanics rather than resting fibrosis quantification).

## Evidence

ECV significantly elevated in HFpEF vs. controls (32.9% vs. 28.9%, P<0.01) and independently predicted the invasively-measured LV stiffness constant β on multivariate regression (β-standardized=0.75, P<0.01) — the strongest single predictor among ECV, E/e', and LA volume index. Median-ECV split identified two subphenotypes: a high-ECV/high-stiffness (fibrosis-dominant) group and a low-ECV group with prolonged relaxation time-constant (τ) and exaggerated hypertensive response to afterload — a relaxation-dominant mechanism not attributable to fibrosis. See [[rommel2016stiffmap]] for full results.
