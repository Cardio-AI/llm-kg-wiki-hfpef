# TOPCAT

> RCT of spironolactone vs. placebo in HFpEF (LVEF ≥45%); neutral overall; Americas subgroup significantly positive; suspected enrollment contamination in Eastern Europe makes the overall result permanently contested.

---

## Description

- **Intervention:** Spironolactone vs. placebo
- **Population:** HF with LVEF ≥45%; either ≥1 HF hospitalization in the prior year or elevated [[natriuretic-peptides]]; enrolled across Americas (US, Canada, Brazil, Argentina) and Russia/Georgia
- **Primary endpoint:** Composite of CV death, resuscitated cardiac arrest, or HF hospitalization
- **Full paper stub:** [[pitt2014topcat]]

## Role in HFpEF

TOPCAT is a cautionary example of the **heterogeneity problem** in HFpEF trials: when enrollment criteria are broad enough to include patients without true HFpEF, treatment effects are diluted. It is also an example of geographic variation in HF trial populations. The Americas subgroup data is widely cited in the field as suggestive evidence for MRA benefit in HFpEF, despite the overall negative result. See [[hfpef-treatment-gap]] and [[spironolactone]]. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

## Evidence

- **Overall:** Neutral primary endpoint (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)
- **Americas subgroup:** Spironolactone significantly reduced the primary endpoint (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)
- **Post-hoc EF analysis:** Significant benefit for patients with LVEF <55% (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)
- **Regional heterogeneity:** Very low urinary spironolactone metabolite levels in non-Americas controls suggest patients in Russia and Georgia may not have received placebo — suspected contamination of the comparator arm, diluting any true treatment effect in the overall analysis (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

## Status

- **ESC 2021:** No recommendation for [[spironolactone]] in [[hfpef]]; overall neutral result insufficient
- **FDA:** Did not approve spironolactone for HFpEF based on TOPCAT

(source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)
