# sGC Stimulators

> Soluble guanylate cyclase (sGC) stimulators (vericiguat, praliciguat) elevate cGMP independently of nitric oxide availability, distinguishing them from organic nitrates and PDE5 inhibitors; three HFpEF RCTs (SOCRATES-PRESERVED phase 2b, VITALITY-HFpEF phase 3, CAPACITY-HFpEF phase 2) were neutral or harmful, closing the class for HFpEF despite the drug's positive HFrEF result (VICTORIA).

## Description

sGC stimulators directly activate soluble guanylate cyclase, elevating intracellular cyclic GMP (cGMP) without requiring nitric oxide (NO) as a substrate. This distinguishes them mechanistically from organic nitrates (which donate NO) and PDE5 inhibitors such as sildenafil (which block cGMP degradation but still require adequate endogenous NO production to be effective). In HFpEF, reduced NO bioavailability (from comorbidity-driven oxidative stress and endothelial dysfunction) is proposed to reduce downstream cGMP-PKG signalling, decreasing titin phosphorylation and increasing cardiomyocyte passive stiffness — the core mechanistic rationale for the NO/cGMP/PKG axis in HFpEF. (source: [[nitric-oxide-pathway]])

Two agents have been tested in HFpEF: **vericiguat** (Bayer/Merck) and **praliciguat** (Cyclerion Therapeutics).

## Role in HFpEF

sGC stimulation was proposed as a way to bypass the NO-deficiency bottleneck implicated in HFpEF diastolic dysfunction — directly stimulating sGC downstream of the impaired NO signal, rather than depending on NO substrate availability (as nitrates and PDE5 inhibitors do). Three RCTs tested this hypothesis in HFpEF, all with negative or harmful results:

1. **SOCRATES-PRESERVED** (vericiguat, phase 2b, N=477): co-primary NT-proBNP and LA volume endpoints both negative (P=0.20 and P=0.37); an exploratory KCCQ-CSS signal at 10 mg (+9.2 pts, P=0.016) motivated the phase 3 trial. (source: [[pieske2017socrates]])
2. **VITALITY-HFpEF** (vericiguat, phase 3, N=789): neutral on primary KCCQ-PLS endpoint at both 10 mg and 15 mg (P=0.80 and P=0.47); the phase 2b exploratory signal did not replicate. (source: [[armstrong2020vitality]])
3. **CAPACITY-HFpEF** (praliciguat, phase 2, N=196): neutral on primary peak VO₂ (P=0.37); KCCQ overall summary score significantly **worse** on active drug (−7.2 pts, P=0.007); significant harm signal in patients ≥70 years (P interaction=0.004) — the very subgroup enriched for as the presumed NO-deficient phenotype. (source: [[udelson2020capacity]])

VITALITY-HFpEF and CAPACITY-HFpEF were published simultaneously in JAMA (2020), together closing the sGC stimulator class for HFpEF. This contrasts with vericiguat's positive result in HFrEF (VICTORIA, HR 0.90), pointing to EF-dependent responsiveness of the cGMP-PKG pathway, or a different underlying bottleneck in HFpEF than in HFrEF. (source: [[armstrong2020vitality]]; [[udelson2020capacity]])

The class failure sits alongside other NO/cGMP pathway trials in HFpEF — organic nitrates (NEAT-HFpEF, isosorbide mononitrate), inorganic nitrate (INDIE-HFpEF, beetroot juice), and PDE5 inhibition (RELAX, sildenafil) — all neutral, forming a five-trial pattern of NO/cGMP pathway failure specific to HFpEF. (source: [[nitric-oxide-pathway]])

## Evidence

**SOCRATES-PRESERVED (vericiguat, 12 weeks, N=477):**
- Co-primary: log NT-proBNP P=0.2017; LA volume P=0.3688 — both negative
- Exploratory (10 mg arm): KCCQ-CSS +9.2 pts vs. placebo (P=0.016); E wave +9 cm/s (P<0.05); medial e' slope +0.08 cm/s (P<0.05); heart rate −5.9 bpm (P=0.0045); no BP change
- Protocol violation: 48 patients received lower doses than randomised (IxRS error), excluded from exploratory analyses
(source: Pieske2017SOCRATES)

**VITALITY-HFpEF (vericiguat, 24 weeks, N=789):**
- KCCQ-PLS: LS diff −1.5 pts (95% CI −5.5 to 2.5), P=0.47 (15 mg); −0.5 pts (−4.6 to 3.5), P=0.80 (10 mg)
- 6MWD: −5.5m (P=0.45, 15 mg); −1.8m (P=0.81, 10 mg)
- Placebo KCCQ-PLS response +6.9 pts, exceeding the 5-point MCID — natural post-decompensation recovery inflated the placebo arm
- Numerically more CV deaths in the 10 mg arm (4.6% vs. 1.5% placebo); underpowered for mortality conclusions
(source: Armstrong2020VITALITY)

**CAPACITY-HFpEF (praliciguat, 12 weeks, N=196, metabolic-phenotype enrichment):**
- Peak VO₂ (primary): adjusted difference −0.30 mL/kg/min (95% CI −0.95 to 0.35), P=0.37
- KCCQ overall summary score: −7.2 pts (95% CI −12.3 to −2.0), P=0.007 — worse on drug
- 6-MWT responders (≥15m): 37% praliciguat vs. 59% placebo (OR 0.39, P=0.007)
- Age ≥70 subgroup: adjusted difference −1.22 (harm) vs. +0.46 in <70y (P interaction=0.004); SBP fell 6.3 mmHg on praliciguat vs. 1.1 mmHg placebo, proposed mechanism for harm in preload-dependent elderly patients
(source: Udelson2020CAPACITY)

## Status

No guideline recommendation for sGC stimulators in HFpEF. Neither vericiguat nor praliciguat has regulatory approval for the HFpEF indication; vericiguat is approved for HFrEF (post-VICTORIA). The class is considered closed for HFpEF following the concurrent 2020 JAMA publication of VITALITY-HFpEF and CAPACITY-HFpEF. (source: Armstrong2020VITALITY; Udelson2020CAPACITY)

## Contradictions
- Vericiguat positive in HFrEF (VICTORIA, HR 0.90) vs. neutral in HFpEF (VITALITY-HFpEF) — same drug, same year, opposite EF phenotype result.
- SOCRATES-PRESERVED exploratory KCCQ signal (10 mg, P=0.016) failed to replicate in VITALITY-HFpEF (P=0.80 at 10 mg) — a clear example of phase 2b surrogate/exploratory-endpoint failure to predict phase 3 outcome.
- CAPACITY-HFpEF's age ≥70 enrichment criterion (chosen to select for the hypothesised NO-deficient phenotype) was the subgroup significantly *harmed* by the drug (P interaction=0.004) — the enrichment strategy backfired in its own target population.
- Numerically more CV deaths in the VITALITY-HFpEF 10 mg arm (4.6% vs. 1.5% placebo), directionally consistent with the CAPACITY-HFpEF age-related harm signal, though both are underpowered for mortality conclusions.
