# HEART Camp

> Multi-site RCT of an 18-month behavioral exercise-coaching intervention vs. enhanced usual care; in the HFpEF subgroup (N=59), adherence to exercise guidelines rose to 56% vs. 0% in controls at 18 months, translating into superior 6MWT and KCCQ improvement — while the HFrEF subgroup showed no benefit, suggesting HFpEF-specific responsiveness to adherence-focused coaching.

## Description

- **Design:** Multi-site RCT; parent study N=204 (all HF phenotypes); HFpEF secondary analysis N=59 (25 HEART Camp, 34 Enhanced Usual Care [EUC]) (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp)
- **Sponsor/site:** University of Nebraska Medical Center (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp)
- **Intervention:** 18-month behavioral coaching program — exercise coach contacts (phone/in-person), self-monitoring tools, goal setting, social support scaffolding from family/peers, problem-solving support; **no additional supervised exercise sessions** beyond what the control arm received — purely motivational/behavioral scaffolding (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp)
- **Comparator (EUC):** Written exercise materials + monthly nurse calls (attention control) (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp)
- **HFpEF subset population:** LVEF ≥40%; N=59; age 64.6 ± 9.3 years in both arms; 54% male in both arms; LVEF 55 ± 6% in both arms; NYHA II 56%, NYHA III 32% in both arms (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp)
- **Primary outcome (HFpEF subgroup):** Exercise adherence (% meeting AHA/ACC exercise guidelines) (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp)
- **Secondary outcomes:** Six-minute walk test (6MWT), Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire (KCCQ — overall [OSS], clinical [CSS], and total [TSS] summary scores), NYHA class (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp)
- **Full paper stub:** [[alonso2022heartcamp]]

## Role in HFpEF

HEART Camp addresses exercise adherence — the primary real-world barrier to exercise benefit in HFpEF — rather than exercise dose itself. Supervised exercise training (SET) reliably improves peak VO₂ and KCCQ under RCT conditions, but real-world programs achieve far lower adherence; Ex-DHF (2025) reported only ~53% adherence even under close supervision. HEART Camp is a purely behavioral intervention (coaching, self-monitoring, goal-setting, social support) layered onto the same exercise recommendations given to controls, isolating the effect of adherence support itself (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp). Its most striking finding is HFpEF-specific responsiveness: the HFrEF subgroup in the parent trial showed no adherence or physical-function benefit from HEART Camp, while the HFpEF subgroup showed a large effect. The mechanism for this difference is not established in the source but is discussed as possibly reflecting HFrEF patients' greater medical complexity, more intensive concurrent cardiac monitoring, or a larger unlocked exercise-capacity reserve in HFpEF's peripheral-limitation phenotype (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp).

## Evidence

| Endpoint | HEART Camp | EUC | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adherence at 12 months | 42% | 14% | 0.025 |
| Adherence at 18 months | 56% | 0% | <0.001 |
| 6MWT at 18 months (change) | +63.25 m | +13.16 m | 0.048 |
| KCCQ-OSS (Time×Group interaction) | — | — | 0.009 |
| KCCQ-CSS (Time×Group interaction) | — | — | 0.001 |
| KCCQ-TSS (Time×Group interaction) | — | — | 0.003 |
| HFrEF subgroup adherence benefit | None | None | NS |
| HFrEF subgroup physical function benefit | None | None | NS |

(source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp)

- At 18 months, EUC (control) patients in the HFpEF subgroup had a 0% adherence rate — a complete abandonment of exercise, against which HEART Camp's 56% represents a large absolute gap (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp)
- All three KCCQ summary scores (overall, clinical, total) showed statistically significant Time×Group interactions favoring HEART Camp (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp)

## Status

Completed; published as a secondary analysis (J Card Fail 2022;28(3):431–442) of the parent HEART Camp RCT (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp). The HFpEF vs. HFrEF differential response is based on a post-hoc secondary analysis of a small subset (N=59 HFpEF) and has not been replicated in a powered, prospective HFpEF-specific adherence intervention trial (source: Alonso2022HEARTcamp).

## Contradictions
- HEART Camp's behavioral coaching improved adherence and outcomes in the HFpEF subgroup but produced no measurable benefit in the HFrEF subgroup of the same parent trial — a phenotype-specific responsiveness that remains unexplained and unreplicated.
