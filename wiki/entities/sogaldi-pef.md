# SOGALDI-PEF

> Portuguese investigator-led, two-centre crossover RCT (N=108) directly comparing dapagliflozin + spironolactone combination against dapagliflozin alone in HFmrEF/HFpEF; the first dedicated trial evidence for SGLT2i/MRA combination therapy in this population — combination reduced NT-proBNP by 11% more than monotherapy, with the expected MRA-class trade-off of greater eGFR decline and hyperkalemia.

## Description

**Full title:** SOdium-Glucose Cotransporter 2 Inhibitor With and Without an ALDosterone AntagonIst for Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction: a Two-centre Randomised Crossover Trial
**NCT:** NCT05676684 · **EudraCT:** 2020-000973-26
**Sponsor:** Faculty of Medicine, University of Porto (investigator-initiated/-led)
**Sites:** Centro Hospitalar Universitário São João; Unidade Local de Saúde Gaia/Espinho, Portugal
**N (actual):** 108 randomised — enrolled September 2022 – June 2024
**Status:** Completed; results published

Investigator-initiated, open-label, blinded-endpoint crossover trial testing whether adding spironolactone to dapagliflozin provides incremental benefit over dapagliflozin alone in HFmrEF/HFpEF (LVEF >40%). Originally a 3-sequence crossover (dapagliflozin alone, spironolactone alone, combination); mid-trial protocol amendment (October 2023) dropped the spironolactone-alone arm once EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER had established SGLT2i as background standard-of-care, leaving a 2-sequence comparison of dapagliflozin alone vs. dapagliflozin+spironolactone.

## Role in HFpEF

The first dedicated randomised trial testing an SGLT2i/MRA combination head-to-head against SGLT2i monotherapy in HFpEF/HFmrEF — directly relevant to the emerging "combination therapy paradigm" tracked via [[sauer2026pharmacological]] and [[khidihir2026finerenone]] (which covers the analogous finerenone+SGLT2i combination rationale, tested prospectively in the ongoing BALANCED-HF and EASi-HF trials for other MR-targeted agents).

## Evidence

Combination reduced log NT-proBNP more than dapagliflozin alone (difference −0.11 log units, 95% CI −0.22 to −0.01, P=0.035; 11% relative reduction); odds of ≥20% NT-proBNP reduction OR 2.27 (P=0.016). Combination also reduced systolic blood pressure (−5.2 mmHg) and urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio, but produced a larger eGFR decline (−6.4 mL/min/1.73m²) and more hyperkalemia (K >5.5 mmol/L: 4.8% vs. 0.9%) than dapagliflozin alone — the expected added-MRA trade-off profile. Treatment effect on NT-proBNP was seen in sinus/paced rhythm but not in atrial fibrillation (P-interaction=0.084, underpowered). See [[ferreira2025sogaldipef]] for full results.

## Status

**Completed; results published:** Ferreira JP, et al.; SOGALDI-PEF Investigators. *JACC Heart Fail.* 2025;86(5):320–333. doi:10.1016/j.jacc.2025.05.033. A secondary renin/aldosterone treatment-response analysis has since been published (Ferreira 2026, JACC Heart Fail) but is not yet individually ingested.
