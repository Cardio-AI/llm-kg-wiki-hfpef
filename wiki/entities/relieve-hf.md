# RELIEVE-HF

> V-Wave Ltd's randomized, blinded, placebo-procedure-controlled interatrial shunt trial in symptomatic HF of any LVEF (N=508); safe, but effectiveness diverged sharply by baseline LVEF — patients with preserved LVEF (>40%) had significantly worse outcomes with shunt treatment, including a tripling of all-cause mortality.

## Description

**Full title:** RELIEVE-HF TRIAL: REducing Lung congestIon Symptoms Using the v-wavE Shunt in adVancEd Heart Failure
**NCT:** NCT03499236
**Sponsor:** V-Wave Ltd
**N (actual):** 508 randomized (250 shunt, 258 placebo procedure); 94 sites, 11 countries (US, Canada, Israel, Germany, Spain, Switzerland, Belgium, Poland, Australia, Netherlands, New Zealand)
**Enrolment:** October 2018 – October 2022
**Status:** Completed; results published

Randomized, double-blind, multicentre, placebo-procedure-controlled trial of the V-Wave Ventura transcatheter interatrial shunt (a permanent nitinol-framed, ePTFE-encapsulated device implanted across the fossa ovalis) vs. a sham transseptal catheterisation, in patients with symptomatic HF (NYHA II–IVa ambulatory) of **any** LVEF on stable GDMT. Randomisation was stratified by baseline LVEF (≤40% "reduced" vs. >40% "preserved").

## Role in HFpEF

Along with [[reduce-lap-hf-ii]] and [[responder-hf]] (Corvia's device programme), RELIEVE-HF is one of the pivotal blinded, sham-controlled interatrial-shunt trials in HF. It is distinguished by enrolling **any-LVEF** patients (not HFpEF-only) and by **not** requiring invasively-confirmed elevated exercise PCWP for enrolment (unlike REDUCE LAP-HF II) — a design difference the trial's own authors cite when discussing why RELIEVE-HF's preserved-EF result diverges from REDUCE LAP-HF II's more favourable PVR-stratified subgroup signal.

## Evidence

Primary safety endpoint met (0/250 device-related major adverse events at 30 days). Primary effectiveness endpoint (hierarchical win-ratio composite) neutral overall: win ratio 0.86 (95% CI 0.61–1.22, P=0.20). Sharp LVEF-stratified divergence (P-interaction=0.03): reduced-LVEF (≤40%) patients trended toward benefit (win ratio 1.40; composite CV-event RR 0.55, P<0.0001), while **preserved-LVEF (>40%) patients had significantly worse outcomes** (win ratio 0.61, P=0.009; composite CV-event RR 1.68, P=0.0001; **all-cause death HR 3.24**, P=0.004; HFH HR 2.05, P=0.0008). No between-group difference in quality of life (KCCQ-OSS) in either stratum. See [[stone2024relievehf]] for full results and discussion.

## Contradictions
HFpEF-specific harm signal (excess mortality with interatrial shunt treatment) directly cautions against generalising benefit from other HFpEF device trials (REDUCE LAP-HF II, REBALANCE-HF, CHAMPION) to this device class as a whole. See `wiki/contradictions.md`.
