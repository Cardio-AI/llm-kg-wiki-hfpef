# Haemodynamic Monitoring in HFpEF

> PA pressure monitoring with CardioMEMS reduces hospitalisation in HFpEF-inclusive populations through pre-emptive medication adjustment; NP-guided therapy — effective in HFrEF — is ineffective and potentially harmful in HFpEF due to lower NP signal and risk of over-diuresis.

**Note — device therapy is not uniformly beneficial in HFpEF:** contrast with [[relieve-hf]] (interatrial shunt device), which showed *harm* (tripled all-cause mortality) in the preserved-LVEF stratum despite this and other HFpEF device trials being positive. See [[contradictions]] #39.

## Device-Based: PA Pressure Monitoring (CardioMEMS)

### CHAMPION Trial (N=550, 37% HFpEF subgroup)
- HF hospitalisation: **28% reduction** (HR 0.72; P=0.0002) over 6 months
- 18-month open-access phase: 33–48% hospitalisation reduction
- HFpEF subgroup analysis (Adamson 2014): confirmed benefit in LVEF ≥40% subgroup
(source: [[abraham2011champion]]; source: [[adamson2014champion]])

### GUIDE-HF Trial (N=1,000, ~47% LVEF >40%)
- Primary composite (all-cause mortality + HF events): HR 0.88 (P=0.16) — **not significant**
- **Pre-COVID sensitivity analysis:** HR 0.81 (P=0.049) — borderline significant
- COVID-19 disrupted usual care in control group → attenuated apparent benefit
- HFpEF subgroup (N=469): HR 0.85 (P=0.28) — directional, not significant
(source: [[lindenfeld2021guidehf]])

### Mechanism
Daily PA diastolic pressure transmission detects subclinical congestion **1–2 weeks before symptom onset** → pre-emptive outpatient medication adjustment (primarily diuretics, vasodilators) → prevents decompensation escalation [needs source]

### FDA Approval
CardioMEMS approved for HFpEF based on accumulated CHAMPION + GUIDE-HF data [needs source]

## Biomarker-Based: NP-Guided Therapy

**NP-guided therapy is not recommended in HFpEF** and may be harmful:
- TIME-CHF HFpEF subgroup: NP-guided therapy **tended to worsen outcomes** (trend, underpowered)
- Multiple meta-analyses: no benefit in HFpEF; some trend to harm
- Mechanism of potential harm: aggressive diuretic escalation to lower NPs → electrolyte abnormalities, renal impairment, volume depletion (particularly problematic in HFpEF where filling pressure optimisation is critical)
- NP levels are **lower in HFpEF** at equivalent filling pressures (especially Black patients, obese patients) → targets derived from HFrEF trials are inappropriate
(source: [[horiuchi2022npguided]])

## PA Pressure vs. NP Monitoring: Comparison

| Approach | HFrEF Benefit | HFpEF Benefit | Mechanism |
|---------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| PA pressure (CardioMEMS) | Proven | Supported | Direct haemodynamic signal |
| NP-guided therapy | Proven | Not demonstrated | Indirect biomarker |

## Open Questions

- Should CardioMEMS be first-line haemodynamic monitoring in HFpEF? (supported by FDA approval; cost and access barriers remain)
- Does PA pressure-guided therapy improve hard outcomes (mortality) in HFpEF? (CHAMPION and GUIDE-HF powered for hospitalisation, not mortality)
- Are there HFpEF phenotypes more or less likely to benefit from PA pressure monitoring?
