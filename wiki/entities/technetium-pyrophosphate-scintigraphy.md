# Technetium Pyrophosphate Scintigraphy

> Diphosphonate bone-tracer scintigraphy (Tc-PYP/Tc-DPD) repurposed as a non-invasive cardiac imaging test; myocardial tracer uptake grade 3+, combined with a negative serum/urine monoclonal-protein screen, is accepted as diagnostic for ATTR cardiomyopathy without requiring endomyocardial biopsy — the current non-invasive gold standard for excluding this HFpEF mimic.

## Description

Technetium pyrophosphate (Tc-PYP) and related diphosphonate tracers (Tc-DPD) are bone-seeking nuclear medicine radiotracers that also bind ATTR amyloid fibrils, allowing repurposing of a routine bone scan for non-invasive cardiac amyloid detection. Visual and quantitative myocardial tracer uptake is graded; a grade 3+ uptake pattern is treated as diagnostic for ATTR-CM without biopsy when serum/urine immunofixation electrophoresis has excluded a monoclonal protein (i.e., excluded AL/light-chain amyloidosis, which can also produce false-positive uptake). (source: [[masri2026attrcm]])

The technique has emerged over the past decade as an accurate, non-invasive alternative to endomyocardial biopsy. Historically, ATTR-CM diagnosis required invasive biopsy, which — in the absence of disease-modifying therapy — was not routinely performed, contributing to underdiagnosis. Trials of the newer ATTR-CM agents (patisiran, acoramidis, vutrisiran), all enrolling from 2019 onward, incorporated scintigraphy-based imaging as an alternative to biopsy for diagnosis, whereas earlier tafamidis trials (enrollment before 2016) still required biopsy confirmation. (source: [[masri2026attrcm]])

## Role in HFpEF

ATTR-CM is a critical HFpEF mimic/subphenotype, clinically indistinguishable from primary HFpEF without specific testing. (source: [[attr-cm]]) Diagnostic exclusion pathway: serum/urine immunofixation electrophoresis (exclude AL amyloidosis) → bone scintigraphy (Tc-PYP/DPD) → genetic testing if positive. (source: [[attr-cm]])

In patients with HFpEF and left ventricular hypertrophy (LVH), ATTRwt amyloidosis has been found present in 13% of cases; in a cohort of HFpEF patients without LVH, ATTR prevalence was 5%. (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]]) Diphosphonate tracer scintigraphy is described as useful for the early detection of cardiac ATTR amyloidosis, particularly when non-hypertensive patients present with significant LVH and low-voltage QRS on ECG. (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]]) The statement raises — without resolving — whether the entire HFpEF population with unexplained LVH, especially those older than 60 years, should be screened for ATTR amyloidosis. (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]])

For the "very high LVEF" HFpEF subphenotype (LVEF >65–70%), extended diagnostic phenotyping including cardiac MRI, bone Tc-scintigraphy, endomyocardial biopsy, or specific blood tests (galactosidase activity, Bence-Jones protein, electrophoresis) is recommended to identify secondary causes such as cardiac amyloidosis, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, or Fabry disease. (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]])

## Evidence

- 13–19% of unselected HFpEF patients may have ATTR-CM on autopsy/biopsy series; Tc-99m-PYP scintigraphy is described as the non-invasive diagnostic gold standard, with grade 3+ uptake sufficient for diagnosis without biopsy. (source: [[masri2026attrcm]])
- The 2024 ACC/AHA performance and quality measure update (QM-6) formalises this pathway as a quality measure: monoclonal protein screening is required before interpreting bone scintigraphy in suspected cardiac amyloidosis, specifically to exclude light-chain amyloidosis. (source: [[kittleson2024accaha]])
- Relative to [[cardiac-mri]]: CMR (tissue characterisation via T1 mapping/ECV/LGE) is used as either a complementary or alternative modality for suspected cardiac amyloidosis, particularly when suspicion arises at very high LVEF; scintigraphy is favoured as the primary non-invasive amyloid-specific test while CMR contributes broader tissue characterisation. (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]])

## Status

Standard of care for non-invasive ATTR-CM exclusion in contemporary HFpEF diagnostic workup; formalised as a US quality measure (QM-6, 2024 update to ACC/AHA performance and quality measures). (source: [[kittleson2024accaha]]) Active exclusion of ATTR-CM via this pathway is considered required before labelling a patient as having "idiopathic HFpEF," given the availability of disease-modifying ATTR-CM therapy. (source: [[attr-cm]])
