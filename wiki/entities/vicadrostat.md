# Vicadrostat

> First-in-class aldosterone synthase inhibitor (ASI/CYP11B2 inhibitor) targeting aldosterone production upstream of the mineralocorticoid receptor; in Phase 3 EASi-HF trial (~N=6000, EF ≥40%) in combination with empagliflozin vs. placebo + empagliflozin.

## Description

**Class:** Aldosterone synthase inhibitor (ASI) — a novel pharmacological class distinct from MRAs (steroidal or non-steroidal). Rather than blocking the mineralocorticoid receptor, vicadrostat inhibits CYP11B2 (aldosterone synthase), the enzyme that catalyses the final steps of aldosterone biosynthesis.

**Mechanism and theoretical advantages over MRAs:**
1. **Upstream suppression:** Reduces aldosterone production rather than blocking its receptor → eliminates ALL downstream aldosterone effects at every aldosterone-responsive tissue
2. **No compensatory aldosterone rise:** MRAs (by blocking receptor feedback) lead to compensatory rises in plasma aldosterone; ASIs suppress synthesis and therefore avoid this compensatory response
3. **Off-target elimination:** MRAs may have partial agonist activity at other steroid receptors (gynecomastia risk with spironolactone via androgen receptor); ASIs have no direct receptor interaction
4. **Preserved glucocorticoid pathway:** CYP11B1 (cortisol synthesis) vs. CYP11B2 (aldosterone synthesis) selectivity is key — vicadrostat must maintain CYP11B1/CYP11B2 selectivity to avoid cortisol deficiency

**In the RAS pathway (Sauer 2026 Figure 2):** Vicadrostat acts at the aldosterone synthesis node (angiotensin-II → aldosterone step), upstream of mineralocorticoid receptor binding. Downstream of vicadrostat: nsMRA (finerenone), sMRA (spironolactone), MR modulator (balcinrenone).

---

## Role in HFpEF

Aldosterone-mediated pathology in HFpEF includes myocardial fibrosis (via collagen synthesis), endothelial dysfunction, and sodium retention. TOPCAT and FINEARTS-HF established MRA class benefit in HFpEF/HFmrEF (HR 0.87, pooled; Turgeon 2025 Bayesian meta-analysis). ASI represents a mechanistically cleaner approach to blocking this pathway — without receptor-level partial agonism and with potential for fewer off-target effects.

The EASi-HF design tests vicadrostat as an ADD-ON to empagliflozin background therapy, reflecting the combination paradigm: SGLT2i provides baseline cardiorenal protection; ASI adds aldosterone suppression on top. This mirrors CONFIRMATION-HF (finerenone + empagliflozin).

## Contradictions

CYP11B2 selectivity vs. CYP11B1 (cortisol) is a theoretical concern with all ASIs — earlier ASI compounds had cross-inhibition causing cortisol deficiency. Whether vicadrostat maintains adequate selectivity in long-term use is an open safety question not addressed in the review.
