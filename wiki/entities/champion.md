# CHAMPION Trial

> Wireless pulmonary artery haemodynamic monitoring reduces HF hospitalizations by 28% through pre-emptive medication adjustment based on subclinical congestion detection; the first device-based remote monitoring intervention to demonstrate benefit across EF subtypes, including HFpEF.

## Aliases
| Alias | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CardioMEMS | device-name | implantable PA pressure sensor used in trial |
| NCT00531661 | Trial-ID | clinicaltrials.gov identifier |

## Trial Design

**Population:** N=550 NYHA III HF patients with ≥1 hospitalization in prior year; LVEF-unrestricted (37% with LVEF ≥40% = HFpEF subgroup)  
**Intervention:** CardioMEMS wireless PA diastolic pressure sensor; patients transmit daily readings; clinicians adjust medications remotely via web platform  
**Control:** Standard care without haemodynamic monitoring  
**Primary endpoint:** Cumulative HF hospitalizations over 6 months  
**Follow-up:** 6 months (primary), extended to 18 months with open-access phase  

## Primary Results (6-month, 2011)

| Outcome | Active | Control | HR (95% CI) | P-value |
|---------|--------|---------|-------------|---------|
| **HF hospitalizations/patient-year** | 0.32 | 0.44 | **0.72 (0.60–0.87)** | **0.0002** |
| **Reduction** | | | **28%** | |

- All-cause hospitalizations also reduced
- No significant mortality difference at 6 months
- Device-related serious adverse events <1%

## Mechanism

Daily PA diastolic pressure monitoring detects subclinical haemodynamic congestion (PAD >18 mmHg) **1–2 weeks before symptom onset**. Pre-emptive outpatient medication adjustment (primarily diuretic up-titration, vasodilator adjustment) prevents decompensation escalation to hospitalization. This treats the elevated filling pressure phenotype directly — a mechanism-appropriate strategy for HFpEF.

## HFpEF Subgroup Analysis

[[adamson2014champion]] (2014) confirmed benefit specifically in HFpEF (37% of trial population; LVEF ≥40%):
- HFpEF subgroup showed consistent hospitalization reduction
- PA pressure monitoring effective in preserved ejection fraction state
- Haemodynamic-guided approach validated regardless of EF

## Longer-term Follow-up

[[abraham2016champion]] (2016, 18-month follow-up with open-access phase):
- Sustained 33–48% HF hospitalization reduction
- Long-term safety profile acceptable
- Open-access phase: control patients crossed over to monitoring; showed similar benefit

## Clinical Impact

- First device-based trial to prove hospitalization reduction across EF subtypes
- Established wireless PA pressure monitoring as evidence-based treatment option
- Led to FDA approval and clinical adoption of CardioMEMS device
- Demonstrated that HFpEF responds to haemodynamic-targeted intervention
- Compared to: [[reduce-lap-hf-ii]] (mechanical device), [[rebalance-hf]] (newer-generation PA pressure monitoring)

## Relevance to HFpEF Treatment Gap

CHAMPION addresses the HFpEF treatment bottleneck: while pharmacological options remain limited, haemodynamic-guided device therapy provides proven hospitalization benefit independent of EF. PA diastolic pressure elevation is a core HFpEF mechanism; remote monitoring enables pre-emptive management — particularly relevant given obesity and comorbidity-driven elevation of baseline filling pressures in HFpEF.
