# VITALITY-HFpEF

> Vericiguat neutral on KCCQ-PLS in HFpEF (LS diff −1.5 pts at 15 mg, P=0.47; −0.5 pts at 10 mg, P=0.80); large placebo response (+6.9 pts) driven by natural post-decompensation recovery; closes sGC stimulator class for HFpEF alongside concurrent CAPACITY-HFpEF publication (JAMA 2020).

## Description

**Full title:** Vericiguat Global Study in Subjects with Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction  
**NCT:** NCT03547583  
**Intervention:** Vericiguat 15 mg/day or 10 mg/day vs. placebo (1:1:1)  
**Population:** HFpEF (LVEF ≥45%), NYHA II–III, recent decompensation within 6 months, elevated NPs, structural disease  
**N:** 789 (264 / 263 / 262)  
**Duration:** 24 weeks  
**Primary endpoint:** Change in KCCQ Physical Limitation Scale (PLS) from baseline to week 24  
**Published:** JAMA 2020;324(15):1512–1521. doi:10.1001/jama.2020.15922  
**Sponsors:** Bayer and Merck Sharp & Dohme Corp

Vericiguat is a soluble guanylate cyclase (sGC) stimulator that enhances the NO–cGMP–PKG pathway, targeting the myocardial stiffness and fibrosis thought to drive HFpEF. Mechanistic rationale: HFpEF is associated with reduced cGMP/PKG signalling due to oxidative stress and NO bioavailability impairment, leading to cardiomyocyte hypertrophy and titin hyperphosphorylation. Phase 2a predecessor SOCRATES-PRESERVED (Pieske, Eur Heart J 2017) showed an NT-proBNP signal at 10 mg, motivating this phase 2b trial with higher dose range and longer follow-up. (source: Armstrong2020VITALITY)

## Role in HFpEF

VITALITY-HFpEF, published simultaneously with CAPACITY-HFpEF (praliciguat, also neutral) in JAMA 2020, effectively closed the sGC stimulator drug class for HFpEF. Together with NEAT-HFpEF (isosorbide mononitrate), INDIE-HFpEF (inorganic nitrate), and RELAX (sildenafil/PDE5i), this represents the fifth negative trial targeting the NO/cGMP pathway in HFpEF.

The contrast with VICTORIA (vericiguat positive in HFrEF, HR 0.90, NEJM 2020, same drug, same year) reinforces EF-dependent cGMP biology: the PKG/titin/diastolic stiffness axis may not be the therapeutic bottleneck in HFpEF, or the benefit in HFrEF reflects adverse remodelling reduction (systolic) rather than diastolic improvement.

## Evidence

**Primary endpoint — both neutral:**
- KCCQ-PLS: diff −1.5 pts (95% CI −5.5 to 2.5), P=0.47 (15 mg vs. placebo)
- KCCQ-PLS: diff −0.5 pts (95% CI −4.6 to 3.5), P=0.80 (10 mg vs. placebo)

**Secondary — both neutral:**
- 6MWD: diff −5.5 m (−19.7 to 8.8), P=0.45 (15 mg); −1.8 m (−16.2 to 12.6), P=0.81 (10 mg)

**Placebo response:** +6.9 KCCQ-PLS pts (exceeds 5-pt MCID) — driven by natural recovery after index decompensation (enrolled within 30 days); vericiguat arms +5.5 and +6.4 pts.

**Mortality signal (exploratory, not powered):**
- CV death: 8 (3.0%) / 12 (4.6%) / 4 (1.5%) — 15 mg / 10 mg / placebo
- Numerically more CV deaths in 10 mg arm; too small for conclusions; directional consistency with CAPACITY-HFpEF age-related harm

(source: Armstrong2020VITALITY)

## Status

**Published:** JAMA September 2020. No guideline recommendation for vericiguat in HFpEF (trial neutral). VICTORIA positive result in HFrEF did not translate to HFpEF guideline adoption. sGC stimulator class effectively closed for HFpEF.

## Contradictions

Vericiguat positive in HFrEF (VICTORIA HR 0.90) vs. neutral in HFpEF (VITALITY) — same drug, same year, EF-dependent response. Numerically more CV deaths in 10 mg vericiguat arm (4.6% vs. 1.5% placebo) — directionally consistent with harm in vulnerable post-decompensation patients but underpowered for conclusions.
