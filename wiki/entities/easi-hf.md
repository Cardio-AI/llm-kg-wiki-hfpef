> Boehringer Ingelheim Phase III trial testing vicadrostat (BI 690517, an aldosterone synthase inhibitor) added to empagliflozin, vs. placebo added to empagliflozin, in symptomatic heart failure with LVEF ≥40%.

## Description

**Full title:** EASi-HF Preserved - A Phase III Double-blind, Randomised, Parallel-group Superiority Trial to Evaluate Efficacy and Safety of the Combined Use of Oral Vicadrostat (BI 690517) and Empagliflozin Compared With Placebo and Empagliflozin in Participants With Symptomatic Heart Failure (HF: NYHA II-IV) and Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction (LVEF) ≥40%
**Registration source:** ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT06424288, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06424288, accessed 2026-09-21
**Sponsor:** Boehringer Ingelheim
**Status:** Recruiting
**Design:** Phase III, double-blind, randomised, parallel-group, superiority trial; ~652 sites (per registry page location count)
**N (estimated):** 6,000, randomised 1:1

**Arms:** (1) Vicadrostat/empagliflozin, once daily tablets; (2) Placebo/empagliflozin, once daily tablets. No fixed study duration — continues until sufficient data accrue to assess efficacy.

**Population:** Adults ≥18y with heart failure symptoms and LVEF ≥40%.

**Purpose (per registry page):** Determine whether vicadrostat in combination with empagliflozin helps people with heart failure, compared with empagliflozin alone.

## Role in HFpEF

Directly HFpEF/HFmrEF-relevant (LVEF ≥40% eligibility, explicitly stated on the registry page). Part of the emerging aldosterone-synthase-inhibitor + SGLT2i combination-therapy frontier tracked via [[khidihir2026finerenone]] and [[sauer2026pharmacological]] — vicadrostat ([[vicadrostat]]) tests upstream aldosterone suppression as an alternative mechanism to direct MR antagonism (spironolactone, finerenone).
