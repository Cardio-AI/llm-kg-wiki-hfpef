# Obesity in HFpEF

> Obesity defines the largest modifiable comorbidity cluster in HFpEF — excess pericardial and visceral fat elevate filling pressures through mechanical restraint and inflammatory pathways; GLP-1RA therapy (semaglutide, tirzepatide) is the first pharmacotherapy to show both symptomatic and event-level benefit in this phenotype.

## Prevalence and Clinical Profile

Obesity (BMI ≥30) is present in 30–40% of HFpEF patients in clinical trial cohorts; higher in US-based registries. The obese HFpEF phenotype has distinct characteristics compared to non-obese HFpEF: (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]])

| Feature | Obese HFpEF | Non-obese HFpEF |
|---|---|---|
| Sex | More women | Mixed |
| Age | Younger (vs. elderly-dominant non-obese HFpEF) | Older |
| Comorbidities | T2DM, sleep apnea, dyslipidaemia | Hypertension, AF, CKD |
| Exercise intolerance | Severe; skeletal muscle + cardiac components | Predominantly cardiac |
| Prognosis | Worse symptom burden; "obesity paradox" controversy | Better survival in some cohorts |
| Natriuretic peptides | Lower NP levels for same haemodynamic severity | Higher NP relative to severity |

BMI is a poor discriminator of the obesity phenotype in HFpEF — visceral/central obesity (waist circumference, waist-hip ratio) is more mechanistically relevant than total BMI. (source: [[borlaug2023statement]])

## Pathophysiology

Four overlapping mechanisms link obesity to HFpEF: (source: [[borlaug2023statement]], [[paulus2013novelparadigm]])

**1. Pericardial restraint (mechanical)**
Excess paracardiac adipose tissue (epicardial fat + pericardial fat beyond the parietal pericardium) physically constrains cardiac chamber expansion. During exercise, as the RV fills and right heart volume increases, pericardial constraint raises LV filling pressure via ventricular interdependence. This mechanism is acutely reversible with weight loss. SUMMIT CMR substudy (Kramer 2025): tirzepatide reduced paracardiac adipose tissue by 45 mL (pericardial AT −43 g, P<0.001) alongside LV mass reduction of 11 g (P=0.004). (source: [[kramer2025summit-cmr]], [[packer2025summit]])

**2. Expanded plasma volume and venous return**
Obesity is associated with expanded total blood volume and impaired venous capacitance. Even without frank fluid overload, the stress blood volume (mobilised during exertion or recumbency) is elevated → ↑venous return → ↑LA pressure. This is exacerbated by hypoalbuminaemia in some obese HFpEF patients. (source: [[borlaug2023statement]])

**3. Adipose tissue inflammation → coronary microvascular dysfunction**
Visceral adipose tissue secretes pro-inflammatory adipokines (leptin, TNF-α, IL-6, resistin) → systemic low-grade inflammation → coronary microvascular endothelial inflammation → ↓NO bioavailability → ↓cGMP/PKG → titin hypophosphorylation → cardiomyocyte stiffness → diastolic dysfunction. This is the Paulus-Tschöpe 2013 cascade with obesity as the primary upstream driver. (source: [[paulus2013novelparadigm]])

**4. Myocardial lipotoxicity and altered energetics**
Excess free fatty acid delivery to cardiomyocytes impairs mitochondrial efficiency. In HFpEF, gene expression analysis shows upregulation of mitochondrial pathways with obesity but impaired substrate switching during stress. Epicardial adipose tissue (within the visceral pericardium) may directly diffuse fatty acids into the myocardium. (source: [[borlaug2023statement]])

## Treatment Evidence

### Lifestyle Intervention
Hypocaloric diet combined with aerobic exercise training reduces body weight and improves exercise capacity in obese HFpEF. Meta-analysis (Lee 2024; 6 RCTs; N=375): weight reduced −5.3 kg, 6MWD +43.6m (P<0.001), MLHFQ −17.77 (P<0.001), SBP −3 mmHg. The SECRET trial (Kitzman 2016; N=100; diet+exercise vs. diet alone vs. exercise alone) showed additive benefit of combined approach. (source: [[lee2024lifestyle]], [[kitzman2016secret]])

### GLP-1 Receptor Agonists
**STEP-HFpEF** (semaglutide 2.4 mg, N=529, NEJM 2023): dual primary endpoints both P<0.001:
- KCCQ-CSS: diff +7.8 pts (95% CI 4.8–10.9)
- Body weight: diff −10.7 pp (sema −13.3% vs. placebo −2.6%)
- 6MWD: diff +20.3m; CRP ratio 0.61; HF events 1 vs. 12 (exploratory)
Not powered for hard outcomes. (source: [[kosiborod2023stephfpef]])

**STEP-HFpEF DM** (semaglutide in HFpEF+T2DM, N=616, NEJM 2024): similar symptomatic benefit in diabetic HFpEF. Pooled NT-proBNP analysis (Petrie 2024; N=1,145): semaglutide reduced NT-proBNP by 22% vs. 4.9% placebo (ETR 0.82, P=0.0002); effect independent of weight loss magnitude — suggesting direct HF disease-modifying mechanism beyond mechanical unloading. (source: [[petrie2024stephfpef]])

**SUMMIT** (tirzepatide [dual GLP-1/GIP], N=731, NEJM 2025): co-primary endpoints:
- KCCQ-CSS: significant improvement
- CV death/worsening HF events: HR 0.62 (95% CI 0.41–0.95; P=0.026) — first GLP-1RA class event reduction in HFpEF
SUMMIT CMR substudy: pericardial AT −43 mL (P<0.001), LV mass −11 g (P=0.004). (source: [[packer2025summit]])

### SGLT2 Inhibitors (foundation therapy, all BMI groups)
SGLT2i (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin) showed consistent benefit across BMI subgroups in EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER. Not specifically obesity-targeted but mechanistically relevant: SGLT2i induce caloric deficit, reduce visceral fat, and lower filling pressures via natriuresis. (source: [[anker2021emperor]]; source: [[solomon2022deliver]])

### Phenotype-Guided Recommendation (Anker 2023)
Treatment wheel position: semaglutide / tirzepatide as phenotype-specific add-on for the obesity branch, on top of universal SGLT2i. (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]])

## Diagnostic Considerations

**NP paradox:** Obese HFpEF patients have lower BNP/NT-proBNP levels for the same haemodynamic severity (obesity suppresses NP production via increased clearance and adipocyte-mediated degradation). Diagnostic algorithms (HFA-PEFF, H₂FPEF) require BMI-adjusted or obesity-specific NP thresholds. (source: [[pieske2019hfapeff]])

**LVEF:** Obese HFpEF often has preserved or even supra-normal LVEF (>65%) due to reduced LV end-diastolic volume from pericardial constraint. Stroke volume is maintained by tachycardia rather than LV volume augmentation during exercise.

## Contradictions

**Obesity paradox:** Some observational studies report lower mortality in obese vs. non-obese HFpEF — possibly reflecting selection bias (obese patients diagnosed earlier due to more symptomatic disease), reverse causation (weight loss in advanced HFpEF as marker of poor prognosis), or genuinely different disease biology. RCT data (STEP-HFpEF, SUMMIT) show weight loss improves symptoms and events, supporting weight loss as beneficial. See [[contradictions]].

**Lean mass loss:** GLP-1RA (semaglutide) reduces lean muscle mass as well as fat. In HFpEF patients with sarcopenic obesity, muscle loss may worsen skeletal muscle component of exercise intolerance despite symptom improvement. SUMMIT combined exercise + tirzepatide — whether the exercise arm preserves lean mass is an open question. See [[contradictions]].
