# ATTR Cardiomyopathy

> Transthyretin cardiac amyloidosis is an important HFpEF subphenotype present in 13–19% of HFpEF patients — actively excluding it is required because tafamidis is disease-modifying and changes management.

## Description

ATTR cardiomyopathy results from accumulation of TTR amyloid fibrils in myocardial tissue, causing restrictive cardiomyopathy with preserved or mildly reduced LVEF, elevated filling pressures, exercise intolerance, and progressive conduction disease.

**Wild-type (ATTRwt):** Age-related TTR tetramer instability; predominantly elderly males; often presents as HFpEF. Estimated prevalence: 13–19% of HFpEF in series using systematic screening. (source: raw/2019-CIRCRESAHA-Pfeffer-HFpEF_perspective.pdf)

**Variant (ATTRv):** Autosomal dominant TTR gene point mutation; earlier age of onset; may include cardiomyopathy + polyneuropathy; 100+ pathogenic mutations; Val122Ile common in African Americans.

## Role in HFpEF

ATTR-CM is an HFpEF mimic/subphenotype clinically indistinguishable from standard HFpEF without specific testing. Red flags warranting workup:
- Male sex, age >65
- Disproportionate LVH without hypertension history
- Low-flow low-gradient AS pattern
- Bilateral carpal tunnel syndrome / spinal stenosis
- Intolerance of RAAS inhibitors (hypotension)
- Cardiac scintigraphy uptake on bone-scan (DPD/PYP)

Diagnosis: serum/urine immunofixation electrophoresis (exclude AL) → bone scintigraphy (DPD/Tc-PYP) → genetic testing if positive. Cardiac biopsy definitive if equivocal.

**Guideline position:** AHA 2022 lists ATTR-CM explicitly as an HFpEF mimic in Table 1 of [[kittleson2023acc]]; active exclusion recommended before labelling as "idiopathic HFpEF."

## Treatment

Tafamidis (TTR tetramer stabiliser, oral 80 mg once daily) is the only approved disease-modifying agent. Established in [[attr-act]] (NEJM 2018, N=441): all-cause mortality RR 0.70 (95% CI 0.51–0.96); CV hospitalisations reduced; disease progression (KCCQ, 6MWT) attenuated. AHA 2022: Class I, Level B-R for tafamidis in ATTR-CM with HF symptoms. (source: [[heidenreich2022aha]])

RNA silencing agents (patisiran, vutrisiran) approved for ATTRv polyneuropathy; cardiac benefit in ATTRv cardiomyopathy under investigation.
