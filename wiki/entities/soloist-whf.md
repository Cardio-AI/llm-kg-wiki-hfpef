# SOLOIST-WHF

> Trial of combined SGLT1/SGLT2 inhibition (sotagliflozin) in patients with type 2 diabetes and recent worsening HF across the LVEF spectrum; positive despite early termination for COVID-19 funding loss, with an effect consistent in both HFrEF and HFpEF subgroups — but underpowered specifically for the HFpEF subgroup and restricted to a diabetic population, leaving the case for combined SGLT1/2 inhibition in the broader (non-diabetic) HFpEF population an acknowledged evidence gap.

## Description

- **Intervention:** Sotagliflozin — an inhibitor of both SGLT1 (intestinal glucose absorption, cardiac glucose uptake) and SGLT2 (renal glucose reabsorption); at the time of the Kittleson 2023 ECDP, "not currently Food and Drug Administration [FDA]-approved" (source: Kittleson2023ACC)
- **Population:** N=**1,222**; patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus who were recently hospitalised for worsening heart failure, enrolled before or shortly after discharge, across the full spectrum of LVEF — 21% of the study population had an LVEF ≥50% (source: Kittleson2023ACC; source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)
- **Primary result:** Significantly lower total number of cardiovascular deaths, HF hospitalisations, and urgent HF visits vs. placebo, regardless of LVEF (source: Kittleson2023ACC); a significant reduction in total HHF/CV death, with the effect "consistent in both HFrEF and HFpEF" on subgroup analysis (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)
- **Underlying primary trial publication (cited by Kittleson2023ACC and Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype, not independently ingested):** Bhatt DL, Szarek M, Steg PG, et al. Sotagliflozin in patients with diabetes and recent worsening heart failure. *N Engl J Med.* 2021;384:117–128.

## Role in HFpEF

**Population definition:** SOLOIST-WHF's eligibility criterion — patients with T2DM and "recently worsening heart failure," enrolled at or shortly after hospital discharge — corresponds approximately to level 1 (hospitalisation requiring IV diuretics) of the hierarchical worsening-heart-failure (WHF) taxonomy formalised by Chaudhary et al. 2025; see [[worsening-heart-failure]] for the general concept and its trial-design implications, and [[paraglide-hf]] for a directly comparable "post-WHF" HFpEF trial with an explicit 30-day WHF-event inclusion window. (source: [[chaudhary2025worseninghf]])

SOLOIST-WHF is the only trial in this wiki's ingested sources testing combined SGLT1/SGLT2 inhibition in HFpEF, and is cited across three independent 2023–2024 guideline/consensus documents (Kittleson ACC ECDP, Anker HFA/ESC/ESH phenotyping statement, Mahmood systematic guideline review) as the evidentiary basis for sotagliflozin's limited endorsement in HFpEF. All three sources caution that the HFpEF-specific evidence is indirect: the trial enrolled only patients with type 2 diabetes and was "underpowered to study patients with HFpEF" as a dedicated subgroup (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype). Mahmood 2024 lists "combined SGLT2/1 inhibition (sotagliflozin)" explicitly as one of the field's evidence gaps, noting that SOTA-P-CARDIA ([[sota-p-cardia]], NCT05562063) is the ongoing trial designed to test sotagliflozin specifically in HFpEF without T2DM (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines).

## Evidence

- **Population:** N=1,222; T2DM + recent worsening HF; 21% LVEF ≥50% (source: Kittleson2023ACC)
- **Primary result:** Significant reduction in the total number of CV deaths, HF hospitalisations, and urgent HF visits vs. placebo, regardless of LVEF (source: Kittleson2023ACC)
- **Subgroup consistency:** Effect on total HHF/CV death consistent in both HFrEF and HFpEF subgroups (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)
- **Caution flagged by Anker 2023:** "the results of this trial should be viewed with caution as it was underpowered to study patients with HFpEF and only included patients with diabetes" (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)
- **Trial course:** Terminated early "following the COVID-19 pandemic and insufficient funding"; described as producing "positive results" despite this (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)
- **Adverse effects:** Diarrhoea, genital infections, and diabetic ketoacidosis reported more commonly with sotagliflozin than placebo across the sotagliflozin trial programme (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines) — note: in the underlying guideline-review PDF, the specific footnote for this adverse-event sentence points to a related sotagliflozin trial in diabetic chronic kidney disease (SCORED) rather than to SOLOIST-WHF itself; flagged here for accuracy rather than resolved.

## Status

**Published (2021); guideline uptake limited and cautious.** Per Mahmood 2024: "ESC and AHA/ACC/HFSA have recommended sotagliflozin in select patients with T2DM but benefits of widespread use remain unclear" (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines). No dedicated HFpEF (non-diabetic) trial of sotagliflozin has yet reported; SOTA-P-CARDIA (NCT05562063) is ongoing to address this gap (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines).

## Contradictions
Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype reports the sotagliflozin effect as "consistent in both HFrEF and HFpEF," while in the same breath cautioning the trial "was underpowered to study patients with HFpEF" — a consistent point estimate from an underpowered subgroup is weaker evidence than the "consistent" framing implies.
