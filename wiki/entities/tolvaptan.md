# Tolvaptan

> Aquaretic agent added to loop diuretics for volume overload with hyponatraemia in acute heart failure; recommended by 2 of 7 major HFpEF guidelines (CCS/CHFS, JCS/JHFS), discussed but not formally endorsed by 2 more (AHA/ACC/HFSA, NHFA/CSANZ); trial evidence (EVEREST, and subsequent smaller studies) shows symptomatic improvement but no demonstrated effect on mortality or rehospitalisation, and is drawn from general acute HF populations rather than HFpEF specifically.

## Description

Tolvaptan is discussed in the wiki only through Mahmood 2024's systematic review of 7 rigorously developed HFpEF clinical practice guidelines (AHA/ACC/HFSA, ESC, NICE, CCS/CHFS, SHA, NHFA/CSANZ, JCS/JHFS). No dedicated HFpEF trial of tolvaptan is otherwise summarised in this wiki; the drug's evidence base as cited by Mahmood 2024 (EVEREST, and subsequent smaller acute-HF studies) is drawn from general acute heart failure populations, not an HFpEF-specific trial population. (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)

## Role in HFpEF

Tolvaptan is positioned in guideline text as an adjunct to loop diuretics for acute decongestion, specifically for **fluid overload with symptomatic hyponatraemia**, and — per JCS/JHFS specifically — also for **oedema refractory to loop diuretics**. It is one of the guideline-level disagreement areas Mahmood 2024 identifies for acute HFpEF management, alongside acetazolamide and ultrafiltration. (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)

Guideline-by-guideline stance (acute setting):
- **CCS/CHFS:** recommends tolvaptan consideration for volume overload with symptomatic hyponatraemia (Class II, moderate-quality evidence)
- **JCS/JHFS:** recommends tolvaptan for poor response to loop diuretic (Class IIa, evidence grade A) or for fluid retention with hyponatraemia (Class IIa, evidence grade C); uniquely among the 7 guidelines, JCS/JHFS also recommends **long-term continuation** of tolvaptan in chronic treatment if it was initiated acutely, alongside a stated preference for long-acting loop diuretics (e.g., azosemide) over furosemide to reduce neurohormonal (sympathetic/RAAS) stimulation
- **AHA/ACC/HFSA and NHFA/CSANZ:** discuss tolvaptan as an option but do not issue a formal recommendation, citing the paucity of positive trial results to date
- **ESC and SHA:** not reported as discussing tolvaptan in the acute-treatment recommendation matrix (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)

Mahmood 2024's discussion section explicitly frames the evidence as **ambiguous**: "while some symptomatic improvement has been reported, no effect on mortality or rehospitalization has yet been observed," citing the EVEREST outcome trial and subsequent smaller acute-HF studies of tolvaptan's short-term effects, effectiveness in renal dysfunction, and efficacy/safety in hospitalised acute HF. None of these cited studies are HFpEF-specific trials — they enrolled broader acute HF populations. (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)

## Evidence

No HFpEF-specific randomised trial data for tolvaptan exists in this wiki. The evidence cited by Mahmood 2024 is limited to general acute HF trials (not restricted to LVEF ≥50%) showing symptomatic/decongestive benefit without demonstrated mortality or rehospitalisation benefit. (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)

## Status

**Guideline disagreement (Mahmood 2024, 7-guideline systematic review):** Tolvaptan use in acute HF is explicitly flagged as an area of disagreement across international HFpEF guidelines — only CCS/CHFS and JCS/JHFS formally recommend it; AHA/ACC/HFSA and NHFA/CSANZ discuss without recommending; ESC and SHA are not reported as addressing it in the acute-treatment matrix. (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)
