# Acetazolamide

> Carbonic anhydrase inhibitor used adjunctively with loop diuretics for resistant fluid overload in acute heart failure; a prespecified ADVOR trial subgroup analysis found increased successful decongestion across the LVEF spectrum (including preserved EF), but only 1 of 7 major HFpEF guidelines (SHA) formally recommends it, and ESC's 2023 Focused Update explicitly called for further data before endorsing routine use.

## Description

Acetazolamide is discussed in this wiki only through Mahmood 2024's systematic review of 7 rigorously developed HFpEF clinical practice guidelines (AHA/ACC/HFSA, ESC, NICE, CCS/CHFS, SHA, NHFA/CSANZ, JCS/JHFS). No dedicated acetazolamide trial is independently summarised in this wiki; the supporting evidence Mahmood 2024 cites is a prespecified subgroup analysis of the ADVOR trial (Martens et al., *Circulation* 2023) examining decongestion across the LVEF spectrum in acute decompensated heart failure, rather than an HFpEF-restricted RCT. (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)

## Role in HFpEF

Acetazolamide is positioned in guideline text as an adjunct to loop diuretics for **resistant oedema, or insufficient decongestion despite loop diuretic dose increases**, in acute heart failure. This is one of the guideline-level disagreement areas Mahmood 2024 identifies for acute HFpEF management, alongside tolvaptan and ultrafiltration. (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)

Guideline-by-guideline stance (acute setting):
- **Saudi Heart Association (SHA):** the only one of the 7 guidelines to formally recommend acetazolamide, for resistant oedema or insufficient symptomatic response to loop diuretics (Class IIb in the acute-treatment recommendation matrix)
- **ESC 2023 Focused Update:** discussed acetazolamide but explicitly emphasised the need for further data before advocating routine use — i.e., considered but not formally endorsed
- The remaining guidelines (AHA/ACC/HFSA, NICE, CCS/CHFS, NHFA/CSANZ, JCS/JHFS) are not reported as making a formal acetazolamide recommendation (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)

Mahmood 2024's discussion section notes that "addition of acetazolamide alongside loop diuretics has recently been shown to increase incidence of successful decongestion across the LVEF spectrum," citing the ADVOR trial's prespecified subgroup analysis (Martens et al., *Circulation* 2023) — meaning the reported decongestion benefit spans HFrEF, HFmrEF, and HFpEF rather than being HFpEF-specific evidence generated by a dedicated HFpEF trial. (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)

## Evidence

No HFpEF-specific randomised trial of acetazolamide is independently ingested in this wiki. The only supporting evidence identified is the ADVOR trial's prespecified analysis across the LVEF spectrum (referenced, not itself summarised, in Mahmood 2024), reporting an increased rate of successful decongestion with acetazolamide added to loop diuretics. (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)

## Status

**Guideline disagreement (Mahmood 2024, 7-guideline systematic review):** Acetazolamide use in acute HF is explicitly flagged as an area of disagreement — only SHA formally recommends it; ESC's 2023 Focused Update discussed it but called for further data; the remaining guidelines do not address it. (source: Mahmood2024Guidelines)
