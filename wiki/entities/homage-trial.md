# HOMAGE (Heart 'OMics' in AGEing)

> Multicentre, open-label, blinded-endpoint RCT testing spironolactone vs. usual care on serum collagen-metabolism markers and cardiac structure/function in patients at high risk of developing heart failure (LVEF ≥45%, "Stage B"). Pre-specified primary endpoint — interaction between baseline galectin-3 and change in serum PIIINP — was negative (P=0.947); secondary endpoints (blood pressure, NT-proBNP, PICP, LA volume, LVEF) were favourable. Also one of three trials (with Aldo-DHF and TOPCAT) pooled in an individual patient data meta-analysis showing spironolactone favourably remodels cardiac structure in HFpEF-adjacent populations.

## Description

- **Design:** Multicentre, prospective, randomised, open-label, blinded-endpoint trial (PROBE-type design); echocardiographic core-lab assessment was blinded to treatment allocation despite open-label drug administration and unblinded investigators. Enrolment January 2016 – June 2018; follow-up visits at 1 week, 1, 2, 3, 6, and 9 months
- **Intervention:** Spironolactone initiated 25 mg/day, titratable to 50 mg/day (or reduced/stopped per serum potassium and renal function) vs. control/usual care (no MRA or other potassium-sparing diuretic permitted in either arm)
- **Population ("Stage B" heart failure — at increased risk of developing HF, not yet diagnosed):** Either sex, aged ≥65 years (protocol amended to ≥60 years) with established coronary artery disease or at high risk of coronary disease, and plasma NT-proBNP 125–1000 ng/L or BNP 35–280 ng/L; excluded if eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m², serum potassium >5.0 mmol/L, LVEF <45%, atrial fibrillation, a diagnosis of heart failure, or treatment with loop diuretics
- **N:** 877 assessed for eligibility → 527 randomised (262 control, 265 spironolactone) → 506 (96%) attended the final visit (255 control, 251 spironolactone analysed). Baseline: median age 73 (IQR 68–78), 26% women, 72% coronary artery disease, 42% diabetes; median NT-proBNP 214 ng/L, median galectin-3 16.1 µg/L (both above published normal values)
- **Follow-up:** Echocardiogram performed at baseline and at the final visit (planned 9 months; occurred between 3–8 months for some participants owing to fixed trial-funding duration)
- **N contributed to the separate, later pooled IPD analysis** ([[ferreira2023spironolactone]]): 452 of 984 total patients across HOMAGE + Aldo-DHF + TOPCAT-Americas (45.9%); echocardiographic sub-analysis N ranged 256–419 depending on parameter (LAVi n=331, LVMi n=415, IVS n=419, E/e′ n=402, LVEF n=256)
- **Funding:** European Union 7th Framework Programme for Research and Technological Development (grant 305507); sponsor ACS Biomarkers (Amsterdam); monitoring/data management by European Drug Development Hub (Nancy, France)

(source: cleland2021homage; pooled-cohort N detail source: ferreira2023spironolactone)

## Role in HFpEF

HOMAGE is not itself an HFpEF trial — it enrolled patients at increased risk of developing heart failure ("Stage B", pre-clinical), not patients with a clinical HFpEF diagnosis. Its relevance to this wiki is as one of three trials (alongside [[aldo-dhf]] "Stage B" and [[topcat]] "Stage C" HFpEF) whose individual patient data were pooled in Ferreira et al. 2023 to test spironolactone's effect on echocardiographic structure and function across the HF risk/diagnosis spectrum. Unlike Aldo-DHF and TOPCAT, which were placebo-controlled, HOMAGE was open-label (with blinded echocardiographic assessment) — a design difference explicitly noted as a source of between-trial heterogeneity in the pooled analysis. (source: ferreira2023spironolactone)

## Evidence

### HOMAGE's own primary and secondary endpoints

**Primary endpoint (pre-specified):** Interaction between baseline serum galectin-3 (above vs. below median) and change in serum procollagen type-III N-terminal pro-peptide (PIIINP) from baseline to final visit, comparing spironolactone vs. control — i.e. a biomarker-interaction hypothesis test (galectin-3 as a putative marker of who would show a greater fibrosis/collagen-synthesis response to spironolactone), **not a clinical or functional outcome**.

**Primary result: NEGATIVE.** No interaction between baseline galectin-3 and treatment effect on PIIINP change (P=0.947 for interaction). Serum PIIINP changed little overall with spironolactone vs. control (mean difference -0.15 µg/L; 95% CI -0.44 to 0.15; P=0.323). Galectin-3 did not identify a subgroup with a greater collagen-marker response to spironolactone. (source: cleland2021homage)

**Secondary endpoints (positive; exploratory, no adjustment for multiple comparisons):**

| Endpoint | Effect (spironolactone vs. control) | P |
|---|---|---|
| Serum PICP | mean diff -8.1 µg/L (95% CI -11.9 to -4.3) | <0.0001 |
| PICP/CITP ratio | mean diff -2.9 (95% CI -4.3 to -1.5) | <0.0001 |
| Systolic blood pressure | mean diff -10 mmHg (95% CI -13 to -7) | <0.001 |
| NT-proBNP | mean diff -57 ng/L (95% CI -81 to -33) | <0.001 |
| Left atrial volume index | mean diff -1 mL/m² (95% CI -2 to 0) | 0.010 |
| LVEF | mean diff +1.2% (95% CI +0.2 to +2.2) | 0.022 |

No interaction with baseline galectin-3 was found for any secondary marker. No treatment effect was observed on symptoms, exercise capacity (incremental shuttle walk-test), haemoglobin, troponin, or GDF-15. A composite clinical endpoint (HF hospitalisation/atrial fibrillation, non-fatal MI, stroke, or cardiovascular death) occurred in 11 control participants (14 events) vs. 9 spironolactone participants (12 events), P=0.50 — the trial was not powered for clinical outcomes. (source: cleland2021homage)

**Authors' interpretation:** the specific mechanistic hypothesis (galectin-3 as a selection biomarker for collagen-synthesis response) was not confirmed, but spironolactone showed early (within 1 month), sustained effects on blood pressure and NT-proBNP accompanied by favourable echocardiographic remodelling (reduced LA volume, increased LVEF) by the final visit. Authors explicitly state the trial "was neither large nor long enough" to show whether this delays or prevents progression to symptomatic heart failure. (source: cleland2021homage)

### Pooled echocardiographic IPD meta-analysis (HOMAGE + Aldo-DHF + TOPCAT)

From the separate, later pooled IPD analysis (spironolactone vs. control, ANCOVA, covariate-adjusted for baseline value; N=984 total across HOMAGE + Aldo-DHF + TOPCAT-Americas):

| Parameter | Pooled effect (spiro vs. control) | P value | Heterogeneity (P) |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAVi (mL/m²) | −1.1 (−2.0 to −0.1) | 0.03 | 0.23 |
| LVMi (g/m²) | −3.6 (−6.4 to −0.8) | 0.01 | 0.28 |
| IVS thickness (mm) | −0.2 (−0.3 to −0.1) | 0.01 | 0.74 |
| E/e′ ratio | −1.3 (−2.4 to −0.2) | 0.02 | <0.01 |
| LVEF (%) | +1.7 (0.8–2.6) | <0.01 | 0.67 |

HOMAGE-specific detail on the E/e′ heterogeneity: HOMAGE showed a smaller E/e′ reduction (−0.38) than Aldo-DHF (−1.27) or TOPCAT (−2.41), explained in the source by HOMAGE's lower baseline E/e′ (less margin for reduction) — consistent with HOMAGE enrolling an earlier-stage, pre-HFpEF population. No treatment-by-subgroup interaction was found for age, sex, diabetes status, CAD, or hypertension across the pooled cohort. (source: ferreira2023spironolactone)

Note: the pooled analysis's stated "primary hypothesis" (that spironolactone reduces LAVi) is the pooled meta-analysis's own hypothesis, distinct from HOMAGE's independently pre-specified trial-level primary endpoint (galectin-3 × PIIINP interaction, see above) — the two should not be conflated. (source: cleland2021homage; ferreira2023spironolactone)

## Status

**Published, primary results ingested.** HOMAGE's primary-results paper (Cleland et al. 2021, *Eur Heart J*) is now ingested as [[cleland2021homage]]. Trial-level primary endpoint (interaction between baseline galectin-3 and change in serum PIIINP) was **negative** (P=0.947 for interaction) — spironolactone did not reduce PIIINP overall, and galectin-3 did not identify a subgroup with a greater collagen-marker response. Secondary endpoints (blood pressure, NT-proBNP, PICP, PICP/CITP ratio, left atrial volume, LVEF) were favourable but exploratory and not adjusted for multiplicity; a composite clinical events endpoint showed no between-group difference (trial not powered for clinical outcomes). HOMAGE also separately contributed patient-level data to the later pooled echocardiographic IPD meta-analysis (Ferreira 2023, [[ferreira2023spironolactone]]).

## Contradictions
None specific to HOMAGE itself is registered as a cross-source contradiction. Note the within-trial primary/secondary discordance: HOMAGE's pre-specified primary endpoint (galectin-3 × PIIINP interaction) was negative, while secondary structural/haemodynamic endpoints were favourable — this reflects a neutral mechanistic primary endpoint with hypothesis-generating secondary findings, not a trial "success," per the authors' own caveats in [[cleland2021homage]]. The pooled analysis's own limitations (post-hoc echocardiographic subsample, non-identical protocols across trials, no clinical outcome data) are noted in [[ferreira2023spironolactone]].
