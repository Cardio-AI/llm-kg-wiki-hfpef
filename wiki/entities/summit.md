# SUMMIT

> Phase 3 RCT of tirzepatide (dual GLP-1/GIP agonist; Eli Lilly) in HFpEF with obesity — first HFpEF trial to show event reduction (38% decrease in CV death/worsening HF; HR 0.62; P=0.026); published NEJM 2025 (Packer M et al.; NCT04847557).

## Description

**Full title:** A Study of Tirzepatide (LY3298176) in Participants With Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction (HFpEF) and Obesity: The SUMMIT Trial  
**NCT:** NCT04847557  
**Sponsor:** Eli Lilly and Company  
**Intervention:** Tirzepatide (dual GLP-1 and GIP receptor agonist, LY3298176), 2.5 mg SC weekly up-titrated to maximum 15 mg weekly vs. matching placebo  
**Population:** HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%), obesity (BMI ≥30 kg/m²), NYHA II–IV, 6MWD 100–425m, KCCQ-CSS ≤80; T2DM permitted (HbA1c ≤9.5%)  
**N:** 731 (357 tirzepatide, 374 placebo)  
**Co-primary endpoints:**
1. Change in KCCQ-CSS at 52 weeks
2. Time-to-first CV death or worsening HF event  
**Status:** Published — NEJM 2025;392(5):427-437 (online November 16, 2024; print January 30, 2025)

Tirzepatide co-activates both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, distinguishing it mechanistically from semaglutide (pure GLP-1 agonist, [[step-hfpef]]). GIP co-agonism amplifies weight loss (SURMOUNT-1 obesity data: ~22% vs. ~15% with semaglutide) and may preserve lean mass through GIP receptor expression in adipose tissue.

**SUMMIT vs. STEP-HFpEF:**

| Feature | SUMMIT | STEP-HFpEF |
|---|---|---|
| Drug | Tirzepatide (GLP-1/GIP dual) | Semaglutide (GLP-1 only) |
| Sponsor | Eli Lilly | Novo Nordisk |
| N | 731 | 529 |
| T2DM | Permitted (HbA1c ≤9.5%) | Excluded |
| Co-primary 1 | KCCQ-CSS change | KCCQ-CSS change |
| Co-primary 2 | CV death/worsening HF | Body weight % change |
| Hard event endpoint | Yes — powered; P=0.026 | No (exploratory; HR 0.08) |
| LV mass (imaging) | −11 g by CMR (P=0.004) | No change by echo |
| Published | NEJM 2025 | NEJM 2023 |

## Role in HFpEF

SUMMIT is the first HFpEF trial to demonstrate a statistically significant reduction in CV death and worsening HF with a GLP-1RA class agent, upgrading the evidence base for the obesity-HFpEF phenotype from "symptom improvement" ([[step-hfpef]]) to "event reduction." The obesity-HFpEF phenotype affects ~50–60% of HFpEF patients (BMI ≥30). Together, SUMMIT and STEP-HFpEF establish GLP-1RA/GLP-1+GIP agonism as an evidence-based intervention for this phenotype. (source: raw/2025-JACC-Packer-SUMMIT_study.pdf)

The 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA guideline (Class IIb, Level B-NR for GLP-1 RA in HFpEF with obesity) pre-dates SUMMIT. Updated guideline positioning is anticipated to Class I given the event endpoint, pending the next guideline cycle. (source: [[heidenreich2022aha]])

## Evidence

**Primary results (Packer M et al., N Engl J Med. 2025;392(5):427-437; doi:10.1056/NEJMoa2410027):**

| Endpoint | Effect | 95% CI | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| CV death/worsening HF (HR; co-primary) | 0.62 | 0.41–0.95 | 0.026 |
| KCCQ-CSS change (co-primary) | +6.9 pts | 3.3–10.6 | <0.001 |
| Body weight change (tirzep vs. placebo) | ~−13 to −15% vs. ~−2% | — | <0.001 |
| hsCRP change | −35 to −44% vs. −4 to −11% | — | <0.001 |
| 6MWD change | +19 to +37m vs. +10–11m | — | reported |

*Results above derived from secondary analyses pending primary PDF ingest.* (source: raw/2025-JACC-Packer-SUMMIT_study.pdf)

**CMR substudy (Kramer CM et al., JACC 2025;85:699-706; N=106):**
- LV mass: −11 g placebo-corrected (95% CI −19 to −4 g; P=0.004) — weight-loss dependent (r=0.34)
- Paracardiac adipose tissue: −45 mL (95% CI −69 to −22 mL; P<0.001) — driven by pericardial fat
- No change in LVEF, LV global strain, or LA volumes
- First RCT to show LV mass reduction by GIP/GLP-1 agonism in HFpEF (source: raw/2025-JACC-Kramer-SUMMIT-CMR_substudy.pdf)

**CKD subanalysis (Packer M et al., JACC 2025;85:1721-1735):**
- 61% had CKD (eGFR <60 by cystatin C)
- Benefit consistent across CKD/no-CKD; interaction P=0.86 (source: raw/2025-JACC-Packer-SUMMIT_study.pdf)

## Contradictions

**LV mass — tirzepatide vs. semaglutide:** SUMMIT CMR substudy shows LV mass −11 g (P=0.004) with tirzepatide; STEP-HFpEF echocardiographic substudy shows no LV mass change with semaglutide. Whether this reflects GIP co-agonism or CMR/echo sensitivity difference is unresolved. See [[contradictions]].
