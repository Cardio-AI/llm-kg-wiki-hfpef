# CAPACITY-HFpEF

> Praliciguat, a soluble guanylate cyclase (sGC) stimulator, did not improve peak VO₂ in metabolically-enriched HFpEF (adjusted difference −0.30 mL/kg/min, P=0.37); KCCQ overall summary score was significantly *worse* on active drug (P=0.007), and patients ≥70 years — the population most enriched for the hypothesised NO-deficient phenotype — showed a significant harm signal (P for interaction=0.004), driven likely by blood-pressure lowering in a preload-dependent population.

## Description

- **Intervention:** Praliciguat 40 mg/day vs. placebo (protocol amended mid-trial from an initial 1:1:1:1 dose-ranging design; 10 mg and 20 mg arms discontinued and excluded from efficacy analysis)
- **Population:** HFpEF, LVEF ≥40%, impaired peak VO₂ (<80% age/sex-predicted normal), NYHA II–IV, metabolic-phenotype enrichment (≥2 of: type 2 diabetes, hypertension, obesity [BMI ≥30], age ≥70); AF capped at ~20% of sample (source: udelson2020capacity)
- **N:** 196 randomised; primary analytic population n=143 (65 praliciguat, 78 placebo) after attrition; 59 centres, US and Canada only; enrolled Nov 15, 2017–Apr 30, 2019; sponsor Cyclerion Therapeutics (source: udelson2020capacity)
- **Primary endpoint:** Change in peak VO₂ from baseline to week 12
- **Full paper stub:** [[udelson2020capacity]]

## Role in HFpEF

CAPACITY-HFpEF was published in JAMA simultaneously (same issue, same day) with [[vitality-hfpef]] (vericiguat), the two sGC stimulators sharing an identical neutral-to-harmful conclusion and effectively closing the sGC-stimulator drug class for [[hfpef]]. Together with [[neat-hfpef]] (isosorbide mononitrate), [[indie-hfpef]] (inorganic nitrate), and [[relax]] (sildenafil/PDE5i), CAPACITY-HFpEF is part of a five-trial series converging on the failure of the NO/cGMP therapeutic hypothesis in the general HFpEF population. Its most striking finding is that the enrichment criterion (age ≥70, intended to select for the NO-deficient metabolic phenotype most likely to respond) instead identified the subgroup that was significantly *harmed*, suggesting the sGC-cGMP pathway is not the primary impairment driver in this population, or that the drug's blood-pressure-lowering effect is poorly tolerated in older, preload-dependent HFpEF patients. (source: udelson2020capacity)

## Evidence

- **Peak VO₂ (primary): NEUTRAL** — adjusted difference −0.30 mL/kg/min (95% CI −0.95 to 0.35), P=0.37 (source: udelson2020capacity)
- **KCCQ overall summary score:** change −7.2 (95% CI −12.3 to −2.0), P=0.007 — significantly *worse* on praliciguat; KCCQ responders (≥5-point improvement) 41% praliciguat vs. 63% placebo, OR 0.43 (95% CI 0.22–0.84), P=0.01 (source: udelson2020capacity)
- **6-MWT:** adjusted difference −16.7 m (95% CI −47.4 to 13.9), P=0.28 — neutral overall, but responders (≥15 m) only 37% praliciguat vs. 59% placebo, OR 0.39 (95% CI 0.19–0.77), P=0.007 (source: udelson2020capacity)
- **VE/VCO₂ slope and NT-proBNP:** both neutral (P=0.65 and P=0.50 respectively); no significant echocardiographic differences (source: udelson2020capacity)
- **Age interaction (harm signal):** patients ≥70y — treatment difference −1.22 (95% CI −2.44 to −0.46, harm); patients <70y — +0.46 (95% CI −0.39 to 1.32, trend to benefit); P for interaction=0.004 (source: udelson2020capacity)
- **Sex interaction:** men −0.89 (95% CI −1.74 to −0.04, harm); women +0.49 (95% CI −0.51 to 1.48, neutral/trend); P for interaction=0.04 (source: udelson2020capacity)
- **Blood pressure:** SBP fell −6.3 mmHg on praliciguat vs. −1.1 mmHg on placebo at week 12 — proposed mechanism for the harm signal in older, preload-dependent patients (source: udelson2020capacity)
- **Safety:** hypotension 8.8% vs. 0%; dizziness 9.9% vs. 1.1%; drug-related TEAEs 24.2% vs. 10.0% (source: udelson2020capacity)

## Status

- No guideline recommendation for praliciguat or sGC stimulators in HFpEF; trial was phase 2, neutral to harmful (source: udelson2020capacity)
- Contributed, together with [[vitality-hfpef]], to closing the sGC-stimulator class as a therapeutic avenue in HFpEF, in contrast to VICTORIA's positive vericiguat result in HFrEF (source: udelson2020capacity)

## Contradictions
- KCCQ was significantly worse on active drug (P=0.007) despite the trial specifically enriching for the hypothesised NO-deficiency metabolic phenotype — the mechanistically-targeted population fared worse, not better. (source: udelson2020capacity)
- Age ≥70 was one of the four enrichment criteria intended to select for NO-deficiency-phenotype responders, yet this exact subgroup showed the significant harm signal (P for interaction=0.004) — the enrichment strategy backfired in its most-enriched subgroup. (source: udelson2020capacity)
- Published the same day as the companion trial VITALITY-HFpEF (vericiguat, same drug class): both neutral on their primary endpoint with no quality-of-life benefit, closing the sGC-stimulator class for HFpEF — yet VICTORIA (vericiguat in HFrEF) was positive, implying EF-specific biology or a patient-selection problem specific to HFpEF trials. (source: udelson2020capacity)
