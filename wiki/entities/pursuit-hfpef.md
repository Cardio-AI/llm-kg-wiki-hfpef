# PURSUIT-HFpEF

> Prospective multicenter observational registry of acute decompensated HFpEF patients in the Osaka urban area, Japan (UMIN000021831); N=1,026 analyzed; the data source underlying Tamaki 2023's finding that combined high NLR + PLR predicts cardiac death.

## Description

- **Design:** Prospective, multicenter, observational registry of consecutive acute decompensated heart failure (ADHF) patients with preserved ejection fraction, run by collaborating hospitals in the Osaka urban area, Japan (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- **Inclusion criteria:** Acute decompensated HFpEF diagnosed using Framingham criteria; LVEF ≥50% (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- **Exclusions (for the NLR/PLR analysis):** Chronic kidney disease on dialysis; missing NLR or PLR data (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- **Enrollment:** 1,095 consecutive patients admitted from June 2016 to December 2020; after excluding dialysis patients (n=17) and those with missing NLR/PLR data (n=52), **1,026 patients** analyzed (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- **Data coordination:** Anonymized data transferred to the data center of Osaka University Hospital for analysis (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- **Study protocol:** Design published separately as a protocol paper (Suna S, Hikoso S, Yamada T, et al. Study protocol for the PURSUIT-HFpEF study: a prospective, multicenter, observational study of patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction. *BMJ Open.* 2020;10:e038294) (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- **Endpoints:** Cardiac death (primary); all-cause death (secondary) (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- **Follow-up:** Median 429 days after discharge (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- **Full paper stub:** [[tamaki2023nlrplr]]

## Role in HFpEF

PURSUIT-HFpEF is a named, Japanese, single-region (Osaka urban area) prospective ADHF-HFpEF registry that provides the population base for inflammatory-biomarker prognostication research in HFpEF. Its cohort is elderly (median age 83 years), 55% female, with LVEF 62%, high comorbidity burden (72% anemia, 46% atrial fibrillation, median eGFR 45 mL/min/1.73m² indicating moderate CKD), and essentially unexposed to SGLT2 inhibitors (~2% use) — reflecting an East-Asian ADHF-HFpEF population studied largely prior to widespread SGLT2i adoption (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR). This makes it a useful contrast cohort to Western HFpEF trial populations for biomarker generalizability questions. As an observational, non-ischemic-cardiomyopathy-agnostic ADHF registry, it complements interventional trial data by capturing real-world postdischarge outcomes in the acute HFpEF population (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR).

## Evidence

Using PURSUIT-HFpEF data (N=1,026), [[tamaki2023nlrplr]] found:
- Combined high neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR >4.5) and high platelet-to-lymphocyte ratio (PLR >193) predicted cardiac death: HR 2.66 (95% CI 1.51–4.70; P=0.0008) — the strongest predictor identified (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- High NLR alone: HR 2.49 (1.51–4.10; P=0.0003); high PLR alone: HR 1.76 (1.09–2.84; P=0.0215) (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- Serial (admission + discharge) high NLR+PLR: HR 2.71 (1.33–5.53; P=0.0061) for cardiac death — temporal persistence of inflammation was a stronger predictor than a single timepoint measurement (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- CRP tertiles were **not** independently associated with cardiac death in the multivariable model, dissociating from the NLR/PLR signal (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)
- 195 total deaths occurred over follow-up (85 cardiac, 110 non-cardiac) (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR)

## Status

**Active/completed enrollment period reported:** Enrollment window June 2016–December 2020 for the cohort analyzed in Tamaki 2023; registry infrastructure (Osaka urban area collaborating hospitals, UMIN000021831) established via a separate 2020 protocol publication (source: Tamaki2023NLRPLR). No wiki source currently documents whether enrollment is ongoing beyond December 2020.
