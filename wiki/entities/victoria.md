# VICTORIA

> Vericiguat reduced CV death/HF hospitalisation (HR 0.90) in HFrEF — a positive result that directly contrasts with the neutral VITALITY-HFpEF, revealing EF-specific differences in the sGC stimulation pathway.

## Description

**Full title:** Vericiguat Global Study in Subjects With Heart Failure With Reduced Ejection Fraction  
**NCT:** NCT02861534  
**Intervention:** Vericiguat (sGC stimulator, titrated to 10 mg) vs. placebo  
**Population:** HFrEF, LVEF <45%, worsening HF (recent hospitalisation or IV diuretics), NT-proBNP elevated  
**N:** 5,050  
**Median follow-up:** ~10.8 months  
**Primary endpoint:** Composite of CV death + HF hospitalisation  
**Published:** NEJM 2020 (Armstrong PW, Roessig L, Patel MJ, et al.)

Vericiguat stimulates the soluble guanylate cyclase (sGC) enzyme directly — independently of nitric oxide (NO). In heart failure, impaired NO bioavailability leads to reduced sGC activity → reduced cGMP → impaired PKG signalling → myocardial stiffness (HFpEF) and adverse remodelling (HFrEF). Vericiguat works upstream of the cGMP degradation step targeted by PDE5 inhibitors such as sildenafil.

VICTORIA enrolled high-risk HFrEF patients (post-hospitalisation or IV diuretic use) — a population with substantially elevated NT-proBNP. The trial was positive, providing the evidence base for vericiguat's FDA approval in HFrEF.

## Role in HFpEF

VICTORIA is tracked in this wiki primarily because it was conducted in parallel with VITALITY-HFpEF — both testing the same drug (vericiguat) in opposite EF groups. VICTORIA was positive (HFrEF) while VITALITY-HFpEF was neutral. This divergence is a critical observation:

| Trial | EF | N | Primary result |
|---|---|---|---|
| VICTORIA | HFrEF (<45%) | 5,050 | HR 0.90, P=0.02 — positive |
| VITALITY-HFpEF | HFpEF (≥45%) | 789 | KCCQ-PLS neutral (P=0.47/0.80) |

The EF-specific divergence suggests that sGC stimulation — and possibly the NO/cGMP pathway generally — is more relevant to the pathophysiology of HFrEF (adverse remodelling, systolic dysfunction) than HFpEF (fibrosis, diastolic stiffness, peripheral limitation). (source: [[armstrong2020vitality]])

Together with RELAX (PDE5i, neutral), NEAT-HFpEF (organic nitrate, neutral), and INDIE-HFpEF (inorganic nitrate, neutral), VICTORIA-vs-VITALITY forms the final data point in the pattern of cGMP pathway failures in HFpEF.

## Evidence

Primary endpoint HR 0.90 (95% CI 0.82–0.98; P=0.02). NNT ~24 at ~10 months. Absolute risk reduction modest. Benefit concentrated in high-risk (high NT-proBNP) patients. Consistent with expected treatment effect in an enriched worsening HF population. [Source: Armstrong2020VICTORIA — pending full ingest.]

## Status

**Published:** NEJM 2020. FDA approved vericiguat (Verquvo) for HFrEF in January 2021.  
**Guidelines:** AHA 2022 Class 2b for vericiguat in HFrEF on top of GDMT. ESC 2021 does not include vericiguat (post-publication date at time of guideline). ESC 2023 Focused Update: not specifically included for HFrEF (focus was on HFpEF SGLT2i).  
**HFpEF:** No recommendation. VITALITY-HFpEF neutral. See [[vitality-hfpef]].

## Contradictions

VICTORIA (positive in HFrEF) vs. VITALITY-HFpEF (neutral in HFpEF) with the same drug class — strongest pharmacological demonstration of EF-specific pathway relevance. Also contrasts with SOCRATES-PRESERVED phase 2b NT-proBNP signal that did not translate to VITALITY phase 3 outcomes. See [[contradictions]].
