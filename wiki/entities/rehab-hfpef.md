## Description

**Full title:** Rehabilitation for Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction  
**NCT:** NCT05525663  
**Intervention:** Structured cardiac rehabilitation vs. usual care  
**Population:** HFpEF  
**Status:** Ongoing (referenced in Mahmood 2024 guideline systematic review)

Cardiac rehabilitation (CR) is a comprehensive, multidisciplinary programme encompassing exercise training, patient education, psychological support, and risk factor management — distinct from supervised exercise training (SET) trials that test only the exercise component. All major HFpEF guidelines recommend CR/exercise rehabilitation (ESC 2021/2023 Class IIa, AHA 2022 Class I, Level B-R). However, this recommendation is largely extrapolated from HFrEF data and the general exercise meta-analyses (Sachdev 2023: VO₂ +2.8 mL/kg/min). A dedicated HFpEF-specific CR RCT is absent. (source: [[mahmood2024guidelines]])

REHAB-HFpEF alongside REACH-HFpEF will provide this evidence. The distinction from SET trials (EX-DHF, OPTIMEX-CLIN, SECRET, HEX-CHF, HEART Camp): CR adds a non-exercise educational/psychological component; it is also a reimbursable service in most healthcare systems, making the trial design relevant to implementation as well as efficacy.

## Role in HFpEF

Mahmood 2024 identified exercise/cardiac rehabilitation as one of the most consistently recommended HFpEF interventions across 7 guidelines but noted the recommendation rests on relatively weak HFpEF-specific evidence compared to HFrEF. (source: [[mahmood2024guidelines]])

If REHAB-HFpEF is positive and documents improvements in VO₂, KCCQ, and HF events, it would strengthen the Class I recommendation for CR in HFpEF and facilitate reimbursement advocacy. (source: [[sachdev2023exercise]])
