# HFpEF

> Clinical syndrome of heart failure with LVEF ≥50%, elevated LV filling pressures, and a high comorbidity burden; no proven disease-modifying therapy across the full population.

---

## Description

HFpEF requires all three of:
1. Symptoms ± signs of heart failure
2. LVEF ≥50%
3. Objective evidence of structural/functional cardiac abnormality consistent with LV [[diastolic-dysfunction]] / raised LV filling pressures (raised [[natriuretic-peptides]] or echocardiographic/haemodynamic markers)

(source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

The LVEF ≥50% cut-off is acknowledged as arbitrary. LVEF is a continuous variable; its measurement by [[echocardiography]] carries substantial variability across sex, age, and imaging modality. Patients with very high EF (>65–70%) should prompt investigation for cardiac amyloidosis or HCM. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

Patients who previously had HFrEF and recovered LVEF to ≥50% should be considered 'HF with improved LVEF' rather than HFpEF; HFrEF treatment should be continued. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

## Role in HFpEF

HFpEF accounts for ~50% of all HF patients in population studies (though only 16% in the ESC Long-Term Registry outpatient setting vs. 60% HFrEF). HFpEF patients are older, more often female, and carry more [[atrial-fibrillation|AF]], CKD, and non-CV comorbidities than [[hfref]] or [[hfmref]] patients. Prognosis is generally better than HFrEF in observational studies, though the MAGGIC meta-analysis found considerably lower adjusted mortality risk in HFpEF vs. HFrEF. Many hospitalizations are non-CV in cause. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

Core mechanism: [[diastolic-dysfunction]] — impaired LV relaxation and increased myocardial stiffness → elevated filling pressures → pulmonary congestion and [[exercise-intolerance]]. HFpEF is pathophysiologically heterogeneous; underlying phenotypes include hypertensive heart disease, obesity-related HF, infiltrative cardiomyopathy (amyloid), and others. Treatment of the underlying phenotype may improve outcomes. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

**Physiologic HFpEF (HFpEF_phys):** Defined by invasive [[cardiopulmonary-exercise-testing|CPET]] as PCWP ≥15 mmHg at rest or ≥25 mmHg during exercise. In a referred cohort (n=461, LVEF ≥50%, NYHA II–IV), 53% met HFpEF_phys criteria. HFpEF_phys independently predicts CV events (HR 1.62, p=0.01) regardless of guideline classification. (source: 2019-CirculationAHA-Ho-exercise-response.pdf)

## Evidence

### Diagnostic Markers
Full marker table with sensitivities/specificities: → [[hfpef-diagnosis]] (canonical). Key threshold summary: E/e' >9 (sens 78%, spec 59%), LA volume index >34 mL/m², NT-proBNP >125 pg/mL (SR) or >365 pg/mL (AF). More abnormalities = higher probability. (source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf)

### Trial Landscape
HFpEF represents the core challenge described in [[hfpef-treatment-gap]]. No large RCT has convincingly reduced mortality or morbidity across the full HFpEF population. Key trials: [[charm-preserved]], [[i-preserve]], [[topcat]], [[paragon-hf]].

## Status

| Recommendation | ESC 2021 | AHA 2022 |
|---|---|---|
| Diuretics (congestion) | Class I, C | Class I, C |
| Comorbidity treatment | Class I, C | Class I, C |
| SGLT2i | No recommendation | **Class 2a, B-R** |
| MRA (spironolactone) | No recommendation | Class 2b, B-R |
| Sacubitril/valsartan | No recommendation | Class 2b, B-R |
| Exercise training | Class I (HFrEF) | Class I, A (EF-agnostic) |

(source: 2021-ESC-Guidelines-Heart-Failure.pdf; source: 2022-CirculationsAHA-Heidenreich-AHA-ACC-HFSA-Guideline-HF.pdf)

**SGLT2i are the first pharmacological therapy with a guideline recommendation above Class 2b for HFpEF** (AHA 2022 Class 2a, based on EMPEROR-Preserved + DELIVER). Benefit is driven by HF hospitalization reduction; CV mortality was not significantly reduced. **The mortality gap in HFpEF persists.** See [[hfpef-treatment-gap]] and [[guideline-comparison]] for full analysis.

**Exercise training** ([[supervised-exercise-training]]) remains the most consistently positive intervention for functional outcomes: peak VO2 +2.8 mL/kg/min (8 RCTs, n=503). (source: 2023-CirculationAHA-Sachdev-hfpef-exercise.pdf)
