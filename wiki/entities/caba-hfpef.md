## Description

**Full title:** Catheter Ablation for Atrial Fibrillation in Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction  
**NCT:** NCT05508256  
**Intervention:** Catheter ablation (pulmonary vein isolation) vs. conventional rate control  
**Population:** HFpEF with atrial fibrillation  
**Status:** Ongoing; DZHK (German Centre for Cardiovascular Research) international multicentre trial

AF affects ~50% of HFpEF patients and has a bidirectional relationship with HFpEF: elevated left atrial pressure drives AF, while AF reduces diastolic filling time and worsens filling pressures. The mechanistic rationale for ablation: restoring sinus rhythm reduces LA pressure and potentially reverses some of the atrial and ventricular remodelling. (source: [[atrial-fibrillation]])

Background evidence: EAST-AFNET4 showed that early rhythm control (pharmacological or ablation) reduced CV outcomes in patients with AF and comorbidities — a substantial fraction had HF. However, EAST-AFNET4 was not HFpEF-specific and enrolled predominantly paroxysmal/early-onset AF. CABA-HFpEF will provide the HFpEF-specific ablation evidence.

The DZHK institutional connection links CABA-HFpEF to the [[torch]] registry, [[zeid2025myomobile]], and the broader German HFpEF research infrastructure.

## Role in HFpEF

The AF phenotype is one of the 18 comorbidity phenotypes identified in the Anker 2023 HFA phenotype wheel (Figure 1). AF-adjusted NP thresholds are required for HFpEF diagnosis in AF patients (Pieske 2019 HFA-PEFF: BNP ≥105, NT-proBNP ≥365 in AF). (source: [[pieske2019hfapeff]])

If CABA-HFpEF is positive, catheter ablation would become the treatment of choice for the AF-HFpEF phenotype, adding rhythm control to the treatment wheel alongside SGLT2i and phenotype-guided add-ons. If negative, it would reinforce rate control as equivalent and inform the AF management strategy for HFpEF patients across all guidelines. (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]])
