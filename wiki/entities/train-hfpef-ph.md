# TRAIN-HFpEF-PH

> Randomised controlled multicentre trial testing whether standardised low-intensity exercise and respiratory rehabilitation improves 6-minute walk distance, quality of life, and haemodynamics in patients with HFpEF and invasively confirmed pulmonary hypertension — the first exercise trial designed specifically for this phenotype.

## Description

**Full title:** Implementation, Safety, Tolerability, and Effect of Exercise and Respiratory Training on 6-min Walking Distance in Patients With Pulmonary Hypertension and Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction (HFpEF-PH): A Randomized Controlled Multicenter Trial in European Countries
**NCT:** NCT05464238; registered 2022-07-19
**Sponsor:** Heidelberg University · **PI:** Ekkehard Grünig, MD (Thoraxklinik Heidelberg) · **Sites:** Germany, Lithuania
**Design:** Randomised (1:1), controlled, parallel-group with waiting-group crossover of the control arm, single-blind (outcomes assessor)
**N (target):** 90 (45 per arm)
**Status at last available data (2023 protocol publication):** Not yet recruiting; estimated primary completion 2025 Q4

**Intervention:** Standardised low-intensity rehabilitation (interval ergometer training, dumbbell training, respiratory therapy, mental/guided-walk training) — 3-week in-hospital phase followed by 12-week home continuation — vs. standard care, with control-arm patients offered the programme after 15 weeks.

**Population:** WHO/NYHA FC II–IV; HFpEF-PH confirmed by right heart catheterisation; LVEF ≥50%; stable diuretic therapy ≥1 month.

**Primary outcome:** Change in 6-minute walk distance (6MWD), baseline to 15 weeks. **Secondary outcomes:** WHO functional class, SF-36 quality of life, HADS anxiety/depression, Borg dyspnoea scale, clinical worsening, echocardiographic parameters, NT-proBNP, CPET parameters, optional invasive RHC/CMR at rest and exercise.

## Role in HFpEF

Addresses a population-specific gap in the HFpEF exercise-training literature: pulmonary hypertension is common in HFpEF (52–83% by echo, ~57% by invasive haemodynamics in cohorts cited in the design paper) but prior exercise trials in PH (PAH/CTEPH) explicitly excluded left-heart-disease-associated PH, and general HFpEF exercise trials ([[optimex-clin]], [[rehab-hf]], [[ex-dhf]]) have not focused on right-heart/pulmonary-vascular involvement specifically. TRAIN-HFpEF-PH tests whether a rehabilitation protocol already validated for safety/efficacy in PAH/CTEPH translates to this distinct, haemodynamically more burdened HFpEF subgroup.

## Status

**Ongoing — design/protocol published, no results yet:** Paleviciuté E, et al. *Trials.* 2023;24:281. doi:10.1186/s13063-023-07297-x. Estimated primary completion 2025 Q4 per the protocol; current recruitment/completion status not independently re-verified beyond the design paper.
