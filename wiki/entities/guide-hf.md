# GUIDE-HF

> Haemodynamic-guided management with CardioMEMS did not significantly reduce all-cause mortality + total HF events across all EF groups overall (HR 0.88, P=0.16), but a pre-COVID sensitivity analysis (excluding pandemic-disrupted care) showed borderline significant benefit (HR 0.81, P=0.049); the HFpEF subgroup (LVEF >40%, N=469) trended in the same direction but was not individually significant.

## Description

- **Intervention:** All patients implanted with CardioMEMS PA pressure sensor; randomised to haemodynamic-guided management (PA pressure data transmitted to the clinical team, who adjusted medications) vs. sham control (data collected but not transmitted) (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)
- **Population:** HF, NYHA class II–IV, across all EF categories, with prior HF hospitalisation or elevated BNP/NT-proBNP; ~47% LVEF >40% (broad HFpEF/HFmrEF) (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)
- **N:** 1,000 (497 treatment, 503 control); 118 sites, USA and Canada (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)
- **Primary endpoint:** All-cause mortality + total HF hospitalisations + urgent HF visits (composite rate ratio), 12-month follow-up
- **Full paper stub:** [[lindenfeld2021guidehf]]

## Role in HFpEF

GUIDE-HF provides the broadest EF-inclusive evidence base for CardioMEMS pulmonary artery pressure monitoring, extending the device-guided management strategy first validated in [[champion]] (NYHA III HF, LVEF-unrestricted, 37% HFpEF subgroup, HR 0.72 for HF hospitalisations) to a contemporary, larger, all-EF cohort. Whereas CHAMPION demonstrated a clearly significant hospitalisation reduction, GUIDE-HF's overall result was neutral — a difference attributable in large part to the COVID-19 pandemic, which disrupted usual care in the control group (more telemedicine, better remote management, lower control-arm event rates) and attenuated the apparent benefit of sensor-guided therapy. The pre-COVID sensitivity analysis, considered the more clinically meaningful estimate, aligns directionally with CHAMPION. (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)

## Evidence

- **Primary composite (all patients, all EF): NOT SIGNIFICANT** — HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.74–1.05), P=0.16 (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)
- **Pre-COVID sensitivity analysis** (patients/events before March 2020): HR 0.81 (95% CI 0.66–1.00), P=0.049 — borderline significant (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)
- **HFpEF subgroup (LVEF >40%, N=469):** HR 0.85 (95% CI 0.66–1.09), P=0.28 — directionally consistent but not significant; effect size similar across EF subgroups with no significant EF interaction (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)
- **Haemodynamic effect:** PA pressure-guided therapy reduced PA pressures in the active arm; more medication adjustments (diuretics, vasodilators) made in the active arm (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)
- **Comparison to CHAMPION:** CHAMPION (LVEF-unrestricted, 37% HFpEF, NYHA III only) showed a clearly significant 28% reduction in HF hospitalisations (HR 0.72, P=0.0002) over 6 months; GUIDE-HF's overall neutrality is attributed to COVID-era care disruption rather than a true absence of biological effect (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)

## Status

- No specific guideline recommendation change resulting from GUIDE-HF's overall neutral result; CardioMEMS clinical adoption and FDA approval predate GUIDE-HF and rest primarily on CHAMPION-era data (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)
- The pre-COVID analysis is regarded as supportive of the haemodynamic-guided management strategy but the overall trial result is officially neutral (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)

## Contradictions
- Overall primary result (HR 0.88, NS) vs. pre-COVID sensitivity analysis (HR 0.81, P=0.049): reflects the challenge of conducting an RCT during a pandemic rather than a true biological contradiction, but leaves the trial's headline result ambiguous. (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)
- CHAMPION demonstrated clear significant benefit in a similar device/mechanism (LVEF-unrestricted, NYHA III only); GUIDE-HF's broader, less-restricted population (NYHA II–IV, all EF) showed only a directionally consistent, underpowered effect — whether this reflects patient-population dilution or the COVID confound is unresolved. (source: lindenfeld2021guidehf)
