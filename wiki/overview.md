# Overview (HFpEF Wiki)

## High-Level Summary
HFpEF (heart failure with preserved ejection fraction, LVEF ≥50%) affects ~50% of heart failure patients and is characterised by a high comorbidity burden, dominant [[diastolic-dysfunction]], and — until 2022 — the complete absence of a proven disease-modifying pharmacotherapy. The wiki now covers both the 2021 ESC Guidelines (foundational scaffolding) and the 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA Guidelines (key update). The AHA 2022 guideline marks a partial treatment gap closure: SGLT2i elevated to Class 2a for HFpEF (EMPEROR-Preserved + DELIVER), making it the first pharmacological therapy with guideline recommendation above Class 2b for HFpEF. The EF taxonomy now includes a fourth category (HFimpEF). The mortality gap in HFpEF, however, remains open — EMPEROR-Preserved benefit was driven by hospitalization reduction, not CV mortality. See [[guideline-comparison]] for the ESC 2021 vs. AHA 2022 contrast.

## Key Concepts
- **[[hf-phenotype-classification]]**: EF-based three-way classification; LVEF cut-offs are pragmatic, not mechanistic; diagnostic decision tree from symptoms → NPs → echocardiography → LVEF → phenotype
- **[[hfpef-diagnosis]]**: Pragmatic approach using echocardiographic markers + NPs; AF requires adjusted thresholds (LA volume index >40 mL/m²; NP thresholds >365/>105 pg/mL); gold standard is invasive haemodynamic exercise testing
- **[[hfpef-diagnostic-definitions]]**: 7 competing definitions enroll 12–90% of same cohort; only 53% have invasively confirmed HFpEF_phys; HFpEF_phys independently predicts CV events (HR 1.62); trial populations are not interchangeable
- **[[diastolic-dysfunction]]**: Core mechanism — impaired LV relaxation → elevated filling pressures; assessed by E/e' (>9: sens 78%, spec 59%; >13: sens 46%, spec 86%), LA volume index, TR velocity >2.8 m/s; cardiac filling pressure is secondary (not primary) exercise limiter
- **[[exercise-intolerance]]**: Dominant HFpEF symptom; primary mechanism is skeletal muscle myopathy (A-VO2 diff >50% of VO2 reduction); cardiac (chronotropic incompetence ~50%), pulmonary, and vascular contributions secondary; SET reverses skeletal muscle myopathy
- **[[natriuretic-peptides]]**: BNP/NT-proBNP; key diagnostic biomarkers but limited sensitivity in obesity; AF-adjusted thresholds required
- **[[hfpef-treatment-gap]]**: No pharmacological RCT has convincingly reduced HFpEF mortality/morbidity across full population; SET (exercise training) is most consistently positive for functional outcomes; SGLT2i now positive post-2021; hard outcomes for SET unknown
- **[[atrial-fibrillation]]**: Major comorbidity in HFpEF; bidirectional relationship with elevated LA pressure; requires adjusted diagnostic thresholds
- **[[echocardiography]]**: Primary imaging modality for HFpEF; measures LVEF, diastolic markers, structural changes
- **[[cardiac-mri]]**: Gold standard LVEF; tissue characterisation; second-line for HFpEF workup
- **[[cardiopulmonary-exercise-testing]]**: Invasive CPET gold standard for HFpEF confirmation (PCWP) and exercise intolerance mechanistic dissection
- **[[supervised-exercise-training]]**: Class I, Level A (AHA/ACC 2022); peak VO2 +2.8 mL/kg/min; most consistently positive HFpEF intervention; hard outcomes unknown

## Active Debates
- **LVEF threshold for HFpEF**: ≥50% is arbitrary; EACVI uses sex-specific cut-offs; continuous EF distribution means the label is imprecise
- **TOPCAT controversy**: Americas subgroup suggests MRA benefit; non-Americas data (Russia/Georgia) suspected contaminated (urinary metabolites near-zero in controls); overall result negative — true treatment effect unresolved. Now AHA 2022 Class 2b based on Americas data alone.
- **Sacubitril/valsartan in HFpEF**: FDA endorsed for LVEF "below normal"; AHA 2022 Class 2b; ESC 2021 no recommendation. Is the PARAGON-HF signal real or driven by HFmrEF subgroup?
- **HFmrEF identity**: Recovering HFrEF vs. declining HFpEF vs. distinct phenotype? No dedicated trial evidence. AHA 2022 separates HFimpEF from HFmrEF.
- **SGLT2i: hospitalisation vs. mortality benefit in HFpEF**: AHA 2022 Class 2a based on EMPEROR-Preserved + DELIVER; benefit driven by hospitalisation, not CV mortality — partial gap closure only
- **SET hard outcomes**: Exercise training improves VO2 consistently but effect on mortality/hospitalization unknown; individual trials underpowered
- **Serum Mg and SGLT2i**: Ferreira 2026 (EMPEROR-Preserved secondary analysis) shows higher Mg → more empagliflozin benefit in HFpEF; opposite to HFrEF direction — mechanism and clinical implication uncharacterised
- **HFpEF operational definition**: ESC 2021 (Table 9 structural markers) vs. AHA 2022 (elevated filling pressures) may enroll different patient populations

## Knowledge Gaps
- **SGLT2i mortality gap in HFpEF**: EMPEROR-Preserved benefit was hospitalisation-driven; CV mortality was not significantly reduced — the core HFpEF mortality gap remains
- **SET hard outcomes**: Effect of exercise training on mortality and HF hospitalisation in HFpEF unknown — underpowered trials; dedicated powered RCT needed
- **HFpEF phenotyping**: Which HFpEF subtypes (obese, hypertensive, amyloid) respond to which treatments? No systematic taxonomy in current sources
- **Mg-SGLT2i biology**: Does the Mg-treatment interaction in EMPEROR-Preserved (higher Mg → more benefit) represent causal biology or statistical artefact? Is Mg supplementation a therapeutic target?
- **ESC 2025 update**: ESC 2021 will be superseded — anticipated to add SGLT2i as Class I/2a for HFpEF based on EMPEROR-Preserved + DELIVER; monitoring needed
- **HFmrEF-specific trials**: No prospective RCT in HFmrEF; all evidence extrapolated from subgroups
- **DECIPHER-HFpEF results**: CMR vs. PV loops validation study (NCT03251183); results not yet ingested; if CMR validates against invasive PV loops, could reshape non-invasive HFpEF diagnosis
- **CMR in HFpEF diagnosis**: Role of T1-mapping / ECV / diffuse fibrosis quantification as diagnostic/prognostic biomarker; DECIPHER-HFpEF is testing this directly
- **Sex differences in HFpEF**: HFpEF more common in women; PARAGON-HF sex-stratified signal for sacubitril/valsartan; sex-specific thresholds underexplored
- **AF-HFpEF interaction**: Dedicated AF management in HFpEF not addressed by current sources
- **HFpEF_phys in community populations**: Ho 2019 is a tertiary referral cohort; community prevalence unknown
