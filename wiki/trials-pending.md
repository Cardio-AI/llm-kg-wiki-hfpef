## Pending Trials

### RELAX
**Full title:** Phosphodiesterase-5 Inhibition to Improve Clinical Status and Exercise Capacity in Diastolic Heart Failure  
**Abbreviation:** RELAX  
**Intervention:** Sildenafil (PDE5 inhibitor) vs. placebo  
**Condition:** HFpEF, LVEF ≥50%  
**Study ID:** NCT00763867 [verify on ingest]  
**Reference:** Redfield MM et al. *JAMA.* 2013;309(12):1268–1277. doi:10.1001/jama.2013.2024  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[ho2019hfpefdefinitions]] — the RELAX trial enrollment criteria were one of 7 HFpEF definitions applied to the same cohort (Sen ~50%, Spec ~70% for invasive HFpEF)  
**Description:** Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of sildenafil (60–120 mg/day) in 216 patients with HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%) and exertional limitation. Primary endpoint was peak VO₂. Sildenafil did not improve peak VO₂, 6-minute walk distance, or clinical status vs. placebo — fully neutral result. Part of the series of neutral NO/cGMP-pathway trials in HFpEF alongside NEAT-HFpEF, INDIE-HFpEF, and VITALITY-HFpEF.

### ATTR-ACT
**Full title:** Transthyretin Amyloid Cardiomyopathy Clinical Trial  
**Abbreviation:** ATTR-ACT (also: ATTR-AMYLOIDOSIS trial)  
**Intervention:** Tafamidis (transthyretin stabiliser) vs. placebo  
**Condition:** ATTR cardiac amyloidosis (wild-type or variant) with HF symptoms  
**Study ID:** NCT01994889 [verify on ingest]  
**Reference:** Maurer MS et al. *N Engl J Med.* 2018;379(11):1007–1016. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1805689  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[heidenreich2022aha]] — cited as basis for Class I recommendation for tafamidis in ATTR-CM with HF symptoms  
**Description:** Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of tafamidis (20 or 80 mg) vs. placebo in 441 patients with ATTR cardiomyopathy followed for 30 months. Significant reduction in all-cause mortality (risk ratio 0.70) and CV hospitalizations. Established tafamidis as the first disease-modifying therapy for ATTR-CM. Relevant to HFpEF because ATTR-CM is an important HFpEF subphenotype that requires active exclusion.

### SOCRATES-PRESERVED
**Full title:** Soluble Guanylate Cyclase Stimulator in Heart Failure Patients with Preserved Ejection Fraction  
**Abbreviation:** SOCRATES-PRESERVED  
**Intervention:** Vericiguat (sGC stimulator, multiple doses) vs. placebo  
**Condition:** HFpEF, LVEF ≥45%, worsening HF  
**Study ID:** [verify on ingest]  
**Reference:** Pieske B, et al. *Eur Heart J.* 2017;38(15):1127–1138.  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[armstrong2020vitality]] — VITALITY-HFpEF was the phase 3 successor; SOCRATES-PRESERVED was the phase 2b dose-finding trial  
**Description:** Phase 2b dose-finding trial of vericiguat in HFpEF that showed a signal for NT-proBNP reduction and LA volume at higher doses. Results motivated the phase 3 VITALITY-HFpEF trial. Important for understanding the dose-selection and mechanism rationale behind the vericiguat programme in HFpEF.

### VICTORIA
**Full title:** Vericiguat Global Study in Subjects With Heart Failure With Reduced Ejection Fraction  
**Abbreviation:** VICTORIA  
**Intervention:** Vericiguat vs. placebo  
**Condition:** HFrEF, LVEF <45%, worsening HF  
**Study ID:** NCT02861534  
**Reference:** Armstrong PW, et al. *N Engl J Med.* 2020;382(20):1883–1893. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1915928  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[armstrong2020vitality]] — VITALITY-HFpEF (HFpEF) and VICTORIA (HFrEF) tested vericiguat in parallel; VICTORIA was positive while VITALITY was neutral  
**Description:** Phase 3 RCT of vericiguat in HFrEF (N=5,050) showing a significant reduction in the primary composite of CV death or HF hospitalisation (HR 0.90). Positive in HFrEF contrast with neutral VITALITY-HFpEF highlights an EF-specific difference in the NO/cGMP pathway. Relevant for guideline comparison and understanding drug class behaviour across EF spectrum.

### Butler 2022 — EMPEROR-Preserved EF Spectrum Analysis
**Full title:** Effect of empagliflozin in patients with heart failure across the spectrum of left ventricular ejection fraction  
**Abbreviation:** (secondary analysis of EMPEROR-Preserved)  
**Intervention:** Empagliflozin vs. placebo; EF-stratified subgroup analysis  
**Condition:** HFpEF/HFmrEF (LVEF >40%)  
**Study ID:** NCT03057951 (parent EMPEROR-Preserved)  
**Reference:** Butler J, Packer M, Filippatos G, et al. *Eur Heart J.* 2022;43:416–426.  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[solomon2022deliver]] — cited as context for DELIVER dual-primary design addressing concern about attenuated benefit at high EF  
**Description:** Pre-specified subgroup analysis of EMPEROR-Preserved showing that empagliflozin benefit appeared attenuated in patients with LVEF ≥65%. This finding motivated the DELIVER trial's dual-primary statistical design (overall + LVEF <60%). DELIVER subsequently found no EF heterogeneity, suggesting the EMPEROR-Preserved signal may have been a chance finding. Directly relevant to the EF-benefit relationship question for SGLT2i in HFpEF.

### SUMMIT
**Full title:** Semaglutide Treatment Effect in People with Obesity and HFpEF  
**Abbreviation:** SUMMIT  
**Intervention:** Semaglutide (GLP-1 receptor agonist) vs. placebo  
**Condition:** HFpEF with obesity  
**Study ID:** NCT04847557  
**Reference:** Referenced in Kittleson MM et al. *J Am Coll Cardiol.* 2023;81(18):1835–1878 as ongoing  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[kittleson2023acc]] — referenced as ongoing trial that will define GLP-1RA role in obese HFpEF  
**Description:** Phase 3 RCT evaluating semaglutide once weekly in patients with obesity and HFpEF. Primary endpoint: peak VO₂ and KCCQ composite. The SUMMIT trial is the first major RCT to test a GLP-1 receptor agonist specifically in HFpEF. The 2023 ACC ECDP references it as the evidence needed to establish semaglutide's role in obese HFpEF management; flagged alongside STEP-HFpEF (tirzepatide). Results expected to have major impact on comorbidity management recommendations.

### STEP-HFpEF
**Full title:** Study of Tirzepatide in People with Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction and Obesity  
**Abbreviation:** STEP-HFpEF  
**Intervention:** Tirzepatide (GLP-1/GIP dual receptor agonist) vs. placebo  
**Condition:** HFpEF with obesity  
**Study ID:** NCT04788511  
**Reference:** Referenced in Kittleson MM et al. *J Am Coll Cardiol.* 2023;81(18):1835–1878 as ongoing  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[kittleson2023acc]] — referenced alongside SUMMIT as ongoing GLP-1RA trial in obese HFpEF  
**Description:** Phase 3 RCT of tirzepatide (up to 20% weight reduction in SURMOUNT-1) in HFpEF with obesity. Tests whether GIP/GLP-1 dual agonism provides cardiovascular benefit beyond weight loss. The 2023 ACC ECDP flags lean muscle mass loss as a caveat (tirzepatide reduces lean mass as well as fat mass); STEP-HFpEF results will determine whether pharmacological weight loss is safe and beneficial in HFpEF.

### FINEARTS-HF
**Full title:** Finerenone Trial to Investigate Efficacy and Safety Superior to Placebo in Patients With Heart Failure  
**Abbreviation:** FINEARTS-HF  
**Intervention:** Finerenone (non-steroidal, selective mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist) vs. placebo  
**Condition:** HFpEF/HFmrEF, LVEF ≥40%, eGFR ≥25 ml/min/1.73 m²  
**Study ID:** NCT04435626  
**Reference:** Referenced in Anker SD et al. *Eur J Heart Fail.* 2023;25:936–955. doi:10.1002/ejhf.2894  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]] — cited as ongoing first test of non-steroidal MRA class in HFpEF; also referenced in [[kittleson2023acc]]  
**Description:** Phase III RCT evaluating finerenone (non-steroidal MRA) vs. placebo in patients with HFpEF/HFmrEF (LVEF ≥40%) and eGFR ≥25 ml/min/1.73 m². Primary endpoint: CV death + total HF hospitalizations. Finerenone has a more favourable electrolyte and renal profile than steroidal MRAs (spironolactone/eplerenone). Results will determine whether the non-steroidal MRA class overcomes the TOPCAT compliance/contamination limitations and whether the MRA question in HFpEF can finally be resolved.

### CABA-HFpEF
**Full title:** Catheter Ablation for Atrial Fibrillation in Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction  
**Abbreviation:** CABA-HFpEF  
**Intervention:** Catheter ablation (pulmonary vein isolation) vs. conventional rate control  
**Condition:** HFpEF with atrial fibrillation  
**Study ID:** NCT05508256  
**Reference:** Referenced in Anker SD et al. *Eur J Heart Fail.* 2023;25:936–955. doi:10.1002/ejhf.2894  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]], [[atrial-fibrillation]]  
**Description:** Phase III international multicentre RCT led by DZHK (German Centre for Cardiovascular Research) — the same network as the [[torch]] registry. First trial dedicated to testing catheter ablation vs. rate control specifically in HFpEF patients with AF. EAST-AFNET4 showed early rhythm control benefit in AF + HF broadly but was not HFpEF-specific. CABA-HFpEF will provide direct evidence for the AF phenotype branch of the HFpEF treatment approach.

### FAIR-HFpEF
**Full title:** Ferric Carboxymaltose Assessment of Morbidity and Mortality in Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction  
**Abbreviation:** FAIR-HFpEF  
**Intervention:** Intravenous ferric carboxymaltose vs. placebo  
**Condition:** HFpEF with iron deficiency  
**Study ID:** NCT03074591  
**Reference:** Referenced in Anker SD et al. *Eur J Heart Fail.* 2023;25:936–955. doi:10.1002/ejhf.2894  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]]  
**Description:** RCT of IV iron supplementation (ferric carboxymaltose) vs. placebo in HFpEF patients with iron deficiency. Primary endpoint: change in 6-minute walk test at 52 weeks. Iron deficiency affects 50–75% of HFpEF patients and is associated with reduced VO2, dyspnoea, and worse HRQOL. This trial, alongside PREFER-HF (NCT03833336), will determine whether IV iron corrects the exercise limitation caused by iron deficiency in HFpEF.

**Abbreviation:** SPIRIT-HF

### PEP-CHF
**Full title:** Perindopril in Elderly People With Chronic Heart Failure  
**Abbreviation:** PEP-CHF  
**Intervention:** Perindopril (ACE inhibitor) vs. placebo  
**Condition:** HF aged ≥70 years, LVEF >40%, echocardiographic evidence of diastolic dysfunction  
**Study ID:** [verify on ingest]  
**Reference:** Cleland JGF, Tendera M, Adamus J, et al. *Eur Heart J.* 2006;27:2338–2345.  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[pfeffer2019hfpef]] — included in the Pfeffer 2019 review of Phase III HFpEF RAAS trials (HR 0.92, P=0.55, neutral); oldest RAAS trial in HFpEF alongside CHARM-Preserved  
**Description:** Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of perindopril vs. placebo in 850 elderly HF patients (aged ≥70 years, LVEF >40%) with echocardiographic features of diastolic dysfunction. Primary composite endpoint (all-cause mortality + unplanned HF hospitalisation) was not significantly reduced (HR 0.92, P=0.55 at mean 26.2 months). Secondary endpoint HF hospitalisation also neutral (HR 0.86, P=0.38). Substantial drug discontinuation occurred by 1 year. Neutral result is consistent with other RAAS trials in HFpEF and is partly explained by inadequate follow-up and early attrition.

### REDUCE LAP-HF II (Interatrial shunt device)
**Full title:** Reduce Elevated Left Atrial Pressure in Patients With Heart Failure — Phase II  
**Abbreviation:** REDUCE LAP-HF II  
**Intervention:** Interatrial shunt device (L→R atrial shunt for LA pressure decompression) vs. sham  
**Condition:** HFpEF/HFmrEF with elevated LA pressure  
**Study ID:** NCT03088033  
**Reference:** [verify on ingest]  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[pfeffer2019hfpef]] — listed as one of two device-based HFpEF trials ongoing in 2019  
**Description:** Randomised, double-blind, sham-controlled trial of an implantable interatrial shunt device designed to reduce left atrial pressure by creating a controlled L→R shunt. Represents a haemodynamic (non-pharmacological) approach to the elevated filling pressure that is the central pathophysiological feature of HFpEF. This trial tests the hypothesis that direct LA pressure offloading, independent of drug therapy, can improve HFpEF outcomes.

### Minimally Invasive Pericardial Modification
**Full title:** [verify on ingest]  
**Abbreviation:** [verify on ingest]  
**Intervention:** Minimally invasive pericardial modification vs. sham/control  
**Condition:** HFpEF with suspected extrinsic pericardial constraint  
**Study ID:** NCT03923673  
**Reference:** [verify on ingest]  
**Referenced in wiki:** [[pfeffer2019hfpef]] — listed as one of two device-based HFpEF trials ongoing in 2019  
**Description:** Trial of a minimally invasive procedure to modify pericardial constraint in HFpEF, targeting the extrinsic (pericardial) component of elevated filling pressures that is particularly prominent in obese HFpEF. Pericardiotomy in animal models and small human studies has shown acute improvement in haemodynamics. This trial provides Phase II/III evidence for a device-based approach addressing extrinsic restraint rather than intrinsic myocardial stiffness.
