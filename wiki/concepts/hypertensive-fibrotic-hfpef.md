# Hypertensive-Fibrotic HFpEF Phenotype

> The hypertensive-fibrotic phenotype represents the classical HFpEF archetype: older patients with long-standing hypertension, concentric LV hypertrophy, and diastolic dysfunction driven by interstitial fibrosis; this phenotype has the best prognosis among HFpEF subtypes but is the most common and most studied, forming the background of most HFpEF RCTs.

## Definition and Epidemiology

**Clinical profile:** Hypertension (72% prevalence in HFpEF; Anker 2023), concentric LV remodelling, elderly, primarily non-obese
**Prevalence:** ~50% of HFpEF in Shah 2014 phenomapping cluster analysis (source: [[shah2015phenomapping]])
**Demographics:** Older patients (median age >70), more women, European ancestry enrichment relative to younger obese phenotype
**Prognosis:** Best among HFpEF phenotypes; 5-year mortality ~10–20% vs. ~50% for PH phenotype (source: [[shah2015phenomapping]])

## Pathophysiology

### Pressure Overload Pathway
Chronic hypertension → LV pressure overload → sarcomere parallel addition → ↑ wall thickness → concentric LV hypertrophy → ↑ LV mass → diastolic stiffness → elevated filling pressures (source: [[paulus2013novelparadigm]])

### Myocardial Fibrosis
Interstitial fibrosis is the structural hallmark:
- Fibroblast activation via TGF-β, aldosterone, angiotensin II
- Collagen deposition → extracellular matrix stiffness → ↑ ECV on CMR
- sST2 (soluble ST2) reflects fibrotic burden: meta-analysis HR 2.76 (95% CI 1.87–4.08; I²=0%; N=3,105; source: [[shi2022sst2]])
- ECV (extracellular volume fraction) is non-invasive fibrosis marker on CMR; does not correlate with MPR (r=−0.06), confirming fibrosis and CMD are independent mechanisms (source: [[arnold2022diamond]])

### Diastolic Dysfunction Mechanism
1. ↑ Titin stiffness (N2B isoform predominance in HFpEF) → reduced passive recoil
2. ↑ Interstitial fibrosis (collagen type I/III ratio) → impaired relaxation
3. Hypertensive LV hypertrophy → reduced lusitropy
4. NO deficit (inflammation-driven, less prominent than in obese phenotype) → ↑ titin phosphorylation loss
(source: [[paulus2013novelparadigm]])

## Key Clinical Features

|---------|-----------------------------|------------------------|

| BMI | Normal/overweight | Obese (≥30 kg/m²) |
| LV geometry | Concentric hypertrophy | Eccentric or concentric |
| NT-proBNP | Elevated | Lower (blunted by obesity) |
| CRP, IL-6 | Moderate | High |
| Fibrosis (ECV) | High | Moderate |
| EAT/pericardial fat | Low | High |
| Primary mechanism | Pressure overload + fibrosis | Metabolic-inflammatory |
| Best treatment evidence | BP control, SGLT2i | GLP-1 RA, SGLT2i |

## Treatment Evidence

### Blood Pressure Control
- ACC/AHA 2022 HF guidelines (Class 2a): SBP <130 mmHg reduces HFpEF risk (source: [[heidenreich2022aha]])
- HYVET: indapamide 64% reduction in HF occurrence in elderly hypertensives (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]])
- BP-lowering effect of RAAS inhibitors (ACEi, ARB, ARNi) partially explains benefit; BP-independent mechanisms remain uncertain
- SPRINT analysis supports SBP <130 mmHg benefit in HFpEF-risk groups (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]])

### SGLT2 Inhibitors (Benefit Across Phenotypes Including Fibrotic)
- EMPEROR-Preserved + DELIVER: consistent benefit including non-obese, non-diabetic HFpEF — overlaps with hypertensive-fibrotic phenotype (source: [[anker2021emperor]]; source: [[solomon2022deliver]])
- SGLT2i may reduce cardiac fibrosis via anti-inflammatory and renal mechanisms (source: [[albulushi2025sglt2fibrosis]])

### MRA (Spironolactone / Finerenone)
- TOPCAT: Americas subgroup showed benefit (HR 0.82; P=0.026 for HHF); overall neutral due to Russian site compliance issue (source: [[pitt2014topcat]])
- Finerenone (FINEARTS-HF, NCT04435626; Solomon 2024, NEJM; **published**): non-steroidal selective MRA in HFpEF LVEF ≥40%; N=6,001; total HHF + CV death rate ratio 0.84 (95% CI 0.74–0.95; P=0.007); KCCQ-TSS +1.6 pts (P=0.002); no significant effect on CV mortality alone. (source: [[solomon2024finearts]])
- MRA mechanism: anti-aldosterone → anti-fibrotic, anti-remodelling, anti-hypertensive — directly targets fibrotic phenotype mechanisms
- Current ESC position: "may be considered" after risk-benefit (hyperkalaemia) assessment (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]]); FINEARTS-HF may elevate finerenone above Class 2b for HFpEF in next guideline update

### ARNi (Sacubitril/Valsartan)
- PARAGON-HF: overall neutral, but LVEF 45–57% subgroup showed benefit; women showed benefit (HR 0.73 vs. HR 1.03 in men)
- PARAMOUNT: sacubitril/valsartan reversed LA remodelling vs. valsartan (LA volume −4.6 vs. +0.37 mL; P=0.003; source: [[solomon2012paramount]])
- ARNi effect partially via BP reduction + natriuretic peptide augmentation → anti-fibrotic/anti-remodelling pathway

### Anti-Fibrotic Therapy
[knowledge gap — no approved disease-modifying anti-fibrotic therapy for HFpEF]:
- Pirfenidone, nintedanib (studied in IPF): not tested in HFpEF
- GLP-1 RA reduce fibrosis biomarkers (indirect evidence via weight loss in obese phenotype)
- TGF-β pathway inhibition: investigational; no clinical evidence in HFpEF

## Biomarker Profile

| Biomarker | Value | Significance |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| NT-proBNP | Elevated (less blunted than obese) | Reflects wall stress |
| sST2 | Elevated | HR 2.76 for adverse outcome (source: [[shi2022sst2]]) |
| PIIINP/PICP | Elevated | Collagen synthesis markers; fibrosis extent |
| ECV (CMR) | ↑ (>30%) | Structural fibrosis measure |

## Cardiac Remodelling Patterns

Concentric geometry patterns predominate (source: [[shah2015phenomapping]]):
- Concentric hypertrophy (~50% of HFpEF): ↑ LV mass + ↑ relative wall thickness
- Concentric remodelling (~25% of HFpEF): normal LV mass + ↑ relative wall thickness
- QRS ≥120 ms independently predicts adverse outcomes in TOPCAT (HR 1.27; P=0.009); reflects advanced remodelling (source: [[joseph2016qrs]])

## LA Remodelling in Hypertensive Phenotype

LA dilation is especially prominent in hypertensive-fibrotic HFpEF:
- Chronically elevated filling pressures → LA wall stress → LA hypertrophy and fibrosis
- LAVI >34 mL/m² is a major ESC HFA-PEFF diagnostic criterion (source: [[pieske2019hfapeff]])
- AF substrate creation → bidirectional aggravation (source: [[anker2023hfpefphenotype]])

## Open Questions

- Does disease-modifying anti-fibrotic therapy improve hard outcomes in fibrotic HFpEF? (no current RCT)
- Does MRA provide specific benefit in the fibrotic subtype vs. other HFpEF subtypes?
- Can CMR ECV normalization serve as a treatment-response endpoint for anti-fibrotic trials?
- Is LVEF 45–57% a distinct response phenotype within hypertensive-fibrotic HFpEF?
