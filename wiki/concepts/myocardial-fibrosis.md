# Myocardial Fibrosis in HFpEF

> Myocardial fibrosis is a primary structural substrate of diastolic dysfunction in HFpEF; both focal and diffuse forms occur, are independently prognostic, and are driven by sustained comorbidity-driven inflammation and neurohormonal activation.

## Aliases
| Alias | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Interstitial fibrosis | alternate-name | diffuse form of myocardial fibrosis |
| Focal fibrosis | alternate-name | scar; detected as LGE on CMR |
| Diffuse fibrosis | alternate-name | detected by ECV/T1 mapping on CMR |
| Myocardial stiffness | mechanism | functional consequence of fibrosis |

## Types of Fibrosis in HFpEF

| Type | CMR Marker | Mechanism | Consequence |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Focal fibrosis (scar)** | Late gadolinium enhancement (LGE) | Ischaemia, inflammation, prior injury | Regional wall motion abnormalities; arrhythmia substrate |
| **Diffuse fibrosis (interstitial)** | ECV fraction (extracellular volume) on T1 mapping | Collagen deposition by activated fibroblasts | Uniform ↑ passive stiffness; impaired relaxation |
| **Reactive fibrosis** | ECV elevation | Hypertension, aging, metabolic | Reversible with treatment (partially) |
| **Replacement fibrosis** | LGE | Ischaemic scar, infiltration | Irreversible |

## Mechanisms Driving Fibrosis in HFpEF

Comorbidity-mediated pathway (Paulus-Tschöpe paradigm, source: [[paulus2013novelparadigm]]):
1. Obesity, diabetes, hypertension → systemic inflammation (TNF-α, IL-6, TGF-β)
2. Cytokines activate cardiac fibroblasts → ↑ collagen I/III synthesis → interstitial fibrosis
3. RAAS activation → aldosterone → fibroblast proliferation → ↑ fibrosis
4. Reduced NO bioavailability → impaired cGMP-PKG pathway → ↑ titin phosphorylation deficiency → ↑ passive stiffness

## Fibrosis and Prognosis

### sST2 as Fibrosis Biomarker (Shi 2022, 16 studies, N=2,761 HFpEF)

- **All-cause mortality:** log sST2 HR 2.76; I²=0%; P=0.013
- **Composite endpoint (mortality + hospitalisation):** HR 6.52; P<0.001
- sST2 = IL-33 decoy receptor; sST2 elevation → blocks IL-33/ST2L cardioprotection → ↑ fibrosis
- sST2 correlates with galectin-3 (fibrosis marker) and E/e' (diastolic dysfunction severity)
- **sST2 is a poor diagnostic biomarker** (AUC <0.70 for HFpEF diagnosis) but a strong prognostic marker (source: [[shi2022sst2]])

### Fibrosis vs. Microvascular Dysfunction (DIAMOND-HFpEF)

Critically, diffuse myocardial fibrosis (ECV) and microvascular dysfunction (MPR by CMR) are **uncorrelated** in HFpEF:
- ECV vs. MPR: r=−0.06, P=0.638
- Both independently predict death/HF hospitalisation
- They are **distinct and co-existing prognostic mechanisms**, not components of a single pathway (source: [[arnold2022diamond]])

## ECV Directly Predicts Invasive LV Stiffness (Rommel 2016 / STIFFMAP)

Invasive pressure-volume-loop validation (N=36, HFpEF vs. controls) shows ECV independently predicts the load-independent LV stiffness constant β (β-standardized=0.75, P<0.01) — the strongest of all noninvasive predictors tested (ahead of E/e' and LA volume index). ECV elevated in HFpEF vs. controls (32.9% vs. 28.9%, P<0.01). Splitting HFpEF by median ECV identified two mechanistically distinct subphenotypes: a high-ECV/high-stiffness (fibrosis-dominant) group, and a low-ECV group characterised instead by prolonged active-relaxation time-constant (τ) and exaggerated hypertensive response to afterload — demonstrating that diastolic stiffness in HFpEF is not always fibrosis-driven. Focal fibrosis (LGE) was present in only 4/24 HFpEF patients, reinforcing diffuse (not focal) fibrosis as the dominant structural abnormality where present. (source: [[rommel2016stiffmap]])

## CMR Assessment

- **T1 mapping (native):** Elevated native T1 → increased interstitial space; most reproducible measure of diffuse fibrosis
- **ECV fraction:** Gold standard for diffuse fibrosis quantification; ECV >27–28% typically abnormal
- **LGE:** Focal fibrosis/scar; present in 20–30% of HFpEF; pattern (subendocardial, midwall, epicardial) helps determine etiology
- DIAMOND-HFpEF demonstrated MPR and ECV are independently prognostic markers, not redundant (source: [[arnold2022diamond]])

## Treatment Targeting Fibrosis

- **MRA (spironolactone, finerenone):** Reduces aldosterone-driven fibroblast activation; some anti-fibrotic evidence in observational data
- **SGLT2 inhibitors:** Reduce myocardial fibrosis in preclinical models; clinical ECV data emerging
- **ARNi (sacubitril/valsartan):** Neprilysin inhibition → ↑ BNP-mediated anti-fibrotic signalling; PARAGON-HF: LA reverse remodelling suggests anti-fibrotic effect (source: [[solomon2012paramount]])
- **Anti-fibrotic agents** (e.g., pirfenidone, colchicine): under investigation; no completed HFpEF RCT [knowledge gap]

## Open Questions

- Is diffuse fibrosis (ECV) reversible with SGLT2 inhibitors, GLP-1 RA, or MRA in humans?
- Does ECV-guided therapy improve outcomes vs. standard biomarker-guided therapy?
- Are there HFpEF subphenotypes defined by predominantly fibrotic vs. vascular mechanisms with differential treatment responses?
