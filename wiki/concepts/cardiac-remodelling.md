# Cardiac Remodelling in HFpEF

> HFpEF remodelling is characterised by concentric LV geometry, myocardial fibrosis, LA dilation, and preserved systolic function — fundamentally different from HFrEF and driven by pressure overload, metabolic stress, and aging rather than volume overload or ischaemia.

| Reverse remodelling | treatment-response | recovery of abnormal geometry with therapy |

## LV Geometry Patterns in HFpEF

| Geometry | Definition | Prevalence in HFpEF | Prognosis |
|----------|-----------|---------------------|----------|
| **Concentric hypertrophy** | ↑ LV mass + ↑ relative wall thickness | ~50% | Intermediate | [needs source]
| **Concentric remodelling** | Normal LV mass + ↑ relative wall thickness | ~25% | Better | [needs source]
| **Eccentric hypertrophy** | ↑ LV mass + normal relative wall thickness | ~15% | Worse |
| Normal geometry | Normal LV mass + normal relative wall thickness | ~10% | Best |

Concentric patterns (HTN-driven pressure overload) predominate; eccentric (volume overload, ischaemia) less common but carries worse prognosis in HFpEF.

## Drivers of Remodelling

1. **Hypertension:** Chronic pressure overload → sarcomere parallel addition → ↑ wall thickness → concentric hypertrophy
2. **Aging:** ↑ myocardial stiffness, LV dilation, loss of cardiomyocytes → structural changes without significant pressure overload
3. **Metabolic stress (obesity, diabetes):** Lipotoxicity, mitochondrial dysfunction → cardiomyocyte hypertrophy + loss
4. **Inflammation:** Fibroblast activation → interstitial fibrosis → ↑ stiffness without hypertrophy
5. **Neurohormonal activation (RAAS, SNS):** Pathological hypertrophy, fibrosis

## QRS Duration as Remodelling Marker

Wider QRS in HFpEF reflects more advanced LV remodelling (TOPCAT, N=3,445):
- Wide QRS (≥120 ms) associated with: more LV hypertrophy, larger volumes, higher NT-proBNP, more comorbidities
- QRS ≥120 ms independently predicts HF hospitalisation (HR 1.38) and composite outcome (HR 1.27; P=0.009)
- Continuous risk starting at ~100 ms (source: [[joseph2016qrs]])

## LA Remodelling (Atrial Component)

See [[left-atrial-remodelling]] for details:
- LA dilation from chronically elevated filling pressures
- LA fibrosis → AF substrate
- PARAMOUNT showed ARNi reverses LA remodelling in HFpEF

## Reverse Remodelling with Treatment

- **ARNi (sacubitril/valsartan):** Reverses LA remodelling (PARAMOUNT, source: [[solomon2012paramount]])
- **Antihypertensive therapy:** Reduces LV wall thickness (LV hypertrophy regression) with BP control
- **SGLT2 inhibitors:** May reduce LV mass (some observational data); mechanism: reduce preload + afterload + inflammation [needs source]
- **GLP-1 RA:** Weight loss → reduced metabolic stress → partial LV geometry improvement [needs source]
