# Haemodynamics in HFpEF

> HFpEF haemodynamics exhibit a 'rest-exercise disconnect': filling pressures are elevated or borderline at rest, and dramatically worsen with exercise; both cardiac and vascular components are impaired during exercise, driving the symptom burden and limiting functional capacity.

## Diagnostic Haemodynamics (Right-Heart Catheterisation)

### Diagnostic Criteria (Invasive)
- **Rest diagnosis:** PCWP ≥15 mmHg
- **Exercise diagnosis (exertional HFpEF):** PCWP ≥25 mmHg during exercise **OR** rise ≥15 mmHg from rest to peak

### Exercise Right-Heart Catheterisation
- Gold standard for equivocal HFpEF cases (normal resting echo/NPs but symptoms of HF with exertion)
- Supine cycle ergometry; simultaneous cardiac output, PCWP, PA pressures
- Key limitation in obese HFpEF: measured PCWP may overestimate transmural LV filling pressure due to dynamic hyperinflation-driven intrathoracic pressure elevation (source: [[leahy2025heartlung]])

### NP-Guided Haemodynamic Management: Failure in HFpEF

NP targets derived from HFrEF trials do not translate to HFpEF:
- NP levels lower in HFpEF at equivalent filling pressures
- Aggressive NP-target diuresis → over-diuresis → renal impairment in HFpEF
- TIME-CHF HFpEF subgroup: NP-guided therapy tended to **worsen outcomes** (source: [[horiuchi2022npguided]])

Contrast: PA pressure monitoring (CardioMEMS) provides direct haemodynamic signal appropriate for HFpEF management (source: [[hemodynamic-monitoring]])

## Haemodynamic Reserve Impairment

The fundamental haemodynamic problem in HFpEF is inability to augment CO adequately while containing filling pressures during exercise:
- **Normal:** CO ↑ by vasodilation + HR increase; PCWP relatively flat
- **HFpEF:** CO ↑ limited (impaired contractile reserve); PCWP rises steeply (stiff LV requires more filling pressure to augment SV)
- Net: exercise intolerance from both inadequate CO and excessive filling pressure elevation

## Haemodynamic Profiles at Presentation

| Profile | Haemodynamics | HFpEF Subtype |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Wet and warm | ↑ PCWP, normal CO | Most common; congested |
| Dry and warm | Normal PCWP, normal CO | Some HFpEF with exertional symptoms only |
| Elevated PVR | ↑ PCWP + ↑ PVR | CpcPH phenotype; worst prognosis |
| Normal rest, abnormal exercise | Normal rest PCWP, ↑ exercise PCWP | Exertional HFpEF; requires exercise catheterisation |
