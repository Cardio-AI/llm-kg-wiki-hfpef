# Titin

> Titin is the major cellular determinant of myocyte passive stiffness in HFpEF; its stiffness is set by a dynamically modifiable isoform shift (compliant N2BA → stiffer N2B) and by phosphorylation state, both downstream of impaired NO–cGMP–PKG signalling — making it a mechanistically attractive but so far therapeutically unproven target.

## Mechanism

Titin is a giant sarcomeric protein spanning from the Z-disk to the M-band, functioning to prevent sarcomere overstretching; it is the major cellular determinant of cardiomyocyte passive (diastolic) stiffness. (source: damario2019cmd; source: Pfeffer2019HFpEF)

Diastolic stiffness attributable to titin in HFpEF arises from two mechanisms, both described as dynamically modifiable rather than fixed:

1. **Isoform shift:** a shift from the more compliant **N2BA** isoform toward the stiffer **N2B** isoform increases passive myocyte stiffness.
2. **Hypophosphorylation:** reduced titin phosphorylation — a direct consequence of reduced protein kinase G (PKG) activity — further reduces myocyte elastance.

(source: damario2019cmd)

### Position in the Paulus–Tschöpe Cascade

Titin hypophosphorylation is the terminal cellular step of the dominant mechanistic model for HFpEF: comorbidities (hypertension, T2DM, obesity, COPD, CKD) → systemic inflammation → coronary microvascular endothelial inflammation → ↓NO bioavailability → ↓cyclic GMP → ↓PKG activity → **titin hypophosphorylation** (with the N2BA→N2B isoform shift) + cardiomyocyte hypertrophy + interstitial fibrosis → diastolic stiffness → HFpEF. (source: Paulus2013NovelParadigm)

Human HFpEF LV biopsy data corroborate this pathway: reduced PKG activity and cGMP concentrations correlate with increased passive myocyte stiffness, alongside microvascular endothelial activation (upregulated E-selectin, ICAM-1), eNOS uncoupling, and reduced myocardial nitrite/nitrate concentration. (source: Pfeffer2019HFpEF)

The Borlaug 2023 JACC Scientific Statement frames titin phosphorylation changes as one of three components of "intrinsic myocardial dysfunction" (ventricular stiffening) — alongside collagen cross-linking and interstitial fibrosis — one of four overlapping HFpEF pathophysiological pathways; moderate fibrosis is present in 27% of patients on endomyocardial biopsy. (source: borlaug2023statement)

### Titin in the Type 2 Diabetes Phenotype

In the T2DM comorbidity phenotype specifically, a "titin switch → maladaptive relaxation" is named as one of several convergent mechanisms driving HFpEF, alongside advanced glycation end-product (AGE)–mediated myocardial stiffness/fibrosis, free fatty acid lipotoxicity, and hyperinsulinaemia-driven endothelial dysfunction. (source: Anker2023HFpEFPhenotype)

## Therapeutic Relevance

Because titin phosphorylation and expression are dynamically modifiable, they have been proposed as a potential therapeutic target. (source: Pfeffer2019HFpEF) The proposed route to modulating titin phosphorylation is pharmacological augmentation of the NO–cGMP–PKG axis — the same axis implicated in its dysregulation.

This route has not translated into clinical benefit. In RELAX, sildenafil (a PDE5 inhibitor) significantly raised myocardial cGMP (P=0.006) — confirming molecular target engagement upstream of PKG and titin — yet produced no improvement in peak VO₂ or any secondary functional endpoint, and worsened renal/neurohumoral markers relative to placebo. (source: Redfield2013RELAX) This dissociation between confirmed target engagement and absent functional benefit is consistent with the wider pattern of NO/cGMP-pathway drug failures in HFpEF (isosorbide mononitrate, inorganic nitrate, vericiguat), and implies that restoring cGMP/PKG signalling — and by extension titin phosphorylation — is not, by itself, sufficient to rescue exercise capacity in unselected HFpEF.

## Open Questions

- Can titin phosphorylation or isoform composition be therapeutically restored through a route other than upstream NO/cGMP/PKG augmentation, given that pathway's repeated clinical failure?
- Is the N2BA→N2B isoform shift reversible once established, or does it represent fixed structural remodelling?
- How much of the variance in myocardial passive stiffness across HFpEF phenotypes (hypertensive, obese/metabolic, T2DM) is attributable specifically to titin versus collagen-based fibrosis?

## Contradictions

The Paulus–Tschöpe/titin model predicts that restoring NO–cGMP–PKG signalling should reduce titin-mediated stiffness and improve function. RELAX confirmed cGMP target engagement with sildenafil but found no functional benefit — either titin hypophosphorylation is not rate-limiting for exercise capacity in unselected HFpEF, or pharmacological correction of cGMP alone is insufficient to normalise titin phosphorylation in vivo.
