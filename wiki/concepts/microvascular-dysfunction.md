# Microvascular Dysfunction in HFpEF

> Microvascular dysfunction is the most prevalent structural abnormality detectable on CMR in HFpEF (70%), is independently prognostic, and is mechanistically distinct from myocardial fibrosis — addressing both mechanisms simultaneously may be required for effective treatment.

## Prevalence in HFpEF

| Source | Population | CMD Prevalence | Definition |
|--------|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| DIAMOND-HFpEF ([[arnold2022diamond]]) | N=101 HFpEF (no obstructive CAD) | **70%** | MPR <2.0 on CMR |
| Controls | N=43 | 48% | MPR <2.0 |
| Other CMR series | Multiple | 50–70% | Variable |

CMD is present even in HFpEF patients without obstructive coronary artery disease — it is an intrinsic feature of HFpEF, not simply a consequence of epicardial disease.

## Prognostic Impact

Impaired MPR (per 1 SD increase) independently predicts death/HF hospitalisation in DIAMOND-HFpEF:
- Clinical model: HR 0.673 (P=0.038)
- Full imaging model: HR 0.690 (P=0.034)
- Optimal MPR cut-off 1.82 (ROC): significant Kaplan-Meier separation (log-rank P=0.020)
(source: [[arnold2022diamond]])

## CMD vs. Fibrosis: Independent Mechanisms

**A critically important finding from DIAMOND-HFpEF:**
- ECV (diffuse fibrosis) vs. MPR: **r=−0.06; P=0.638** — essentially zero correlation
- CMD and diffuse fibrosis are **independent, co-existing mechanisms** that both predict outcomes but arise through separate pathways
- Implication: A patient can have predominantly CMD or predominantly fibrosis or both — these are additive prognostic risks

## ECG Surrogate for CMD: fQRS

Anterior/lateral QRS fragmentation (fQRS) in HFpEF (source: [[sung2023fqrs]]):
- Associated with higher rates of **myocardial perfusion defects** and **coronary slow flow** on nuclear imaging/angiography
- HR 1.90 (P<0.001) for HF hospitalisation — comparable to CMD detected by CMR
- Anterior/lateral territory specificity aligns with LAD microvascular territory
- fQRS provides a **non-invasive, low-cost ECG surrogate** for CMD in HFpEF

## Mechanisms Driving CMD in HFpEF

From [[coronary-microvascular-dysfunction]] page:
1. Systemic inflammation → microvascular endothelial inflammation → impaired vasodilatory reserve
2. Hypertension → microvascular remodelling + rarefaction
3. Obesity/metabolic syndrome → microvascular dysfunction
4. EAT-derived paracrine inflammation → microvascular rarefaction

## Therapeutic Implications

[knowledge gap — no RCT has specifically targeted CMD in HFpEF]
- SGLT2 inhibitors: reduce microvascular inflammation; improve endothelial function
- Ranolazine (RALI-DHF): showed haemodynamic benefit in HFpEF; mechanism may include coronary microvascular effects
- Exercise training: improves peripheral and coronary microvascular function
