# HFpEF Fibrosis Paradigm

> Myocardial fibrosis and microvascular dysfunction are distinct, uncorrelated, and independently prognostic mechanisms in HFpEF; their coexistence explains why neither imaging parameter alone captures the full prognostic picture, and why dual targeting may be necessary.

## Aliases
| Alias | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fibrosis-centric HFpEF | alternate-name | emphasizes fibrosis as primary mechanism |
| HFpEF structural remodelling paradigm | alternate-name | includes fibrosis + geometry |

## Key Evidence: DIAMOND-HFpEF

CMR-based multiparametric study (N=101 HFpEF, 43 controls; Leicester; 3.1-year follow-up):

### Microvascular Dysfunction (CMD)
- Myocardial perfusion reserve (MPR) by adenosine stress CMR: **1.74 vs. 2.22 (controls); P=0.001**
- MVD prevalence (MPR <2.0): **70% HFpEF vs. 48% controls; P=0.014**
- MPR independently predicts death/HF hospitalisation in 3 adjusted models (HR 0.673–0.694; P=0.034–0.039)

### Diffuse Fibrosis (ECV)
- ECV by T1 mapping: elevated vs. controls (values above threshold in HFpEF)
- ECV independently predicts adverse outcomes

### Critical Finding: CMD and Fibrosis are Uncorrelated
- **ECV vs. MPR: r=−0.06; P=0.638** — effectively no correlation
- MPR similar in patients with vs. without focal fibrosis (LGE): NS
- Both **independently** predict outcomes — they are **parallel, not sequential mechanisms**

(source: [[arnold2022diamond]])

## Integration with Paulus 2013 Paradigm

The Paulus-Tschöpe paradigm ([[paulus2013novelparadigm]]) proposed comorbidity-driven inflammation → endothelial NO deficit → cardiomyocyte stiffness:
- Inflammation → fibroblast activation → fibrosis (one arm)
- Endothelial inflammation → microvascular dysfunction (separate arm)

DIAMOND-HFpEF data confirms these are **truly independent arms** — not downstream of a single shared mechanism.

## Implications for Diagnosis

- CMR multiparametric assessment (ECV + MPR + LGE + volumes) provides a more complete picture than any single parameter
- ECV alone underestimates the structural disease burden in HFpEF patients with predominantly CMD
- MPR alone underestimates burden in predominantly fibrotic HFpEF
- Both should be assessed when CMR is performed for HFpEF workup [knowledge gap — cost-effectiveness and clinical pathway not established]

## Implications for Treatment

| Mechanism | Current treatment targets |
|----------|--------------------------|
| Diffuse fibrosis | MRA, SGLT2i (preclinical), ARNi, GLP-1 RA (indirect) |
| Focal fibrosis | No direct target; upstream comorbidity management |
| Microvascular dysfunction | NO pathway augmentation; anti-inflammatory; SGLT2i |

Both mechanisms may need to be targeted simultaneously for meaningful benefit — a paradigm shift from single-mechanism drug development approaches.
