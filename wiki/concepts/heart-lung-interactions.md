# Heart-Lung Interactions in HFpEF

> In obese HFpEF, the lungs impose a mechanical load on the heart during exercise — dynamic hyperinflation raises intrathoracic pressure, externally compresses pulmonary vasculature, and elevates measured (atmospheric-referenced) PCWP beyond what true LV transmural filling pressure alone would predict.

## Core Mechanism

**Dynamic hyperinflation → ↑ intrathoracic pressure → external cardiac compression → ↑ measured PCWP:**

1. During exercise in obese HFpEF, EELV increases (dynamic hyperinflation)
2. Hyperinflated lungs → ↑ positive intrathoracic pressure (less negative than normal)
3. Pulmonary vasculature is compressed externally by hyperinflated lung tissue
4. PCWP measured in clinical catheterisation is referenced to atmosphere — includes contribution of elevated intrathoracic pressure
5. True transmural LV filling pressure = PCWP − pleural pressure → lower than measured PCWP

This mechanism is **additive** to intrinsic LV diastolic dysfunction; in obese HFpEF, both contribute to elevated measured PCWP.

## Evidence: Leahy 2025

**N=55 obese HFpEF (BMI 38±7 kg/m²):**

| Timepoint | DH Group (n=34–47) | No-DH Group | P |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------|---|
| 20W PCWP | 23±8 mmHg | 16±6 mmHg | 0.005 |
| Peak PCWP | 38±11 mmHg | 28±6 mmHg | 0.007 |

- ΔEELV correlated with ΔPCWP at 20W (r²=0.112; P=0.013) and peak (r²=0.167; P=0.002)
- **EFL severity did NOT correlate with PCWP** — DH and EFL are distinct phenomena; DH is the haemodynamically relevant driver (source: [[leahy2025heartlung]])

## Companion Finding: Babb 2026

After reducing PCWP with nitroglycerin (NTG) in 42 obese HFpEF:
- PCWP reduced significantly (P<0.01) — confirmed cardiac unloading
- **No change in lung volumes or breathing mechanics** — ventilatory ceiling unchanged
- **No improvement in exercise capacity** — patients stopped at same point

This confirms that DH → elevated PCWP is unidirectional: DH compresses the cardiovascular system, but lowering PCWP cannot undo the ventilatory constraint (source: [[babb2026ventilatorylimit]])

## Implications for HFpEF Diagnosis

- Exercise right-heart catheterisation (gold standard for HFpEF diagnosis) in obese patients may **overestimate true LV filling pressures**
- The diagnostic threshold (exercise PCWP ≥25 mmHg) may include cases where elevation is partly ventilatory rather than purely cardiac
- Transmural pressure correction (esophageal manometry for pleural pressure) would be needed for precise assessment — not routinely used [knowledge gap — clinical validation needed]

## Implications for HFpEF Treatment

- Haemodynamic interventions targeting PCWP reduction alone may not improve exercise capacity in obese HFpEF
- Weight loss (via GLP-1 RA or lifestyle) reduces abdominal/chest wall mass → reduces EFL and DH → lowers exercise PCWP through both cardiac (less EAT, less pericardial restraint) and ventilatory mechanisms
- Pericardial restraint from EAT (source: [[zamani2023pericardialfat]]) and DH are **additive** contributors to elevated filling pressures in obese HFpEF
