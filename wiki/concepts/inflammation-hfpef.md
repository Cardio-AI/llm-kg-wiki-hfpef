# Inflammation in HFpEF

> Systemic inflammation is both a cause and consequence of HFpEF; links comorbidities to myocardial dysfunction through endothelial injury, oxidative stress, and fibrosis; and provides actionable biomarkers (NLR, IL-6, CRP) for risk stratification and therapeutic targeting.

## Core Mechanism

HFpEF is characterized by **chronic activation of innate and adaptive immunity**, distinguishing it from structural inflammatory cardiomyopathies (myocarditis):

### Inflammatory Cascade in HFpEF
1. **Comorbidity-driven initiation:** Obesity, diabetes, hypertension, and aging trigger endothelial inflammation and macrophage infiltration
2. **Myocardial response:** Cardiomyocyte exposure to pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-α) drives oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, and increased passive stiffness
3. **Fibroblast activation:** IL-6, TNF-α, and angiotensin II promote cardiac fibroblast proliferation and collagen deposition → diastolic stiffness
4. **Endothelial dysfunction:** Reduced NO bioavailability from inflammation impairs vasodilatory capacity and myocardial relaxation
5. **Immune perpetuation:** Activated cardiomyocytes and fibroblasts produce cytokines → positive feedback loop

## Biomarkers of Inflammation in HFpEF

### Circulating Inflammatory Markers
| Marker | Role | Clinical Relevance |
|--------|------|-------------------|
| **IL-6** | Central pro-inflammatory cytokine; drives fibrosis and stiffness | Elevated in 40–50% HFpEF; correlates with diastolic dysfunction severity |
| **TNF-α** | Induces myocardial oxidative stress and fibroblast activation | Associated with worse exercise tolerance |
| **CRP** | Systemic inflammation proxy; non-specific | Often elevated; predicts hospitalization |
| **NLR** (neutrophil-lymphocyte ratio) | Innate/adaptive immune balance | **Independent mortality predictor** (source: [[boralkar2019nlr]]) |

### NLR as Prognostic Tool

[[boralkar2019nlr]] (N=443 ADHF-HFpEF, Stanford STRIDE):
- **Admission NLR** independently predicts all-cause mortality (HR 1.18; P=0.04) beyond GWTG-HF clinical risk score
- **NLR trajectory** (admission to discharge) is strongest predictor (HR 1.26; P=0.001)
- Serial measurement of NLR adds unique prognostic information beyond baseline
- Improved risk stratification at 1-, 2-, and 3-year follow-up

→ **Clinical interpretation:** Persistent elevation or worsening NLR during hospitalization marks patients at highest mortality risk and may indicate need for intensified post-discharge follow-up.

## Inflammatory Subtypes and Phenotypes

### Paulus 2013 Inflammatory Paradigm

[[paulus2013novelparadigm]] proposed that HFpEF is driven by **comorbidity-induced systemic inflammation** + **coronary microvascular endothelial inflammation**:

- Hypertension → endothelial oxidative stress → reduced NO → increased cardiomyocyte stiffness
- Diabetes + obesity → systemic TNF-α, IL-6 elevation → myocardial inflammation + fibrosis
- Aging + metabolic dysfunction → persistent immune activation

### Shah 2018 PROMIS Phenotyping

[[shah2018promis]] (prospective phenomapping, N=413 HFpEF):
- **Inflammatory phenotype** (elevated IL-6, TNF-α, CRP): ~30% of HFpEF cohort
- Associated with obesity, lower exercise capacity, higher NT-proBNP
- Distinct biomarker signature independent of EF or congestion markers

### Tamaki 2023 NLR + PLR Analysis

[[tamaki2023nlrplr]] (N=118 HFpEF):
- Both NLR (neutrophil-lymphocyte ratio) and PLR (platelet-lymphocyte ratio) independently predict HFpEF hospitalization
- Immune imbalance (neutrophil dominance, relative lymphopenia) associates with worse prognosis
- Suggests both innate (neutrophil) over-activation and adaptive (lymphocyte) suppression in severe HFpEF

## Mechanistic Links to HFpEF Pathophysiology

### Inflammation → Diastolic Dysfunction
- IL-6, TNF-α → increased ROS → impaired SERCA2a function → slowed LV relaxation
- Cytokine-driven fibroblast activation → myocardial fibrosis → stiffness (source: [[paulus2013novelparadigm]])
- Endothelial inflammation → reduced NO → impaired cardiomyocyte relaxation

### Inflammation → Exercise Intolerance
- Myocardial stiffness + impaired relaxation → blunted cardiac output reserve
- Skeletal muscle inflammation → impaired O₂ extraction → reduced exercise tolerance
- Vascular stiffness (arterial + microvascular) → elevated exercise BP, reduced capacity

### Inflammation → Atrial Fibrillation Substrate
- IL-6 driven atrial fibroblast proliferation → atrial remodeling
- Oxidative stress in atrial myocardium → electrical heterogeneity
- Comorbidities (obesity, diabetes) drive both inflammation and AF risk

## Therapeutic Implications

### Targeting Inflammation in HFpEF
- **SGLT2 inhibitors** (e.g., dapagliflozin, empagliflozin): reduce systemic inflammation + improve mitochondrial function
- **IL-1β inhibition** (e.g., anakinra, canakinumab): direct immune modulation; limited evidence in HFpEF
- **GLP-1 RA** (e.g., semaglutide): weight loss + inflammatory reduction
- **Physical activity:** exercise reduces IL-6, TNF-α, improves immune-metabolic balance

### Inflammation Biomarker-Guided Therapy
- NLR trajectory during hospitalization may identify patients needing intensified outpatient follow-up
- Serial IL-6 or CRP monitoring may guide therapy escalation (investigational)
