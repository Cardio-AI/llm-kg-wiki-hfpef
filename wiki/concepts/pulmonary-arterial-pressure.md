# Pulmonary Arterial Pressure in HFpEF

> Elevated PA pressures in HFpEF reflect backward transmission of elevated LV filling pressures; when PVR also rises (CpcPH), RV afterload increases, leading to the worst prognostic HFpEF phenotype; PA diastolic pressure monitoring enables early detection of subclinical congestion.

## Aliases
| Alias | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| PA pressure | abbreviation | pulmonary arterial pressure |
| PCWP | abbreviation | pulmonary capillary wedge pressure — LV filling pressure proxy |
| PAP | abbreviation | sometimes used; distinguish from PAH (arterial hypertension) |
| mPAP | measurement | mean pulmonary arterial pressure |

## Classification of PH in HFpEF

| Type | Haemodynamics | Prevalence | Prognosis |
|------|------------|-----------|----------|
| **Isolated post-capillary PH (IpcPH)** | ↑ PCWP; normal PVR (<3 WU) | ~70% of HFpEF | Better |
| **Combined pre/post-capillary PH (CpcPH)** | ↑ PCWP + ↑ PVR (≥3 WU) | ~20–30% | Worst |
| No PH | Normal PCWP; normal PVR | Minority of HFpEF | Best |

See also [[pulmonary-hypertension-hfpef]]

## PA Pressure as Monitoring Target

CardioMEMS device monitors **PA diastolic pressure (PAd)** — a surrogate for PCWP:
- PAd >18 mmHg indicates elevated filling pressures
- Rising PAd **1–2 weeks before symptom onset** enables pre-emptive outpatient intervention
- Daily transmission allows detection of subclinical congestion before decompensation
(source: [[abraham2011champion]], [[lindenfeld2021guidehf]])

### PA Pressure Values in HFpEF

At rest in invasively characterised HFpEF patients (source: [[reddy2017artstiff]]):
- Mean PA pressure: ~28 mmHg (vs. ~16 mmHg in hypertensive controls; P<0.0001)
- PCWP at rest: ~18 mmHg (vs. ~8 mmHg controls; P<0.0001)
- PCWP at 20W exercise: ~32 mmHg (vs. ~14 mmHg controls; P<0.0001)

## Dynamic Nature: Exercise Haemodynamics

PA pressures in HFpEF are characteristically **disproportionately elevated during exercise** vs. rest:
- Diagnostic criterion: PCWP ≥25 mmHg with exercise (or ≥15 mmHg rise) for exertional HFpEF
- In obese HFpEF, dynamic hyperinflation additionally elevates measured PCWP via intrathoracic pressure transmission (source: [[leahy2025heartlung]])

## Therapeutic Implications

- Diuretics: first-line to reduce PCWP → reduce PA pressure
- PA pressure-guided therapy (CardioMEMS): guides pre-emptive diuretic/vasodilator adjustment
- In CpcPH: addition of pulmonary vasodilators may be considered, but limited evidence; [[pulmonary-hypertension-hfpef]] covers this
