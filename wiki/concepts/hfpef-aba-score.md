# HFpEF-ABA Score

> A three-variable clinical screening score (Age, BMI, history of Atrial fibrillation) used prior to natriuretic peptide testing or echocardiography to assess HFpEF likelihood and triage patients toward echocardiographic referral.

## Aliases
| Alias | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ABA score | abbreviation | Age–BMI–AFib |
| Age-BMI-AFib score | descriptive | expansion of the acronym |

---

## Mechanism

The HFpEF-ABA score incorporates three clinical variables — age, body mass index (BMI), and a history of atrial fibrillation — to efficiently assess the likelihood of underlying HFpEF and identify patients who should be referred for an echocardiogram, applied *before* natriuretic peptide (NP) testing or echocardiography in the diagnostic sequence. It was developed by Reddy et al. (2024, *Nat Med*) as an evidence-based screening tool for HFpEF (source: achten2025screening).

Unlike the two established post-echo diagnostic scoring systems — H2FPEF and HFA-PEFF, which incorporate echocardiographic parameters (E/e', LA volume index, LV mass index, estimated RV pressure) — the HFpEF-ABA score operates at an earlier, pre-imaging stage, using only clinical variables that are readily available without specialist testing. (source: achten2025screening)

## Clinical Relevance

**Position in the screening pathway:** In the proposed stepwise HFpEF screening algorithm for individuals with obesity, the HFpEF-ABA score is used at the first step — population-level risk stratification — in combination with natriuretic peptide assessment, before any echocardiographic referral. High-risk individuals (BMI ≥30 kg/m² plus elevated NP with signs/symptoms) proceed to targeted echocardiographic workup. (source: achten2025screening)

**Performance:** The score outperformed NT-proBNP alone for pre-echocardiographic risk stratification (source: achten2025screening).

**Validation status:** Not yet validated in prospective screening populations specifically targeting individuals with obesity. This matters because HFpEF-ABA incorporates BMI as a component variable, yet its derivation and the broader diagnostic landscape it was tested against were not developed in an obesity-enriched screening cohort — the review notes this as an open uncertainty rather than a resolved validation. (source: achten2025screening)

## Evidence

- Developed by Reddy et al. (2024, *Nat Med*) — cited within the Achten 2025 review as the source of the score's three-variable structure and its stated superiority over NT-proBNP alone for pre-echocardiographic risk stratification (source: achten2025screening).
- No dedicated prospective screening study in an obese population has been reported; how effective the screening approach is in a population with obesity "remains uncertain" (source: achten2025screening).

## Open Questions

- What are the score's actual point weightings, cut-off thresholds, and sensitivity/specificity in the derivation cohort? — not detailed in wiki sources.
- Does the HFpEF-ABA score retain its performance advantage over NT-proBNP specifically in obese populations (BMI ≥30), or was its derivation cohort demographically different?
- Should BMI be replaced by waist circumference (better correlate of visceral/epicardial adipose tissue) as the obesity-related input variable?
- How does HFpEF-ABA performance compare directly to the machine-learning-derived score (Bermea 2024, using BMI/eGFR/LVMI/LA:LV volume ratio) — no head-to-head comparison reported.
