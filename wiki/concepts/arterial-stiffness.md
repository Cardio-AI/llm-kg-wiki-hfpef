# Arterial Stiffness in HFpEF

> Arterial stiffness in HFpEF is most critical during exercise — while resting values are similar to hypertensive controls, exercise unmasks dramatically impaired arterial compliance and increased wave reflections in HFpEF, contributing directly to elevated filling pressures and reduced cardiac output.

## Aliases
| Alias | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vascular stiffness | alternate-name | includes both large and small vessel stiffness |
| Pulse wave velocity (PWV) | measurement | clinical metric of arterial stiffness |
| Arterial elastance (Ea) | measurement | load parameter for LV-arterial coupling |
| Total arterial compliance index (TACI) | measurement | inverse of stiffness; decreases with stiffer arteries |

## Resting vs. Exercise: Key Distinction

**At rest:** Arterial stiffness (Ea, TACI, aortic pulse pressure) similar between HFpEF and hypertensive controls — stiffness is **concealed at rest** (source: [[reddy2017artstiff]])

**During exercise (20W):**

| Parameter | HFpEF | HTN Controls | P |
|----------|-------|-------------|---|
| Arterial elastance (Ea indexed) | 3.26±0.92 | 2.64±0.71 | 0.04 |
| Total arterial compliance (TACI) | 0.50±0.18 | 0.70±0.22 | <0.0001 |
| Peripheral pulse pressure amplification | ↓ (P=0.02) | — | — |
| PCWP | 32±7 mmHg | 14±5 mmHg | <0.0001 |

Exercise arterial stiffening **directly correlates** with higher exercise PCWP (r=−0.42 for TACI vs. PCWP) and lower CO (r=0.56 for TACI vs. CO) (source: [[reddy2017artstiff]])

## Mechanism

During exercise in HFpEF, normal vasodilation (mediated by endothelium-derived NO + ANP) is impaired:
- Reduced NO bioavailability → failure to dilate during exercise → ↑ reflected wave pressure → elevated aortic augmentation index (AIx)
- ↑ wave reflections → ↑ LV afterload at systole + ↑ diastolic pressure → impaired LV relaxation
- Combined: ↑ filling pressure + ↓ cardiac output → reduced exercise capacity

## SDB as a Driver of Arterial Stiffness (HFpEF-Specific)

Severe SDB (AHI >30/h) independently predicts higher pulse wave velocity (PWV) **in HFpEF but not HFrEF** (source: [[suzuki2018sdb]]):
- HFpEF: β=0.234; P=0.005 (adjusted for age, sex, BMI, HTN, DM, medications)
- HFrEF: P=0.068 (NS after adjustment)

Mechanism: SDB → intermittent hypoxia → sympathetic activation + ROS production → endothelial dysfunction → vascular stiffening

This creates a vicious cycle: SDB → arterial stiffness → exercise PCWP elevation → worsening HFpEF

## Reversal with Nitrite

Inorganic sodium nitrite (a NO precursor) at exercise (20W) vs. placebo in N=52 HFpEF:
- PCWP: −8±1 mmHg (P<0.0001)
- CO: +0.8±0.3 L/min (P=0.01)
- TACI: +0.10±0.02 (P=0.0005) — improved arterial compliance
- AIx: −8±2% (P<0.0001) — reduced wave reflections

Confirms NO pathway as a targetable mechanism (source: [[reddy2017artstiff]])

## Clinical Implications

- Arterial stiffness is a treatment target in HFpEF — BP control, particularly with agents improving NO bioavailability, may reduce exertional arterial stiffening
- SGLT2 inhibitors have shown some effect on arterial stiffness in HF
- Weight loss (GLP-1 RA) reduces sympathetic tone and vascular inflammation → reduced arterial stiffness
- NO donors (nitrites/nitrates): acute hemodynamic benefit shown; long-term RCT data limited
