# ECG Biomarkers in HFpEF

> The 12-lead ECG contains prognostic information beyond standard risk scores in HFpEF; QRS duration (conduction delay) and QRS fragmentation (microvascular scar) are distinct ECG phenotypes that independently predict hospitalisation and mortality.

## Aliases
| Alias | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Electrocardiographic biomarkers | alternate-name | preferred in formal settings |
| QRS abnormalities in HFpEF | alternate-name | general descriptor |

## QRS Duration

### Prevalence and Risk (TOPCAT, N=3,445)

- **QRS ≥120 ms prevalence:** 17.9% of HFpEF patients
- **Primary composite (CV death, arrested cardiac arrest, HF hospitalisation):** HR 1.27 (P=0.009)
- **HF hospitalisation:** HR 1.38 (P=0.003)
- **Continuous risk relationship:** Risk increases progressively; inflection near **~100 ms** (below standard LBBB threshold of 120 ms)
- **No treatment interaction:** QRS duration did not modify spironolactone effect (P for interaction NS)
- Wide QRS associated with: more LV hypertrophy, larger LV volumes, lower (but preserved) LVEF, higher NT-proBNP (source: [[joseph2016qrs]])

### Clinical Interpretation

QRS ≥120 ms in HFpEF identifies **more advanced LV remodelling** — a marker of myocardial disease severity, not a CRT target (as in HFrEF). The continuous relationship starting at ~100 ms suggests intraventricular conduction delay is on a spectrum with underlying myocardial disease.

## QRS Fragmentation (fQRS)

### Prevalence and Risk (N=960 HFpEF, 657-day follow-up)

| fQRS Category | Prevalence | HF Hospitalisation | CV/All-cause death |
|---------------|------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Non-fQRS | 68.4% | Reference | Reference |
| Inferior fQRS | 22.9% | NS | NS |
| **Anterior/lateral fQRS** | **8.7%** | **HR 1.90; P<0.001** | **Significantly elevated** |

- **Territorial specificity:** Anterior/lateral fQRS is prognostic; inferior fQRS is not
- Anterior/lateral fQRS associated with higher rates of **myocardial perfusion defects** and **coronary slow flow** — suggests microvascular disease (source: [[sung2023fqrs]])

### Mechanism

fQRS (splintering or notching of QRS, RSR' pattern without RBBB) reflects heterogeneous depolarisation due to:
- Myocardial scar (focal fibrosis, infarct)
- Microvascular disease / ischaemia → patchy muscle loss
- Interstitial fibrosis (diffuse) disrupting conduction

Anterior/lateral territory predilection suggests the left anterior descending microvascular territory as most vulnerable in HFpEF.

## Distinction: QRS Duration vs. fQRS

| Feature | Wide QRS (≥120 ms) | fQRS (anterior/lateral) |
|---------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Prevalence | 17.9% | 8.7% |
| Mechanism | Intraventricular conduction delay; advanced remodelling | Microvascular scar/ischaemia |
| Prognostic target | HFH, composite outcome | HFH, CV death, all-cause death |
| Clinical action | Risk stratification; not CRT target in HFpEF | Consider CMD/ischaemia workup |

## Clinical Utility

- Both markers are available from the standard 12-lead ECG at no additional cost
- Could augment existing HFpEF risk scores (H2FPEF, HFA-PEFF) for prognostication
- Anterior/lateral fQRS may prompt evaluation for coronary microvascular dysfunction (CMD) or subclinical ischaemia
- [knowledge gap: no RCT has tested ECG-guided risk stratification strategies in HFpEF]
