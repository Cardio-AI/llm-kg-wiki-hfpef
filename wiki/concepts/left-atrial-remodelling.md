# Left Atrial Remodelling in HFpEF

> LA dilation and dysfunction in HFpEF reflects chronic exposure to elevated LV filling pressures; LA remodelling is both a consequence of HFpEF and an amplifier — enlarged LA predisposes to atrial fibrillation, which further elevates filling pressures, creating a vicious cycle.

## Aliases
| Alias | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LA remodelling | abbreviation | structural and functional changes |
| LA enlargement | alternate-name | volumetric component |
| Atrial myopathy | mechanism | functional impairment of atrial reservoir/conduit/booster pump |

## Mechanism

Elevated LV filling pressure → ↑ LA afterload (LA must pump into stiff LV) → chronically elevated LA wall stress → LA hypertrophy and fibrosis → LA dilation → impaired atrial function:

1. **LA reservoir function:** First impaired — reduced LA reservoir strain
2. **LA conduit function:** Subsequently impaired — restricted passive filling
3. **LA booster pump function (atrial kick):** Lost in AF — critical for HFpEF where atrial contribution can be 30–40% of LV filling
4. **AF substrate:** LA fibrosis + electrical heterogeneity → re-entrant arrhythmias → AF → further loss of atrial kick

## Diagnostic Significance

LA volume index (LAVI) is a core diagnostic criterion for HFpEF across all major guidelines (ESC, ACC/AHA):
- LAVI >34 mL/m² (ESC HFA-PEFF) is a major diagnostic criterion for elevated filling pressures
- LAVI correlates with chronicity of elevated LA pressure — more reliable than single PCWP measurement
- See [[hfpef-diagnosis]] for diagnostic role

## Treatment Evidence: ARNi Reverses LA Remodelling

PARAMOUNT trial (N=301, HFpEF, LVEF ≥45%, sacubitril/valsartan vs. valsartan):

| Endpoint | ARNi (LCZ696) | Valsartan | P |
|---------|--------------|---------|---|
| **LA volume change (36 wk; mL)** | **−4.6** | **+0.37** | **0.003** |
| LA volume index change (36 wk) | Improved | Worsened | 0.007 |
| NT-proBNP ratio (12 wk) | 0.77 (23% lower) | Reference | 0.005 |
| Diastolic function (E/e', E/A) | NS at 36 wk | NS | NS |

(source: [[solomon2012paramount]])

**Clinical interpretation:** LA reverse remodelling suggests ARNi reduces LA wall stress over time (via BP reduction + neprilysin inhibition → natriuretic peptide → anti-remodelling); this persisted even when NT-proBNP difference attenuated at 36 weeks. Provided mechanistic rationale for PARAGON-HF (which ultimately did not meet the primary endpoint overall but showed benefit in LVEF 45–57% subgroup).

## Echocardiographic Assessment

- LAVI measurement: biplane Simpson's method; index to body surface area
- LA strain (speckle tracking): more sensitive early marker of LA dysfunction
- E/e' ratio: reflects LV filling pressure transmitted to LA; correlates with LA pressure
