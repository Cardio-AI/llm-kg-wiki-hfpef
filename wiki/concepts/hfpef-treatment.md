# HFpEF Treatment

> HFpEF has a marked treatment gap for mortality reduction — only SGLT2 inhibitors have proven hospitalisation reduction with a guideline class recommendation; symptom-focused treatment, comorbidity management, and device therapy provide additional benefit; pharmacological options remain far fewer than in HFrEF.

## Guideline Recommendations

### Class 2a / Moderate Evidence
| Therapy | Guideline | Indication |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| **SGLT2 inhibitors** (dapagliflozin, empagliflozin) | ACC/AHA 2022 Class 2a; ESC 2023 Class 1 | HFpEF, LVEF ≥50%; reduces HFH and CV death |
| **BP control** (<130/80 mmHg) | ACC/AHA 2022 PM-2 | HFpEF with hypertension; performance measure |
| **Diuretics** | Class 2a | Symptom relief for congestion |

### Class 2b / Weaker Evidence
| Therapy | Guideline | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------|
| **Spironolactone/MRA** | ACC/AHA Class 2b | TOPCAT: benefit in Americas subgroup; significant heterogeneity |
| **ARNi (sacubitril/valsartan)** | Class 2b | PARAGON-HF neutral overall; benefit in LVEF 45–57% subgroup and women |
| **Rhythm control** for AF | Not yet separately graded | Strong meta-analytic evidence for catheter ablation (see [[rhythm-control]]) |

### Not Recommended for Mortality Reduction
| Therapy | Evidence | Note |
|---------|---------|------|
| **Organic nitrates** | NEAT-HFpEF: no benefit | NP-guided therapy |
| **NP-guided titration** | TIME-CHF subgroup: trend to harm | Contrast to HFrEF benefit |
| **PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil)** | RELAX: no benefit | cGMP pathway approach failed |

## Pharmacotherapy Summary

### SGLT2 Inhibitors (First-Line)
- DELIVER (dapagliflozin) and EMPEROR-Preserved (empagliflozin): ~20% relative reduction in composite HFH+CV death
- Mechanisms: natriuretic effect, EAT reduction, anti-inflammatory, CaMKII inhibition, mitochondrial function
- Effective across subgroups; consistent effect regardless of diabetes status (source: [[anker2021emperor]]; source: [[solomon2022deliver]])

### Statins (Non-Ischaemic HFpEF)
- RICA registry (N=2,788 HFpEF): statin therapy HR 0.74 for 1-year mortality (P=0.002) in non-ischaemic HFpEF
- Benefit NOT significant in ischaemic HFpEF subgroup (HR 0.69; P=0.110)
- Mechanism: EAT reduction + anti-inflammatory + anti-fibrotic effects
- No HFpEF-specific statin RCT completed (source: [[ortegahernandez2024statins]])

### GLP-1 Agonists (Obese HFpEF)
- Semaglutide (STEP-HFpEF, SUMMIT): significant improvement in KCCQ and 6MWT in obese HFpEF
- Mechanism: weight loss → reduced EAT + pericardial restraint → reduced filling pressures; reduced SDB
- Now Class 2a or 2b depending on guideline; strongest evidence in BMI ≥30 [knowledge gap — long-term hard outcome data pending]

### MRA / Spironolactone
- TOPCAT overall: neutral; Americas subgroup: significant benefit; Russia/Georgia contamination concerns
- ALDO-DHF: improved E/e' but no QoL/exercise benefit (see [[edelmann2013aldodhf]])
- Generally used for comorbidity management (HTN, hyperaldosteronism) in HFpEF

### Haemodynamic Monitoring (Device)
- CardioMEMS PA pressure monitoring: 28% HFH reduction in CHAMPION; pre-COVID GUIDE-HF data supportive
- First-line device option for persistent high-risk HFpEF (see [[hemodynamic-monitoring]])

## Comorbidity-Targeted Treatment (Key)
- **Hypertension:** BP control <130/80 mmHg (PM-2)
- **AF:** Rhythm control superior to rate control (see [[rhythm-control]])
- **Obesity:** Weight loss (GLP-1 RA, lifestyle) — EAT reduction + ventilatory mechanics
- **Iron deficiency:** IV FCM (limited evidence, see [[iron-deficiency]])
- **ATTR-CM:** Tafamidis (Class I if ATTR confirmed; [[attr-cm]])
- **SDB:** CPAP/weight loss; no large HFpEF RCT

## Treatment Gap

Despite 20+ large RCTs, **no therapy has shown mortality benefit in HFpEF as a primary outcome**. SGLT2 inhibitors show the most robust evidence but the absolute mortality reduction is modest. The gap likely reflects HFpEF heterogeneity — phenotype-directed therapy may be required to match intervention to mechanism.
